## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Feeling dizzy and slightly faint, as if you might lose your balance.

> [!example] Examples
> - I felt lightheaded after standing up too fast.
> - If you skip lunch, you might get lightheaded during the lab.
> - The altitude made her feel lightheaded.

## 🔗 Collocations
- feel / get / become lightheaded
- a bit lightheaded
- lightheaded and dizzy

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Light* + *headed*; in the 16th century it could also mean "frivolous, foolish", a sense that is now rare.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in health conversations: *Do you feel lightheaded?*
> - Close to [[dizzy]]: *lightheaded* = feeling faint; *dizzy* often = the room is spinning.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[dizzy]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
