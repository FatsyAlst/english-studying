## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To repeat a word or phrase many times, usually loudly and rhythmically, especially as a group.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fans were chanting the team's name.
> - Protesters chanted slogans outside the parliament.
> - The crowd chanted "One more song!"

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A word or phrase repeated rhythmically by a group; a simple religious song sung on a few notes.

> [!example] Examples
> - The stadium echoed with football chants.
> - Gregorian chant is sung by monks.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Less Common)
To sing or recite a prayer or (in fantasy) a spell on one or a few notes.

> [!example] Examples
> - The monks chanted their morning prayers.
> - The mage chanted an incantation before casting the spell.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *chanter* "to sing", from Latin *cantāre*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In Mushoku Tensei, *chantless magic* = magic cast without reciting an incantation.
> - In sport, *chants* are the songs and slogans fans sing together.
> - Portuguese relatives: *cantar*, *canto* (cognate root).
