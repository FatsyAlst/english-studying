## 📖 Definition
*idiom* (Common — Informal)
To make a physical or mental effort; mostly negative — *without breaking a sweat* / *didn't even break a sweat* = very easily.

> [!example] Examples
> - She solved the problem without breaking a sweat.
> - He carried my suitcase up five floors and didn't even break a sweat.
> - Let's go for a run — I need to break a sweat.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Literally, to start sweating from effort; 20th-century American English. The figurative "with no effort" meaning comes from the negative form.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Mostly used in the negative to show something was easy.
> - The positive form can just mean *start exercising*.
> - Similar: *a piece of cake*, *effortlessly*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[cinch]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
