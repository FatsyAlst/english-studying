## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
(Of a quality or ability) present from birth; not learned or acquired.

> [!example] Examples
> - She has an innate talent for music.
> - Curiosity is innate in children.
> - Some researchers argue that language ability is partly innate.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *innātus* "inborn", past participle of *innāscī* "to be born in", from *in-* + *nāscī* "to be born" (cf. *native*, *nature*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
