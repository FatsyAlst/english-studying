## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To become worse in quality, condition or behaviour (often *degenerate into*).

> [!example] Examples
> - The discussion quickly degenerated into an argument.
> - His eyesight degenerated over the years.
> - The match degenerated into chaos.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
A person with very low moral standards; (internet/gaming slang, often humorous) someone with shameless habits.

> [!example] Examples
> - Only a degenerate would play ranked at 4 a.m. before an exam.
> - The story's hero starts out as a lazy degenerate.

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (Less Common)
Having low moral standards; worse than before.

> [!example] Examples
> - a degenerate lifestyle

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *dēgenerātus*, past participle of *dēgenerāre* "to fall from its kind", from *dē-* "down from" + *genus* "race, kind".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Pronunciation changes: verb di-JEN-uh-rayt; adjective/noun di-JEN-er-it.
> - *Degenerate into* is the key pattern for the verb.
> - *Degenerative disease* (medicine) = gets progressively worse.
> - A strong insult when used seriously about a person.
> - Portuguese cognate: *degenerar / degenerado*.
