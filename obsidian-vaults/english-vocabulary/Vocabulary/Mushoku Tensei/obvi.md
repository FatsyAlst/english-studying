## 📖 Definition
*adverb / interjection* (Common — Slang)
Short for *obviously*; used in very informal speech and texting.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Are you coming to the party?" "Obvi!"
> - I'll be late, obvi — the trains are on strike.
> - She's the best player on the team, obvi.

---
## 📜 Etymology
A clipping of *obviously*; spread in American youth slang and on social media in the 2000s–2010s. British speakers prefer *obvs*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very informal and playful (often ironic) — never in academic writing.
> - Similar clippings: *totes* (totally), *def* (definitely), *adorbs* (adorable).
