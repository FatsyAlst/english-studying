## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A cruel, violent and insensitive person, usually a man.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her ex-boyfriend was a brute.
> - Don't be such a brute — you'll hurt him!

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
A large, strong animal or thing; something big and difficult.

> [!example] Examples
> - That dog is a real brute.
> - This exam was a brute.
> - The final boss is an absolute brute.

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (Common)
Using only physical strength (or raw computing power) instead of intelligence, as in *brute force*.

> [!example] Examples
> - They opened the door by brute force.
> - A brute-force algorithm tries every possible solution.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *brut* "rough, raw", from Latin *brūtus* "dull, stupid, heavy".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Brute force* in programming = trying every possibility instead of a smart method.
> - *Brutal* = very cruel or harsh: *a brutal exam*.
> - Portuguese *bruto* is a partial false friend: *salário bruto* = gross salary (not *brute*), but *força bruta* = brute force ✓.
