## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Slang)
A person considered ordinary or conventional, with mainstream tastes and interests — especially from the point of view of internet, gaming or anime communities.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's such a normie — he's never even watched an anime.
> - This meme is way too niche for normies.
> - My normie friends don't get why I play Overwatch every night.

## 📜 Etymology
From *normal* + the informal suffix *-ie*; spread through internet forums and meme culture in the 2010s.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Slang — playful or mildly insulting.
> - Typical of gaming, anime and internet communities.
> - Avoid in formal contexts.
