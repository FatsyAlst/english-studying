## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
A person who lives alone and avoids other people, originally for religious reasons, now often by choice.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old hermit lived alone in a cabin in the forest.
> - During exam season I turn into a total hermit.
> - He became a bit of a hermit after he retired.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *(h)ermite*, from Late Latin *erēmīta*, from Greek *erēmitēs* "person of the desert", from *erēmos* "solitary, desolate".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used humorously for someone who rarely goes out.
> - *Hermit crab* = a crab that lives in an empty shell.
> - Synonym: *recluse* — see [[reclusive]].
> - Portuguese cognate: *eremita / ermitão*.
