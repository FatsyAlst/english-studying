## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To cut someone or something with a quick, violent sweeping movement using a sharp object.

> [!example] Examples
> - He slashed at the monster with his sword.
> - Someone slashed the tyres of my bike.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common — Informal / news)
To reduce something such as prices, costs or jobs by a large amount.

> [!example] Examples
> - The shop slashed its prices by 50%.
> - The government slashed the research budget.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
The punctuation mark ( / ); a long cut.

> [!example] Examples
> - The address is kuleuven.be slash en.
> - He had a deep slash on his arm.

## 📜 Etymology
Late Middle English *slaschen*, perhaps from Old French *esclachier* "to break", or imitative.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In speech *slash* can mean *and/or*: *She's a student slash streamer.*
> - Headlines love *slash* = cut dramatically.
> - Computing: forward slash ( / ) vs backslash ( \ ).
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[slit]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
