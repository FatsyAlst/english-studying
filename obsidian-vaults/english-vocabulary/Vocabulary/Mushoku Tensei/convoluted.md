## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Extremely complicated and difficult to follow or understand.

> [!example] Examples
> - The instructions for the lab report were so convoluted that nobody understood them.
> - The plot of the series is convoluted but fun.
> - The registration process at the town hall is incredibly convoluted.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Less Common — Technical / literary)
Having many twists, folds or coils.

> [!example] Examples
> - the convoluted surface of the brain
> - a convoluted path through the hills

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *convolūtus*, past participle of *convolvere* "to roll together", from *com-* "together" + *volvere* "to roll".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Negative: suggests unnecessary complexity.
> - Synonyms: complicated, intricate, tangled.
> - Engineering link: *convolution* (signals & systems, image processing, CNNs) comes from the same root.
> - Portuguese: *convoluto* (rare), *convolução* (maths/engineering).
