## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun / interjection* (Common)
The sound of something moving very fast through the air. (Also spelled *woosh*.)

> [!example] Examples
> - The train went past with a loud whoosh.
> - I heard the whoosh of an arrow.
> - Whoosh! The fireball flew across the room.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To move very fast with a rushing sound.

> [!example] Examples
> - A car whooshed past us.
> - The ball whooshed over my head.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative (onomatopoeic), from the mid-19th century, copying the sound of rushing air.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Onomatopoeia — common in comics, subtitles and sound design.
> - *Woosh* (as written in the original note) is a less common spelling.
> - Similar sounds: *swish*, *swoosh* (the Nike logo), *zoom*.
