## 📖 Definition
*adjective / verb (present participle of "billow")* (Common)
(Of smoke, clouds, steam or fabric) rising, swelling or spreading outward in large rolling masses or waves.

> [!example] Examples
> - Billowing black smoke rose from the factory.
> - Her billowing dress caught the wind.
> - Clouds of steam were billowing out of the kettle.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *bylgja* "a wave"; first used in English for large sea waves (16th century).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used for things pushed by air: smoke, steam, sails, curtains, loose clothes.
> - Very visual — common in descriptive writing.
> - Similar: *swell*, *puff out*, *balloon*.
