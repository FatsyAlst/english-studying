## 📖 Definition
*noun (plural, "the boonies")* (Less Common — Informal, AmE)
A remote area in the countryside, far from towns and cities; the middle of nowhere.

> [!example] Examples
> - My grandparents live way out in the boonies.
> - There's no phone signal out in the boonies.
> - They bought a cheap house in the boonies.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Short for *boondocks*, from Tagalog *bundók* "mountain"; brought into American English by US soldiers in the Philippines in the early 20th century.
