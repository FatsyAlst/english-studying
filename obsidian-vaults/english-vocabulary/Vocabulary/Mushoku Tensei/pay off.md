## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
(Of a plan or effort) to be successful; to bring good results.

> [!example] Examples
> - All those hours of practice finally paid off.
> - Her hard work paid off — she passed with distinction.
> - The risky strategy paid off.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (Common)
To finish paying back money that you owe.

> [!example] Examples
> - It took him ten years to pay off his student loan.
> - We've finally paid off the car.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb* (Less Common — Informal)
To give someone money so that they keep quiet or do something dishonest (to bribe).

> [!example] Examples
> - The witness had been paid off.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Pay* (Old French *payer*, from Latin *pācāre* "to pacify, satisfy", from *pāx* "peace") + *off*. The sense "to bring good results" is from the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 has no object: *It paid off.*
> - Sense 2 is separable: *pay off the debt* / *pay it off*.
> - Present perfect favourite: *It has finally paid off!*
