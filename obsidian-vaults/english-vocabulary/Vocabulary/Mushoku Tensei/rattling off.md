## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb (present participle of "rattle off")* (Common — Informal)
Saying or listing things quickly and easily from memory.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was rattling off the names of every Overwatch hero.
> - She rattled off the formulas without looking at her notes.
> - The waiter rattled off the specials so fast I didn't catch any of them.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Rattle* (Middle English *ratelen*, imitative of a quick series of short sharp sounds) + *off*: words come out in a fast, clattering stream.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable: *rattle off the list* / *rattle the list off* / *rattle it off*.
> - Suggests speed and ease (sometimes without much thought).
> - *Rattled* (adjective) = nervous, unsettled — a different meaning.
> - Synonym: *reel off*.
