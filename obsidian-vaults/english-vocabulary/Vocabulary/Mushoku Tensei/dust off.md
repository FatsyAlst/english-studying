## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Common)
To remove dust from something by brushing or wiping it.

> [!example] Examples
> - She dusted off the old books on the shelf.
> - He fell, got up and dusted himself off.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb (figurative)* (Common)
To start using something again after a long time without using it.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm dusting off my grammar book after a long break.
> - It's time to dust off my running shoes.
> - The band dusted off some old songs for the tour.

## 🔗 Collocations
- dust off old skills / notes / a textbook
- dust oneself off (after falling or failing)
- dust off an old plan / idea

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Dust* (Old English *dūst*) + *off*. The figurative sense — taking something out of storage to use it again — developed in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable: *dust off the book* / *dust the book off* / *dust it off*.
> - *Dust yourself off* = recover after a fall or a failure.
> - Related: *brush up on* (= improve a skill you haven't used for a while).
