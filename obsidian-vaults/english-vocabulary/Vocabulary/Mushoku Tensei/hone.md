## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To improve a skill or ability through practice over time.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm honing my English by writing a diary every day.
> - She honed her aim by playing Widowmaker for hours.
> - He spent years honing his craft.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Less Common)
To sharpen a blade or tool.

> [!example] Examples
> - The chef honed his knife before cooking.

## 🔗 Collocations
- hone your skills / craft / technique
- finely honed
- hone in on (variant of *home in on*)

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *hān* "stone"; the noun *hone* (a whetstone) later gave the verb "to sharpen", and then "to perfect".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Hone* = gradually perfect something you already have, not learn from zero.
> - *Hone in on* is widespread, but many style guides prefer the original *home in on* (like a missile).
