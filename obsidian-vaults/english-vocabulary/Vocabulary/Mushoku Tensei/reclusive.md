## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Living alone and avoiding other people; deliberately isolated from society.

> [!example] Examples
> - The reclusive billionaire rarely leaves his mansion.
> - After the scandal, she became increasingly reclusive.
> - He's a reclusive genius who works alone in his lab.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *recluse*, from Old French *reclus*, from Latin *reclūsus* "shut up", past participle of *reclūdere* (*re-* + *claudere* "to shut").

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes

> - Noun for the person: *a recluse* — see also [[hermit]].
