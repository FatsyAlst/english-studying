## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A long, loose outer garment without sleeves that hangs from the shoulders.

> [!example] Examples
> - The wizard wore a dark cloak.
> - She wrapped her cloak around her against the cold.
> - an invisibility cloak

### Sense 2 — *noun (figurative)* (Common)
Something that hides or covers something else.

> [!example] Examples
> - The plan was carried out under a cloak of secrecy.
> - A cloak of fog covered the city.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To hide or cover something (often passive: *be cloaked in*).

> [!example] Examples
> - The mountains were cloaked in mist.
> - The project was cloaked in secrecy.
> - The spaceship cloaked itself and disappeared from the radar.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old North French *cloque*, from Medieval Latin *clocca* "bell" — because of the cloak's bell shape. The same Latin word gave English *clock*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Cloakroom* = the place to leave coats at clubs, theatres and university events.
> - *Cloak-and-dagger* = involving secret plots and spies.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[shrouded]] (from [[My Hero Academia Vigilantes]])
