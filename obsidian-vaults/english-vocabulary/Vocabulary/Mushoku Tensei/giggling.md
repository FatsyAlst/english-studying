## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (present participle of "giggle")* (Common)
Laughing lightly in a nervous, silly or excited way that is hard to control.

> [!example] Examples
> - The kids were giggling at the back of the class.
> - Stop giggling — this is serious!
> - They couldn't stop giggling during the presentation.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
The sound or act of laughing in this way.

> [!example] Examples
> - I could hear giggling from the next room.

## 📜 Etymology
Imitative of the sound; first recorded in the early 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Associated with children, nervousness or embarrassment — adults also *get the giggles* at the worst moments.
> - Laugh scale: *chuckle* (quiet) → *giggle* (silly/nervous) → [[crack up]] (burst out laughing) → *cackle* (loud, harsh).
