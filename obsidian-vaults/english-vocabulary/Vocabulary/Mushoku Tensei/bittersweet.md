## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Producing happy and sad feelings at the same time; (of taste) both bitter and sweet.

> [!example] Examples
> - Leaving Brazil to study in Belgium was a bittersweet moment.
> - The ending of the series was bittersweet.
> - Graduation day is always bittersweet.
> - bittersweet dark chocolate

## 📜 Etymology
*Bitter* + *sweet*, recorded since the 14th century. The idea is ancient: the Greek poet Sappho called love *glukupikron*, "sweet-bitter".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very common for endings, farewells and nostalgia.
> - Portuguese: *agridoce* (for feelings and for taste).
> - Great word for diary entries.
