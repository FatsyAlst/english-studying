## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Rare — Literary / archaic)
To ask someone for something in an urgent, emotional way; to beg. Past: *besought* or *beseeched*.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Spare him, I beseech you!" she cried.
> - He besought the king for mercy.
> - The villagers beseeched the gods for rain.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Middle English *bisēchen*, from *be-* (intensive) + *sēchen* "to seek" — literally "to seek earnestly".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literary/archaic — common in fantasy and historical drama (and their translations).
> - Modern equivalents: *beg*, *plead*, *implore*.
> - *A beseeching look* = a look that begs.
> - Related noun: [[plea]].
