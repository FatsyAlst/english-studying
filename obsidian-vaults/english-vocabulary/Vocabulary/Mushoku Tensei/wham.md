## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *interjection* (Common — Informal)
Used to express the sound of a sudden hard hit, or to show that something happened suddenly.

> [!example] Examples
> - I was looking at my phone and — wham! — I walked straight into a glass door.
> - Wham! The door slammed shut.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Less Common)
A forceful blow, or the sound it makes.

> [!example] Examples
> - The car hit the barrier with a loud wham.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Less Common — Informal)
To hit something hard.

> [!example] Examples
> - He whammed the ball into the net.

## 🔗 Collocations
- and then — wham!
- wham into (something)
- wham, bam (comic style)

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative of the sound of a heavy blow; recorded in American English from the 1920s. Probably also the source of [[whammy]].

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Comic-book onomatopoeia (*Wham! Pow!*).
> - In storytelling, *and then — wham!* signals a sudden event.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[smack]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
