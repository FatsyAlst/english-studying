## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense of "whack")* (Most Common — Informal)
Hit someone or something very hard.

> [!example] Examples
> - He whacked the ball over the fence.
> - I whacked my head on the cupboard door.
> - She whacked the vending machine until the snack fell.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common — Informal, BrE)
Extremely tired; exhausted.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm absolutely whacked after that exam week.
> - You look whacked — go to bed.

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (Less Common — Slang, AmE)
Crazy or very strange (also *whacked out*).

> [!example] Examples
> - That plan is totally whacked.

## 🔗 Collocations
- whack something / someone
- give it a whack
- out of whack (= not working properly)
- have a whack at (= try)
- whacked out

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative of the sound of a sharp blow; recorded from the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Out of whack* = not working or not aligned properly: *My sleep schedule is out of whack.*
> - *Have a whack at it* = try it.
> - Don't confuse with [[wacky]] (= funny and strange).
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[clobbered]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[worn out]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
