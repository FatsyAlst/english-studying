## 📖 Definition
*expression* (Common — Formal)
Used to accept that something is true while saying that it doesn't change your opinion or what you are going to say; *even so*, *nevertheless*.

> [!example] Examples
> - The course is hard. Be that as it may, I'm not giving up.
> - "It's raining." "Be that as it may, the match will go ahead."
> - Be that as it may, we still need to submit the report by Friday.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Expression | be that as it may (fixed) |
| Related | even so, nevertheless, that said, regardless |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- Be that as it may, …
- But be that as it may, …

---
## 📜 Etymology
Uses the old subjunctive *be* (= "let that be as it may be"), the same structure found in *come what may* and *so be it*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal or slightly literary, but also used in speech for emphasis.
> - Usually starts the sentence and is followed by a comma.
> - A frozen subjunctive — the form never changes.
