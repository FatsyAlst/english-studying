## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Less Common)
A heavy iron block with a flat top on which a blacksmith hammers hot metal into shape; also one of the three small bones of the middle ear (the incus).

> [!example] Examples
> - The blacksmith hammered the red-hot sword on the anvil.
> - In cartoons, a heavy anvil always falls on someone's head.
> - The hammer, anvil and stirrup are tiny bones in the middle ear.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *anfilte*, from *an-* "on" + a verb meaning "to beat" — literally "something that is beaten on".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Metallurgy and blacksmithing vocabulary (also crafting systems in RPGs).
> - *Anvil cloud* = a cumulonimbus with a flat, spreading top (meteorology).
> - Ear bones: malleus (hammer), incus (anvil), stapes (stirrup).
