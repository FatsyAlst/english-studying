## 📖 Definition
*idiom* (Common — Informal)
To manage to understand something complicated, strange or hard to believe. Variants: *wrap one's brain / mind around*.

> [!example] Examples
> - I still can't wrap my brain around Fourier transforms.
> - It took me a while to wrap my head around the Belgian tax system.
> - I can't wrap my mind around how fast this year went.

## 📜 Etymology
American English idiom from the late 20th century: the image is of your mind physically surrounding — and so holding — an idea.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very common in the negative: *I can't wrap my head around it.*
> - *Head* is the most frequent; *brain* (as seen in Mushoku Tensei) and *mind* are common variants.
> - More neutral synonyms: [[grasp]], *comprehend*, *make sense of*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[grasp]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
