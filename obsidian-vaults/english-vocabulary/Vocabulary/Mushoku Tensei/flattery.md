## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
Praise that is too much or not sincere, especially when used to get something from someone or to please them.

> [!example] Examples
> - Flattery won't get you a better grade.
> - He used flattery to convince his boss.
> - Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *flaterie*, from *flater* "to flatter, caress, deceive".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually negative or ironic — it suggests insincerity.
> - But *I'm flattered* (= pleased and honoured) is positive and polite.
> - *Flattery will get you nowhere* — a joking reply to a compliment.
> - Related adjective: [[flattering]].
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[suck up]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
