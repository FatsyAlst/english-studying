## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A collection of valuable things such as gold, silver or jewels.

> [!example] Examples
> - The pirates buried their treasure on an island.
> - They found a treasure chest in the dungeon.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A very valuable object; a person who is loved and valued.

> [!example] Examples
> - The museum is full of art treasures.
> - Our neighbour is a real treasure — she always helps us.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To value something very much; to keep something carefully because it is important to you.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'll treasure this memory forever.
> - She treasures the letters her grandfather wrote.
> - Treasure your friends.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *tresor*, from Latin *thēsaurus*, from Greek *thēsauros* "storehouse, treasure" — also the origin of *thesaurus*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *A treasure trove* = a valuable collection: *a treasure trove of information*.
> - *Treasurer* = the person who manages a club's money.
> - Portuguese cognate: *tesouro*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[cherish]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
