## 📖 Definition
*idiom* (Common — Informal, mainly AmE)
To ignore something or someone; not to pay attention to it or worry about it.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's just in a bad mood — pay it no mind.
> - If anyone laughs at your accent, pay them no mind.
> - I heard a strange noise but paid it no mind.

## 📜 Etymology
*Mind* here means attention, as in *pay attention*. The construction is typical of informal American (especially Southern US) speech; British English prefers *take no notice*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Friendly and reassuring in tone.
> - Standard alternatives: *Don't worry about it*, *Ignore it*, *Take no notice*.
> - The object changes: pay **him / her / them** no mind.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[brush off]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
