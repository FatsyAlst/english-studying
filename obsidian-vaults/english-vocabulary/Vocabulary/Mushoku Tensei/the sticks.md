## 📖 Definition
*noun phrase* (Common — Informal)
Areas in the countryside that are far from cities and towns; a remote rural area.

> [!example] Examples
> - We live in the sticks of the outer border.
> - My family lives out in the sticks, two hours from the nearest city.
> - There's nothing to do in the sticks at night.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Early 20th-century American slang: *sticks* = trees, timber — the backwoods, far from the city.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always *the sticks*; informal and slightly negative or humorous.
> - Synonyms: [[boonies]], the middle of nowhere, the back of beyond (BrE).
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[boonies]] (from [[Mushoku Tensei]])
