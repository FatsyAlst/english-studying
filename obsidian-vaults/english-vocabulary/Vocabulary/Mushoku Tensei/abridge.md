## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Less Common — Formal)
To make a book, play, speech etc. shorter by removing parts while keeping the general meaning.

> [!example] Examples
> - The novel was abridged for younger readers.
> - I read the abridged version of *Les Misérables*.
> - The professor had to abridge the lecture because of the fire drill.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Rare — Legal)
To limit or reduce a right or freedom.

> [!example] Examples
> - The First Amendment says Congress shall make no law "abridging the freedom of speech".

- an abridged version / edition

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *abregier*, from Late Latin *abbreviāre* "to shorten" — *abridge* and *abbreviate* come from the same Latin word.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most often used as the adjectives *abridged* / *unabridged* (books, audiobooks).
> - Not related to *bridge*.
> - Portuguese: *resumir, condensar, abreviar*.
