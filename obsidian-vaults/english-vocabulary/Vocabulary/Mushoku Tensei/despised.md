## 📖 Definition
*verb (past of "despise") / adjective* (Common)
Hated very much and regarded with contempt; *despise* = to dislike someone or something strongly and have no respect for them.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was despised by everyone in the village.
> - She despised liars.
> - Spam calls are universally despised.

---

| Part of Speech | Forms |

| Verb | despise, despises, despised, despising |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- widely / universally despised
- despise someone for (something)
- utterly despise
- despicable behaviour

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *despire* (stem *despis-*), from Latin *dēspicere* "to look down on", from *dē-* "down" + *specere* "to look".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Stronger than *dislike* or *hate*: it includes contempt (looking down on someone).
> - A state verb — not used in continuous forms (*I'm despising* ✗).
> - *Despicable* = deserving to be despised.
> - Portuguese: *desprezar / desprezado*.
