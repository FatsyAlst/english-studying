## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
The quality, standard or level of ability of someone or something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The university attracts students of a very high caliber.
> - We need a player of her caliber on the team.
> - Engineers of that caliber are hard to find.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Technical)
The internal diameter of a gun barrel, or the diameter of a bullet.

> [!example] Examples
> - a .22-caliber rifle
> - large-caliber weapons

## 📜 Etymology
From French *calibre*, from Italian *calibro*, probably from Arabic *qālib* "mould, shoemaker's last".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Spelling: *caliber* (AmE) / *calibre* (BrE).
> - Engineering link: *calibrate* a sensor or instrument = adjust it to measure correctly.
> - Portuguese cognate: *calibre*.
