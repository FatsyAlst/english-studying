## 📖 Definition
*noun (plural)* (Less Common — Informal / old-fashioned)
Trousers, especially short trousers fastened just below the knee (*breeches*); today used mostly humorously and in idioms.

> [!example] Examples
> - The boy tore his britches climbing the fence.
> - He's getting too big for his britches since he got promoted.
> - Hold on to your britches — this is going to be fast!

---
## 📜 Etymology
A dialect variant of *breeches*, from Old English *brēc*, plural of *brōc* "garment covering the legs and trunk".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Too big for your britches* (AmE) = too self-important; BrE says *too big for your boots*.
> - Mostly humorous or old-fashioned today.
> - Pronunciation: BRIT-chiz.
