## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Informal, offensive, AmE)
A stupid, annoying or contemptible person; a jerk.

> [!example] Examples
> - Some schmuck parked across two spaces.
> - I felt like a real schmuck when I realised my mistake.
> - Don't be a schmuck — apologise to her.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Recorded in American English in 1892, from East Yiddish *shmok* (literally "penis"), probably from Old Polish *smok* "grass snake, dragon". Probably not the same word as German *Schmuck* "jewellery".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - An insult, but in modern AmE it's milder than its origin suggests.
> - Still vulgar for Yiddish speakers — avoid in formal situations.
> - *Poor schmuck* = an unlucky person you feel sorry for.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[chump]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
