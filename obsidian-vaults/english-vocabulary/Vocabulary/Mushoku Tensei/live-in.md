## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Living in the place where you work (for example in someone's house), or living with a partner in the same home.

> [!example] Examples
> - They hired a live-in nanny to help with the kids.
> - Her live-in boyfriend works from home.
> - The hotel has some live-in staff.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Adjective | live-in (opposite: live-out) |
| Verb phrase | live in (BrE: live at your workplace or in university accommodation) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- a live-in nanny / maid / carer / housekeeper
- a live-in partner / boyfriend / girlfriend
- live-in staff

---
## 📜 Etymology
A compound adjective formed from the verb phrase *live in* (20th century).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Only before a noun: *a live-in* [[nanny]].
> - Pronunciation: LIV-in (like *to live*).
> - *Live in* (two words, verb): *Most first-year students live in* (= in university halls).
