## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
The flat shelf at the bottom of a window, inside or outside.

> [!example] Examples
> - I keep a small basil plant on the windowsill.
> - The cat likes to sleep on the windowsill.
> - She left her coffee mug on the windowsill.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Noun | windowsill, windowsills (also *window sill*, *sill*) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- on the windowsill
- a kitchen windowsill
- a wide windowsill
- windowsill herbs

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Window* (Old Norse *vindauga*, literally "wind-eye") + *sill* (Old English *syll* "beam, threshold").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Also written as two words: *window sill*.
> - *Window ledge* is similar (often the outside part).
> - Useful house vocabulary for describing your room or flat.
