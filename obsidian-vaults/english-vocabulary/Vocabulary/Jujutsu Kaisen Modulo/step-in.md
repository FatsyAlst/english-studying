## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Phrasal Verb* (Most Common)
To enter a place or situation; to move into a space or position.

> [!example] Examples
> - He stepped in through the door.
> - She stepped in to greet us.
> - They stepped in from the rain.

### Sense 2 — *Phrasal Verb* (Most Common)
To intervene or take action in a situation, especially to help resolve a problem or take over a role.

> [!example] Examples
> - When the conflict escalated, the teacher stepped in.
> - A new manager stepped in after the resignation.
> - Friends stepped in to help during the crisis.

## 📜 Etymology
"Step" comes from Old English "steppan" (to step, walk); "in" is a particle indicating entering or involvement. Combined as phrasal verb meaning to enter or intervene.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (entering a space) or figurative (intervening)
> - Separable phrasal verb: "step in" or "step in and help"
> - Often implies taking control or responsibility
> - Common in both casual and formal contexts
> - "Step in for someone" means to substitute or replace temporarily
