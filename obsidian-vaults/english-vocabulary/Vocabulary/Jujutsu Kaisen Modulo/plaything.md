## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A toy or object that a child plays with.

> [!example] Examples
> - The teddy bear was his favorite plaything as a child.
> - Toys and playthings were scattered across the nursery.
> - The dog treated the ball as its plaything.

### Sense 2 — *Noun (figurative)* (Common)
A person or thing that is treated carelessly, used for amusement or advantage, without regard for their feelings or well-being.

> [!example] Examples
> - She refused to be his plaything any longer.
> - The environment must not become the plaything of corporations.
> - These workers are just playthings for the wealthy factory owners.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound word from "play" + "thing." The term has been used in English since at least the 16th century, originally referring to objects used in play or games.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The literal sense refers to toys or objects for play
> - The figurative sense carries a negative connotation, suggesting misuse or exploitation
> - More common in the figurative sense in modern usage
> - Formal English, appropriate for written communication
