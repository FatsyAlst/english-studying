## 📖 Definition
*Adverb* — Common

In a quiet, gentle, and submissive manner; showing patience and humility; without assertiveness or aggression.

> [!example] Examples
> - She meekly accepted the criticism without argument.
> - He meekly followed the instructions.
> - The child meekly apologized for the mistake.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the adjective "meek," possibly from Old Norse *mjúkr* (tender, soft, gentle), though the exact etymology is uncertain. The adverb form adds "-ly" to the adjective.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Expresses passivity, gentleness, and lack of resistance
> - Often used to describe someone accepting something difficult without complaint
> - Can have slightly literary or archaic connotation in modern usage
> - May carry subtle judgment: "meekly accepted" can imply someone should have resisted
> - Contrasts with "boldly," "assertively," or "defiantly"
> - Commonly appears in religious or character description contexts
