## 📖 Definition
*phrase/idiom* — Common

An expression indicating that something has been approved, confirmed, or is ready to happen; that a plan or event is officially happening or is definitely happening.

> [!example] Examples
> - The project is a go—we start next Monday.
> - Is the launch a go, or do we need to reschedule?
> - The concert is a go despite the rain forecast.

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- (something) is a go
- mission is a go
- launch is a go
- it's a go
- we're a go
- it's officially a go
- green light/go signal

---
## 📜 Etymology
From NASA/aerospace terminology in the 1960s, where "go" meant a system was ready or approved for launch. Extended to general use for any confirmed event or plan.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/colloquial register, commonly used in American English.
> - Often used as a status check: "Are we a go?" (Is everything confirmed?)
> - Opposite: "no-go" or "it's a no-go" (not happening, cancelled).
> - Comes from space program terminology but is now widely used.
> - Implies official approval or readiness to proceed.
