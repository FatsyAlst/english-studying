## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To strike repeatedly with fists or heavy blows; to beat or pound repeatedly.

> [!example] Examples
> - He pummeled the opponent with a flurry of strikes.
> - She pummeled the wall in frustration.
> - The warrior pummeled his enemy during the battle.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "pomelen," possibly from Old French "pomele," meaning "studded with knobs" or from a variant of "pommel" (a knob on a sword handle). Eventually came to mean striking repeatedly.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb meaning to strike repeatedly with force.
> - Common in action/battle sequences to describe intense physical combat.
> - Can be spelled "pummeled" (US) or "pummelled" (UK).
> - Emphasizes the repeated nature of strikes rather than a single blow.
