## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

An informal or affectionate term for grandfather; a colloquial family term of endearment for one's grandfather.

> [!example] Examples
> - I spent the afternoon at gramps' house.
> - Gramps always tells the best stories.
> - My gramps taught me how to fish.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Shortened form of "grandfather," which comes from Old French "grant" (great) + "father". The "mp" pronunciation likely came from colloquial shortening and affectionate speech patterns.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and affectionate in tone
> - More common in American English than British English
> - Typically used by grandchildren or close family members
> - Often shows a warm, familial relationship
> - Related terms: "grandpa," "granddad," "grandfather" (formal)
