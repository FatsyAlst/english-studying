## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Most Common)
(Of land) unable to produce vegetation; bleak and desolate with little or no plant life.

> [!example] Examples
> - The desert stretched out as a barren wasteland.
> - Barren rocks and sand filled the landscape.
> - After the fire, the forest became barren and lifeless.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
(Of a woman or female animal) unable to have children; infertile or unproductive.

> [!example] Examples
> - The barren earth seemed to mock their hopes for crops.
> - She was devastated by her barren condition.
> - The barren years of their marriage were difficult.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "berrr" (bare), related to "bare". Entered Middle English through Old French "baren".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Modern use primarily focuses on desolate landscapes
> - The fertility meaning is now considered outdated and potentially offensive
> - Often paired with other descriptive words (bleak, harsh, desolate)
> - Evokes imagery of emptiness and lifelessness
