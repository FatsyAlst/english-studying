## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun, humorous/literal* (Less Common)
A hat made from tinfoil, believed to block mind control; mentioned jokingly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The character wore a tinfoil hat to keep aliens from reading his mind.
> - He joked he needed a tinfoil hat to block signals.
> - Pictures of people in tinfoil hats mock conspiracy theories.

### Sense 2 — *noun, figurative, mocking* (Common)
A person who believes in extreme conspiracy theories; suggests paranoia.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm not some tinfoil hat who thinks the moon landing was fake.
> - They dismissed him as a tinfoil hat.
> - Online forums attract a lot of tinfoil hats.

### Sense 3 — *adjective, informal, mocking* (Common)
Describing paranoid, irrational, or conspiratorial ideas.

> [!example] Examples
> - That sounds like a tinfoil-hat conspiracy theory.
> - I don't want to sound like a tinfoil-hat nut, but something feels off.
> - The comment section is full of tinfoil-hat stuff.

## 📜 Etymology
From the real-world practice/trope of wearing aluminum foil hats, popularized in fiction and conspiracy culture, to supposedly block electromagnetic waves.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always informal and usually mocking.
> - Calling someone a "tinfoil hat" is insulting.
> - Sometimes used ironically in first person to soften a statement.
> - Common online, rare in formal writing.
