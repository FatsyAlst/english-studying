## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb (present participle)* (Most Common)
Making sharp, high-pitched barking sounds, typically used of small dogs.

> [!example] Examples
> - The small dog was yapping incessantly.
> - Dogs yapping outside disturbed the neighborhood.
> - A yapping Chihuahua ran past us.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common - Slang/Informal)
Talking persistently, loudly, or irritatingly; chattering or complaining continuously.

> [!example] Examples
> - Stop yapping and listen to what I'm saying.
> - She keeps yapping about her complaints.
> - He was yapping away on his phone.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Less Common)
The sharp, high-pitched barking sound; also used as the action of talking persistently.

> [!example] Examples
> - The constant yapping from the neighbor's dog was annoying.
> - I can't focus with all that yapping.

## 📜 Etymology
From imitative origin, likely echoing the sound of sharp barking. The term developed from onomatopoeia.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (dog barking): most literal and commonly used with animals
> - Sense 2 (persistent talking): informal and often carries negative connotation
> - Imitative word; the sound of "yap" mimics what it describes
> - Can be used as verb or noun depending on context
> - Sense 2 is somewhat derogatory when applied to human speech
> - Common in informal speech and colloquial writing
