## 📖 Definition
*Adverb* — (Common)
In a stealthy, secretive, or deceptive manner; done in a way intended to avoid notice or detection.

> [!example] Examples
> - He sneakily left the room without anyone noticing.
> - She sneakily added the poison to the drink.
> - The thief sneakily approached the vault.

## 📜 Etymology
From "sneak" (of unknown origin, possibly from Old English "snican" meaning to creep) + "-y" (adjective suffix) + "-ily" (adverb suffix).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adverb form of "sneaky," which describes the adjective.
> - Often conveys a sense of cunning, deception, or moving without being noticed.
> - Common in action/thriller contexts.
