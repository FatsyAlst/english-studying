## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb / Noun* — Common

To dismiss someone curtly or treat them with indifference; to brush away or remove something by brushing; a curt or offhand dismissal.

> [!example] Examples
> - She brushed off his concerns without listening.
> - He tried to brush off the criticism with a joke.
> - His request was given the brush-off by the authorities.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "brush" (to clean with a brush) + "off" (away, removed). The phrasal verb combines the literal action of brushing something away with the figurative meaning of dismissing.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb: to dismiss or treat casually
> - Noun (brush-off): a curt dismissal or rejection
> - Often implies the person being dismissed deserves better treatment
> - Less harsh than "ignore" but shows indifference
> - Suggests a quick, careless response
> - "The brush-off" is a noun phrase meaning rejection or dismissal
