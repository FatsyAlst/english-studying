## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common — Slang)
(slang) To kill someone. The past tense of *off* used as a verb meaning to murder or eliminate.

> [!example] Examples
> - The mob boss ordered his henchmen to off the informant.
> - Rumour has it he got offed in a back alley.
> - They offed him before he could testify.

## 📜 Etymology
Slang usage emerging in American English in the 1960s–1970s, likely from the idea of sending someone "off" (away, out of existence). The verb *off* meaning "to kill" is informal and originated in criminal and street slang. It became widely known through crime fiction and film.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very informal/slang — not appropriate for formal writing.
> - Used almost exclusively in crime, thriller, and action contexts.
> - The phrase "in the offing" (meaning something is about to happen) is a completely unrelated nautical idiom.
> - Synonyms: kill, whack, bump off, take out, eliminate, do in.
