## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Unaware of or not noticing something; failing to recognize or understand what is happening around oneself; lacking awareness or consciousness.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was oblivious to the danger surrounding him.
> - She remained oblivious of his feelings for her.
> - The character was oblivious to the real threat.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *obliviosus* (forgetful), derived from *oblivio* (forgetting), composed of *ob-* (against) + *livium* (forgotten). Related to "oblivion."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can use both "oblivious to" and "oblivious of"
> - Suggests unintentional lack of awareness
> - "Blissfully oblivious" means happily unaware
> - Often creates dramatic tension (audience knows, character doesn't)
> - Related noun: "oblivion" (state of being forgotten or unaware)
> - Implies a gap between reality and perception
> - Common in narrative/storytelling contexts
