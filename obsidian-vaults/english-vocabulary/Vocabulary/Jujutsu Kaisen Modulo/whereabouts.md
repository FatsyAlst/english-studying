## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

The place where someone or something is located or can be found; the location or position of a person or object.

> [!example] Examples
> - The whereabouts of the fugitive remain unknown.
> - Nobody knew the whereabouts of the missing student.
> - He refused to reveal his whereabouts to anyone.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "where" (Old English *hwær*) + "abouts" (from "about," Old English *abūtan*). The term originally meant "in the vicinity of" and came to mean "location" in the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually singular despite plural form ("where" + "abouts")
> - Often used in formal or investigative contexts
> - Commonly appears in detective/mystery narratives
> - "Whereabouts unknown" is a frequent phrase
> - Can be used as both singular and plural in modern English
> - Often conveys secrecy or mystery
