## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A summary or brief explanation of the main points; a quick overview or briefing about something.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can you give me a rundown of what happened at the meeting?
> - Here's a rundown of the project details.
> - I need a quick rundown of the situation.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A general statement of the main aspects of something; a summary account.

> [!example] Examples
> - The article provides a comprehensive rundown of the event.
> - He gave them a rundown of his plans.
> - The report includes a rundown of all incidents.

### Sense 3 — *Adjective* (Less Common)
In a state of disrepair or decline; physically deteriorated or worn out.

> [!example] Examples
> - The building was a rundown structure.
> - The neighborhood had rundown properties.
> - The car was old and rundown.

## 📜 Etymology
From the phrasal verb "run down" (to trace/pursue to conclusion, or to deteriorate). The noun "rundown" developed from this meaning in two directions: the summary sense (tracing information down to its essentials) and the physical deterioration sense.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (summary): most common in modern usage; very practical and widely used
> - Sense 2 (account): often appears in formal contexts like reports or articles
> - Sense 3 (deteriorated): typically appears as adjective before a noun
> - Spelling varies: "rundown" (one word) or "run-down" (hyphenated)
> - The hyphenated form "run-down" is more common for the adjective sense
> - "Give someone a rundown" is a frequent phrase in professional settings
