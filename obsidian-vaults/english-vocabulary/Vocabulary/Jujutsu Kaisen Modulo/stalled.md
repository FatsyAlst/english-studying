## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To stop making progress; to come to a halt or standstill, especially unexpectedly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The negotiation stalled over budget disagreements.
> - The project stalled due to lack of funding.
> - Progress on the construction stalled last month.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
(Of an engine) to stop running; to lose power suddenly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The car stalled at the traffic light.
> - My motorcycle stalled on the hill.
> - The engine stalled during the acceleration.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To use delaying tactics; to delay or avoid action deliberately.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was stalling for time.
> - Don't stall—answer the question.
> - They stalled the decision until next month.

### Sense 4 — *noun* (Common)
A compartment or small enclosure; a booth in a market; a seat in a theater.

> [!example] Examples
> - The horse was led into a stall.
> - The market stall sold fresh produce.
> - They sat in the orchestra stalls.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *stöll* 'position, place'; German *Stall*. Originally meant a place where animals stood. Verb meaning "to stop" developed from mechanical use.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb: most commonly means to stop making progress unexpectedly.
> - "Stall for time" = to delay deliberately to gain more time.
> - Engine context: the engine loses power and stops.
> - Noun: various enclosures (market, restroom, horse, theater).
> - Adjective form: "stalled" describes the state of being stopped.
