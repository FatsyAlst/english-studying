## 📖 Definition
*Adjective/Adverb* — Common

Caught or found in the act of committing a crime or wrongdoing, with clear evidence of guilt; caught red-handed.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was caught red-handed stealing from the store.
> - The thief was caught red-handed by police.
> - They caught her red-handed lying about her credentials.

## 📜 Etymology
From the image of a person's hands stained with blood (red) after committing a violent crime. The phrase dates to Scottish law in the 15th century, where "red hand" referred to catching someone with evidence of crime literally on their hands.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually appears in the phrases "catch someone red-handed" or "be caught red-handed"
> - Implies undeniable, incontrovertible evidence of wrongdoing
> - Can be used with various crimes, not just violent ones
> - Hyphenation varies; sometimes appears as "red handed" (without hyphen) in modern usage
> - Evokes the historical image of bloody hands as evidence
