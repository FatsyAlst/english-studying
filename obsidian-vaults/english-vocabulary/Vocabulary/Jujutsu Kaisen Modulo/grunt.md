## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
(Of a person) To make a short, low sound instead of speaking, usually because of anger, pain, or effort.

> [!example] Examples
> - He hauled himself over the wall, grunting with the effort.
> - "Too tired," he grunted and sat down.
> - He didn't answer, only grunted.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
(Of a pig) To make a low, rough noise.

> [!example] Examples
> - The pigs were grunting contentedly as they ate their food.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Most Common)
A short snorting sound, often to show disapproval or used as a minimal reply.

> [!example] Examples
> - The only conversation I get from my teenagers is a few grunts.
> - He answered her with a grunt.

### Sense 4 — *noun, informal/slang* (Common)
A person who does very hard or dull work; a low-ranking worker or infantry soldier.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was a grunt who worked his way up to become an officer.
> - When the seed list is complete, the grunt work truly begins.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *grunnettan* ("to grunt"), from Proto-Germanic *grunnatjaną*. The word is **onomatopoetic** — it imitates the sound it describes.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Onomatopoeia — the word mimics the actual sound made by pigs and people.
> - "Grunt work" = tedious, boring, necessary labor (very common phrase).
> - Military slang: "grunt" = infantry soldier (popularised during the Vietnam War).
> - Can express: disapproval, effort, acknowledgment, or reluctance to speak.
> - 📺 Also seen in [[Dragon Ball Z]]
