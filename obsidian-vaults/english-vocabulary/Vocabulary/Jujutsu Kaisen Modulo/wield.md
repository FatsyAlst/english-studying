## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To hold and use a tool, weapon, or instrument; to grip and employ something for a specific purpose.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior wielded his sword skillfully.
> - She wielded the hammer to build the shelf.
> - He wielded the axe with great precision.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To exercise power, influence, or authority; to exert control or command over something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The CEO wields significant power in the company.
> - He wielded influence over the government.
> - The king wielded absolute authority.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "wealdan" (to rule, govern, control), from Germanic roots meaning "to have power." Related to German "walten" (to rule).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary in tone
> - Emphasizes control, skillfulness, and command
> - More common in fantasy, historical, and formal contexts
> - Can be used both literally (with tools/weapons) and figuratively (with power/influence)
> - Often appears in martial or leadership contexts
