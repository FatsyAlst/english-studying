## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Most Common)
Large, impressive, and stately in appearance; magnificent, imposing, or splendid.

> [!example] Examples
> - The grand palace stood at the end of the avenue.
> - She wore a grand ballgown to the celebration.
> - The grand opening attracted thousands of visitors.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
Of great importance, significance, or ambition; comprehensive or all-encompassing.

> [!example] Examples
> - He outlined the grand plan for the company.
> - The grand scheme seemed impossible.
> - Their grand vision inspired the team.

### Sense 3 — *Adjective* (Less Common)
Relating to or denoting a particular rank or status; superior or distinguished.

> [!example] Examples
> - The grand duke ruled the province.
> - He was awarded the Grand Cross of the Order.
> - The Grand Master oversaw the organization.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "grant" (large), from Latin "grandis" (great, large). The word entered Middle English and has been used since the 13th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to describe impressive or prestigious settings and events
> - "Grandeur" is the noun form expressing the quality of being grand
> - "Grand total" means the complete or final sum
> - Used in titles and ranks (Grand Master, Grand Duke)
> - Can sometimes carry a slightly formal or pretentious tone
> - Common in architecture, music, and descriptions of important events
