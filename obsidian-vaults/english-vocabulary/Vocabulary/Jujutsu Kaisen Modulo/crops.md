## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
Plants or vegetables grown on a large scale for food, animal feed, or commercial use; agricultural products.

> [!example] Examples
> - The farmer harvested his crops in the autumn.
> - Drought destroyed the entire crop this year.
> - Wheat and corn are major crops in the region.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Less Common)
A group or batch of items produced at the same time; a new group or generation of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The latest crop of graduates showed great promise.
> - This year's crop of films includes some acclaimed works.
> - A fresh crop of ideas emerged from the brainstorming session.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "cropp", related to "crop" (head or top of a plant), from Germanic roots meaning "to pluck" or "to cut off".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Plural form "crops" is most common when discussing agriculture
> - Can be countable (multiple types of crops) or uncountable (the harvest)
> - Extended to mean any new batch or group
> - "Bumper crop" means an exceptionally large harvest
