## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Most Common

A large rock, typically one that has been worn smooth by erosion and is found loose on the ground or embedded in soil.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hikers had to climb over a massive boulder blocking the trail.
> - Boulders from the landslide scattered across the valley.
> - Children climbed on the giant boulders by the river.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "bulder", possibly from Old Norse "bulr" (log), or from Middle Dutch "bolder". The etymology is debated among linguists but likely Scandinavian in origin.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Distinct from "rock" (smaller) and "cliff" (attached formation)
> - Often formed by glacial movement or erosion
> - Common in geology and geography contexts
> - Sometimes used metaphorically for obstacles
