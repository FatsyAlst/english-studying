## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To choose, appoint, or assign someone for a specific role or purpose; to mark or point out something as being for a particular use or function.

> [!example] Examples
> - She was designated as the team leader.
> - The area has been designated a nature reserve.
> - They designated him to handle the negotiations.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *designatus* (marked out, indicated), from *designare* (to mark out), from *de-* (down, out) + *signare* (to mark, sign).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal register; commonly used in official, administrative, or organizational contexts
> - Can designate a person (to a role), a place (for a purpose), or a function
> - "Designated" as an adjective is very common: "designated driver," "designated area"
> - Often implies an official or formal assignment rather than casual selection
> - Related to but distinct from "design" (to create) or "designer" (one who designs)
