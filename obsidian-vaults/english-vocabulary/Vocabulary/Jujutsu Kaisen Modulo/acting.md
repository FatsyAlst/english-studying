## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The art or profession of performing roles in films, plays, or other dramatic productions.

> [!example] Examples
> - She started her acting career while still at school.
> - I did quite a lot of acting when I was at college.
> - The film is spoiled by some very wooden acting.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Serving temporarily as a substitute; holding a position on an interim basis.

> [!example] Examples
> - The college's acting president will be replaced with a permanent one.
> - Ms. Lopez-Smith will be the acting treasurer.
> - He served as acting director during her absence.

### Sense 3 — *present participle* (Common)
Ongoing action of behaving or functioning in a particular way.

> [!example] Examples
> - Stop acting like a child and listen to me.
> - The medication is acting as a pain reliever.
> - She was acting all poor-me pitiful.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin **"agere"** meaning *to do, to drive, to perform*. The theatrical sense developed from the idea of performing actions on stage.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As an **adjective** (temporary position): indicates someone fulfilling interim duties while the permanent occupant is absent. Common in government, business, and academia.
> - Synonyms for the adjective sense: temporary, interim, provisional, pro tem.
> - As a **noun** (performance): refers to the craft and skill of performing roles.
