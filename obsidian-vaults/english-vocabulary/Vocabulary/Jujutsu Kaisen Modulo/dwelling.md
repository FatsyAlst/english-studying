## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A place where someone lives; a house, apartment, or any residence or shelter.

> [!example] Examples
> - The family's modest dwelling was nestled in the countryside.
> - Urban dwellings are becoming increasingly expensive.
> - They found shelter in a small wooden dwelling.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *dwellan* (to lead astray, delay), later developed the sense of "to remain in a place." The noun form refers to the place where one dwells or resides.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary register
> - Often appears in compounds like "dwelling place"
> - The verb "dwell" is less common in modern usage except in phrases like "dwell on" (to think repeatedly about)
> - Can refer to any type of residence, from luxurious to humble
