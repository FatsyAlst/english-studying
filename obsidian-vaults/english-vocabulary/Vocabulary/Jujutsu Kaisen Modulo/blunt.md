## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Common)
Having a rounded or dull edge or end, not sharp; lacking a sharp point.

> [!example] Examples
> - The knife is blunt and won't cut properly.
> - He used a blunt pencil to sketch.
> - The blunt end of the arrow hit the target.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
Direct and frank in speech, often in a way that seems rude or insensitive; saying exactly what one thinks without concern for feelings.

> [!example] Examples
> - He gave a blunt assessment of the situation.
> - Her blunt comments hurt his feelings.
> - To be blunt, your plan won't work.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Less Common)
To make something less sharp; to make dull or less intense.

> [!example] Examples
> - The repeated use will blunt the blade.
> - Alcohol can blunt your senses.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "bluntr" meaning "dull" or "obtuse." The term entered Middle English and has been used since the 14th century to describe both physical dullness and direct speech.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primary meaning: having a dull or rounded edge (physical property)
> - Secondary meaning: direct and frank speech, often insensitive
> - Verb form means to make dull or to reduce intensity
> - "Bluntly" is the adverb form, meaning "directly" or "frankly"
> - In communication contexts, often implies rudeness or lack of tact
