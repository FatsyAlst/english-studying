## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
To repair something quickly or imperfectly; a temporary fix.

> [!example] Examples
> - They managed to patch up the roof before the storm.
> - We patched up the car enough to reach the garage.
> - He patched up his old jacket with a piece of fabric.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (Common)
To give basic medical treatment to someone who is injured.

> [!example] Examples
> - The nurse patched him up after the fight.
> - Paramedics patched up the injured passengers.
> - The doctor will patch you up in no time.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb, figurative* (Common)
To restore a relationship after an argument or disagreement.

> [!example] Examples
> - They finally patched up their differences.
> - You should try to patch things up.
> - The countries are trying to patch up their relationship.

### Sense 4 — *phrasal verb* (Less Common)
To deal with a problem quickly or temporarily without a thorough solution.

> [!example] Examples
> - The agreement only patches up the situation.
> - We can patch things up for now, but need a long-term solution.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "patch" (a piece of material used to cover a hole). Extended metaphorically to mean any quick or temporary repair, whether physical, medical, or relational.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always implies a basic or temporary fix, not a thorough or permanent one.
> - "Patch things up" is a very common expression specifically for repairing relationships.
