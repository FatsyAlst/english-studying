## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To crawl on the ground on one's hands and knees; to behave in an obsequious or servile manner to obtain forgiveness or favor.

> [!example] Examples
> - The defeated villain was forced to grovel before the victor.
> - He refused to grovel for forgiveness despite his serious mistake.
> - The character groveled desperately to escape punishment.

- reduce to groveling

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *gruflen*, possibly from Old Norse *grúfa* (to grovel), related to creeping or crawling.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often conveys degradation or humiliation
> - Can be literal (physically crawling) or figurative (emotional submission)
> - Strong negative connotation in most contexts
> - Common in combat/power hierarchy scenarios
