## 📖 Definition
*Idiom/Adjective Phrase* — (Common)
Extremely high, exceptional, or beyond normal measurement; exceeding expectations or standard scales.

> [!example] Examples
> - His power level was completely off the charts.
> - The performance was off the charts in terms of quality.
> - Her intelligence is off the charts.

## 📜 Etymology
From the idea of data or measurements being so extreme that they go beyond the range of a chart or graph. First popularized in the mid-20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/colloquial phrase commonly used in casual speech and modern writing.
> - Often used to describe exceptional talent, power, performance, or measurements.
> - Conveys that something is not just good, but extraordinarily beyond normal range.
> - Common in anime/manga dialogue and informal character descriptions.
