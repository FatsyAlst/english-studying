## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun/adjective* (Common — Informal)
Short for *preliminary* — an initial examination, contest, or round that precedes the main event.

> [!example] Examples
> - The prelims start at 9 a.m. before the main event.
> - She passed her prelims and advanced to the final round.
> - The prelim results suggest we're on the right track.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Informal clipping of *preliminary*, from French *préliminaire*, from Latin *prae-* ("before") + *limen* ("threshold"). Thus literally "before the threshold" — the stage before the main event. The shortened form *prelim* became common in academic and sports contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very informal — the full word *preliminary* is preferred in formal writing.
> - Common in academic contexts: *prelims* can refer to preliminary examinations in graduate school.
> - In combat sports (boxing, MMA), the *prelims* are the undercard fights before the main event.
> - Can be used as either noun or adjective: *"the prelim exam"* or *"the prelims."*
