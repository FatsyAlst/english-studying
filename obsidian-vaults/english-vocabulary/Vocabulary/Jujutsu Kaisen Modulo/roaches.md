## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

Plural of roach; insects with flat bodies and long antennae, belonging to the order Blattodea; also a street name for a marijuana cigarette (slang).

> [!example] Examples
> - The kitchen was infested with roaches.
> - Roaches can survive in extreme conditions.
> - The janitor set traps to eliminate the roaches.

## 📜 Etymology
From Spanish *cucaracha*, a word of unclear origin but possibly from a Taino (Caribbean) source. The term entered English via Spanish colonial traders.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily refers to cockroaches
> - "Roach" is the singular form (less common than "cockroach")
> - Associated with dirt, filth, and pest infestations
> - Often appears in discussions of sanitation or urban environments
> - Has secondary slang meanings in some contexts
> - Can be used in Jujutsu Kaisen metaphorically for undesirable creatures
