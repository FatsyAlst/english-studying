## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
To protect or defend against danger, illness, or attack; to keep something unwanted away; to repel.

> [!example] Examples
> - I tried different remedies to ward off a cold.
> - She put up her hands to ward him off.
> - I take vitamin C to ward off colds.
> - Exercise can help ward off diseases.
> - The charm was believed to ward off evil spirits.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English **"weardian"** meaning *to guard, protect, watch over*. Related to **"guard"** and **"warden."**

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - **Separable phrasal verb:** you can say "ward off danger" or "ward it off."
> - Most commonly used in health and medical contexts (warding off illness).
> - Don't confuse **"ward off"** (prevent/repel) with **"warn off"** (tell someone to stay away).
