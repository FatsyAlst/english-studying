## 📖 Definition
*Adverb* — (Common)
Completely, totally, or wholly; all together as a whole; on the whole or in total.

> [!example] Examples
> - The situation changed altogether after his arrival.
> - She abandoned her plan altogether.
> - They were altogether different in personality.

## 📜 Etymology
From "all together," which evolved into a single adverb "altogether." The word "all" comes from Old English "eal" and "together" from Old English "togæder."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adverb meaning "completely" or "wholly."
> - Should not be confused with "all together" (two words), which means "as a group."
> - Common in narrative descriptions, especially when describing change or transformation.
> - Adds emphasis to the totality or completeness of something.
