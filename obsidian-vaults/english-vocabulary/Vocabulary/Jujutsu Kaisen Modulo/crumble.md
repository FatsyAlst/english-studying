## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To break into small fragments; to fall apart or disintegrate into pieces.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old building began to crumble.
> - The ancient structure crumbled under the attack.
> - The cookie crumbled in his hand.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To collapse or fail; to lose strength or confidence gradually.

> [!example] Examples
> - His resolve crumbled in the face of adversity.
> - Her confidence crumbled after the defeat.
> - The alliance crumbled under pressure.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "crumblen," possibly from Old English "cruma" (crumb), influenced by "crumb." The "-le" suffix suggests repeated action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (physical destruction) or figurative (collapse of resolve, confidence, etc.).
> - Emphasis on gradual disintegration or breaking into small pieces.
> - Common in both action scenes (structural destruction) and emotional narratives (collapse of character state).
