## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A prolonged and intensely difficult experience; a painful or trying trial or test.

> [!example] Examples
> - Surviving the accident was an ordeal for the entire family.
> - The character endured a terrible ordeal during the mission.
> - The trial became an ordeal that tested their resolve.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "ordæl" or "ordel," from Germanic origins. Originally referred to a primitive trial by fire or combat to determine guilt or innocence in ancient law.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun describing a difficult trial or experience, often one that tests character or endurance.
> - Often used in dramatic contexts to emphasize the severity of a situation.
> - Frequently appears in battle narratives and character development arcs.
> - Implies both hardship and the need for perseverance.
