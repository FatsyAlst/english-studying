## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
An official order or edict issued by someone in authority; a law or regulation proclaimed by a governing body or leader.

> [!example] Examples
> - The king issued a decree forbidding the practice.
> - The jujutsu society's decree was absolute.
> - By royal decree, the celebration was mandated.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To issue an official order or command; to proclaim formally and authoritatively.

> [!example] Examples
> - The leader decreed that all must obey the rules.
> - She decreed the new policy immediately.
> - The council decreed the punishment for the crime.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *decre*, from Latin *decretum* (something decided), past participle of *decernere* (to decide), composed of *de-* (thoroughly) + *cernere* (to sift, decide).
