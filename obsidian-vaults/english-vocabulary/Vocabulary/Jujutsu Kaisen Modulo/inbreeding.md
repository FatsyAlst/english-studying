## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The practice of breeding from closely related people or animals, especially over many generations, often resulting in genetic defects or reduced fitness.

> [!example] Examples
> - Inbreeding among the royal families of Europe led to numerous hereditary diseases.
> - The endangered species suffered from the effects of inbreeding due to its small population.
> - Geneticists warned that inbreeding in the isolated community was increasing health risks.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of *in-* (within) + *breeding* (reproduction). First recorded in the 1840s. The concept reflects breeding "within" a closed group rather than introducing outside genetic material. Related to the root verb *breed* from Old English *brēdan* ("to bring young to birth, keep warm").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In biology, "inbreeding depression" refers to the reduced biological fitness caused by breeding of related individuals.
> - The adjective **inbred** can also be used figuratively (and often offensively) to describe someone perceived as unsophisticated or backward.
> - Distinct from *crossbreeding* (breeding between different varieties) and *outbreeding* (breeding between unrelated individuals).
