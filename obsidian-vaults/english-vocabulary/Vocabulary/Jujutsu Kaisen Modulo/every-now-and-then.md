## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adverbial phrase / idiom* (Most Common)
Occasionally; from time to time; sometimes but not regularly or frequently.

> [!example] Examples
> - I still think about her every now and then.
> - Every now and then, he'd stop and look around nervously.
> - We go out for dinner every now and then — maybe once a month.

---
## 📜 Etymology
A compound temporal expression from Old English elements. *Now* and *then* represent two points in time; the phrase "now and then" (meaning "occasionally") has been in English since at least the 15th century. The addition of *every* (16th century onward) emphasizes regularity of the occasional occurrence.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Synonymous with *occasionally, from time to time, once in a while, now and again*.
> - Can be placed at the beginning or end of a sentence: *"Every now and then I see him"* or *"I see him every now and then."*
> - Implies less frequency than *sometimes* but more than *rarely*.
> - Variants: *every now and again*, *now and then*, *every once in a while* — all interchangeable.
