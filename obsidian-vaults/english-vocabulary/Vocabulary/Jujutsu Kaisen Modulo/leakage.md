## 📖 Definition
*noun* — Common

The act or process of leaking; the escape or seeping of liquid, gas, or information from a container, system, or organization unintentionally; the amount that leaks out.

> [!example] Examples
> - The water leakage from the pipe caused damage.
> - Gas leakage was detected in the building.
> - Information leakage compromised the project security.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *leaka* 'to leak' (verb) + "-age" (suffix forming nouns indicating state, condition, or action). Entered English in 15th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: describes both the process of leaking and the amount leaked.
> - Can be literal (water, gas, oil) or metaphorical (information, data).
> - Common in technical, medical, and security contexts.
> - Synonyms: leak, seepage, escape, breach (for information).
> - Often used in problem-solving contexts: detect, prevent, fix leakage.
