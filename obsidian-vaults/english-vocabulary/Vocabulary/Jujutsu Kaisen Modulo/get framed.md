## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To be falsely accused or incriminated for a crime or wrongdoing that one did not commit; to be set up as the guilty party through deception or manipulation.

> [!example] Examples
> - He claimed he was framed for the robbery.
> - She got framed by her business partner.
> - The innocent man was framed for murder.

## 📜 Etymology
"Frame" originally meant to construct or shape; extended in criminal contexts to mean to construct false evidence. The phrasal verb "get framed" uses "frame" in this sense, with "get" indicating receiving/suffering an action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually implies deliberate wrongdoing by another party
> - Common in crime fiction, legal contexts, and news reporting
> - "Frame-up" (noun) refers to the false accusation itself
> - Often used when someone is unjustly punished for another's crime
> - Related to concepts of injustice and conspiracy
