## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Unwilling or disinclined to do something; showing resistance or opposition to an action or decision.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was reluctant to admit his mistake.
> - She gave a reluctant agreement to the plan.
> - The reluctant witness finally testified in court.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "reluctans," from "reluctari" (to struggle against), composed of "re-" (against) + "luctari" (to struggle). Entered English in the 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Expresses unwillingness more strongly than "hesitant"
> - Often implies resistance or opposition to something
> - "Reluctantly" is the adverbial form
> - "Reluctance" is the noun expressing the quality or state
> - Common in formal and literary contexts
> - Often used to describe people forced into situations against their will
