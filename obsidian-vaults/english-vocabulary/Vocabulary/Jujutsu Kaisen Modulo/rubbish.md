## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
Waste material; trash or garbage; discarded items of no value.

> [!example] Examples
> - Please take out the rubbish before it piles up.
> - The alley was filled with rubbish and debris.
> - They sorted through the rubbish to find recyclables.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
Something worthless or of poor quality; nonsense or foolish talk.

> [!example] Examples
> - This film is complete rubbish.
> - Don't talk rubbish; that makes no sense.
> - His excuse was utter rubbish.

### Sense 3 — *Adjective* (Common)
Of very poor quality; worthless or bad.

> [!example] Examples
> - That's a rubbish idea.
> - The weather has been rubbish all week.
> - I had a rubbish day at work.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *rubbous*, possibly from Old French *robe* (booty, plunder), or from an imitative origin related to rubble. The British usage as a general term for garbage developed in the 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily British English; Americans typically use "trash" or "garbage"
> - Can function as noun (mass noun) or adjective
> - Informal and colloquial; slightly derogatory when describing quality
> - The adjective form is common in British casual speech: "That's rubbish!"
> - The verb "to rubbish" means to criticize harshly (mainly British)
