## 📖 Definition
*Verb (present participle)* — Common

To move in a twisting or spiraling pattern; to rotate or spin around rapidly; to move with a continuous circular motion. Often used to describe the movement of liquids, fabrics, or visual effects.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dancer was swirling across the stage.
> - The water was swirling down the drain.
> - The fog was swirling around the streetlight.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "swirlen," of uncertain origin, possibly from Old English or Scandinavian roots. The term describes a spiraling or rotating movement and has been used since at least the 15th century.
