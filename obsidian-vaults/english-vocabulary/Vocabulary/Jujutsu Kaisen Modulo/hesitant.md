## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Slow to act or speak; tending to pause or be uncertain before taking action; lacking confidence or conviction.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was hesitant to speak in front of the crowd.
> - She gave a hesitant response to the question.
> - The hesitant student raised her hand slowly.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "haesitans," from "haesitare" (to stick, be undecided), possibly related to "haerere" (to stick). Entered English in the 17th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes uncertainty and lack of confidence
> - More about indecision than outright unwillingness (different from "reluctant")
> - "Hesitantly" is the adverbial form
> - "Hesitation" is the noun expressing the tendency or instance of hesitating
> - Often describes physical actions (hesitant steps, hesitant voice)
> - Implies a momentary pause before proceeding
