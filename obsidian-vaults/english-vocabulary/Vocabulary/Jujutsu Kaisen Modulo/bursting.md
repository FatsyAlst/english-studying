## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To break open or split suddenly due to pressure; to explode or rupture; to be very full of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dam burst from the pressure of water.
> - The balloon bursting made a loud noise.
> - He was bursting with excitement.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
Completely full of something; ready to break open; overflowing with emotions or energy.

> [!example] Examples
> - Bursting with confidence, he approached the fight.
> - The bursting energy of youth filled the room.
> - Bursting at the seams with pride.

### Sense 3 — *noun, "a burst (of)"* (Common)
A short, sudden period of activity, energy or sound; (gaming) *burst damage* = a lot of damage dealt in a very short time.

> [!example] Examples
> - She finished the report in a final burst of energy.
> - There was a burst of applause.
> - Tracer's burst damage is huge at close range.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *bursten*, from Old English *berstan*, related to Old Norse *bresta* (to break). Proto-Germanic root indicating sudden breaking or rupture.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb: sudden, violent breaking or explosion
> - Adjective: filled to capacity; overflowing with
> - Past tense: "burst" (not "bursted") in standard usage
> - "Bursting with" = full of emotions or qualities
> - "Burst into" = suddenly start doing something
> - Creates vivid imagery of release or overflow
> - Common in action and emotional contexts
> - 📺 Also encountered in [[Dragon Ball Super]]
