## 📖 Definition
*Adverb* — Common

To an excessive or greater degree than necessary; too much; more than is needed or appropriate.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was overly cautious about spending money.
> - Don't be overly critical of yourself.
> - The instructions were overly complicated.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "over" (beyond, excessive) + "-ly" (adverbial suffix). The prefix "over-" has Old English roots and the adverbial form developed in English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very common in modern English for expressing excess or exaggeration
> - Often modifies adjectives to indicate something is "too" something
> - Slightly more formal than "too" or "way too" in casual speech
> - Used in both positive and negative contexts
> - "Overly" emphasizes that something exceeds what is necessary or appropriate
> - Can appear before adjectives (overly cautious) or adverbs (overly carefully)
