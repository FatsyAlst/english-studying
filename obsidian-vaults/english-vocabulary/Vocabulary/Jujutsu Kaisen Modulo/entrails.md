## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

The internal organs of the body, especially the intestines; the intestinal or visceral parts of an animal or human.

> [!example] Examples
> - The cursed spirit's entrails spilled out from the wound.
> - Ancient priests read entrails to predict the future.
> - The warrior's entrails were exposed by the deadly strike.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *entrailles*, derived from Medieval Latin *intranea*, from Latin *interaneus* (internal), composed of *inter-* (within) + *-aneus* (suffix meaning "of").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal anatomical term for internal organs
> - Often used in grotesque or violent contexts
> - Associated with sacrifice or divination in historical/mythological contexts
> - More literary or clinical than "guts" or "intestines"
> - Singular form "entrail" is rare; usually plural
> - Common in fantasy, horror, and combat narratives
> - Can create visceral imagery in descriptions
> - Related to augury (reading entrails for prophecy in ancient Rome)
