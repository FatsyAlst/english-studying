## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To deliberately make someone or something seem less important; to treat or speak of contemptuously; to diminish or minimize.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't belittle his efforts just because they weren't perfect.
> - She constantly belittles her opponent's abilities.
> - He refused to belittle their accomplishments.

## 📜 Etymology
From "be-" (prefix making verbs) + "little." First used in the 18th century to mean "to make little or small."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Transitive verb; requires a direct object (person or achievement being belittled).
> - Often used to describe disrespectful or insulting behavior.
> - Common in contexts of rivalry, competition, or power dynamics.
> - Related to but distinct from "demean" (to lower in dignity) and "disparage" (to speak negatively of).
