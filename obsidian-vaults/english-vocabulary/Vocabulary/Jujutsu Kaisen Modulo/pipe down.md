## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To become quiet or stop talking; to tell someone to be silent or stop making noise.

> [!example] Examples
> - The teacher told the noisy students to pipe down.
> - Everyone piped down when the leader entered the room.
> - Can you please pipe down? I'm trying to concentrate.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "pipe" (to make a shrill sound) + "down" (to reduce or stop). Originally nautical slang, where a bosun would pipe commands; to "pipe down" meant to stop piping.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational register
> - Usually a command or request
> - Similar to: shut up, be quiet, hush, hold your tongue
> - Often used in group settings or with authority figures
> - Less harsh than "shut up" but more direct than "please be quiet"
