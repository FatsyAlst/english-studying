## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To stay in a place longer than usual; to move slowly; to remain or continue to exist, especially when disappearing slowly or reluctantly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The guests lingered after the party ended.
> - The smell of her perfume lingered in the room.
> - He lingered over his coffee, not wanting to leave.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "lingeren," possibly from Old Norse "leikinn" meaning "weary" or from German "länger" meaning "longer." The term has been used since medieval times to describe staying in one place or prolonging an action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can refer to staying physically in a place or remaining over time
> - Often implies reluctance to leave or a slow disappearance
> - "Lingering" as an adjective describes something that persists or remains
> - Common in descriptive writing to convey a sense of slowness or persistence
> - Frequently used with "on" (focus on) or "over" (take time with)
