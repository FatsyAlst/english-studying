## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

Past tense of "deem"; to judge, regard, or consider something in a specified way; to think or believe that something has a particular quality.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was deemed a threat to society.
> - The character was deemed worthy of the position.
> - Her actions were deemed acceptable by the council.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *dēman* (to judge, doom), related to Old Saxon *dōmian* and Old Norse *dœma*. Related to the noun "doom" (originally "judgment").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal, official register
> - Often used in legal, organizational, or judicial contexts
> - Usually passive voice: "be deemed [adjective]"
> - Implies judgment or official determination
> - Can indicate both positive and negative assessments
> - Similar in meaning to: regarded as, considered, judged to be
> - Common in official statements, regulations, and narrative judgments
> - Suggests some authority or basis for the assessment
