## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To clean something vigorously by rubbing hard with a brush or abrasive material.

> [!example] Examples
> - She scrubbed the floor until it gleamed.
> - He scrubbed away the dirt and grime.
> - The team scrubbed the training area clean.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To cancel or abandon a plan, mission, or operation.

> [!example] Examples
> - They had to scrub the mission due to technical failures.
> - The event was scrubbed at the last minute.
> - All training exercises were scrubbed for the day.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Low German *schrubbe*, related to Dutch *schrobben*. Originally meant to rub with a coarse brush.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Physical cleaning with vigorous rubbing
> - Sense 2: Cancellation, more common in technical/military contexts
> - "Scrub in" refers to washing hands before surgery
> - Noun form: a type of vegetation or a lowly person (slang)
