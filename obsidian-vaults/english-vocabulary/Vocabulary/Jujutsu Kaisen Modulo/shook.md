## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Most Common

Past tense of "shake"; to move rapidly back and forth or up and down; to disturb emotionally or physically; to tremble or vibrate.

> [!example] Examples
> - The explosion shook the entire building.
> - Fear shook him to his core.
> - He shook his head in disagreement.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *scacan* or *sceacan*, related to Old Saxon *skakan* and Old Norse *skaka*. Proto-Germanic root suggesting "to move quickly."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past tense of "shake" (shook → shaken is the past participle)
> - Can be literal (physical movement) or figurative (emotional disturbance)
> - "Shook hands" is a common greeting phrase
> - "Shook him up" means disturbed or upset him emotionally
> - Creates vivid imagery in action sequences
> - Common in both formal writing and casual speech
> - "Shook and shaken" have different uses in sentences
