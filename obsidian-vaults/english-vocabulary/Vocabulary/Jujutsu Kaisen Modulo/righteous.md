## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Morally right, virtuous, or in accordance with what is just and honorable; acting in accord with divine or moral law; free from guilt or sin.

> [!example] Examples
> - He believed his cause was righteous.
> - Her righteous anger at the injustice was justified.
> - The righteous path is not always the easy one.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "rihtwis," combining "riht" (right) + "wis" (wise/manner). The term has Old Germanic roots and was used in religious contexts to describe those living in accordance with divine law. Extended meaning in medieval and modern English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes moral correctness and virtue
> - Often used in religious or ethical contexts
> - "Righteous anger/indignation" are common collocations describing justified emotional reactions
> - "Self-righteous" is negative, meaning overly convinced of one's own righteousness
> - Can describe people, causes, actions, or judgments
> - Formal register
