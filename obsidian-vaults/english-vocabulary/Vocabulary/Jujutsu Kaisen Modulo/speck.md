## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A tiny particle or spot of something; a small amount of something; a very small piece or mark.

> [!example] Examples
> - A speck of dust floated in the sunlight.
> - There was a speck of blood on his uniform.
> - The island appeared as a mere speck on the horizon.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *specca* or *speccan*, related to Middle Low German *spec* and Dutch *spek* (bacon, fat). The term shifted to mean a small spot or particle.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes extreme smallness
> - Often used metaphorically (e.g., "a speck on the horizon")
> - "Not a speck" means "absolutely none"
> - Can be countable (multiple specks) or uncountable
> - Related adjectives: speckled, spotted, dotted
> - Creates vivid imagery through the implied tininess
