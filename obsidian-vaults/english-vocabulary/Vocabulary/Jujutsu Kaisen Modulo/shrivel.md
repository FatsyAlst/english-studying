## 📖 Definition
*verb* — Common

To become smaller, dryer, and wrinkled; to shrink and wither, especially due to loss of moisture or heat.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fruit shriveled in the hot sun.
> - The flowers shriveled after the frost.
> - His confidence shriveled under criticism.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from Old Norse *skreppa* 'to wrinkle' or Middle Dutch *schrompelen*. Entered English in 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Describes physical withering and drying out (fruit, flowers, skin).
> - Can be used metaphorically for confidence, dreams, or other abstract things.
> - Often paired with "up" (shrivel up) for emphasis.
> - British spelling: shrivelled (double l).
> - Past tense: shriveled (US) or shrivelled (UK).
