## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
Past tense and past participle of "sting"; to pierce or prick the skin with a sharp, pointed part (like a bee's stinger) causing pain.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bee stung him on the arm.
> - A wasp stung her ankle.
> - I was stung by a jellyfish.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To cause a sharp, burning pain or sensation; to hurt sharply.

> [!example] Examples
> - The cold air stung her face.
> - The harsh words stung him deeply.
> - The antiseptic stung when applied.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To cause emotional pain; to wound feelings or pride; to feel hurt or offended.

> [!example] Examples
> - The criticism stung her pride.
> - His betrayal stung deep.
> - The rejection stung for weeks.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *stingan* 'to prick, pierce'; Proto-Germanic *stinganan*. Related to German *Stachel* (sting).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past tense: stung (not "stinged").
> - Can be literal (physical injury from insect) or metaphorical (emotional hurt).
> - Noun form: "sting" refers to the sharp pain or the wound itself.
> - Verb forms: sting, stings, stung, stinging.
> - Adjective: "stinging" describes sharp pain or harsh criticism.
