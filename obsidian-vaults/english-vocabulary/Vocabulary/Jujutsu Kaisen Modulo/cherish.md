## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To hold something or someone dear; to care deeply about and value greatly; to maintain or nurture something with care and affection.

> [!example] Examples
> - She cherishes the memory of her childhood.
> - He cherished his family above all else.
> - We should cherish the time we have together.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *cherir* (to hold dear, value), derived from *cher* (dear), from Latin *carus* (dear, precious).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary register; expresses deep emotion
> - Often used with abstract nouns (memories, hopes, values) or loved ones
> - Implies both feeling affection and actively maintaining or protecting something
> - "Cherished" as an adjective describes something treasured and protected
> - The verb suggests a deliberate commitment to valuing something over time
> - Can refer to emotional, spiritual, or material treasures
