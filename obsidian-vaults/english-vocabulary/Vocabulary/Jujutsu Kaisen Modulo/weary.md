## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Tired or exhausted, physically or mentally; lacking energy or patience; wary or cautious (less common usage).

> [!example] Examples
> - After the long battle, the warrior was weary and defeated.
> - She gave him a weary look, tired of his excuses.
> - The weary travelers finally reached shelter.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *werig*, related to Old Saxon *worig* (confused, drunk) and Old High German *worag*. Originally may have meant "worn out" or "worn."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Conveys both physical and emotional exhaustion
> - More literary or formal than "tired"
> - Can indicate mental fatigue from repetition or monotony
> - "Weary of" means tired of or sick of something
> - Often appears in dramatic or combat narratives
> - Synonyms: tired, exhausted, fatigued, worn out
> - Suggests a deeper, more lasting tiredness
