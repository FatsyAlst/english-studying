## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A female domestic servant or housemaid; a woman employed to clean and perform household duties.

> [!example] Examples
> - The maid cleaned the mansion every morning.
> - She hired a maid to help with household chores.
> - The royal maid attended to the princess.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "maide," short form of "maiden," from Old English "magaden," originally meaning a young woman or virgin. Eventually specialized to mean a female domestic servant.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Refers to a female domestic worker, usually in a household.
> - "Maid of honor" has a special meaning in weddings (the bride's principal attendant).
> - Often used in historical or formal contexts.
> - Distinct from "maiden" (archaic for young woman) though etymologically related.
