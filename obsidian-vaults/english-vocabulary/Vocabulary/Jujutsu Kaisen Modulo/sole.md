## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Being the only one; single and exclusive.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sole survivor of the accident was rescued.
> - That is the sole reason for my decision.
> - The sole purpose of this meeting is to discuss strategy.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "sol," from Latin "solus" meaning "alone, solitary." Related to "solitary" and "solo."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily used as an adjective meaning "only/single."
> - Also exists as a noun (bottom of a shoe or a type of fish), which has different etymological origins.
> - Often used in formal/legal contexts.
