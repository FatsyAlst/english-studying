## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

The action of making amends or reparation for guilt or wrongdoing; compensation or restitution for harm caused; the state of being reconciled.

> [!example] Examples
> - He sought atonement for his past mistakes.
> - The atonement process required genuine remorse.
> - She made atonement for her betrayal.

## 📜 Etymology
From "at" + "one" + "-ment," literally meaning "at-one-ment." The word originally meant "reconciliation" and developed from the religious concept of restoration of harmony between humanity and God. Developed in Middle English from the verb "atone."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in religious, moral, or literary contexts
> - Implies not just apology but active reparation or compensation
> - Can be secular or spiritual depending on context
> - Related to redemption, forgiveness, and reconciliation but distinct
> - "Atone" is the verb form; "atonement" is the noun
> - Suggests a process or journey rather than a simple apology
> - Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur) is a significant religious observance in Judaism
