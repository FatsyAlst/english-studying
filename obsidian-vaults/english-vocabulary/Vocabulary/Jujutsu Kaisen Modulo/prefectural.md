## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Less Common

Relating to a prefecture (an administrative division or province, especially in Japan); governed by a prefect or prefectural authority.

> [!example] Examples
> - The prefectural government issued new regulations.
> - Prefectural schools follow different curriculum standards.
> - The jujutsu society had prefectural representatives at the meeting.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *praefectura* (office of a prefect), from *praefectus* (appointed over), derived from *praeficere* (to place in charge). The term became particularly associated with Japanese administrative divisions.
