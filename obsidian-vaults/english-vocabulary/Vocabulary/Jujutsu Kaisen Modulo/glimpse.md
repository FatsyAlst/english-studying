## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A brief or partial view of something; a quick look.

> [!example] Examples
> - I caught a glimpse of the enemy's technique in that moment.
> - She saw only a glimpse of the shadow before it disappeared.
> - The tourists got a glimpse of the rare bird.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To see or view briefly or partially; to catch a quick look at.

> [!example] Examples
> - He glimpsed the artifact through the window.
> - I glimpsed her face in the crowd.
> - They glimpsed the distant mountains.

## 📜 Etymology
Of uncertain origin, possibly from a Scandinavian source or related to "glance." First recorded in English in the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with "catch" or "get" as a verb: "catch a glimpse of."
> - Emphasizes brevity and incompleteness—a partial view rather than a full one.
> - Common in narrative description, especially in action scenes.
