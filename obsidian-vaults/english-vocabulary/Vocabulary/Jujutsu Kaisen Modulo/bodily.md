## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Of or concerning the body; relating to physical rather than mental or spiritual matters.

> [!example] Examples
> - He suffered serious bodily harm in the accident.
> - The law protects citizens from bodily injury.
> - Bodily functions are a natural part of life.

### Sense 2 — *adverb* (Common)
By taking hold of a person's body with force; by moving the entire body physically.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was carried bodily from the room.
> - She lifted the child bodily off the ground.
> - The protester was removed bodily by security guards.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *bodili*, formed from *body* (Old English *bodig*, of uncertain origin) + *-ly* (suffix forming adjectives and adverbs). The word has maintained its core meaning of "relating to the physical body" since Old English times.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - **"Grievous bodily harm" (GBH)** is a legal term, especially in British law, for serious physical injury — a more severe charge than "actual bodily harm" (ABH).
> - As an adverb, *bodily* always implies forceful physical movement of the whole body: *"She was lifted bodily into the air."*
> - More formal than alternatives like *physical* or *physically*: *"bodily harm"* sounds more legal/serious than *"physical harm."*
> - Compare with [[grievous]] (Dragon Ball Z) — *grievous bodily harm* uses both words together in legal contexts.
