## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A structure with bars or wires designed to confine or contain animals; an enclosure.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bird was kept in a small cage.
> - The lions paced back and forth in their cage.
> - She opened the cage door to let the rabbit out.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
(Figuratively) Any situation or structure that confines, restricts, or limits freedom.

> [!example] Examples
> - He felt trapped in the cage of his responsibilities.
> - Society's expectations became a cage for her ambitions.
> - The rules created an invisible cage around his behavior.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "cage", from Latin "cavea" (hollow place, cavity), related to "cavus" (hollow).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As a verb: "to cage" means to put into a cage or to confine
> - Often used metaphorically in literature and psychology
> - "Golden cage" refers to a comfortable but restricting situation
