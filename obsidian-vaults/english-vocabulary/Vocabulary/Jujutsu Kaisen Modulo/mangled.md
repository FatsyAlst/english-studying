## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb / adjective* (Most Common)
To severely injure or damage by cutting, tearing, or crushing; severely damaged, mutilated, or destroyed beyond recognition.

> [!example] Examples
> - His body was crushed and mangled beyond recognition.
> - My sweater got mangled in the washing machine.
> - The mangled wreckage of the car was barely recognizable.

### Sense 2 — *verb / adjective* (Common)
To ruin or spoil something through ineptitude or carelessness, making it incoherent or unrecognizable.

> [!example] Examples
> - He mangled the rhythm so badly that it hardly made any sense.
> - There is almost no phrase so simple that he cannot mangle it.
> - They mangle scientific information.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *mangelen*, from Anglo-French *mangler, mahangler* ("to injure, mutilate"), related to *mahaigner* ("to maim"). Similar trajectory to "butcher" — from violent action to figurative bungling.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Two distinct uses: **physical damage** (severe, violent) and **ruined by incompetence** (figurative).
> - Stronger than "damage" — implies something is nearly destroyed or unrecognizable.
> - **Mangle vs maim vs mutilate:** mangle = tearing/crushing; maim = loss of limb; mutilate = cutting off parts.
> - Creates vivid, often disturbing imagery — common in news/accident reporting.
