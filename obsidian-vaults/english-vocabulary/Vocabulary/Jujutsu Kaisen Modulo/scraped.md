## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To remove, clean, or scratch a surface by rubbing it with something sharp or rough; to graze the skin causing minor injury.

> [!example] Examples
> - I scraped my knee when I fell.
> - She scraped the ice off the windshield.
> - He scraped the paint off the old wall.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To gather or collect something with effort, usually in small amounts; to accumulate barely enough.

> [!example] Examples
> - They scraped together enough money for rent.
> - He scraped by on a small salary.
> - She managed to scrape a passing grade.

### Note on "Scrapped"
*"Scrapped"* (past tense of "scrap") means to discard, abandon, or cancel something entirely, often used for discarding broken machinery or abandoning plans. This is different from "scraped."

> [!example] Examples
> - The company scrapped the old project.
> - They scrapped the defective parts.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "scrapen", possibly from Old Norse "skrapa" (to scrape). Related to "scrape" with Proto-Germanic roots meaning "to scratch."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Scrape by" is an idiom meaning to barely manage or survive
> - "Scrape the bottom" refers to reaching the lowest point
> - "Scrape together" means to gather with difficulty
> - Easy to confuse with "scrapped" (past of "scrap"), which has a different meaning
> - Often describes removing unwanted surfaces or collecting insufficient resources
