## 📖 Definition
*Verb (present participle)* — Common

To step heavily and repeatedly on something or someone; to crush or tread upon with the feet. Can be used literally (physical trampling) or figuratively (disrespecting or disregarding someone's rights or feelings).

> [!example] Examples
> - The crowd was trampling the flowers in their rush to leave.
> - He felt like they were trampling on his dignity.
> - The soldiers trampled through the village, destroying crops.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "trempan" or related Germanic roots meaning "to step" or "to tread." The term has been used to describe the action of stepping heavily on things since medieval times.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used literally (physically stepping on something) or figuratively (disrespecting)
> - Often implies damage or harm through repeated stepping
> - The present participle "trampling" describes ongoing action
> - "Trampling on/over" is the most common usage pattern
