## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A fee or payment required to use a road, bridge, or facility.

> [!example] Examples
> - You must pay a toll to cross the bridge.
> - The highway toll increases every year.
> - We paid the toll at the gate.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
The number of deaths, casualties, or damages caused by something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The death toll from the accident was high.
> - The pandemic took a heavy toll on society.
> - The emotional toll of the war affected many.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Less Common)
To ring a bell, especially slowly and solemnly; to sound (a bell) at intervals.

> [!example] Examples
> - The church bells tolled at the funeral.
> - The bell tolled midnight.
> - They tolled the bells to announce the death.

## 📜 Etymology
The payment sense comes from Old English "toll" from Latin "telonium" (tax collector's booth), from Greek "telones" (tax collector). The bell-ringing sense comes from Old English "tollan" (to pull or ring).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Take a toll" is an idiom meaning to cause harm or damage over time
> - The "death toll" is commonly used in news reporting
> - The bell-ringing sense is less common in modern usage
> - "Toll" can be singular or plural when referring to fees or deaths
> - Figurative uses emphasize cumulative harm or consequences
> - 📺 Also seen in [[Dragon Ball Z]]
