## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A representative sent by a government or organization to conduct diplomacy or negotiations.

> [!example] Examples
> - The President suggested sending a peace envoy to the Middle East.
> - He served as envoy to the French government.
> - He was a presidential envoy on a special mission to Latin America.

### Sense 2 — *noun, diplomatic* (Less Common)
A minister plenipotentiary ranking between an ambassador and a minister resident in the diplomatic hierarchy.

> [!example] Examples
> - The country appointed a new envoy extraordinary.
> - As an envoy, she had diplomatic privileges but ranked below the ambassador.

### Sense 3 — *noun, literary* (Rare)
A short stanza concluding a poem, especially a ballade, serving as a dedication or summary.

> [!example] Examples
> - The poet added an envoy at the end of the ballade.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French **"envoyé"** (past participle of **"envoyer"** = to send). Literally *"a sent one."*

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Diplomatic hierarchy: **Ambassador > Envoy > Minister Resident.**
> - Modern usage favors **"special envoy"** for specific missions or crises.
> - Similar to **"emissary,"** but emissary implies a more temporary or secret mission.
> - The literary meaning (concluding stanza) is archaic and rarely encountered.
