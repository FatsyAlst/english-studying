## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common - Vulgar/Slang)
To urinate.

> [!example] Examples
> - I need to piss.
> - He went to piss behind a tree.
> - That expression means someone is too drunk to piss straight.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common - Vulgar/Slang)
To annoy or irritate someone; often used with "off."

> [!example] Examples
> - That behavior really pisses me off.
> - Don't piss him off; he has a terrible temper.
> - The constant delays are pissing everyone off.

### Sense 3 — *Phrasal Verb: "Piss Over"* (Vulgar/Slang)
To treat someone with contempt or disrespect; to take advantage of someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't let them piss over you.
> - He was pissed over by his boss repeatedly.
> - They pissed over the agreement and did what they wanted.

### Sense 4 — *Noun* (Vulgar/Slang)
Urine; also used as an expletive or intensifier.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bathroom reeks of piss.
> - What a load of piss!

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *pissen*, from Old French *pissier*, possibly imitative in origin or from Latin *piscāre* (to fish, urinate).

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Vulgar/slang vocabulary - inappropriate for formal contexts
> - "Pissed off" and "pissing me off" are common colloquial expressions for annoyance
> - "Pissed" can mean both "drunk" (British) and "angry" (American)
> - "Piss poor" is a vulgar intensifier meaning "extremely poor quality"
> - "Pissing rain" is slang for heavy, annoying rain
> - All phrasal verb variations maintain the vulgar register
> - Context determines whether it's crude humor or genuine vulgarism
