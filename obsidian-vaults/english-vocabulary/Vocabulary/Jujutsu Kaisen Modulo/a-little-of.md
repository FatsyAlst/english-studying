## 📖 Definition
*Quantifier phrase* — Common

A small amount or portion of something; a limited quantity of a thing. Used when the amount is small in comparison to the whole.

> [!example] Examples
> - I only have a little of this ingredient left.
> - A little of this goes a long way.
> - He spent a little of his savings on the vacation.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "lytel," related to Old Germanic roots. The phrase "a little" has been used in English for centuries to indicate a small quantity or amount.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "A little of" is used with uncountable nouns (water, money, time, etc.)
> - Should not be confused with "a few" which is used with countable nouns
> - Indicates a small but definite amount
> - Very common in everyday English conversation and writing
