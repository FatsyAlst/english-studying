## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

Broken pieces or fragments of something that has been destroyed; debris from collapsed buildings or destroyed structures; loose broken pieces of stone, concrete, and similar materials.

> [!example] Examples
> - The earthquake left the city in rubble.
> - Workers cleared the rubble from the demolished building.
> - The survivors emerged from the rubble.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "rubel," from Old French "ruble," possibly from Latin "rubeus" (red). The term evolved to describe the broken red/brown brick and stone fragments left after building destruction. Has been used in English since the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually uncountable noun (used as "rubble," not "rubbles")
> - Refers to debris from destroyed buildings or structures
> - Often used in contexts of war, natural disaster, or demolition
> - "In rubble" is a common phrase meaning completely destroyed
> - Used in both literal and figurative contexts
