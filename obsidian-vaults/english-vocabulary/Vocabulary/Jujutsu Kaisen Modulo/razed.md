## 📖 Definition
*Verb (past tense and past participle of "raze")* — Common

To completely destroy or demolish a city, building, or structure, especially by tearing it down to the ground. To level or utterly destroy.

> [!example] Examples
> - The town was razed during the bombing.
> - The old building was razed to make room for a new one.
> - The earthquake razed the entire village.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "rasen," meaning to scrape or erase. Derived from Old French "raser" (to scrape or shave), ultimately from Latin "rasare" (to scrape off or shave). The term has been used since the 15th century to describe complete destruction.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes complete and thorough destruction
> - Usually implies tearing down to ground level
> - Past tense of "raze"; present tense is "raze"
> - Often used in historical contexts describing war or natural disaster damage
> - More formal and literary than "destroy" or "demolish"
> - Commonly appears with "to the ground"
