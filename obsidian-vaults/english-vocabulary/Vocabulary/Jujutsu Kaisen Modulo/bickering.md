## 📖 Definition
*Verb (present participle)* — Common

To argue about small, unimportant things in a petty or annoying way; to quarrel over trivial matters.

> [!example] Examples
> - The couple was bickering about who would do the dishes.
> - Stop bickering and listen to what I'm saying.
> - The children were constantly bickering over toys.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "bickren," possibly from Old Norse "bikkja" meaning to chatter or twitter. The term developed to mean arguing over small matters, similar to the chattering or twittering of animals.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies arguing over trivial or petty matters, not serious issues
> - Often has a negative or annoying connotation
> - Common in descriptions of relationships, families, or groups
> - Present participle form often used to describe ongoing behavior
