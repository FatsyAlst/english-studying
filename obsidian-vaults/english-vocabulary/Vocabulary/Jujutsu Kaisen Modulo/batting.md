## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The action of hitting a ball with a bat, especially in baseball or cricket; one's performance as a batter.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her batting improved significantly this season.
> - He stepped up to the plate for batting practice.
> - Strong batting could win the game.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Sheets of cotton, wool, or synthetic fibers compressed together, used for padding, insulation, or stuffing in quilts, furniture, and clothing.

> [!example] Examples
> - The quilt was filled with cotton batting.
> - She stuffed the pillow with batting.
> - Batting provides insulation in winter clothing.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To hit or strike with a bat; to take a turn swinging at a ball in baseball or similar sports.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's batting cleanup tonight.
> - She batted the ball far into the field.
> - They batted the ball back and forth.

## 📜 Etymology
"Batting" (sports) from Middle English *batten* 'to hit,' possibly from Old French *batte* 'stick, club.' "Batting" (fabric) likely related to the verb form or from French *batte* (beating/tufting fibers).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sports sense: hitting with a bat in baseball, cricket, or similar games.
> - "Batting average" = statistical measure of hits divided by at-bats.
> - Textile sense: compressed fibers used as padding or insulation.
> - Verb form: "batting" (present participle) for active play.
> - Can appear in phrases like "batting cleanup" (hitting in a strong position).
