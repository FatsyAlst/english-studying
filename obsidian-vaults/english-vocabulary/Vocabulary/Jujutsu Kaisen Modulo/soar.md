## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To fly or glide high in the air with little effort; to move upward gracefully.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bird soared above the mountains.
> - He soared through the sky with incredible speed.
> - The eagle soared majestically on the wind currents.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To rise rapidly to a high level; to increase sharply or succeed greatly.

> [!example] Examples
> - His power soared to new heights.
> - Prices soared during the crisis.
> - Her confidence soared after the victory.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "sorer," possibly from Latin "ex-" (out) + "aurae" (air). The word entered English in the 13th century meaning to fly upward.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (flight/movement) or figurative (rising in quality, power, or success).
> - Commonly used in action sequences to describe movement and in dramatic scenes for character growth.
> - "Soaring" is often used as an adjective to describe ambitious or grand qualities.
