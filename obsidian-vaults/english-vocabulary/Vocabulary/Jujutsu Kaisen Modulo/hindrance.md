## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A person or thing that causes difficulties or delays progress; an obstacle or impediment.

> [!example] Examples
> - The weather was a hindrance to our travel plans.
> - Without financial burdens as a hindrance, she could pursue her dreams.
> - Bureaucratic regulations became a major hindrance to innovation.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *hindren* (to hinder), with suffix *-ance* indicating a state or condition. Related to the verb "hinder."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Note: Common misspelling is "hinderance" — the correct spelling is "hindrance"
> - Formal and slightly literary register
> - Often used in phrases like "rather a hindrance than a help"
> - The verb form "hinder" is more commonly used than the noun "hindrance" in modern English
