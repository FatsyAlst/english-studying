## 📖 Definition
*Verb (Present Participle)* — (Common)
Sheltering, holding, or maintaining secretly (feelings, thoughts, or a person); nurturing or keeping something concealed.

> [!example] Examples
> - She was harboring resentment toward him.
> - He harbored doubts about their plan.
> - They were harboring a fugitive in their home.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "herebeorh," from "here" (army) + "beorh" (shelter). Originally meant a shelter for an army, later extended to harbors (shelter for ships) and metaphorically to sheltering feelings or people.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb meaning to hold, keep, or shelter something/someone, often secretly or concealed.
> - Commonly used to describe holding feelings (resentment, doubt, suspicion) or protecting a person.
> - Present participle form used as a gerund or adjective.
> - More formal/literary than "holding" or "keeping."
