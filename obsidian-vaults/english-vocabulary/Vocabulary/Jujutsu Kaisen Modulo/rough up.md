## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To treat someone roughly or violently; to beat up or handle someone in a forceful or aggressive manner.

> [!example] Examples
> - The gangsters roughed him up in the alley.
> - They threatened to rough up anyone who didn't pay protection money.
> - The police accused him of roughing up the suspect during arrest.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Rough" comes from Old English "ruh" and Middle English "rouhe"; "up" is a particle that intensifies the action. Combined as phrasal verb to mean handle with force.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/colloquial expression
> - Often implies physical violence or aggressive treatment
> - Used in both literal and exaggerated contexts
> - Separable phrasal verb: "rough up" or "rough them up"
