## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Not anticipated or predicted; occurring unexpectedly or without warning; caught unaware by something unanticipated.

> [!example] Examples
> - An unforeseen obstacle appeared in their plans.
> - The unforeseen consequences of his actions shocked everyone.
> - Due to unforeseen circumstances, the event was postponed.

## 📜 Etymology
From "un-" (not) + "foresee" (to see beforehand), derived from Old English *foresēon*. "Foresee" comes from *fore-* (before) + *see*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and professional register
> - Often used in explanations for delays or changes
> - Implies something that could not have been reasonably anticipated
> - Common phrase: "due to unforeseen circumstances"
> - Suggests helplessness or inability to control events
> - Opposite: anticipated, expected, foreseen, planned
