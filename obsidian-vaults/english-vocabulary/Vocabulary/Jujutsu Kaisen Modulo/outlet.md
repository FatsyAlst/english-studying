## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
An electrical socket or connection point where devices can be plugged in to receive power.

> [!example] Examples
> - I plugged the lamp into the wall outlet.
> - The outlet near the bed wasn't working.
> - We need more outlets in this room.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A retail store that sells goods, often at discounted prices; a point of sale.

> [!example] Examples
> - She bought shoes at the outlet store.
> - The outlet mall had many discounted brands.
> - This is one of our largest outlets in the region.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Common)
A means of expression, release, or satisfaction; a channel through which something is expressed or discharged.

> [!example] Examples
> - Sports became her outlet for stress.
> - Art was his creative outlet.
> - The organization serves as an outlet for charitable work.

## 📜 Etymology
From "out" + "let"; "let" comes from Old English "lætan" (to allow or let flow). Originally meant a place where something flows out or is allowed to escape.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most common in electrical context in everyday use
> - Retail "outlet" often refers to factory outlets selling overstock or slightly damaged goods
> - Metaphorical use (emotional/creative outlet) is common in psychology and personal development
> - British English may use "socket" instead of "outlet" for electrical connections
