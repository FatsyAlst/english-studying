## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A long, pointed, rigid object; a sharp protrusion or a metal fastener with a pointed end.

> [!example] Examples
> - The cursed spirit had sharp spikes protruding from its body.
> - Metal spikes lined the defensive barrier.
> - He drove spikes through the wooden planks.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To pierce or impale with a spike; to add something to a drink secretly; to increase sharply.

> [!example] Examples
> - The technique spiked the opponent into the ground.
> - Crime rates spiked during the crisis.
> - The energy spiked suddenly during the battle.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *spíkr* or Middle Dutch *spijker*, related to Germanic roots meaning "pointed" or "peg." Originally referred to wooden pegs or nails.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: Physical pointed object
> - Verb: Can mean to pierce, poison a drink, or increase suddenly
> - Common in combat/action contexts (Jujutsu Kaisen)
> - "Spike" as verb is often used for sudden increases in data
> - Adjective "spiky" can mean having spikes or being hostile/irritable
