## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Less Common

The act of surrendering, abandoning, or giving up something (such as a right, claim, or possession); the formal renunciation or relinquishing of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The relinquishment of power was sudden and unexpected.
> - His relinquishment of the title left a vacant position.
> - The treaty required the relinquishment of territorial claims.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From verb "relinquish," derived from Latin *relinquere* (to leave behind), composed of *re-* (back) + *linquere* (to leave). The noun form adds the suffix "-ment."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "giving up" or "abandonment"
> - Often used in legal, political, or institutional contexts
> - Can imply reluctance or significance
> - Associated with loss of power, control, or rights
> - The verb form "relinquish" is more commonly used than the noun
> - Carries weight and formality in its tone
