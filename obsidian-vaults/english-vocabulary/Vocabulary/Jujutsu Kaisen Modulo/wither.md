## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To shrivel, wilt, or fade; to lose freshness, strength, or vigor.

> [!example] Examples
> - The flowers withered in the heat.
> - His body withered away from illness.
> - The plant withered without water.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To weaken or cause to fade; to diminish or decline gradually.

> [!example] Examples
> - His courage withered as the battle continued.
> - The hope withered away over time.
> - Fear withered her determination.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "wither," possibly from "weather" or related to Old English "widerian" (to resist). The exact origin is uncertain, but the word has meant to shrivel or fade since the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (plants withering) or figurative (emotions, courage, hope withering).
> - Often paired with "away" in figurative contexts: "wither away."
> - The adjective "withering" can describe a damaging or scathing look, criticism, or remark.
> - Emphasizes gradual decline or weakening rather than sudden collapse.
