## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To fail to care for or attend to something or someone; to leave something unattended or uncared for through lack of attention.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't neglect your health.
> - She neglected to lock the door.
> - The landlord neglected the building's maintenance.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
The state of being neglected; lack of proper care or attention; disregard.

> [!example] Examples
> - The garden fell into neglect.
> - The house showed signs of years of neglect.
> - Child neglect is a serious social issue.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Common)
The act of neglecting; failure to do something required or expected.

> [!example] Examples
> - His neglect of his responsibilities caused problems.
> - The neglect of safety procedures led to accidents.
> - Deliberate neglect of the rules resulted in punishment.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *neglegere* (to disregard), from *neg-* (not) + *legere* (to pick up, choose, care for).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (verb): very common in both positive and negative contexts
> - Sense 2 (noun state): often implies a cumulative problem from long-term inattention
> - Sense 3 (noun action): emphasizes the act of failing to do something
> - "Neglect" is stronger than "overlook"; it implies a repeated or habitual failure
> - Can apply to physical objects, people, responsibilities, or abstract things
> - "Willful neglect" or "criminal neglect" have legal implications
