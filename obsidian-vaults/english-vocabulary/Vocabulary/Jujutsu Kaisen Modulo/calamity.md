## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A disastrous event causing great damage, loss, or misfortune; a serious disaster or tragedy.

> [!example] Examples
> - The earthquake was a terrible calamity for the region.
> - The invasion was viewed as a national calamity.
> - Many feared the appearance of the cursed spirit would bring calamity upon them.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *calamitas*, possibly related to *calamus* (reed, stalk) or indicating a time of crop failure. Originally meant a destructive hailstorm or crop failure.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "disaster" or "catastrophe"
> - Often implies widespread suffering or damage
> - Frequently used in historical or dramatic contexts
> - Can refer to personal or collective misfortune
> - Synonym: catastrophe, disaster, tragedy
