## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
Exceptional ability, skill, or strength, especially in combat or a specific domain.

> [!example] Examples
> - His martial arts prowess was unmatched in the tournament.
> - She demonstrated her athletic prowess on the field.
> - The warrior's prowess in battle made him legendary.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "prouesse," from "prou" (advantage, profit), ultimately from Latin "prodesse" (to be beneficial).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal/literary word, often used in contexts of impressive abilities or achievements.
> - Frequently associated with combat, sports, or intellectual domains.
> - Emphasizes exceptional skill rather than just competence.
