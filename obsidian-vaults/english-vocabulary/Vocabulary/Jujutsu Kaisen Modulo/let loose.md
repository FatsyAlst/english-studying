## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — (Common)
To release from restraint; to allow something or someone to be free; to unleash or set free.

> [!example] Examples
> - He let loose a cry of rage into the night.
> - She finally let loose and stopped holding back her emotions.
> - The warrior let loose his full power without restraint.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "lettan" (to let, hinder) and "los" (loose, free). The phrase combines these to mean freeing from constraint.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Phrasal verb (verb + adverb/preposition combination).
> - Can be literal (releasing a physical object) or figurative (releasing emotions, power, aggression).
> - Often appears in action sequences to indicate unleashing full strength.
> - "Let loose" and "let go" are similar but distinct—"let loose" emphasizes unleashing while "let go" emphasizes releasing grip.
