## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To move back and forth or side to side in a smooth, gentle motion; to oscillate or rock.

> [!example] Examples
> - The trees swayed in the wind.
> - She swayed to the music.
> - The ship swayed on the rough waves.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To influence someone's opinion or decision; to cause someone to change their mind.

> [!example] Examples
> - The argument swayed the jury in his favor.
> - Nothing could sway her from her decision.
> - The evidence swayed public opinion.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
The act of swaying; influence or control over someone or something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The politician held sway over his constituents.
> - The king's sway extended across the realm.
> - She had great sway in the organization.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *swægian* 'swing, rush'; related to Old Norse *sveygja*. Noun sense 'influence' developed in 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe both physical motion and abstract influence.
> - Verb sense of influencing is very common in formal/political contexts.
> - "Hold sway" is an idiom meaning to have influence or power.
> - Present tense: sway, swayed (past), swaying (present participle).
