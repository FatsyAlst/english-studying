## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Enclosed or nearly enclosed by land; having no coast. Describes a country or region completely surrounded by land with no direct access to the ocean.

> [!example] Examples
> - Switzerland is completely landlocked.
> - Afghanistan is a landlocked country.
> - Landlocked Ethiopia has called for access to the sea.

### Sense 2 — *adjective, biology* (Common)
Confined to fresh water by some barrier; remaining in inland waters through the entire life cycle (especially fish).

> [!example] Examples
> - Landlocked salmon live in fresh water permanently isolated from the sea.
> - Some populations of trout have become landlocked over time.

### Sense 3 — *adjective, US real estate* (Less Common)
Surrounded by other property and having no access to a public road.

> [!example] Examples
> - The landlocked parcel of land cannot be developed without an easement.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Earliest known use in the early 1600s (Richard Hawkins, 1622). Compound of *land* + *locked*, literally "locked in by land."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - There are currently **44 landlocked countries** in the world; largest is Kazakhstan, smallest is Vatican City.
> - Only **two double-landlocked countries** exist: Liechtenstein and Uzbekistan (surrounded entirely by other landlocked countries).
> - Being landlocked creates economic disadvantages: no seaports, higher transport costs for trade.
> - Can be used figuratively: feeling "landlocked" = feeling trapped or cut off.
> - Antonyms: coastal, maritime.
