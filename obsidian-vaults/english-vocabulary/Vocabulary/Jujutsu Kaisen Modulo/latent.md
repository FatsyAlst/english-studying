## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — Common

Present or existing, but not visible, active, or apparent; hidden or dormant and capable of becoming active.

> [!example] Examples
> - Latent talent waiting to be discovered.
> - The virus remained latent in his body for months.
> - She had a latent fear of heights.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *latent-* 'lying hidden,' present participle of *latere* 'to lie hidden.' Entered English in 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often describes potential that hasn't yet been realized or activated.
> - Common in medical contexts: latent diseases or infections.
> - Formal register; commonly used in academic and technical writing.
> - Opposite of overt or manifest.
> - Can apply to skills, powers, threats, desires, and conditions.
