## 📖 Definition
*Verb (Past Tense)* — (Common)
Had the opposite of the intended effect; resulted in harm or failure to the person who initiated it.

> [!example] Examples
> - His plan backfired when the enemy anticipated his move.
> - The attack backfired, injuring the user instead.
> - Her strategy backfired spectacularly during the confrontation.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "back" + "fire." Originally referred to a gun or engine firing backwards. Extended metaphorically to mean plans having the opposite effect.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used when a plan, attack, or strategy intended to harm someone ends up harming the initiator instead.
> - Common in action/battle scenarios.
> - Can be used literally (weapon misfiring) or figuratively (plan going wrong).
