## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To fall or drop suddenly from a high level (prices, numbers, temperature).

> [!example] Examples
> - Share prices plummeted after the announcement.
> - The temperature plummeted overnight.
> - His confidence plummeted when he saw the exam.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To decrease very fast by a large amount (popularity, ratings, sales).

> [!example] Examples
> - Sales have plummeted in recent months.
> - The movie's ratings plummeted.
> - Tourist numbers plummeted during the pandemic.

### Sense 3 — *noun, technical* (Less Common)
A heavy weight on a line, used to check verticality (plumb line).

> [!example] Examples
> - The mason used a plummet to make sure the wall was straight.
> - Ancient builders relied on plummets and levels.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin **"plumbum"** (lead) — the same root as "plumber" and "plumbing."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Strong word — implies a big, sudden, steep decrease.
> - Stronger than the neutral "fall" or "drop."
> - Typically intransitive (no direct object).
> - Very common in news, business, and reports.
