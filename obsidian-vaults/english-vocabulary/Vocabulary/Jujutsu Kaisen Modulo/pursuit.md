## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
The act of chasing or pursuing someone or something; the chase itself.

> [!example] Examples
> - The police engaged in a pursuit of the suspect.
> - The cat abandoned its pursuit of the mouse.
> - High-speed pursuits are dangerous for everyone on the road.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
An activity, hobby, or objective that someone pursues or works toward; a goal or interest.

> [!example] Examples
> - Artistic pursuits are his greatest passion.
> - The pursuit of knowledge drives scientific research.
> - They embarked on a pursuit of happiness.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Less Common)
The continuation or following of something in sequence or progression.

> [!example] Examples
> - In pursuit of the argument, he made several points.
> - She continued her research in pursuit of the truth.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *poursuite* (pursuit, prosecution), from *poursuivre* (to pursue), from Latin *prosequi* (to follow, continue).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (chasing): often used in criminal/law enforcement contexts
> - Sense 2 (activity/goal): very common in formal and informal writing; philosophical and motivational contexts
> - "In pursuit of" is a common phrase indicating a goal or objective
> - "Hot pursuit" refers to chasing actively or urgently (often legal terminology)
> - Can refer to literal chasing or figurative pursuit of abstract goals
> - Plural "pursuits" is common when discussing multiple hobbies or activities
