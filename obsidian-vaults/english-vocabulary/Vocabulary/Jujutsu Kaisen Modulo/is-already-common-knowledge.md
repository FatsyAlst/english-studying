## 📖 Definition
*phrase/idiom* — Common

Information or a fact that is widely known or understood by most people; something that does not need explanation because it is general knowledge.

> [!example] Examples
> - That it's dangerous to drink and drive is already common knowledge.
> - The risks of smoking are already common knowledge to most people.
> - The benefits of exercise are already common knowledge these days.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Common knowledge" combines Old English *cumun* (shared by all) with French *knowlege* (understanding). The phrase emerged in Middle English to describe widely understood information.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used to indicate something doesn't require explanation or proof.
> - Often appears as "it is already common knowledge that..."
> - Implies the listener/reader should already be aware of the fact.
> - Can be used to dismiss or simplify discussion of obvious points.
> - Formal enough for academic and professional writing.
