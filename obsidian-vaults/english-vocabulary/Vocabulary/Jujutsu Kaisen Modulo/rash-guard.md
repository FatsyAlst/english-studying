## 📖 Definition
*noun* — Common

A tight-fitting shirt or garment made of stretchy material, typically worn for water sports, swimming, or martial arts to protect the skin from sun exposure, abrasion, or irritation.

> [!example] Examples
> - He wore a rash guard while surfing.
> - The swimmer put on a rash guard before diving.
> - MMA fighters often wear rash guards under their gis.

## 📜 Etymology
"Rash" derives from a skin irritation caused by friction (possibly from chafing in water); "guard" means protection. The term emerged in the 1980s as a piece of athletic/water sports equipment designed to prevent rash/irritation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily used in water sports (surfing, swimming, diving) and martial arts.
> - Provides protection from sun exposure, friction, abrasion, and cold water.
> - Often made of lycra, neoprene, or similar stretchy materials.
> - Available in long-sleeve and short-sleeve versions.
> - Common abbreviation: "rashguard" or "rash guard" (both acceptable).
