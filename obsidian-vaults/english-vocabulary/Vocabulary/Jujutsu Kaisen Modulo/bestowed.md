## 📖 Definition
*verb* — Common

To give or grant something as a gift, honor, or benefit; to confer or present formally, often with a sense of generosity or dignity.

> [!example] Examples
> - The king bestowed a title upon the knight.
> - The organization bestowed an award on the volunteer.
> - Good fortune was bestowed upon them.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *be-* (prefix) + *stow* 'place, position' (from Old Norse *staðr*). Originally meant "to place or put"; developed meaning "to give or confer" in Middle English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat archaic register; less common in casual speech.
> - Implies generosity, grace, or formal ceremony in the giving.
> - Often used with honors, titles, gifts, or blessings.
> - Synonyms: grant, confer, award, present, give.
> - Frequently used in formal or literary contexts.
> - Structure: "bestow something upon someone."
