## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

(Of something undesirable) flourishing or spreading unchecked; growing or occurring in abundance without restraint.

> [!example] Examples
> - Corruption was rampant throughout the city government.
> - The disease spread rampant through the population.
> - Rampant deforestation has destroyed countless ecosystems.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "rampant" (climbing, rearing), from Latin "rampere" (to climb). Originally used in heraldry to describe animals standing on hind legs.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually describes negative or problematic conditions
> - Emphasizes the lack of control or restraint
> - More formal/literary than "widespread" or "prevalent"
