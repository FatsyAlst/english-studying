## 📖 Definition
*Verb (Past Participle)* — (Common)
Filled or saturated with a particular quality, feeling, or substance; infused.

> [!example] Examples
> - The artifact was imbued with magical power.
> - His speech was imbued with passion and conviction.
> - The water was imbued with medicinal herbs.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "imbuere" meaning "to soak in, steep, or stain." The prefix "im-" means "in" and "buere" relates to absorbing or soaking.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used passively ("was imbued with...").
> - Typically describes abstract qualities, powers, or feelings being infused into something.
> - More literary/formal than "filled with" or "full of."
