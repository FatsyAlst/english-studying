## 📖 Definition
*Verb (past tense)* — Common

To attempt to repair, improve, or adjust something, often in an amateur or experimental way; to work with something in a casual or unskilled manner.

> [!example] Examples
> - He tinkered with the engine all afternoon.
> - She tinkered with the recipe until it was perfect.
> - They tinkered with the code to find the bug.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *tinkeren*, related to the occupation of "tinker" (an itinerant mender of pots and pans). The verb developed from the sense of working on metal objects in the manner of a tinker.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually implies casual, amateur, or experimental work rather than professional repair
> - Often followed by "with" when specifying what is being worked on
> - Can have a slightly negative connotation (suggesting ineffective tinkering) or neutral (experimental adjustment)
> - Common in technology and engineering contexts
> - Related expression: "tinker around" suggests aimless experimentation
