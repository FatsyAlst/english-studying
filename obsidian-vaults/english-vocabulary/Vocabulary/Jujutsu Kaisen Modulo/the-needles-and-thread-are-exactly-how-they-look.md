## 📖 Definition
*Expression/Idiom* — Rare (Context-specific)

An expression suggesting that something or someone is exactly as they appear on the surface; what you see is what you get. There is no hidden depth, complexity, or ulterior motive. The needles and thread (tools used for basic sewing) represent simple, straightforward objects with no hidden purposes.

> [!example] Examples
> - These are simple people; the needles and thread are exactly how they look.
> - Don't read too much into it—the needles and thread are exactly how they look.
> - Her intentions are clear; the needles and thread are exactly how they look in this situation.
