## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
Entertaining, pleasurable activity; something that entertains or amuses.

> [!example] Examples
> - The carnival offered various amusements for the children.
> - Video games are a popular source of amusement.
> - They sought amusement in the arcade.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A state of being entertained or finding something funny; the feeling of being amused.

> [!example] Examples
> - He watched with amusement as his rival stumbled.
> - A look of amusement crossed her face.
> - She could not hide her amusement at the situation.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "amusement," from "amuser" (to amuse), from "a-" (to) + "muser" (to make think, ponder). Later extended to mean entertaining or diverting.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can refer to both the activity/entertainment itself and the emotional state of being amused.
> - "Amusement park" is a common compound.
> - The adjective forms are "amused" (feeling amused) and "amusing" (causing amusement).
