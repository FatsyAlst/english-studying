## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A place where stone, marble, slate, or other materials are extracted from the ground through mining or excavation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The granite quarry provided materials for the construction project.
> - Workers extracted limestone from the quarry.
> - The old quarry has been converted into a lake.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Less Common)
An animal or person being hunted or pursued; prey or target.

> [!example] Examples
> - The lion stalked its quarry through the savanna.
> - The detective followed his quarry through the streets.
> - The hunter finally caught his quarry.

## 📜 Etymology
Sense 1 comes from Old French "carriere" (open pit), from Latin "carraria" (road for carts). Sense 2 comes from Old French "querre" (entrails), referring to the part of game given to hunting dogs, eventually meaning the prey itself.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The mining sense is much more common in modern usage
> - "Quarry" can be used as a verb meaning to extract from a quarry
> - The hunting sense appears primarily in literature and historical contexts
> - Important distinction: "quarry" (mining site) vs "query" (question)
> - Often appears in place names (e.g., "the old quarry road")
