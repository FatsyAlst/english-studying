## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A public sale in which goods or property are sold to the highest bidder.

> [!example] Examples
> - The painting will be sold at auction next week.
> - A classic Rolls-Royce fetched £25,000 at auction.
> - They're holding an auction of jewelry on Thursday.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To sell something at auction through competitive bidding.

> [!example] Examples
> - The artwork is being auctioned to benefit UNICEF.
> - The stamps will be auctioned tomorrow.
> - The family is auctioning off its art collection.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin **"auctio"** meaning *an increase*, referring to the increasing bids made during the sale process.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common prepositions: **"at auction,"** **"by auction,"** **"up for auction."**
> - Types of auctions: English (ascending bids), Dutch (descending price), silent, and online.
> - **"Auction off"** is a common phrasal verb meaning to sell items at auction, often to get rid of them.
