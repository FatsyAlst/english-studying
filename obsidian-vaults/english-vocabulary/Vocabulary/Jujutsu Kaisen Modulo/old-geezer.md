## 📖 Definition
*Noun phrase* — Common

An elderly man, often used in a mildly derogatory or dismissive way. The term suggests that the person is old and sometimes considered odd or eccentric.

> [!example] Examples
> - That old geezer sitting on the bench has been there for hours.
> - Don't be such an old geezer about new technology.
> - He's a cranky old geezer, but he's got good stories.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From an obsolete term "guiser," probably altered from Scots "guiser" meaning "person in disguise." First known use was in 1885.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Mildly insulting or dismissive in tone
> - More common in American English
> - Often used informally in speech rather than formal writing
> - Suggests the person is old and possibly odd or eccentric
