## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Less Common

A leader of a tribe, group, or clan; a person who holds the highest rank or authority in a traditional or indigenous community.

> [!example] Examples
> - The chieftain made decisions for the entire tribe.
> - The old chieftain passed his authority to his son.
> - Various chieftains gathered to discuss the treaty.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "chief" (head), from Latin "caput" (head). The "-tain" suffix comes from Old French "-ain", a diminutive or variant suffix. Entered English in the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in historical, anthropological, or fictional contexts
> - Primarily refers to indigenous or tribal leadership
> - More formal/literary than "chief" (though related)
> - Frequently appears in historical narratives and fantasy literature
> - "Chieftaincy" refers to the territory or position of a chieftain
