## 📖 Definition
*Verb (present participle)* — Common

To grip or hold tightly, usually with one's hand; to grasp firmly and desperately. Often suggests holding on to something with intensity, whether from fear, desperation, or strong desire to keep it.

> [!example] Examples
> - She was clutching her purse tightly.
> - The child was clutching the toy, refusing to let go.
> - He clutched at the rope as he fell.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "clyccan," meaning to grip or clench. Related to Old Germanic roots. The term has been used in English since the Middle Ages to describe holding something firmly.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies holding something tightly or desperately
> - Often suggests emotional attachment or fear of losing something
> - "Clutching at" means trying to hold on to something, often figuratively
> - As a noun, "clutch" refers to a tight grip or a group of things held together
> - Common in descriptive writing and narrative
