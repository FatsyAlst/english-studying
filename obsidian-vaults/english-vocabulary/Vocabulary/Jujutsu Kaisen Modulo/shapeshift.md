## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Less Common

To change or transform one's physical form or shape; to assume a different appearance or form, either literally (in fantasy/supernatural contexts) or figuratively (to change one's identity, behavior, or role).

> [!example] Examples
> - The witch can shapeshift into any animal.
> - The creature shapeshifts to escape danger.
> - Companies shapeshift their image to appeal to new markets.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound word formed from "shape" (the form or appearance of something) + "shift" (to change). While ancient mythology and folklore feature shapeshifters, the term "shapeshift" as a single verb is relatively modern, becoming more prominent in 20th-century fantasy and science fiction literature.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in fantasy, supernatural, and science fiction contexts
> - Can be used literally (actual physical transformation) or figuratively (changing identity or role)
> - Related noun is "shapeshifter" (a being that can shapeshift)
> - Often used in gaming, fantasy literature, and anime
