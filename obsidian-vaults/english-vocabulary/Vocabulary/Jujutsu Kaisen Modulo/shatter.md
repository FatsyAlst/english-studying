## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Most Common

To break suddenly and violently into small pieces; to cause to break apart with force.

> [!example] Examples
> - The window shattered when the stone hit it.
> - The impact shattered his confidence completely.
> - Their dreams shattered in an instant.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *shateren*, possibly imitative in origin or related to Old English *sceaterian* (to scatter).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used literally (physical breaking) or figuratively (emotional impact)
> - Often associated with violent or sudden action
> - Common in metaphorical contexts about emotions, relationships, or abstract concepts
