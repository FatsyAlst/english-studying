## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To communicate or express; to make known or understood; to transport or carry from one place to another.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her expression conveyed a deep sense of sadness.
> - He tried to convey his feelings through his words.
> - The power surge conveyed immense strength.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "conveyer," from "con-" (with) + "voie" (way, road). Originally meant "to carry along" or "to transport," extended to mean communicating ideas.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Transitive verb; requires a direct object (what is being conveyed).
> - Can mean either physically transporting something or communicating/expressing something.
> - In narrative contexts, often used to describe expressing emotions or meanings through action or appearance.
