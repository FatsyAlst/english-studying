## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A person who is easily tricked or fooled; a stupid or foolish person. Someone who is very gullible and falls for tricks and schemes.

> [!example] Examples
> - He fell for the scam like a chump.
> - Don't be a chump; think before you believe that story.
> - That chump believed everything I said.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Origin is uncertain, but the term dates back to the 19th century in informal English, likely related to the word "chunk" or possibly from "chop" (the mouth/head area in slang).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very informal and mildly insulting
> - Common in spoken English and informal writing
> - Can also refer to the head in slang usage
> - "Chump change" is a common idiom meaning a negligible amount of money
