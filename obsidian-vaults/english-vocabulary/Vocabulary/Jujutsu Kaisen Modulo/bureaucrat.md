## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

An official in a bureaucracy; a person who works in government administration or large organizations, often associated with strict adherence to rules and procedures.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bureaucrat insisted on following every regulation precisely.
> - Local bureaucrats approved the building permit.
> - She grew tired of dealing with bureaucrats and red tape.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *bureaucrate*, derived from *bureau* (desk, office) + *-crat* (rule, ruler), from Greek *kratia* (rule, power). The term emerged in the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often carries a slightly negative or neutral-to-negative connotation, suggesting excessive adherence to rules
> - Contrasts with policymakers or leaders who have broader decision-making authority
> - Can be used descriptively or pejoratively depending on context
> - Singular form; plural is "bureaucrats"
> - Related to "red tape" and "bureaucracy"
