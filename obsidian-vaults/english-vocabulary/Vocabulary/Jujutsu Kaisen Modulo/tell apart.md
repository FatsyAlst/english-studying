## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
To distinguish between two or more people or things that are very similar; to see or recognize the differences between them.

> [!example] Examples
> - I can never tell the twins apart.
> - It's hard to tell these two brands apart by taste alone.
> - Can you tell them apart just by looking?

## 📜 Etymology
From *tell* (Old English *tellan*, "to count, reckon, recount") + *apart* (from French *à part*, "to the side"). The verb *tell* in the sense of "distinguish, discern" dates from the 14th century — as in *"I can tell"* (= I can perceive/know). Combined with *apart*, it creates the specific meaning of distinguishing between similar things.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable phrasal verb: *"tell them apart"* or *"tell the twins apart."* The object goes between *tell* and *apart*.
> - Almost always used with similar-looking or confusable subjects: twins, similar products, lookalikes.
> - Compare with [[resemblance]] (Dragon Ball Z) — *resemblance* describes **similarity**, while *tell apart* describes the ability to **distinguish** despite similarity.
> - Synonyms: distinguish, differentiate, discriminate between.
