## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Suffering the loss of a close relation or friend through death.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bereaved family gathered at the funeral home.
> - She was deeply bereaved after losing her mother.
> - Counselling services are available for bereaved parents.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Formal)
A person or people who have recently lost a close relation (used with *the*).

> [!example] Examples
> - The

> - Our thoughts are with the bereaved at this difficult time.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *berēafian* ("to deprive of, take away, seize, rob"), from *be-* (intensive prefix) + *rēafian* ("to rob, plunder"). Related to German *rauben* ("to rob"). The original sense was "to be robbed of" — death "robs" you of a loved one. The past participle *bereft* survives as an adjective meaning "deprived of" in a broader sense.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - **"Bereaved" vs. "bereft":** *Bereaved* specifically refers to loss through death. *Bereft* is broader and means "deprived of" or "lacking" anything: *bereft of hope, bereft of ideas*.
> - Formal register — in everyday speech, people more often say "who lost someone" rather than "bereaved."
> - "Bereavement leave" is the standard term for time off work after a death in the family.
> - The noun *bereavement* refers to the state or period of grief after someone's death.
