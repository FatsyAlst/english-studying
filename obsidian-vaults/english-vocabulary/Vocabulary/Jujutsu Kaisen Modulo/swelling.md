## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
An enlargement or protuberance of a body part due to injury, inflammation, or accumulation of fluid; a puffiness or bulge.

> [!example] Examples
> - The swelling in his ankle went down after ice.
> - She had significant swelling around the injury.
> - The swelling should subside within days.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To become larger or puffed up; to increase in size due to fluid accumulation or inflammation.

> [!example] Examples
> - His hand began swelling after the punch.
> - The river swelled with rainfall.
> - Her pride swelled as she won.

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (Common)
Increased in size; puffed up or enlarged from normal.

> [!example] Examples
> - His swollen eye was nearly shut.
> - The swollen glands indicated infection.
> - The swollen river flooded the banks.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *swellan* 'to swell, expand'; Proto-Germanic *swellanan*. Related to German *schwellen*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: refers to the physical enlargement or puffiness.
> - Verb: can be literal (body part swelling) or metaphorical (emotions swelling).
> - Adjective: "swollen" is the more common form (swollen ankle, not "swelled ankle").
> - Medical context: often paired with inflammation, bruising, or injury.
> - Opposite: subsiding, reducing, decreasing.
