## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To bring forth, summon, or create (feelings, memories, images) in the mind.

> [!example] Examples
> - The music evoked feelings of nostalgia in the audience.
> - His words evoked memories of their childhood.
> - The painting evokes a sense of mystery and wonder.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "evocare" - "e-" (out) + "vocare" (to call). Literally "to call out."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Transitive verb; requires a direct object (what is being evoked).
> - Often describes the effect of art, music, words, or atmosphere on emotions/memories.
> - "Evocative" is the common adjective form used to describe something that evokes.
