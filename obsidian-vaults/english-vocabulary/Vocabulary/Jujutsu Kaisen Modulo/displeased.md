## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Dissatisfied, annoyed, or unhappy; not pleased or satisfied.

> [!example] Examples
> - The king looked displeased at the news.
> - She was clearly displeased with his performance.
> - They appeared displeased by the decision.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "dis-" (opposite of) + "pleased" (from Latin "placere," to please). The prefix "dis-" negates the meaning of pleased.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective form; expresses a state of dissatisfaction or annoyance.
> - Often used to describe facial expressions or demeanor indicating unhappiness.
> - More formal than "unhappy" or "angry," suggesting controlled displeasure.
> - Related noun: "displeasure" (feeling of being displeased).
