## 📖 Definition
*verb* — Common

To make something known to the public; to announce or advertise widely; to give public attention or prominence to something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The company publicized the new product launch.
> - They publicized the event through social media.
> - She publicized her findings in a press release.

## 📜 Etymology
From "public" (Latin *publicus* 'of the people') + "-ize" (suffix forming verbs). Entered English in 19th century to mean "make public."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Means to make something widely known or announced to the public.
> - Often used in business, marketing, and media contexts.
> - Related noun: publicity (the public attention itself).
> - Synonyms: advertise, promote, broadcast, announce, publicize.
> - Can be used positively (announce good news) or negatively (expose wrongdoing).
