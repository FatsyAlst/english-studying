## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To reveal or display something that was hidden or covered; to make something known or public for the first time; to remove a veil or covering.

> [!example] Examples
> - The organization unveiled its new strategy at the conference.
> - She unveiled her true power during the battle.
> - They will unveil the monument next week.

## 📜 Etymology
From "un-" (to reverse or remove) + "veil" (a covering). "Veil" comes from Old French *veile*, from Latin *vela* (sails, coverings).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal: to remove a physical veil or covering
> - Figurative: to reveal secrets, plans, or abilities
> - Often used in formal announcements
> - Common in product launches or ceremonial occasions
> - Creates narrative tension when something hidden is revealed
> - Implies something significant or important being disclosed
> - Related noun: "unveiling" (the act or ceremony of revealing)
