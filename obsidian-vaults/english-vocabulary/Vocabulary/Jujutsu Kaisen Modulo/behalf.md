## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun, in "on behalf of"* (Most Common)
As a representative of someone; acting in their name or as their agent.

> [!example] Examples
> - On behalf of the department, I would like to thank you all.
> - His wife will accept the prize on his behalf.
> - I'd like to say on behalf of the whole group that we wish you well.

### Sense 2 — *noun, in "in behalf of"* (Less Common, American)
For the benefit or interest of someone; in support of.

> [!example] Examples
> - He began a petition in behalf of the farmers.
> - We raised money in behalf of the earthquake victims.
> - The employees picketed in behalf of the fired executive.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English **"bihalve"** meaning *by the side of*. Related to **"half"** — originally meaning on someone's side.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost never used alone — always appears in **"on behalf of"** or **"in behalf of."**
> - Traditional distinction: **"on behalf of"** = representing; **"in behalf of"** = for the benefit of.
> - In British English, **"on behalf of"** covers both meanings.
> - Don't confuse with **"on the part of"** (which attributes actions or qualities).
