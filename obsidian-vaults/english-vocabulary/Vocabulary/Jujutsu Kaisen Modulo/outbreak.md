## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A sudden occurrence or increase of something unwanted, especially disease or violence.

> [!example] Examples
> - The festival ended early after an outbreak of violence.
> - At the outbreak of war, he enlisted as a private.
> - An outbreak of typhoid occurred in the region.
> - Health officials are working to contain the outbreak.
> - There was a fresh outbreak of fighting in the region.

## 📜 Etymology
From **"out"** + **"break"** — something breaking out suddenly and often violently.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost always refers to something **negative or unwanted**.
> - In epidemiology: **outbreak** (localized) vs. **epidemic** (community-wide) vs. **pandemic** (international).
> - Using "outbreak" as a verb is archaic; use the phrasal verb **"break out"** instead.
> - Very common in news media and reporting.
