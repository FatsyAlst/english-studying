## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Most Common)
Not wild or fierce; domesticated; docile and submissive; subdued or unexciting.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tame beast no longer posed a threat.
> - A tame existence compared to his previous adventures.
> - The supposedly dangerous creature was remarkably tame.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To domesticate or train a wild animal; to bring under control or subjugate; to make less wild or fierce.

> [!example] Examples
> - He managed to tame the cursed spirit.
> - She tamed her wild instincts through discipline.
> - The beast could never be fully tamed.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *tam*, related to Old Saxon *tam*, Old High German *zam*, and Latin *domatus* (tamed). Originally applied to animals, later extended metaphorically.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective: not wild; domesticated; mild
> - Verb: to make docile or controllable
> - Can refer to both animals and abstract concepts (power, nature)
> - Opposite: wild, fierce, untamed
> - "Taming" suggests difficult control process
> - Common in fantasy with magical creatures or spirits
> - Can imply loss of natural power or essence
> - Related adjective: "tamely" (in a subdued manner)
> - 📺 Also seen in [[Dragon Ball Z]]
