## 📖 Definition
*Verb (Past Tense)* — (Common)
Declared null and void; cancelled, nullified, or made invalid.

> [!example] Examples
> - The contract was voided due to a legal technicality.
> - His power was suddenly voided by the enemy's technique.
> - The agreement was voided when conditions changed.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "voide," from Latin "vocivus" meaning "empty." Related to "vacuous" and "vacant."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used in legal contexts to mean "nullified" or "made invalid."
> - In anime/manga contexts, often describes powers or abilities being cancelled.
> - "Void" as a noun can also mean empty space.
