## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A powder made by grinding grain (usually wheat), used in cooking and baking to make bread, cakes, and other foods.

> [!example] Examples
> - Mix the flour with sugar and eggs.
> - She needs two cups of flour for the recipe.
> - Whole wheat flour is healthier than white flour.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Less Common)
To coat something lightly with flour; to dust with flour.

> [!example] Examples
> - Flour the work surface before kneading.
> - Flour your hands so the dough won't stick.
> - She floured the baking pan.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *flor* 'flower,' metaphorically 'the finest part' (flour being the finest product of grain milling). Latin *florem* 'flower.'

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always uncountable as a noun (flour, not flours).
> - Verb: often used in cooking instructions (flour the surface).
> - Homophone: "flower" (the plant).
> - Different types: all-purpose, cake, bread, whole wheat, pastry.
> - "Floury" means covered with or containing flour.
