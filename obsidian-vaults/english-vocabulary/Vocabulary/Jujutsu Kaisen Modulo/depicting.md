## 📖 Definition
*Verb (present participle)* — Common

To show, describe, or represent something in a picture, film, story, or other form of media; to illustrate or portray something in visual or narrative form.

> [!example] Examples
> - The painting is depicting a scene from ancient Rome.
> - The film depicts the life of a famous scientist.
> - The artist is depicting the emotion of sadness through color.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "depictus," past participle of "depingere," meaning "to paint or describe," from "de-" (down/thoroughly) + "pingere" (to paint or draw). The term has been used in English since the 16th century to mean representing in visual or narrative form.
