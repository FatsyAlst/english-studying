## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
An action or system designed to prevent or respond to an undesirable situation or threat; a measure taken to counteract a danger or attack.

> [!example] Examples
> - The government implemented countermeasures against cyberattacks.
> - Scientists developed countermeasures to slow the spread of the disease.
> - Electronic countermeasures can jam enemy radar signals.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of *counter-* (from Latin *contra*, "against") + *measure* (from Latin *mensura*, "a measuring"). Literally "a measure taken against." The word has been used in military and strategic contexts since at least the early 20th century, gaining broader usage in technology, medicine, and cybersecurity.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - A compound noun — always one word or hyphenated (*countermeasure*), never two separate words.
> - Common in military, cybersecurity, and public health contexts.
> - Compare with [[deterrent]] — a *deterrent* **prevents** action through intimidation, while a *countermeasure* **responds to or neutralizes** an existing threat.
> - Compare with [[ward off]] — both involve defense, but *countermeasure* is more systematic and formal.
