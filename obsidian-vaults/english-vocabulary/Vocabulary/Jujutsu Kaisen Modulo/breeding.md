## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The keeping and managing of animals in controlled conditions in order to produce offspring, especially for particular traits or purposes.

> [!example] Examples
> - The family's business was horse breeding.
> - Certain characteristics can be developed through selective breeding.
> - A campaign to save the condor by captive breeding was launched.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
The natural process by which animals reproduce and produce young.

> [!example] Examples
> - The penguins' breeding season has begun.
> - The wetlands provide a vital breeding habitat for waterfowl.
> - Ocean temperatures affect the fish's feeding and breeding patterns.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Formal)
Good manners, refinement, and social conduct believed to result from a privileged upbringing or aristocratic background.

> [!example] Examples
> - "They may have money, but they have no breeding," he said.
> - The young man clearly has breeding.
> - A woman of impeccable breeding would never behave in such a way.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *brēdan* ("to bring young to birth, cherish, keep warm"), from West Germanic **brōdjan-*. The Proto-Indo-European root is **bhreu-* ("to boil, bubble, burn"), connecting *breed* to words like *brood*, *broth*, *brew*, and even *bread* — all sharing the idea of a warm, generative process. The social sense of "good breeding" (refinement from good upbringing) emerged in the 1590s.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - **"A breeding ground for"** — one of the most powerful figurative uses, describing an environment that encourages something undesirable: *"a breeding ground for corruption."*
> - **"Born and bred"** — fixed alliterative idiom meaning raised entirely in a place: *"I'm London born and bred."*
> - **"Familiarity breeds contempt"** — proverb meaning excessive familiarity leads to disrespect.
> - The "good breeding" (social manners) sense sounds old-fashioned and elitist today.
> - Related to [[inbreeding]] — inbreeding is breeding *within* a closed group, a harmful subset of the general concept.
