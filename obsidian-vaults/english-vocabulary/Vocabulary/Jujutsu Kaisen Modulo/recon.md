## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common — Informal)
Short for *reconnaissance* — the activity of getting information about an area for military or other purposes by sending out soldiers, drones, or scouts to observe; also, a preliminary survey or inspection.

> [!example] Examples
> - The team was sent on a recon mission behind enemy lines.
> - Let's do a quick recon of the area before we set up camp.
> - Aerial recon confirmed the presence of enemy troops.

## 📜 Etymology
Informal clipping of *reconnaissance*, which comes from French *reconnaître* ("to recognize, examine"), from Latin *recognoscere* ("to review, recall to mind"), from *re-* ("again") + *cognoscere* ("to get to know"). The abbreviated form *recon* became widespread in American military slang during World War II.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily military slang that has crossed into everyday informal English.
> - Can be used as both noun ("do a recon") and verb ("recon the area").
> - The full word *reconnaissance* is preferred in formal writing.
> - In gaming and anime contexts, "recon" is widely understood and commonly used.
