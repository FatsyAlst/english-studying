## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A longer or alternative route taken instead of the direct or intended path; a deviation from the planned course.

> [!example] Examples
> - Construction on the highway forced us to take a detour.
> - We made a detour to visit the old castle on the way.
> - The signs indicated a detour around the accident.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
(Figuratively) A temporary or unplanned digression from a main topic, plan, or course of action.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was just a brief detour from our main discussion.
> - His career took an unexpected detour into politics.
> - Let's not get sidetracked with detours from the agenda.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "détour", from Old French "deturner" (to turn away), composed of "de-" (away) + "tourner" (to turn).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used as both noun and verb
> - Often implies necessity or disruption to original plan
> - Frequently used in travel, navigation, and figuratively in conversations
> - "Scenic detour" suggests a pleasant diversion
