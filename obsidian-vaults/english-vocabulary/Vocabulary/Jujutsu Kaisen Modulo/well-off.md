## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

In a good financial position; relatively wealthy or prosperous; having sufficient money or resources; comfortable financially.

> [!example] Examples
> - He comes from a well-off family.
> - She's financially well-off despite her modest lifestyle.
> - The well-off neighborhoods have better schools.

## 📜 Etymology
From "well" (in good condition) + "off" (away from difficulty). This compound developed in English to describe being in a good situation, particularly financially.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Euphemistic and polite way to describe wealth; less direct than "rich"
> - Often hyphenated when used as compound adjective before a noun
> - Can describe individuals, families, or communities
> - Implies comfort and security rather than extreme wealth or luxury
> - More formal and considerate tone than simply saying "rich"
> - Frequently appears in social commentary and demographic analysis
