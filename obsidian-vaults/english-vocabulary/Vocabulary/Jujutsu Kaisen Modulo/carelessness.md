## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The quality or state of being careless; lack of attention to what one is doing, resulting in mistakes, accidents, or avoidable problems.

> [!example] Examples
> - His carelessness caused the accident.
> - The report was full of errors due to sheer carelessness.
> - A moment of carelessness can lead to serious consequences.

## 📜 Etymology
From *careless* + *-ness*. The adjective *careless* dates from Old English *carlēas* ("free from care, untroubled"), from *care* (Old English *caru*, "grief, anxiety") + *-lēas* ("-less"). The original meaning was positive ("free from worry"), but it shifted to the negative sense ("not paying attention") by the 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Distinguished from *negligence* (which implies a duty of care that was breached — a legal concept) and *recklessness* (which implies conscious disregard of risk).
> - *Carelessness* is about lack of attention; *recklessness* is about knowingly ignoring danger.
> - The original Old English meaning was actually positive (carefree), which survives in the word *carefree*.
