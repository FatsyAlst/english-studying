## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To attack and handle roughly or violently, typically causing injury; to assault or beat someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - The wild dog mauled the intruder.
> - He was mauled by the bear in the forest.
> - The victim was mauled during the attack.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To handle roughly or without care; to treat something or someone harshly or carelessly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The critics mauled his new play.
> - His reputation was mauled by the scandal.
> - The rough handling mauled the delicate package.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "maul" or "mall", possibly from Latin "malleus" (hammer), or from Germanic roots referring to hitting or striking.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often describes violent animal attacks or brutal physical assaults
> - Extended use for harsh criticism ("mauled by critics")
> - Past tense is "mauled"; past participle is "mauled"
> - Strong, negative connotations
> - Often used in news reports and dramatic narratives
