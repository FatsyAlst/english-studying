## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — Common

To propose or formally suggest someone for a position, award, or candidacy; to name or appoint officially.

> [!example] Examples
> - The council nominated him as their representative.
> - She was nominated for Best Supporting Actress.
> - The principal nominated three teachers for the award.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *nominatus* (named), derived from *nomen* (name). The term originally meant simply "to name" but evolved to mean "to propose for office or honor."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and official process
> - Usually followed by "for" (position/award) or "as" (role)
> - Common in organizational, political, and awards contexts
> - Related noun: "nominee" (the person nominated)
> - "Nomination" is the act or proposal itself
> - Often the first step toward election or selection
