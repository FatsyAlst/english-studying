## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A ridge of rock, coral, or sand beneath the surface of the sea or ocean that can be dangerous to ships.

> [!example] Examples
> - The ship ran aground on the reef.
> - Divers explored the coral reef.
> - Reefs are home to diverse marine life.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Less Common)
A strip of material that can be folded to reduce the area of a sail.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sailor adjusted the reef in the sail.
> - Let out a reef when the wind calms.
> - The captain ordered the reefs to be taken in.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *rif* 'rib, ridge'; related to German *Riff*. Originally meant a ridge of rock. Nautical "reef" (sail portion) may be separate origin, possibly from Dutch *reef*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most common: coral reefs with abundant marine life (noun).
> - Nautical sense: reefs are sections of sail that can be folded/furled to reduce sail area in strong wind.
> - Often a hazard to navigation in shallow waters.
> - Reefs support diverse ecosystems and are environmentally important.
> - Verb form "reef" = to reduce the area of a sail by folding.
