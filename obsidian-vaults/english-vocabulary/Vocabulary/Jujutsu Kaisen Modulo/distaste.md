## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A strong dislike or aversion; a feeling of disgust or disapproval.

> [!example] Examples
> - He expressed his distaste for the plan.
> - She had a clear distaste for his arrogant attitude.
> - The general viewed the betrayal with distaste.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "dis-" (not, opposite of) + "taste." Literally "bad taste," extended to mean dislike or aversion.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun form describing a feeling of dislike or disgust.
> - Often followed by "for" or "of" to indicate what is disliked.
> - More formal/literary than "dislike."
> - "Distasteful" is the common adjective form.
