## 📖 Definition
At a disadvantage; in a difficult situation where you are reacting rather than controlling events.

---
- put someone on the back foot
- catch someone on the back foot
- be on the back foot

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in sports, politics, and business.
> - Opposite idea: ‘on the front foot’ = in control / attacking.

---

> [!info]- 🇧🇷 Translation (Portuguese)
> **Tradução:** Na defensiva / Em desvantagem / Em situação difícil
>
> | English | Portuguese |
> |---------|------------|
> | The early goal put them on the back foot. | O gol cedo os colocou na defensiva. |
> | After the bad press, the company was on the back foot. | Depois da má repercussão, a empresa ficou em desvantagem. |
