## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Always making secret, dishonest plans to get what you want.

> [!example] Examples
> - The scheming villain betrayed everyone in the end.
> - Don't trust him — he's a scheming liar.
> - She plays a scheming politician in the series.

### Sense 2 — *verb (present participle)* (Common)
Making secret plans, usually to do something dishonest.

> [!example] Examples
> - They were scheming to take over the company.
> - What are you two scheming now?

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *scheme*, from Latin *schēma* / Greek *skhēma* "form, figure, shape"; the negative sense "a crafty plan, plot" developed in the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In BrE the noun *scheme* is often neutral (= official plan or programme); in AmE it often suggests a dishonest plan.
> - The adjective *scheming* is always negative.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[devious]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
