## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective / verb (past participle)* (Most Common)
(Of metal or another material) gradually damaged or destroyed by a chemical reaction, especially rust or oxidation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The battery terminals were corroded, so the car wouldn't start.
> - Salt water had corroded the old pipes.
> - Corroded contacts can cause intermittent electrical faults.

### Sense 2 — *verb (figurative)* (Less Common)
To weaken or damage something gradually, such as trust, confidence or values.

> [!example] Examples
> - Years of lies had corroded their friendship.
> - Constant criticism corroded his confidence.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *corrōdere* "to gnaw to pieces", from *com-* (intensive) + *rōdere* "to gnaw" — the same root as *rodent*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Technical in chemistry and engineering (*corrosion engineering*, *corrosion-resistant steel*).
> - *Rust* is the corrosion of iron and steel specifically.
> - *Corrosive* can describe a harmful influence: *a corrosive effect on trust*.
> - Portuguese cognate: *corroído / corroer*.
