## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
(Of food or drink) pleasant or at least acceptable to taste.

> [!example] Examples
> - The canteen food is cheap and just about palatable.
> - Add some lemon to make the medicine more palatable.
> - The soup was barely palatable.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common — Figurative)
(Of an idea, fact or plan) acceptable; easy to accept or agree with.

> [!example] Examples
> - They tried to make the price increase more palatable to students.
> - The truth isn't always palatable.
> - He found a more palatable way of saying no.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *palate* (the roof of the mouth, once thought to be the seat of taste), from Latin *palātum*, + *-able*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Frequent in the comparative (*more palatable*) and the negative (*unpalatable*).
> - The figurative sense is very common in news and politics.
> - Don't mix up *palate* (taste/mouth), *palette* (artist's colours) and *pallet* (wooden platform).
> - Portuguese cognate: *palatável*.
