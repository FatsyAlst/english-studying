## 📖 Definition
*idiom* (Less Common — Informal, mainly AmE)
To work extremely hard, often physically, usually for someone else's benefit.

> [!example] Examples
> - My parents busted their backs so we could study abroad.
> - I've been busting my back on this project and nobody noticed.
> - He busts his back working two jobs.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Idiom | bust one's back (bust my back, busting her back, busted their backs) |
| Variants | break one's back, bust one's butt / hump (informal), work one's tail off |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- bust my back for (someone)
- bust one's back working
- bust one's back to (do something)

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Bust* is a 19th-century American variant of *burst* (= break). The back stands for hard physical labour, as in the more neutral *break one's back*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal; *break one's back* is the more neutral version.
> - Often expresses frustration about effort that nobody recognises.
> - Literally, *bust your back* can also mean to injure your back.
> - See [[bust]] for the general meanings of *bust*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[work one's tail off]] (from [[Dr. Stone]])
