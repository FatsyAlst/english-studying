## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
Alcoholic drink.

> [!example] Examples
> - His breath reeked of booze.
> - We need to buy some booze for the party.
> - He's trying to stay off the booze.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Less Common — Informal)
To drink a lot of alcohol.

> [!example] Examples
> - They were out boozing all night.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch *būsen* "to drink to excess"; recorded in English as *bouse* in the 13th century, with the spelling *booze* from the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal; neutral words: *alcohol*, *drinks*.
> - *A boozer* in BrE often means a pub.
> - Student vocabulary: *pre-drinks* / *pres* (BrE) = drinks before going out.
> - Seen together with [[reek]] in Dr. Stone (*reeked of booze*).
