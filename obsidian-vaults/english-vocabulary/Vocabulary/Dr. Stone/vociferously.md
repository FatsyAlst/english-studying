## 📖 Definition
*adverb* (Less Common — Formal)
In a loud, forceful and insistent way, especially when complaining, protesting or giving an opinion.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fans protested vociferously when the referee cancelled the goal.
> - Several students complained vociferously about the new exam timetable.
> - She argued vociferously against the plan, but nobody listened.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *vōciferārī* "to shout, yell", from *vōx* "voice" + *ferre* "to carry" — literally "to carry the voice". The adjective *vociferous* entered English in the 17th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Mostly written/formal; in conversation people say *loudly*, *angrily* or *strongly*.
> - Used for opinions, protests and complaints — not for neutral noise (a baby cries *loudly*, not *vociferously*).
> - Portuguese cognate: *vociferar* (to shout angrily) — the meaning is very close.
