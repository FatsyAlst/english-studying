## 📖 Definition
*adjective / verb (past participle)* (Common)
Suddenly surprised or slightly frightened by something unexpected, often making you jump or move quickly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The cat was startled by the noise and ran under the bed.
> - She looked startled when I called her name.
> - Sorry, I didn't mean to startle you.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *steartlian* "to kick, struggle"; the modern sense "to surprise someone so that they start (= jump)" appeared in the 16th century. Related to *start* (to make a sudden movement).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Startled* = a sudden, short surprise, often with a physical reaction.
> - Compare: *frightened* (fear), *shocked* (strong emotional surprise), *surprised* (neutral).
> - *Startling* = surprising: *startling results*.
