## 📖 Definition
*adjective (idiom)* (Common — Informal)
Completely naked; without any clothes at all.

> [!example] Examples
> - A fan ran across the pitch buck naked during the match.
> - The kids were running around buck naked in the garden.
> - I opened the door buck naked thinking it was my roommate — it wasn't.

## 📜 Etymology
First recorded in the US in 1913. The origin of *buck* here is uncertain: it may come from *buck* (a male animal) or refer to skin as smooth as buckskin. The variant *butt naked* appeared about fifty years later.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and humorous; the neutral version is *completely naked*.
> - *Buck* here has nothing to do with money (*a buck* = a dollar).
> - Normally used after a verb: *He was buck naked.*
