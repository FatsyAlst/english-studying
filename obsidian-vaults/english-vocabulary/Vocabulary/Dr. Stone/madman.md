## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A man who behaves in a wild, dangerous or crazy way (older use: a man who is mentally ill — now offensive).

> [!example] Examples
> - Some madman was driving the wrong way down the motorway.
> - He laughed like a madman when the plan worked.

### Sense 2 — *noun, in "like a madman"* (Most Common — Informal)
*Like a madman* = with great speed, energy or intensity.

> [!example] Examples
> - I worked like a madman to finish the project on time.
> - He was typing like a madman ten minutes before the deadline.
> - She drove like a madman to get to the airport.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *mad* (Old English *gemǣd(e)d* "out of one's mind") + *man*; in use since the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Calling someone with a mental illness a *madman* is offensive; use it only for wild behaviour.
> - *Like a madman* is very common and usually harmless.
> - BrE *mad* often = crazy; AmE *mad* often = angry.
> - Similar: *like a maniac*.
