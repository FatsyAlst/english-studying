## 📖 Definition
*idiom* (Common — Informal)
A polite but firm way of refusing an offer, usually because you think the offer is not good, useful or attractive.

> [!example] Examples
> - He offered me a ride on his motorbike in the pouring rain — thanks, but no thanks.
> - "Want to join the 6 a.m. study group?" "Thanks, but no thanks."
> - They offered him the job with a lower salary, and he said thanks, but no thanks.

## 📜 Etymology
A 20th-century English expression that joins a polite *thanks* to a clear refusal. The hyphenated noun form (*a thanks-but-no-thanks*) is also common.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Tone matters: it can sound friendly, ironic or slightly dismissive.
> - Typical when the offer is unattractive or a bit ridiculous.
> - Softer alternatives: *I'll pass, thanks* / *Thanks, but I'm good* / *That's kind, but no.*
> - As a noun: *The company sent me a thanks-but-no-thanks email.*
