## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Less Common)
Having very strict, old-fashioned moral attitudes and disapproving of behaviour that is relaxed, fun or slightly improper.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her strait-laced parents didn't let her go to parties.
> - He looks strait-laced, but he's actually really funny.
> - The strait-laced professor was shocked by the students' jokes.

## 📜 Etymology
From *strait* (archaic "tight, narrow", via Old French *estreit* from Latin *strictus*) + *laced*. In the 16th century it described someone whose bodice or corset was laced very tightly; the figurative sense "morally strict, prudish" followed.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The spelling *straight-laced* is very common and widely accepted, although *strait-laced* is the original.
> - Mildly negative or teasing.
> - Synonyms: prim, prudish, buttoned-up.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[uptight]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
