## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Making you feel very happy, excited and full of energy.

> [!example] Examples
> - Riding a roller coaster for the first time was exhilarating.
> - There's nothing more exhilarating than a last-second comeback.
> - We had an exhilarating hike up the mountain.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *exhilarāre* "to gladden", from *ex-* (thoroughly) + *hilarāre* "to cheer", from *hilaris* "cheerful" — the same root as *hilarious*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Spelling trap: *exhil**a**rating* (not *exhilerating*) — think of *hil**a**rious*.
> - Stronger than *exciting*; often about physical thrills (speed, height, sport).
> - *-ing* = causes the feeling (*an exhilarating ride*); *-ed* = has the feeling (*I felt exhilarated*).
> - Pronunciation: ig-ZIL-uh-ray-ting.
