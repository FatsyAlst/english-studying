## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To pull or carry something heavy with a lot of effort.

> [!example] Examples
> - We hauled the sofa up three flights of stairs.
> - They hauled the boat out of the water.
> - I had to haul my suitcase across the whole station.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
An amount of something that has been taken, won, bought or caught, especially a large one.

> [!example] Examples
> - The police found a huge haul of stolen phones.
> - She posted her shopping haul online.
> - Five gold medals — the team's best haul ever.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
A distance or period of time that is long and difficult, especially in *a long haul* / *for the long haul*.

> [!example] Examples
> - It's a long haul from Leuven to São Paulo.
> - An engineering degree is a long haul.
> - Are you in this for the long haul?

## 🔗 Collocations
- haul something up / out / away
- a big / record haul
- a drugs haul
- a long-haul flight
- for the long haul
- haul someone over the coals (= criticise severely)

---
## 📜 Etymology
A variant of *hale* (to drag, pull), from Old French *haler*, of Germanic origin; related to Dutch *halen* "to fetch".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Airline vocabulary: *long-haul* vs *short-haul* flights.
> - *Shopping haul* / *haul video* is a common social-media term.
> - *Haul* stresses weight and effort.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[lug]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
