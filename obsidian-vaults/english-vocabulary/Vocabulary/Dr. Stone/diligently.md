## 📖 Definition
*adverb* (Common)
In a careful, steady and hard-working way, giving a task a lot of effort and attention.

> [!example] Examples
> - She diligently reviewed every formula before the exam.
> - The team worked diligently to fix the bug before the deadline.
> - He diligently took notes in every lecture.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *diligent*, via Old French, from Latin *dīligēns* "attentive, careful", present participle of *dīligere* "to value highly, love".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Positive: it praises effort and care, not speed.
> - Slightly formal; in speech: *worked really hard*, *carefully*.
> - *Due diligence* = careful investigation before a decision (business, law, engineering projects).
> - Portuguese cognate: *diligentemente*.
