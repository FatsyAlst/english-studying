## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A wicked or cruel person; an evil or malevolent being

> [!example] Examples
> - The fiend destroyed the village without mercy
> - That vicious fiend must be stopped
> - The fiend laughed at their suffering
> - An ancient fiend awakened to threaten the world

### Sense 2 — *noun* (informal)
A person who is intensely interested in or obsessed with something; an enthusiast

> [!example] Examples
> - A martial arts fiend
> - A training fiend
> - A power-hungry fiend

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "feond" meaning enemy or adversary, derived from Proto-Germanic. Originally meant any enemy, but over time became associated with evil supernatural beings or extremely wicked people.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary vocabulary
> - Often used to describe villains or evil antagonists
> - Can imply supernatural or demonic qualities
> - Common in fantasy and action narratives
> - Modern informal use: someone very enthusiastic about something
