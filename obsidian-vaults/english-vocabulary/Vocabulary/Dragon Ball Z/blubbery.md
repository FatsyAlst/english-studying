## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Soft, flabby, and hanging loosely; having excessive body fat

> [!example] Examples
> - The blubbery character struggled to move quickly.
> - His blubbery appearance masked his strength.
> - The blubbery villain was surprisingly quick.

## 📜 Etymology
From *blubber* (fat of whales), possibly from Dutch *blubber* (bubble, foam), or imitative in origin relating to the soft, wobbly nature of the substance.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and somewhat derogatory when describing people
> - Emphasizes the soft, jiggly quality of excess flesh
> - Also relates to whale blubber (the animal substance)
