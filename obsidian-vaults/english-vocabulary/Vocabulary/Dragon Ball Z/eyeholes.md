## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Low-Medium Frequency)

Openings in a mask, helmet, hood, or similar covering that allow a person to see through; the space where the eyes are visible.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior's helmet had narrow eyeholes for better protection.
> - He peered through the eyeholes of his mask.
> - The villain's disguise had slits for eyeholes to maintain anonymity.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound noun from *eye* (organ of sight) + *hole* (opening). Both components have Old English origins.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically appears in plural form "eyeholes"
> - Common when describing masks, helmets, or disguises
> - Usually refers to functional openings for vision
> - Often used in contexts involving armor or protective gear
