## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To make a serious or urgent request; to plead

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To be attractive or interesting to someone

### Sense 3 — *noun* (common)
The quality of being attractive or interesting; an attraction

### Sense 4 — *noun* (formal)
A formal request to a higher court to reverse a decision

> [!example] Examples
> - He appealed to the council for help.
> - The challenge appeals to her competitive nature.
> - The character's strength held great appeal for fans.
> - The verdict was appealed to a higher court.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *apeler*, from Latin *appellare* meaning to address, speak to, or call upon. Related to "call" and "appellation".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Usually followed by "to" + person or "for" + thing
> - Sense 2: What appeals to you = what interests/attracts you
> - The adjective "appealing" is very common (= attractive)
> - Legal sense is formal and specific to court contexts
