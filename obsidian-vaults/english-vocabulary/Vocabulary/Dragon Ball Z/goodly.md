## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (archaic/formal)
Of good size or quality; considerable in amount or appearance; attractive or pleasing in appearance

> [!example] Examples
> - A goodly number of warriors gathered for the tournament
> - The warrior possessed a goodly physique
> - A goodly sum of prize money was offered
> - The hall had goodly proportions

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "godlic" meaning divine or excellent. The "good" derives from Proto-Germanic "gadaz" and was combined with the suffix "-ly" to create an adjective form. The word is now archaic in modern English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Archaic or literary word, rarely used in modern English except for stylistic effect
> - Often appears in formal or historical contexts
> - Can describe both physical appearance and quantity
> - More formal than simply saying "good" or "large"
