## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Greatest in amount, degree, or extent; highest or most extreme.

> [!example] Examples
> - This is a matter of the utmost importance.
> - She showed the utmost respect for her elders.
> - Safety is of the utmost concern here.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
The greatest amount or degree possible; one's best effort.

> [!example] Examples
> - We did our utmost to finish on time.
> - This represents the utmost in technology.
> - He gave his utmost in the competition.

Middle English *utmest/ūtmest*, double superlative of Old English *ūt* 'out' + *-mest* ('-most'); meaning 'farthest, extreme' from 14th c., 'greatest degree' from early 14th c.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and emphatic; used to emphasize the highest degree of something.
> - Always used before noun as an adjective (e.g., 'utmost care', not 'care utmost').
> - Common phrase: 'do one's utmost' = make one's best effort.
> - Often confused with 'upmost' (incorrect usage); 'utmost' is the correct spelling.
> - 📺 Also seen in [[My Hero Academia Vigilantes]]
