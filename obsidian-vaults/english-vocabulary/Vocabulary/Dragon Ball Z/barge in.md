## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To enter a place suddenly and rudely without permission or warning; to interrupt someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't barge in here without knocking!
> - He barged in during the meeting.
> - She barged in and interrupted our conversation.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Barge" comes from Middle English barge, from Old French barge, possibly from Latin barca (boat). The sense of "pushing forcefully" developed from the image of a barge pushing through water.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies rudeness or lack of respect
> - Often used to describe sudden, unwanted interruptions
> - Can be physical (entering a room) or conversational (interrupting)
> - Informal and casual in tone
