## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
An offer of a price, especially in an auction or competitive situation

> [!example] Examples
> - His bidding at the auction was quickly outmatched.
> - The bidding reached millions of dollars.
> - She made a strong opening bid.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A command or instruction; doing what someone wants or orders

> [!example] Examples
> - He was doing the king's bidding.
> - She followed his bidding without question.
> - At your bidding, I will help.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "biddan," meaning to ask or command. The "-ing" form creates the noun "bidding."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is common in auctions and commerce
> - Sense 2 is often used in phrases like "do someone's bidding" or "at your bidding"
> - In Sense 2, it often implies obedience or servitude
