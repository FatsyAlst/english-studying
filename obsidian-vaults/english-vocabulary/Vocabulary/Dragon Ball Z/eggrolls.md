## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Medium Frequency)

A fried appetizer made from a wrapper filled with cabbage, meat, or other ingredients, rolled into a cylindrical shape; common in Asian cuisine.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku devoured a plate of egg rolls in seconds.
> - The restaurant served crispy egg rolls as an appetizer.
> - She ordered egg rolls with sweet and sour sauce.

## 📜 Etymology
From *egg* + *roll*. The name derives from the cylindrical shape and the egg-based wrapper commonly used in Chinese-American cuisine.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often spelled as two words "egg rolls"
> - Common appetizer in Chinese and Pan-Asian restaurants
> - Frequently served with dipping sauces
