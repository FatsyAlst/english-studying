## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Very ugly or unpleasant (synonym: revolting).

> [!example] Examples
> - A hideous face/building/dress.
> - Their new colour scheme is hideous!
> - She was wearing a hideous yellow dress.

---
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Adjective | hideous |
| Adverb | hideously |
| Noun | hideousness |
| Related | hideosity |

---
- hideous face/building/dress/crime/colour
- hideous monster/nightmare
- fairly hideous

---
Middle English (c.1300): from Old French hidos/hideus (earlier hisdos), from hide/hisde 'fear' (unknown origin; possibly Germanic). Original sense: terrifying/horrible; "repulsive" from late 14th c.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Strong negative; for appearance/sound/experience.
> - Synonyms: ghastly, horrible, ugly, disgusting.
> - Often exaggerated for emphasis.
