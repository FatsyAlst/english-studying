## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To die or be destroyed; to cease to exist.

> [!example] Examples
> - Many warriors perished in the great battle.
> - The ancient civilization perished centuries ago.
> - He feared they would all perish in the explosion.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To go bad or deteriorate, especially of food.

> [!example] Examples
> - The food will perish if left unrefrigerated.
> - The fruit perished in the heat.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "perire" meaning to pass away or be lost. Evolved through French to enter English with formal, literary connotations.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "die" or "die out"
> - Often used in dramatic or historical contexts
> - Can describe both people and things
