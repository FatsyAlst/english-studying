## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Soft, delicate, or easily damaged; not tough or hard.

> [!example] Examples
> - His tender wounds still needed care.
> - The tender plant was damaged in the storm.
> - Handle that equipment with tender care.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Young, inexperienced, or vulnerable; at an early or delicate stage.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was only a tender young fighter when he joined the tournament.
> - At the tender age of twelve, he began his training.
> - The tender warriors faced seasoned opponents.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "tendre," from Latin "tenere" (to hold). Related to "tendon" and "tension."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe physical softness or emotional delicacy
> - "At a tender age" is a common idiomatic expression
> - Often implies vulnerability or need for care
> - Can also mean "affectionate" or "loving"
> - Different from "tender" meaning to offer something
