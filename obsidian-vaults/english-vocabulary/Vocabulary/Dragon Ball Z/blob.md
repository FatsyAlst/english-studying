## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A thick, compact mass of something, typically shapeless or amorphous; a viscous or gelatinous lump; an ill-defined or irregular form.

> [!example] Examples
> - A blob of molten energy exploded across the battlefield.
> - The creature appeared as a shifting blob of matter.
> - Blobs of debris scattered after the impact.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *blabbe*, possibly from Old Norse *blabr* or imitative in origin. The word has been used since the 15th century to describe formless or shapeless masses. Modern usage popularized by science fiction and computing contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun describing amorphous or formless mass
> - Often carries informal or casual tone
> - Can be literal (physical blob) or figurative
> - Related: blobby (adjective), blobbing (verb form)
> - Common in science fiction and fantasy contexts
> - Implies lack of defined shape
> - Can describe various substances (liquid, gel, matter)
