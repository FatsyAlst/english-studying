## 📖 Definition
*Idiom/Interjection* — Common

A phrase meaning good luck, typically said to performers before a show or event; an expression wishing someone success, ironically using the phrase about breaking a leg (normally bad) to mean something good.

> [!example] Examples
> - Break a leg in the tournament!
> - Go out there and break a leg!
> - Before the big fight, they told him to break a leg
> - Break a leg out there!

## 📜 Etymology
Origin is uncertain and debated. Theories include: (1) theater superstition that wishing bad luck would reverse it, (2) from German theater traditions, (3) from the idea that if someone doesn't break a leg they haven't performed with enough enthusiasm. The phrase became common in American English by the early 20th century, particularly in theatrical contexts.
