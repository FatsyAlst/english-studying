## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common — Informal)
To drink something quickly in large mouthfuls, without stopping.

> [!example] Examples
> - He chugged an energy drink right before the match.
> - She chugged a whole bottle of water after the run.
> - The crowd started chanting "Chug! Chug! Chug!"

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
(Of an engine or vehicle) to make a regular low knocking sound while moving slowly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old train chugged up the hill.
> - A small boat chugged along the canal.

### Sense 3 — *verb, "chug along"* (Common — Informal)
To continue or make progress slowly but steadily.

> [!example] Examples
> - My thesis is chugging along.
> - The project is chugging along, just slower than planned.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative of the sound of an engine (1860s); the sense "to drink quickly" developed in mid-20th-century American English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal; BrE alternatives for drinking: *down*, *knock back*, *neck* (slang).
> - *Chugging along* = steady (if slow) progress — handy for studies and projects.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[gulp]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
