## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (present participle)* (common)
Feeling great fear or anxiety about something that might happen; anticipating something with dread.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warriors were dreading the arrival of the powerful opponent.
> - She couldn't stop dreading the final confrontation.
> - He was dreading the moment his true power would be revealed.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Causing fear or anxiety; frightening or worrying.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dreading silence before the storm felt eternal.
> - A dreading sense of impending doom filled the arena.
> - The dreading anticipation consumed his thoughts.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "drædan," of uncertain origin but possibly related to Old Norse "dræta" (to shrink, cringe). The word has been used to express fear and anxiety since Old English times.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Participle form indicates ongoing or continuous fear
> - "Dread" as a noun is distinct from the verb
> - "Dreadful" is an adjective meaning causing dread
> - Often used with future events or anticipated situations
