## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Moving or acting in a clumsy, confused, or awkward manner; lacking skill or coordination.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bumbling warrior kept tripping over his own feet.
> - His bumbling attempts at combat failed miserably.
> - She criticized his bumbling efforts in the training session.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (present participle)* (common)
Moving or handling something clumsily; blundering through a task without skill.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was bumbling through the battle without a clear strategy.
> - They were bumbling around trying to fix the machine.
> - Stop bumbling and focus on the task at hand.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "bumble," which combines "bum" (to make a buzzing sound, from imitative origins) with the frequentative suffix "-le." The sense evolved to mean moving or acting in a clumsy, uncoordinated way.
