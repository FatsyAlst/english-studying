## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To pull or jerk something suddenly and forcefully.

> [!example] Examples
> - He yanked the door open.
> - She yanked his arm to pull him away.
> - The fighter yanked his opponent toward him.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A sudden, forceful pull or jerk.

> [!example] Examples
> - With one yank, he freed himself.
> - A quick yank on the rope released the mechanism.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Possibly derived from Dutch "janken" or influenced by Native American languages. The term became common in American English in the 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More informal than "pull" or "jerk"
> - Implies sudden, forceful movement
> - Common in action scenes and dynamic descriptions
