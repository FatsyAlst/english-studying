## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Causing great fear, worry, or concern; extremely bad or unpleasant.

> [!example] Examples
> - They faced a dreadful opponent in the finals.
> - The dreadful power of his attack was unmistakable.
> - The dreadful silence before the battle was ominous.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Extremely poor in quality; terrible or awful.

> [!example] Examples
> - The food was dreadful.
> - His performance was dreadful.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "dredan" meaning to fear. The suffix "-ful" means full of, making "dreadful" literally "full of dread."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and dramatic in tone
> - Emphasizes the severity or intensity of something negative
> - More literary than "bad" or "terrible"
