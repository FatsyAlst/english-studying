## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To try to find out about someone's private life in a way that is rude or annoying.

> [!example] Examples
> - I don't mean to pry, but are you OK?
> - Stop prying into my personal life!
> - She hates it when journalists pry into her family.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common — mainly AmE (BrE: *prise*))
To force something open or apart, especially using a tool as a lever.

> [!example] Examples
> - He pried the lid off the paint can with a screwdriver.
> - We had to pry the window open.
> - Thieves pried open the back door.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Two different words: (1) *pry* (look curiously) from Middle English *prien*, origin unknown; (2) *pry* (lever open) is a back-formation from *prize / prise* "a lever", from Old French *prise* "a hold, a grip".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *I don't mean to pry, but…* = a polite way to ask a personal question.
> - *Prying eyes* = people watching you with too much curiosity.
> - AmE *pry open* = BrE *prise open*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[nosy]] (from [[My Hero Academia Vigilantes]])
