## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun/verb* (common)
Sliced bread browned by exposure to radiant heat; the act of browning bread in this way.

> [!example] Examples
> - I made toast for breakfast.
> - Would you like your toast buttered?
> - She toasted the bread until it was golden brown.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A brief ceremony where people raise glasses and drink to someone's health or honor.

> [!example] Examples
> - The groom's father gave a toast at the wedding.
> - They raised their glasses in a toast to success.
> - Let's make a toast to the birthday girl.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (slang)
A person or situation that is ruined or doomed; someone who is "finished."

> [!example] Examples
> - If the boss finds out, you're toast!
> - After that mistake, his career is toast.
> - They're toast if they don't score soon.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French toster (to roast), from Latin tostus (parched, toasted). The slang sense developed in the 20th century from the idea of being "burnt" or ruined.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The food sense is literal and common in cooking
> - The ceremony sense is used at celebrations and formal events
> - The slang sense "you're toast" is very common in informal speech
> - Often implies imminent doom or failure in the slang usage
