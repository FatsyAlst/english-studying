## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Work done by hand; something made or created by someone's skill or effort.

> [!example] Examples
> - The destruction was clearly his handiwork.
> - Their handiwork was evident in every detail of the construction.
> - This elegant design is the master's handiwork.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
The results or consequences of someone's actions, often used to describe damage or creation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The devastated city was the villain's handiwork.
> - You can see their careful handiwork throughout the structure.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "hand" and "work," literally meaning work done by hand. Originally referred to craftsmanship or manual labor.
