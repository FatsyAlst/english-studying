## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To deal with something difficult or challenging; to manage or handle a situation; to endure or withstand adversity; to contend with difficulty.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior struggled to cope with the overwhelming power.
> - She couldn't cope with the sudden transformation of her opponent.
> - Managing to cope despite severe injuries required tremendous willpower.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *couper* (to cut, strike), from Latin *colpus* (blow, strike). The original sense related to dealing with a blow or strike. Extended metaphorically to mean dealing with challenges. Used in English since the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb meaning to deal with or manage difficulty
> - Usually followed by "with"
> - Suggests struggle or effort to manage
> - Related: coping (noun/adjective), coping mechanism (psychological term)
> - Common in contexts of adversity or challenge
> - Often implies difficulty or effort
> - Can be positive (successfully coping) or negative (unable to cope)
