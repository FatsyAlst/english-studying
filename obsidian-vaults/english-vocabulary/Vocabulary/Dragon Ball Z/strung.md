## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (High Frequency)

Under stress or tension; tense and nervous. Often used in phrases like "strung up" or "strung out."

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior was strung up before the final match, unable to relax.
> - He felt strung out after the intense battle.
> - The team was strung tight, waiting for the signal to attack.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *strung*, past participle of *string*. The metaphorical sense of "under tension" comes from the image of strings pulled tight on an instrument.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past participle of "string"
> - Commonly used in phrases "strung up" (hung, or emotionally tense) and "strung out" (exhausted, addicted)
> - Often appears in emotional or psychological contexts
> - Can describe physical tension or emotional stress
