## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Frequent)
A protective covering or shell created by certain insect larvae (especially silkworms and caterpillars) during metamorphosis.

> [!example] Examples
> - The caterpillar spins a cocoon before transforming into a butterfly.
> - Silkworms produce the cocoons used to make silk fabric.
> - The chrysalis inside the cocoon protects the developing insect.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Frequent)
Any protective wrapping or enclosure; a sheltering environment.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior created an energy cocoon to shield himself from harm.
> - He lived in a cocoon of luxury, isolated from reality.
> - The protective cocoon shielded them from the dangerous radiation.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *cocon*, possibly from Old French *coque* meaning "eggshell" or "shell." Related to "coco" (coconut). Originally described the actual silkworm cocoons, later used metaphorically for protective enclosures.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is biological and specific to insect metamorphosis
> - Sense 2 is figurative, emphasizing protection and isolation
> - Often implies a temporary or transitional phase
> - Can carry both positive (protection) and negative (isolation) connotations
> - Common in fantasy and science fiction for protective barriers
