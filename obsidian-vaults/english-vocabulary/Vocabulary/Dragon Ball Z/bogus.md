## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (High Frequency)

Fake, fraudulent, or counterfeit; not authentic or genuine; false or fraudulent.

> [!example] Examples
> - That bogus fighter used a fake power level to deceive everyone.
> - His credentials turned out to be completely bogus.
> - The tournament seemed to have bogus rules that kept changing.

## 📜 Etymology
From American slang, possibly from bogus coins or from the name of a counterfeiting machine. The exact origin is uncertain, but it appeared in American English in the 1830s-1840s meaning "counterfeit."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and colloquial
> - Strong negative connotation implying deception
> - Common in everyday speech to describe something fake or fraudulent
> - Often used emphatically ("totally bogus," "completely bogus")
> - Popular in action narratives when describing impostors or fraud
