## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb (gerund/present participle)* (Most Common)
Jumping repeatedly or moving in short bouncing motions on one foot or with both feet together.

> [!example] Examples
> - The characters are hopping across the battlefield
> - He's hopping mad about losing the fight
> - They're hopping from island to island

---
### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
Full of energy; lively; busy with activity.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament is hopping with competitors
> - Business is hopping today
> - The city is really hopping this weekend

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "hopian" meaning to jump or leap. Related to "hope" in some etymologies, though the connection is debated. Germanic origin with cognates in Dutch "hoppen" and German "hoffen".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As a verb: describes rhythmic jumping movement
> - As an adjective: describes lively activity or intense emotion (hopping mad)
> - Common in compound expressions like "island-hopping" or "club-hopping"
> - Present participle form used frequently in descriptions
