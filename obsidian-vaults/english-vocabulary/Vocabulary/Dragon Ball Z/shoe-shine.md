## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The act or business of polishing shoes; a service provided to clean and polish footwear to make it shine.

> [!example] Examples
> - The young boy earned money through shoe-shine services outside the martial arts tournament.
> - A local vendor offered shoe-shine services to the spectators.
> - The warrior stopped to get a quick shoe-shine before the final battle.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A place of business or booth where shoe-shining services are offered; a shoe-shine stand.

> [!example] Examples
> - The shoe-shine on the corner was a bustling hub of activity.
> - He visited the local shoe-shine before heading to the tournament.

## 📜 Etymology
A compound noun formed from "shoe" (footwear) and "shine" (to polish and make lustrous). The hyphen in "shoe-shine" indicates it is a compound term. The word reflects the trade and service of polishing shoes, a profession that dates back centuries.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can appear as "shoe-shine" (hyphenated), "shoe shine" (two words), or "shoeshine" (one word)
> - Traditionally refers to a working-class occupation or service
> - Often appears in urban or commercial settings
> - The person providing the service is a "shoeshine boy" or "shoeshine man"
