## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Extremely hot; burning or searing with intense heat.

> [!example] Examples
> - The scorching heat of the arena made the battle exhausting.
> - A scorching blast of energy hit the mountain.
> - The scorching sun beat down on the fighters.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (figurative, common)
Intense, harsh, or severe in criticism or tone; cutting.

> [!example] Examples
> - He delivered a scorching critique of their performance.
> - The press gave them a scorching review.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "scorcnian" meaning to burn or parch. The word evolved to describe anything that is extremely hot or burning.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe both literal heat and figurative intensity
> - Often used for dramatic effect in action scenes
> - Stronger than "hot" or "burning"
