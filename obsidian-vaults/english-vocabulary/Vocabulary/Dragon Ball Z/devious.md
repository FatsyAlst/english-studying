## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Deceitful, cunning, or using dishonest methods; willing to behave unethically to achieve goals.

> [!example] Examples
> - His devious plans involved betrayal and lies.
> - The villain had a devious scheme to gain power.
> - She employed devious tactics in the negotiations.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Roundabout, indirect, or not going in a direct line; circuitous.

> [!example] Examples
> - They took a devious route through the mountains.
> - The devious path wound around obstacles.
> - A devious trail led them deeper into the forest.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "deviosus," meaning "out of the way," from "de-" (away) and "via" (way, road). The sense evolved from physical deviation (not following a direct path) to moral deviation (dishonest behavior).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (dishonest) is most common in modern usage
> - Sense 2 (roundabout) is less common but still used in geographical or descriptive contexts
> - Often carries strong negative connotations
> - Can describe both plans/methods and people's characters
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[scheming]] (from [[Dr. Stone]])
