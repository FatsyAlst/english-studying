## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A sudden short burst of air, wind, smoke, or steam

> [!example] Examples
> - A puff of smoke obscured his face
> - The dragon breathed out puffs of fire
> - With a puff of air, the candles were extinguished

### Sense 2 — *verb* (frequent)
To blow out air in short bursts; to breathe heavily

> [!example] Examples
> - He puffed with exertion after the intense battle
> - She puffed her cheeks out in annoyance
> - The steam puffed from the machinery

### Sense 3 — *noun* (informal)
A light pastry or confection; also slang for a drag on a cigarette

> [!example] Examples
> - He took a puff before continuing
> - The bakery sold cream puffs

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "puffe" with imitative origins—the word mimics the sound of a brief burst of air. Related to Dutch "poffen" and German "puffen."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to describe the visual/auditory effect of energy attacks or smoke
> - "Puff out" can mean to blow forcefully or to swell up
> - Common onomatopoeia in action descriptions
