## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To cry tears, especially quietly or for a long time, usually because of sadness, grief, or emotion.

> [!example] Examples
> - She wept silently as the coffin was lowered into the grave.
> - The mourners wept openly when his death was announced.
> - He wept with joy at the news.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To produce or leak liquid slowly (e.g., a wound, plant, or material); to discharge liquid gradually.

> [!example] Examples
> - The cut on his arm was weeping pus.
> - Tears wept down her cheeks.
> - The wall wept moisture in the damp room.

---
From Old English *wēpan* 'to weep, bewail,' from Proto-Germanic *wōpijanə* ('to weep, cry out'), from Proto-Indo-European *weh₂b-* ('to cry, complain').

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - More poetic/formal/literary than 'cry'; implies prolonged or quiet emotion.
> - Past tense is 'wept' (irregular verb), though 'weeped' is sometimes used colloquially.
> - Common in literature, poetry, and formal contexts.
> - The second sense (leaking liquid) is more technical/medical.
