## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To motivate, encourage, or urge someone to action; to provide incentive or impetus.

> [!example] Examples
> - The crowd's cheers spurred him on to victory.
> - Nothing could spur her on to try harder.
> - Fear spurred them on to escape quickly.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To drive or propel forward with determination.

> [!example] Examples
> - Desperation spurred them on through the night.
> - His anger spurred him on in the fight.

## 📜 Etymology
From "spur" (device to urge a horse) and "on" (forward). The combination creates a metaphor of urging someone forward with motivation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies encouragement and motivation
> - Often used in sports and competitive contexts
> - Can be literal (spurring a horse) or figurative (motivating)
