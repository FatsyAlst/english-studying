## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* — (Common)

To prepare oneself mentally or physically for something difficult; to gather courage or strength; to brace oneself.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku braced up for the final battle.
> - The fighters braced up as their opponent approached.
> - He braced up his courage to face the powerful enemy.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Brace" from Old French "brace" (arms, length of two arms). "Up" particle indicates readiness or preparation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Similar to "brace yourself"
> - Implies mental or emotional preparation
> - Often used before facing a challenge
> - Can be reflexive or transitive
