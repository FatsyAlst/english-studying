## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common, Informal)

A small, insignificant, or annoying person; often used derogatorily to refer to someone, particularly a small or young person who is considered annoying or contemptible.

> [!example] Examples
> - That little twerp stole my bicycle.
> - Don't let that twerp boss you around.
> - He called me a twerp, but I didn't let it bother me.

## 📜 Etymology
Of uncertain origin; possibly related to "twirp" (variant spelling). First attested in the early 20th century, likely from British slang. The exact origin remains unclear but may relate to words suggesting smallness or insignificance.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/slang term; generally considered mildly offensive.
> - Often implies someone is both small and annoying or contemptible.
> - Frequently used in children's insults or comic dialogue.
> - Can refer to age (a young person) or general insignificance.
