## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Very Frequent)

A remarkable achievement, deed, or accomplishment, especially one requiring great skill, strength, or courage.

> [!example] Examples
> - Defeating the powerful enemy was an impressive feat of strength.
> - The warrior's feat of endurance amazed all observers.
> - Mastering that technique is a remarkable feat.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *fait*, from Latin *factum* meaning "deed" or "action" (from *facere* "to do"). Originally described any deed or action, evolved to emphasize remarkable or impressive accomplishments.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes difficulty and impressiveness of achievement
> - Often used with adjectives: amazing, remarkable, impressive, great
> - Common in heroic narratives, sports, and adventure stories
> - More formal and literary than "achievement" or "accomplishment"
> - Suggests skill, courage, or extraordinary effort
