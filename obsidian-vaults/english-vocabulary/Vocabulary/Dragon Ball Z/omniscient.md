## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Knowing everything; having complete or infinite knowledge; possessing universal awareness.

> [!example] Examples
> - King Kai possessed omniscient knowledge of events across the universe.
> - The ancient being claimed to be omniscient, seeing all possible futures.
> - An omniscient observer would understand the true depths of Goku's potential.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "omnis" (all) and "sciensy" (knowing, from "scire" meaning "to know"). The prefix "omni-" means "all," and "-scient" derives from the Latin root meaning "to know." The word literally means "all-knowing."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and often used in philosophical, theological, or speculative contexts
> - Frequently appears in descriptions of gods, ancient beings, or hypothetical observers
> - Often used absolutely (without needing an object of knowledge)
> - Implies complete, universal knowledge without limitation
