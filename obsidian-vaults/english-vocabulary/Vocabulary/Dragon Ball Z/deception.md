## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
The act of deceiving or misleading someone; a trick or stratagem intended to hide the truth; dishonesty or fraud.

> [!example] Examples
> - His attack was hidden by deception; no one saw it coming.
> - The deception was revealed when they discovered his true power.
> - Such deception has no place in honorable combat.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "deceptio" (a deceiving), from "decipere" (to deceive), from "de-" (away from) + "capere" (to take). Literally meant "to take away" or "to lead astray."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and often carries a negative connotation
> - Can refer to both the act of deceiving and the false appearance itself
> - Common in dramatic contexts involving betrayal or trickery
