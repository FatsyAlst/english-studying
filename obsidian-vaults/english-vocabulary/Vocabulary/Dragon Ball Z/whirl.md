## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To turn or spin around very quickly; to make something do this.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dancers whirled around the room.
> - Leaves whirled in the wind.
> - She whirled around when she heard her name.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A fast turning movement; a period of very busy, exciting activity.

> [!example] Examples
> - a whirl of snowflakes
> - My first week in Leuven was a whirl of lectures, paperwork and new friends.

### Sense 3 — *idiom, "give it a whirl"* (Common — Informal)
To try something new.

> [!example] Examples
> - I've never played Juno, but I'll give her a whirl.
> - Why not give the new café a whirl?

### Sense 4 — *verb (figurative)* (Less Common)
(Of your head or thoughts) to feel confused, with too many ideas at once.

> [!example] Examples
> - My head was whirling after the three-hour lecture.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *hvirfla* "to turn about"; related to [[whirlpool]] and *whirlwind*.
