## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Common)

To crouch down in fear; to shrink away from someone or something in fear or apprehension; to show a lack of courage.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dog cowered in the corner during the thunderstorm.
> - She cowered before the angry mob.
> - Don't cower; face your fears.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *curen*, from Old Norse *kúra* (to crouch, squat). Related to "coward" (though "coward" has a different etymology route). The word emphasizes both physical crouching and emotional fear.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Describes both physical posture (crouching) and emotional state (fear).
> - Often implies shame or weakness in response to threat.
> - Can be used literally (animals, people in danger) or figuratively (moral cowardice).
> - Related but distinct from "cower" and "coward" (though both involve fear).
> - Common in dramatic, tense, or action contexts.
