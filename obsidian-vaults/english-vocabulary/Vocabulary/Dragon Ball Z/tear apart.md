## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To rip, shred, or break something into pieces; to destroy by force.

> [!example] Examples
> - The creature tore apart the fortress walls.
> - He tore apart his opponent's defenses.
> - The explosion tore apart the building.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (figurative, common)
To criticize harshly or severely; to destroy a reputation or argument.

> [!example] Examples
> - The critic tore apart the movie's plot.
> - They tore apart his theory with evidence.
> - The press tore apart the government's proposal.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb* (figurative, common)
To damage relationships or cause emotional harm.

> [!example] Examples
> - The scandal tore apart the family.
> - Internal conflict tore the team apart.

## 📜 Etymology
From "tear" (to rip) and "apart" (into separate pieces). Simple combination with clear literal and metaphorical applications.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (physical destruction) or figurative (criticism/damage)
> - Implies complete or thorough destruction
> - Often used for dramatic effect in action and conflict contexts
