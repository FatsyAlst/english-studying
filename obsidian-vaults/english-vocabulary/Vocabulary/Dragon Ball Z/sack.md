## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A large bag made of strong material, used for storing or carrying things

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To dismiss or fire someone from employment

### Sense 3 — *noun* (sports slang)
In football, to tackle the quarterback behind the line of scrimmage

### Sense 4 — *noun* (archaic)
A sack of something (a unit of measurement for certain goods)

> [!example] Examples
> - He carried his belongings in a large sack.
> - The boss sacked him for poor performance.
> - The linebacker had a sack on the third play.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *sacc*, from Latin *saccus*, from Hebrew *sak* or Aramaic *sakka*. The word spread across many Indo-European languages through trade and commerce.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Large, sturdy bag (often burlap or canvas)
> - Sense 2: British slang particularly, meaning to fire/dismiss someone
> - Sense 3: American football terminology
> - All senses are commonly used in different contexts
