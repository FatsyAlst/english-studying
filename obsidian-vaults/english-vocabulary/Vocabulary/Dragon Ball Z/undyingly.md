## 📖 Definition
*Adverb* (Low Frequency)

In a manner that does not die; eternally; perpetually; without end or mortality.

> [!example] Examples
> - His spirit lives on undyingly in the hearts of his friends.
> - The warrior fought undyingly against overwhelming odds.
> - Their friendship burned undyingly throughout the ages.

## 📜 Etymology
From *un-* (not) + *dying* (ceasing to live), + *-ly* (adverb suffix). The adjective "undying" dates back centuries and means eternal or never-ending. The adverb form is less common but follows standard English derivation patterns.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Relatively rare in modern usage
> - More common in poetic or literary contexts
> - Often used metaphorically rather than literally
> - The adjective "undying" (undying love, undying loyalty) is more frequently used
> - Emphasizes eternal nature or permanent quality of something
> - Formal and somewhat archaic in tone
