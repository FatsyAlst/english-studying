## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (High Frequency)
To prod or jab something with a pointed object or finger; to push something into something else.

> [!example] Examples
> - He poked his opponent with a quick jab during the fight.
> - Don't poke the fire with that stick.
> - She poked him in the shoulder to get his attention.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Medium Frequency)
A quick thrust or jab; the act of poking someone or something.

> [!example] Examples
> - He gave his friend a playful poke in the ribs.
> - A sharp poke caught the fighter off-guard.

### Phrasal Verb: Poke Out
To protrude or stick out; to emerge from a covering.

> [!example] Examples
> - His head poked out from behind the rock.
> - Her elbows poked out from her torn sleeve.
> - The tree branch poked out over the fence.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Low German *poken* or Dutch *poken*. The origin is uncertain but possibly imitative, related to the sound of poking.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used literally (physical action) or figuratively
> - "Poke fun at" means to make fun of someone
> - "Poke around" means to explore or search casually
> - "Poke out" is a phrasal verb variant describing protrusion
> - Often used in combat contexts for quick, direct attacks
