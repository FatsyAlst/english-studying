## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
An attitude of doubting that claims are true or that something will happen.

> [!example] Examples
> - The new timetable was met with skepticism by students.
> - Scientists treat extraordinary claims with healthy skepticism.
> - There's widespread skepticism about the government's promises.

## 📜 Etymology
From Greek *skeptikos* "inquiring, reflective", from *skeptesthai* "to look, consider"; the Sceptics were Greek philosophers who doubted that certain knowledge was possible.
