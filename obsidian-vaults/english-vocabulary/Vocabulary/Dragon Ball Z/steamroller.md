## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A heavy, powerful machine with a large roller used to crush and flatten materials, especially asphalt or soil in construction.

> [!example] Examples
> - The construction crew used a steamroller to flatten the road
> - The steamroller crushed everything in its path
> - A steamroller was used to prepare the surface

---
### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To crush, flatten, or overcome with overwhelming force; to eliminate opposition through sheer power; to force through despite resistance.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku steamrollers his opponents
> - The team was steamrolled by the stronger squad
> - Don't let them steamroller you into agreement
> - He steamrolled through all the competitors

---
### Sense 3 — *Noun (figurative)* (Common)
A powerful, unstoppable force or person; something that cannot be stopped.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's a steamroller in the tournament
> - The organization became a steamroller of efficiency
> - Their team is a steamroller this season

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "steam" + "roller". Steam-powered rolling machines for road construction became common in the 19th century. The term evolved to describe the machine itself, then became used figuratively for any overpowering force.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal sense: heavy construction machinery
> - Figurative sense: overwhelming force or power
> - Can be noun (something that steamrolls) or verb (to steamroll)
> - In combat/competition contexts, implies crushing victory
> - Often has negative connotations when used of people (not respecting opposition)
