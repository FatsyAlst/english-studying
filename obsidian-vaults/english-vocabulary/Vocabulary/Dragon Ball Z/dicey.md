## 📖 Definition
Dangerous, uncertain, or unpredictable; risky or unreliable.

---
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Adjective | dicey, dicier, diciest |
| Related | dice |

---
- dicey situation
- dicey proposition
- dicey prospect
- pretty dicey
- quite dicey
- looks dicey

---
1940s, British aviators' slang, from dice ('roll of the dice' = chance/risk) + -y ('full of'). Originally RAF slang for 'risky' (like gambling).

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational; common in BrE (less in AmE).
> - Implies uncertainty + risk, not just 'difficult'.
> - Similar: tricky, dodgy, iffy.
