## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Frequent)

A close friend or companion, often with an implied negative connotation suggesting favoritism or unethical partnership.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dictator surrounded himself with loyal cronies who supported his every decision.
> - The corrupt official gave government contracts to his cronies.
> - He trusted his old cronies from the military academy completely.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Greek *chronios* meaning "longtime" or "of long standing," from *chronos* "time." Originally referred to a long-time companion, but evolved to imply questionable or corrupt relationships.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Generally carries negative connotation, implying favoritism
> - Often used in political or business contexts
> - Suggests relationships based on personal loyalty rather than merit
> - Synonyms: accomplice, associate, buddy, pal (though "crony" is more critical)
