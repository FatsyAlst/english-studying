## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To mix or agitate a liquid or substance by moving something through it.

> [!example] Examples
> - Stir the potion carefully.
> - She stirred the soup with a wooden spoon.
> - Stir the mixture until it's smooth.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (figurative, common)
To cause commotion, excitement, or controversy; to provoke a reaction.

> [!example] Examples
> - His arrival stirred excitement in the crowd.
> - The announcement stirred up controversy.
> - The warrior's power stirred fear in their hearts.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (common)
To move slightly; to show signs of motion or life.

> [!example] Examples
> - He stirred in his sleep.
> - The leaves stirred in the wind.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "styrian" meaning to move or agitate. Has maintained both literal (mixing) and figurative (causing commotion) meanings through its history.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (mixing substances) or figurative (causing commotion)
> - "Stir up" is common phrasal verb form
> - Figurative sense often implies causing trouble or excitement
