## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A room or space located at the very top of a house or building, directly beneath the roof. Most commonly used for storage, though it can be converted into a living space.

> [!example] Examples
> - We stored all the old furniture in the attic after moving into the new house.
> - She climbed up to the attic to find her grandmother's old letters.
> - The children loved playing in the attic on rainy days.

## 📜 Etymology
From late 17th century French *attique*, from Latin *Atticus* ("relating to Athens or Attica"). Originally an architectural term referring to a small decorative structure above a building's main façade — inspired by ancient Athenian architecture. Over time, the word shifted to describe the space just below the roof of any building.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - **"Attic" vs. "loft" vs. "garret":** *Attic* is the most common general term. *Loft* is more British English (in American English, "loft" often means an open industrial-style apartment). *Garret* is literary/old-fashioned, often suggesting a small, cramped room associated with poor artists.
> - Frequently used as a modifier before other nouns: *attic room, attic space, attic window*.
