## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common) — _often disapproving_
Determined not to change your opinion or attitude; refusing to do what other people want.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was too stubborn to admit that he was wrong.
> - She can be as stubborn as a mule.
> - His stubborn refusal to listen caused problems.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Difficult to get rid of, remove, or deal with.

> [!example] Examples
> - That's a stubborn stain — it won't come out.
> - She has a stubborn cough that won't go away.
> - It's a stubborn problem with no easy solution.
