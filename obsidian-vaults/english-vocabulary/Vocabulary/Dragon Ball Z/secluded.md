## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Set apart or isolated from others; removed from public view or society; remote and private; sheltered and secluded from outside interference.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior trained in a secluded mountain temple.
> - A secluded location provided the perfect training ground away from distractions.
> - The secluded sanctuary became his refuge during difficult times.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *secludere*, from *se-* (apart, aside) + *claudere* (to close, shut). The root suggests shutting away or isolating from others. Used in English since the 16th century to describe places or situations set apart from public access.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective describing isolated or remote locations
> - Often positive connotation (peaceful, private)
> - Can describe physical places or situations
> - Related: seclude (verb), seclusion (noun)
> - Common in describing training locations or sanctuaries
> - Emphasizes separation from outside world
> - Implies both privacy and protection
