## 📖 Definition
*Phrase* — (Common)
According to what people say or according to available information; as far as anyone knows; by general consensus.

> [!example] Examples
> - By all accounts, Goku is the strongest fighter in the universe.
> - By all accounts, the tournament was a massive success.
> - By all accounts, their training methods are unorthodox but effective.

---
## 📜 Etymology
A modern English phrase combining "by" (according to) + "all" (everyone/everything) + "accounts" (reports or narratives). "Account" derives from Old French "acounte" meaning "reckoning" or "narrative."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used to introduce hearsay or general opinion
> - Similar to "allegedly" but more confident in tone
> - Often used to present information without taking personal responsibility for its truth
> - Common in news, storytelling, and casual conversation
> - Can be placed at the beginning or middle of a sentence
