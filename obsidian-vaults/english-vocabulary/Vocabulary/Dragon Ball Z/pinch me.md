## 📖 Definition
*Idiom/Interjection* — Common

An expression indicating disbelief or surprise; literally asking for a pinch to confirm one is not dreaming; expressing amazement that something good or incredible is real.

> [!example] Examples
> - Pinch me! This is incredible!
> - I can't believe it—pinch me if I'm dreaming
> - After that power-up, pinch me, this is unreal
> - Is this real? Pinch me!

## 📜 Etymology
From the idea that if you're dreaming, physical sensations like a pinch would wake you up. The expression became common in the late 19th/early 20th century as a way to express disbelief that something wonderful is actually happening. Based on the real physical phenomenon that pain can interrupt dreams.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually used when something amazing or hard-to-believe happens
> - Often indicates joy, surprise, or wonder
> - Implies the speaker is questioning whether the situation is real
> - Informal, conversational usage
> - Can be used literally (asking someone to pinch you) or figuratively (just an expression)
> - Related: "I'm pinching myself" (expressing amazement)
