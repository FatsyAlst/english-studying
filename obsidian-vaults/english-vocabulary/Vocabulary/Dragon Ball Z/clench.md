## 📖 Definition
*verb* — (Common)
To close or squeeze tightly, usually referring to a fist, jaw, or teeth; to grip firmly

> [!example] Examples
> - He clenched his fists in anger.
> - She clenched her teeth to avoid screaming.
> - The boxer clenched his jaw before the match.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "clenchen," from Old English "clencan," meaning to bind or fasten. Related to "clinch."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often indicates tension, anger, or determination
> - Commonly used with fists, teeth, or jaw
> - The clenched position is held and then released
