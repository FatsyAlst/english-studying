## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Common)
Resembling dough in texture, consistency, or appearance; soft, pasty, pale, or flabby; lacking firmness or definition.

> [!example] Examples
> - His doughy face was pale and unremarkable.
> - The doughy consistency of the bread indicated it was undercooked.
> - He had a doughy body lacking any muscular definition.

## 📜 Etymology
From "dough" + "-y" (suffix forming adjectives meaning "having the quality of"). "Dough" comes from Old English *dāh*, Germanic origin, related to "do" in the sense of "make" or "knead."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily descriptive of physical appearance
> - Often carries negative or unflattering connotations (soft, weak, pale)
> - Can describe both literal texture (baked goods) and figurative appearance (skin, body)
> - Synonyms: soft, flabby, pasty, pale, pudgy, weak-looking
> - Antonyms: firm, muscular, toned, defined
> - Common in character descriptions to suggest weakness or unpreparedness
