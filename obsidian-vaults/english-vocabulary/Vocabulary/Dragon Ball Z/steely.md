## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Resembling steel in hardness, coldness, or appearance; cold, hard, or determined in manner or expression; unyielding or firm.

> [!example] Examples
> - His steely gaze pierced through his opponent.
> - She had a steely resolve to win at any cost.
> - The warrior's steely demeanor intimidated many.

## 📜 Etymology
From "steel" (metal) + "-y" (adjective suffix). Steel has long been used metaphorically to describe hardness, coldness, and strength. The adjective "steely" emerged in the 16th century to describe people and expressions with steel-like qualities.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Suggests both physical coldness and emotional hardness
> - Often used to describe determined, intimidating characters
> - Creates a vivid image of unyielding strength
