## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* (High Frequency)

To act without restraint; to behave wildly or freely; to break free from control or restriction; to have fun or celebrate without holding back.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku was finally able to cut loose and fight at full power.
> - The warriors cut loose with devastating energy attacks.
> - After the long training, he cut loose and celebrated with his friends.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *cut* (to sever, release) + *loose* (not restrained, free). The phrase suggests releasing something that was previously held back or constrained.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies releasing pent-up energy or emotion
> - Common in action contexts to describe fighting without restraint
> - Can also mean to behave wildly or celebrate excessively
> - Similar phrase: "let loose"
> - Often followed by "with" when describing the manner of action
