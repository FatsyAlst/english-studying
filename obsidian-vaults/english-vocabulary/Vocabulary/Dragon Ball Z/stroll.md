## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (frequent)
To walk slowly and casually; to take a leisurely walk

> [!example] Examples
> - He strolled through the battlefield without concern
> - She strolled past her opponents confidently
> - They strolled across the arena as if it were easy
> - He strolled forward despite the danger

### Sense 2 — *noun* (frequent)
A leisurely walk taken for pleasure or exercise

> [!example] Examples
> - A pleasant stroll through the park
> - They took a stroll after training
> - A calm stroll to clear his mind

## 📜 Etymology
From German "strollen" and Italian "strollar," with unclear ultimate origins. The word entered English in the 17th century meaning to wander or walk aimlessly, emphasizing the leisurely and casual nature of the movement.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies casual, confident, or unhurried movement
> - Often used to show a character's confidence or dominance (strolling through danger easily)
> - Informal and casual vocabulary
> - Contrasts with quick, urgent, or forceful movement
> - Creates a sense of ease or nonchalance
