## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
Soft material used to cushion, protect, or fill something; a layer of material for comfort or safety

> [!example] Examples
> - The helmet had extra padding to protect against impact.
> - She added padding to the furniture to make it more comfortable.
> - Protective padding was essential for the athlete.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Unnecessary words or content added to increase length without adding meaningful information

> [!example] Examples
> - The essay contained a lot of padding instead of solid arguments.
> - He used padding to meet the word count requirement.
> - The report was full of padding and lacked substance.

## 📜 Etymology
From "pad" (a cushion, soft mass), possibly from Middle Dutch "padde" or Romany "pad" (a straw mattress), plus the suffix "-ing."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 refers to actual physical material
> - Sense 2 refers to figurative filling of space without substance
> - Often used in compound terms (shoulder padding, padding material)
