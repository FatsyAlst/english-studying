## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
A person who talks too much or reveals secrets; someone who cannot keep confidences or tends to boast; a loud or talkative person

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't tell him anything—he's a big mouth and will tell everyone.
> - She's such a big mouth; she can't keep a secret.
> - He's known as a big mouth who talks about his accomplishments constantly.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "big" and "mouth," used idiomatically since the early 20th century. The image of someone with a large mouth literally talking a lot.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually used as an insult or criticism
> - Refers to someone who talks excessively or reveals secrets
> - Can be written as two words or hyphenated
