## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal)
A person who spoils the fun or enjoyment of others; someone who ruins a good time with their negative attitude or behavior.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't be such a party pooper! Come dance with us.
> - He's a party pooper who complains about everything.
> - The teacher seemed like a party pooper for canceling the field trip.

## 📜 Etymology
"Party" refers to a celebration or social gathering. "Pooper" comes from a slang term meaning to defecate or ruin something. The compound developed as a humorous insult in the mid-20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and slightly rude term
> - Used to describe someone with a negative attitude at social events
> - Often used in jest or light-hearted criticism
> - Common in casual, social contexts
