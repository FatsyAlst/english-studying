## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense of expel)* (frequent)
To force someone or something to leave; to remove forcibly

> [!example] Examples
> - He was expelled from the tournament for cheating
> - The blast expelled the enemy across the battlefield
> - Smoke was expelled from the machine

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To discharge or force out a substance from the body or a container

> [!example] Examples
> - The creature expelled poison from its mouth
> - He expelled a long breath
> - The engine expelled harmful gases

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "expellere" where "ex-" means out and "pellere" means to drive or push. The word entered English in the 14th century with the sense of forcing out.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in both literal (physical removal) and figurative (removal from school/organization) contexts
> - In Dragon Ball Z, frequently describes characters being blasted away or removed from an area
> - Formal and serious tone
