## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Common, Literary)

To prevent something from succeeding; to block or hinder someone's plans, efforts, or ambitions; to frustrate or obstruct.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hero thwarted the villain's evil plans.
> - Bad weather thwarted our travel plans.
> - His attempts to escape were thwarted by the guards.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *þvert* (across, athwart), meaning "across" or "in opposition to." The word originally referred to something placed across or in opposition. It evolved to mean preventing someone from achieving their goal.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary in tone; common in adventure and fiction narratives.
> - Implies active opposition or hindrance, not passive prevention.
> - Often used in conflict or adversarial contexts.
> - Related concept: "athwart" (an archaic form, meaning across or in opposition).
> - Frequently appears in fantasy, crime, and action storytelling.
