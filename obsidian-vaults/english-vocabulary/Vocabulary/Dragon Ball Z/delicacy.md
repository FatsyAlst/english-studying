## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Frequent)
A rare, expensive, or specially prepared food; a delicious or sought-after dish.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku treats sushi as a delicacy to savor slowly.
> - Caviar is considered a delicacy in many cultures.
> - The restaurant served rare delicacies from around the world.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Frequent)
Delicate or subtle quality; the state of being physically delicate or fragile.

> [!example] Examples
> - The delicacy of the porcelain required careful handling.
> - Handle the situation with delicacy and sensitivity.
> - The delicacy of her features suggested she was still young.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Less Frequent)
Sensitivity in matters; the need for careful or diplomatic handling.

> [!example] Examples
> - This is a situation requiring delicacy and diplomacy.
> - The matter required great delicacy to resolve peacefully.
> - He handled the delicate topic with surprising delicacy.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *delicatesse*, from Latin *delicatus* meaning "giving oneself up to pleasure" or "luxurious." Evolved to describe both fine foods and the quality of being delicate.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 emphasizes rarity and desirability in food
> - Sense 2 refers to physical fragility or fragile state
> - Sense 3 emphasizes diplomatic or sensitive handling
> - The word conveys appreciation, care, and refinement across all senses
