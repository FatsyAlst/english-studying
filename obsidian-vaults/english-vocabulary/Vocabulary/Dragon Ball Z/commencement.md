## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
The beginning or start of something; a formal ceremony marking the completion of a course of study and conferring degrees; the inaugural moment.

> [!example] Examples
> - The commencement of the tournament was marked by great fanfare.
> - At the commencement of the match, both fighters faced off.
> - The warrior's journey began at commencement of his training.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *commencement*, from *commencer* (to begin), from Vulgar Latin *cominitiare*, from Latin *com-* (with) + *initiare* (to begin). Used in English since the 13th century, particularly in formal academic contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal word for "beginning" or "start"
> - Commonly used in academic contexts (graduation ceremonies)
> - Also used for formal start of events, battles, or significant moments
> - More formal than "start" or "beginning"
> - Related: commence (verb), commencing (adjective)
> - Often appears in formal or literary writing
