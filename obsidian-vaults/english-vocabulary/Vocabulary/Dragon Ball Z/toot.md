## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To make a short, sharp sound with a horn or whistle; to blow or sound a horn.

> [!example] Examples
> - The train tooted its whistle.
> - He tooted the car horn.
> - The referee tooted the whistle to start the game.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A short, sharp sound made by a horn, whistle, or similar instrument.

> [!example] Examples
> - The toot of the horn announced arrival.
> - A quick toot of the whistle
> - The train's toot echoed through the valley.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (slang)
To snort drugs; to use cocaine or similar drugs (offensive/slang).

> [!example] Examples
> - (Not commonly recommended for standard usage)

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative in origin, imitating the sound itself. Developed in English as an onomatopoeia in the late 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily onomatopoetic - mimics the sound it represents
> - Often associated with trains, horns, or whistles
> - "Toot one's own horn" is an idiom meaning to boast
> - The slang sense is outdated and offensive
