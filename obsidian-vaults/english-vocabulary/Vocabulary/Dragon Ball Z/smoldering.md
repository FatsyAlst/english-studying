## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (gerund/participle)* (common)
Burning slowly without flame; glowing with heat and smoke

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Showing intense emotion held in check; burning with suppressed feeling

> [!example] Examples
> - Smoldering embers remained after the explosion.
> - His smoldering rage threatened to burst forth.
> - The battlefield was covered with smoldering rubble.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *smolderen*, possibly from Middle Dutch *smolderen* or from Old Norse *smali* (small) + *der* (smoke). The image is of smoke without visible flame.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe literal fire/heat or metaphorical emotions
> - Suggests intensity held back rather than explosive
> - Often used to convey tension or hidden passion
