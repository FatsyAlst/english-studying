## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal/derogatory)
An insult applied to a male person perceived as weak, cowardly, or overly feminine; a derogatory term

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain called him a sissy to provoke him
> - Don't be such a sissy—stand and fight!
> - He refused to be called a sissy

### Sense 2 — *noun* (informal)
A sister (used affectionately or informally, less common)

> [!example] Examples
> - His sissy was waiting outside
> - My sissy helped me train

## 📜 Etymology
Derived from "sister" with a diminutive suffix, originally used affectionately. Over time, it developed into a derogatory term in certain contexts, particularly when used to shame men for not conforming to masculine stereotypes.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Derogatory term that can be offensive and hurtful
> - Often appears in confrontational dialogue in action shows
> - Reflects outdated gender stereotypes
> - Context matters greatly for understanding its usage
