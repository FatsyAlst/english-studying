## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
The act of holding something firmly with the hand(s); a firm hold.

> [!example] Examples
> - She kept a tight grip on the rope.
> - He lost his grip and fell.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Control or power over somebody/something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The home team took a firm grip on the game.
> - Terrorists have the country in their grip.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
A part of an object designed to be held by the hand.

> [!example] Examples
> - The pistol's grip.

### Sense 4 — *verb* (Common)
To hold something tightly; grasp firmly.

> [!example] Examples
> - He gripped her arm.
> - Grip on to the railing.

### Sense 5 — *verb* (Common)
To interest or excite somebody strongly; hold attention.

> [!example] Examples
> - The story gripped me from start to finish.
> - A gripping film.
