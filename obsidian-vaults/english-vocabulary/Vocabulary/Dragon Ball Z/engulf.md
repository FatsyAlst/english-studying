## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To surround or swallow up completely; to engross entirely

> [!example] Examples
> - The blast engulfed the entire city in flames.
> - Darkness engulfed the battlefield as the smoke cleared.
> - Fear engulfed his mind as the enemy approached.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *engolfen*, from Old French *engolfer*, from *en-* (in) + *golfe* (gulf). Originally meant to swallow up as if by a gulf or whirlpool.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with "in" to indicate what surrounds something
> - Suggests complete, often sudden covering or surrounding
> - Common in dramatic or intense contexts
