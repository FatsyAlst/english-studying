## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To interrupt or stop something before it is finished; to terminate prematurely.

> [!example] Examples
> - The powerful blast cut short his attack before he could finish.
> - His victory speech was cut short by an unexpected challenger.
> - We had to cut short our training session due to the emergency.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To reduce the length or duration of something; to shorten.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament was cut short by one week.
> - Cut short your vacation if you need to return early.
> - The battle was cut short when one fighter surrendered.

## 📜 Etymology
Phrasal verb combining "cut" (from Old Norse "kuti") and "short" (from Old English "scort"). The phrase emphasizes abrupt termination or reduction.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually non-separable: "cut short the attack" rather than "cut the attack short"
> - Can mean both to interrupt and to reduce duration
> - Common in both literal and figurative contexts
