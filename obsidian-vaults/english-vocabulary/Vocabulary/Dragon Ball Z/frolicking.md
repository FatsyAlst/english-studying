## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To play or move around in a lively, carefree manner; to frolic or gambol.

> [!example] Examples
> - The children were frolicking in the water.
> - The animals frolicked across the meadow.
> - They frolicked together without a care.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
Playful activity or fun; a game or lighthearted adventure.

> [!example] Examples
> - Their frolicking was interrupted by the alarm.
> - A day of frolicking at the beach.

## 📜 Etymology
From Dutch "vrolijk" meaning merry or cheerful. The term carries connotations of carefree joy and playfulness.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Conveys a sense of innocence and playfulness
> - Often used to describe playful rather than serious activity
> - Somewhat literary or whimsical in tone
