## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To gradually fall asleep; to enter sleep slowly and gently.

> [!example] Examples
> - After the exhausting battle, Goku drifted off to sleep immediately.
> - The young warrior drifted off during the meditation session.
> - She drifted off while listening to the peaceful sounds of the forest.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To move slowly or gradually away; to depart gradually without intention or effort.

> [!example] Examples
> - The injured fighter drifted off into the distance.
> - His focus drifted off as the meeting continued.
> - The boat drifted off into the ocean during the storm.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "drift," which comes from Old Norse "drifa" (to drive or push). The word originally described the movement of ships being pushed by wind or water. The phrasal verb "drift off" combines "drift" with "off," indicating gradual departure or transition.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Drift off to sleep" is the most common usage (Sense 1)
> - Implies a gradual, gentle, or unintentional process
> - More poetic or literary than simply "fall asleep"
> - The phrasal verb requires "off" to complete its meaning
> - Common in both spoken and written English
