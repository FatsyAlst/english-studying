## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
Something that is owed or deserved; a fee or payment required for membership or service; rightful acknowledgment or respect.

> [!example] Examples
> - He paid his dues through years of hard training.
> - Every warrior must pay their dues to earn respect.
> - She earned her position by paying her dues.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "deu" (owed, expected), from Latin "debitus" (owed), past participle of "debere" (to owe). The plural "dues" developed to represent payments or obligations that accumulate.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in phrase "pay your dues" (earn respect through hard work)
> - Conveys the idea of deserved reward for effort
> - Can be literal (fees) or figurative (respect earned)
