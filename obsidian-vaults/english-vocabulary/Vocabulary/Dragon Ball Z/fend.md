## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb, "fend for yourself"* (Common)
To take care of yourself without help from other people.

> [!example] Examples
> - When I moved to Belgium, I had to learn to fend for myself.
> - After a few months the cubs must fend for themselves.
> - Students living away from home have to fend for themselves.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb, "fend off"* (Common)
To defend yourself against an attack, criticism or difficult questions; to keep someone or something away.

> [!example] Examples
> - She fended off the attacker with her umbrella.
> - The minister fended off questions from journalists.
> - The team fended off a late comeback and won.

## 🔗 Collocations
- fend for oneself

- fend someone off

---
## 📜 Etymology
A shortening of *defend* in Middle English (13th century); *fender* comes from the same word.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost always in one of these two patterns: *fend for yourself* / *fend off*.
> - *Fend off* is separable: *fend off the attack* / *fend it off*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[ward off]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
