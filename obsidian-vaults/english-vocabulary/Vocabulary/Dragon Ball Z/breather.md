## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A short rest or pause from work or activity; a temporary break.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku needed a breather after the intense battle with Frieza.
> - Let's take a breather before the next training session.
> - He finally got a breather from his responsibilities.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "breathe" (to inhale and exhale), with the agentive suffix "-er." Originally used to describe one who breathes, it evolved to mean a brief pause or relief period.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and conversational tone; common in spoken English
> - Often used with "take," "need," or "get"
> - Implies a temporary pause, not a permanent cessation
