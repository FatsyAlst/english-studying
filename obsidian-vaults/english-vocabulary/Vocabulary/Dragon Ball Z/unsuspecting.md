## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Frequent)

Not aware of danger, deception, or what is happening; caught off guard without expectation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The unsuspecting warrior was ambushed by his hidden enemy.
> - The villain launched a sneak attack on the unsuspecting hero.
> - Unsuspecting travelers fell into the trap without realizing it.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *un-* (not) + *suspecting* (having suspicion). The verb "suspect" comes from Latin *suspicere* (to look up at, to regard with suspicion).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies vulnerability or being at a disadvantage
> - Commonly used in contexts of surprise attacks or deception
> - Emphasizes lack of awareness rather than trusting naiveté
> - Similar to "unaware" but more specific about not seeing danger
> - Frequently used in action and suspense narratives
