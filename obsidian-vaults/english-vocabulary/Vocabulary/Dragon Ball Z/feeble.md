## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Most Common)

Lacking physical strength; weak or infirm, especially due to age or illness.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old man's voice was feeble and tired.
> - After the illness, she was feeble for weeks.
> - He made a feeble attempt to lift the heavy box.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *feble*, from Latin *flebilis* ("lamentable, mournful"), related to *fletus* (weeping, tears). The word originally meant "worthy of lamentation" before evolving to mean "weak."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe physical weakness (strength) or weakness of effort/argument.
> - "Feeble-minded" is an archaic/offensive term; use "intellectually disabled" instead.
> - Often conveys a sense of pity or concern for someone's condition.
> - Adverb form: "feebly" (e.g., "he feebly protested").
