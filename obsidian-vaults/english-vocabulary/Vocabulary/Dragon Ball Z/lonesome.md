## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Feeling sad and lonely because one is alone; without companionship; isolated.

> [!example] Examples
> - He felt lonesome on the empty highway.
> - A lonesome figure stood by the window.
> - She's been feeling lonesome since moving to the city.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Remote and isolated, with few people nearby; deserted or desolate.

> [!example] Examples
> - The lonesome cabin in the mountains
> - A lonesome trail through the forest
> - A lonesome road in the middle of nowhere

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English lones (alone) + -some (suffix creating adjectives). Related to "lone" and "alone."

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More poetic or literary than simply "lonely"
> - Often used in country music and folk traditions
> - Can describe both emotional states and physical places
> - Slightly archaic but still used
