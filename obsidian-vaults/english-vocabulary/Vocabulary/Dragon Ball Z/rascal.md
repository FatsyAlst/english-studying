## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A mischievous, playful, or dishonest person; a scoundrel or rogue; someone who behaves badly but often without serious malice.

> [!example] Examples
> - That young rascal always gets into trouble.
> - The rascal stole the dragon balls.
> - Despite his rascal ways, he had a good heart.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "rascaille," meaning rabble or dregs, possibly related to "raser" (to scrape). Originally meant a person of low social status.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used affectionately or playfully for mischievous people
> - Can describe dishonest behavior without being as serious as "criminal"
> - Common in fiction and informal speech
> - Implies roguish charm rather than genuine malice
> - Can be used for both children and adults
