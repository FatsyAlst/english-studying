## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
An urgent, emotional request.

> [!example] Examples
> - The mother made a tearful plea for help.
> - His plea for more time was rejected.
> - They ignored our pleas to turn the music down.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Legal)
A statement in court saying whether you are guilty or not guilty.

> [!example] Examples
> - He entered a plea of not guilty.
> - The defendant accepted a plea bargain.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *plait / plaid* "agreement, lawsuit", from Latin *placitum* "what pleases; a decision" (*placēre* "to please").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Pattern: *a plea **for** + noun* (*a plea for help*).
> - Legal verb: *plead guilty / not guilty*.
> - Literary verb with a similar idea: [[beseech]].
