## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Worn out, torn, or in poor condition from much use; having a neglected, rundown appearance.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old coat looked shabby and worn.
> - They lived in a shabby apartment.
> - The furniture was shabby and falling apart.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (formal)
Showing lack of respect or fair treatment; mean or dishonorable in behavior.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a shabby way to treat your friend.
> - Shabby treatment of the workers
> - A shabby excuse for failure

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English shabbe (related to scab), from Old English sceabb. The sense of "worn out" comes from the appearance of skin affected by scabies.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe physical appearance (worn clothing, deteriorated buildings)
> - Can also describe behavior (unfair, disrespectful treatment)
> - Suggests neglect and poor condition
> - More formal than "shabby" slang
