## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To touch lightly causing laughter or twitching movements; to cause someone to laugh by light touching.

> [!example] Examples
> - The soft fur tickles my skin.
> - Don't tickle me, I can't stop laughing!
> - Her hair tickles his face when they stand close.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To cause amusement, interest, or pleasure; to amuse or entertain.

> [!example] Examples
> - That joke really tickles me.
> - This power tickles my curiosity.
> - The idea tickles their imagination.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Less Common)
To cause a light, itching sensation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The feather tickles my nose.
> - His stubble tickles when he's close.
> - The grass tickles between my toes.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "tikelen," possibly from Old English "cicillan." Origin uncertain but may relate to Proto-Germanic *titilōn meaning "to tickle."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very common with children and casual contexts
> - Figurative uses emphasize amusement or curiosity
> - "Ticklish" is the adjective form
> - Can indicate both physical and emotional reactions
> - Often used humorously in anime dialogue
