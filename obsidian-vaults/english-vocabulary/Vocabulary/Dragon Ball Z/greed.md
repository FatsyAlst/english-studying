## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Very Frequent)

An intense and selfish desire for wealth, power, or possessions; excessive craving for more than one needs.

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain's greed for power led to his downfall.
> - Greed and ambition consumed the corrupt ruler.
> - His greed for the dragon balls blinded him to reality.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *graedig*, related to Old Norse *graðr* meaning "hunger" or "appetite." Originally referred to appetite for food, evolved to describe excessive desire for any possession or power.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Carries strong negative moral connotation
> - One of the seven deadly sins in Christian tradition
> - Describes both motivation and character flaw
> - Often contrasted with generosity, contentment, or virtue
> - Used in philosophical, moral, and character discussions
