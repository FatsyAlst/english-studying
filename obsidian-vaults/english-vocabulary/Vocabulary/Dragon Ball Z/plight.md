## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A dangerous, difficult, or unfortunate situation; a predicament or dire circumstances

> [!example] Examples
> - The warriors faced a dire plight
> - The planet's plight grew worse with each passing day
> - Despite their strength, they couldn't escape the plight
> - The people's plight moved them to action
> - Their desperate plight required immediate intervention

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "pliht" meaning danger or risk, derived from Proto-Germanic. Originally meant a pledge or condition, then evolved to mean a dangerous or unfortunate state or condition.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary vocabulary
> - Often used to describe serious, urgent situations
> - Conveys both danger and difficulty
> - Frequently appears in narratives about conflict or crisis
