## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common — AmE)
A baseball stadium.

> [!example] Examples
> - We went to the ballpark to watch the game.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Most Common — Informal)
(Of a number) approximate; a rough estimate — *a ballpark figure / estimate*.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can you give me a ballpark figure for the project cost?
> - Ballpark estimate: we'll need about 200 hours of testing.
> - I don't need the exact number, just a ballpark.

### Sense 3 — *idiom, "in the (right) ballpark"* (Common)
Approximately correct; within a reasonable range.

> [!example] Examples
> - Your calculation isn't exact, but it's in the right ballpark.
> - Their offer isn't even in the same ballpark as ours.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Ball* + *park* (a baseball field), American English from the late 19th century; the figurative "approximate range" sense appeared in the mid-20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Extremely useful in engineering and business: order-of-magnitude (*Fermi*) estimates.
> - Synonyms: *rough estimate*, *approximation*, *back-of-the-envelope calculation*.
> - *A whole different ballgame* = a completely different situation.
