## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A sudden, involuntary, and painful contraction of a muscle, usually in the leg or abdomen.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku suffered from a leg cramp during the middle of his match.
> - The intense training caused muscle cramps throughout his body.
> - Vegeta pushed through the cramping pain to continue fighting.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To restrict or limit the movement, freedom, or development of someone or something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The weighted training gear cramped his movements initially.
> - Lack of space cramped the fighters' ability to maneuver.
> - His injuries cramped his fighting style considerably.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (formal)
A clamp or constraint that restricts movement or development; a limitation.

> [!example] Examples
> - Breaking free from the cramp of his past limitations was essential.
> - The rules of combat acted as a cramp on his true power.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch "crampe" or Old Norse "kramp," related to Germanic words meaning "to bend or compress." The word originally referred to a clamp or tool, and evolved to describe the muscle contraction or restriction of movement.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (muscle contraction) is most common in everyday usage
> - Sense 2 and 3 (restriction/limitation) are more formal or literary
> - Often appears in medical or sports contexts
> - Can be used metaphorically to describe limiting conditions
