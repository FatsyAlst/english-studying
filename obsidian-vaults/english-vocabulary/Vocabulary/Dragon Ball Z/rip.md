## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To tear or pull something apart forcefully; to make a hole or separation in material.

> [!example] Examples
> - He ripped the paper in half.
> - The fabric ripped when she pulled too hard.
> - Rip the envelope open.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (slang)
To perform excellently or intensely; to play music or do something with great skill and energy.

> [!example] Examples
> - The guitarist ripped an amazing solo.
> - He ripped the backflip!
> - She ripped through the exam.

### Sense 3 — *expression* (internet slang)
Rest in Peace; a tribute to someone who has died (R.I.P.).

> [!example] Examples
> - Rip that champion, he was the best.
> - Rip the good old days.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English ripan (to scratch or scrape). The slang sense "to perform excellently" developed in 20th century music and sports contexts. The "RIP" usage comes from the acronym for "Rest in Peace."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal sense: tearing or separation
> - Slang sense: performing with great skill and intensity
> - The "RIP" usage is very common in internet and casual contexts
> - "Rip off" has a separate meaning (to cheat someone)
