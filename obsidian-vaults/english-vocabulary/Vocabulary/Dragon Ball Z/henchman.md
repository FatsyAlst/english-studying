## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Common)
A man employed by someone (typically a powerful figure or villain) to perform tasks, often dishonest or violent; an associate or subordinate of a criminal or powerful person; a muscular or strong person hired for intimidation or physical work.

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain ordered his henchmen to capture the hero.
> - The crime boss surrounded himself with henchmen.
> - The muscular henchman blocked the entrance.

## 📜 Etymology
From "hench" (a man of the household or household warrior) + "man." "Hench" likely derives from Old English *hengest* (horse) or Old Norse *hengja* (to hang, support). Historically referred to a skilled retainer or warrior in a noble's household.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often pejorative; implies someone does others' bidding
> - Common in crime fiction, action movies, and anime
> - Stereotypically muscular and loyal to a powerful boss
> - Modern slang: "hench" = muscular or physically imposing (British)
> - Synonyms: minion, subordinate, lackey, flunky, goon, thug, associate
> - Often plural in usage (henchmen)
> - Frequently used in villain-character descriptions
