## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A flat object with a handle and a row of thin, closely-spaced teeth, used for straightening and arranging hair.

> [!example] Examples
> - She used a comb to fix her hair
> - The character's distinctive spiky hair doesn't need a comb
> - He grabbed a comb from the bathroom

---
### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To arrange or straighten hair with a comb; to search through something thoroughly; to go through systematically.

> [!example] Examples
> - Comb your hair before going out
> - The police combed the area for clues
> - They combed through the documents carefully
> - The detectives combed the village

---
### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Less Common)
A honeycomb; or anything resembling a comb's structure with teeth or a grid pattern.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bees built a comb in the hive
> - A honeycomb structure
> - The comb pattern was visible on the surface

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "camb" and Germanic origins (Old Saxon "kamb", Old Norse "kambr"). Related to "champ" and other words with hard consonants. The tool has been in use since ancient times, with the word remaining relatively stable across Germanic languages.
