## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (past tense) (frequent)
To become tangled or disordered; to fall apart or disintegrate; to untangle or solve something complex.

> [!example] Examples
> - The plan unraveled when they discovered the betrayal.
> - His composure unraveled under pressure.
> - The conspiracy unraveled before their eyes.

## 📜 Etymology
From "un-" (reverse action) + "ravel" (to tangle or untangle). "Ravel" comes from Dutch "ravelen" and has paradoxically meant both "tangle" and "untangle" throughout its history.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can mean both "to untangle" (solve) and "to fall apart"
> - Context determines which sense is intended
> - Often used metaphorically for plans, conspiracies, or emotional states
