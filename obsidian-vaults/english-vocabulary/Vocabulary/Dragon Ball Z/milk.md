## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A white liquid produced by female mammals for feeding their young; commonly cow's milk used as a beverage

> [!example] Examples
> - She poured a glass of milk for breakfast.
> - The recipe calls for two cups of milk.
> - Fresh milk is delivered daily.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To extract or obtain something; to exploit a person or situation for personal benefit

> [!example] Examples
> - He was milking the situation for all it was worth.
> - They milked the system to get free benefits.
> - The company milked their customers for every penny.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "meolc" or "milc," from Proto-Germanic "*meluks," possibly related to Latin "mulgere" (to milk).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is literal (the beverage)
> - Sense 2 is figurative and often has negative connotations
> - The verb form "milk" implies extracting or exploiting
