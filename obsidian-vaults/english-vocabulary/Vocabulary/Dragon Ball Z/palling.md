## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (gerund/participle)* (informal)
Associating or spending time with someone as a friend; being friends with

> [!example] Examples
> - He's been palling around with the warrior.
> - They were palling together throughout the tournament.
> - Stop palling around and focus on training.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Romani *pal* meaning brother or friend, adopted into English slang in the early 19th century. Used informally to refer to comrades or companions.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and casual in tone
> - Often used with "around" (palling around = hanging out)
> - Suggests friendly, companionable association
> - More commonly seen in spoken English or casual writing
