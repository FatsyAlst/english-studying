## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (frequent)
To obstruct, hinder, or prevent someone from making progress; to slow down or block movement or action.

> [!example] Examples
> - Heavy armor impedes quick movement in combat.
> - Nothing will impede our mission to save Earth.
> - The barrier impedes their path forward.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "impedire" (to shackle, entangle), from "im-" (in) + "pes" (foot). Originally literal (entangling feet), now metaphorically used for any obstruction to progress.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "block" or "stop"
> - Often used in contexts where movement or progress is being slowed
> - Common in formal writing, technical language, and dramatic speech
