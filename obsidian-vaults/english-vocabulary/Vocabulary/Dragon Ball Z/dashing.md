## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Attractive, stylish, and confident in manner; having a fashionable or impressive appearance.

> [!example] Examples
> - The young warrior cut a dashing figure as he entered the tournament arena.
> - His dashing demeanor impressed the spectators.
> - The hero looked dashing in his traditional gi.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (literary)
Characterized by vigor, energy, and boldness; swift and impressive in action.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku made a dashing assault on his opponent.
> - The dashing moves demonstrated his superior speed.
> - His dashing counterattack caught Vegeta off guard.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "dash," which derives from Old Norse "daska" (to strike or hit). The "-ing" suffix creates an adjective. The word originally referred to quick, forceful movement and evolved to describe someone who moves or acts with style and vigor.

> - Often used to describe romantic, heroic, or impressive figures

> - Somewhat literary or formal in usage
