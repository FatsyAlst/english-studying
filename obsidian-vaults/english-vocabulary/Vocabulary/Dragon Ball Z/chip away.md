## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To gradually wear away, reduce, or diminish something through repeated small actions.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior continued to chip away at the enemy's defense with each strike.
> - Years of training chip away at your weaknesses.
> - We must chip away at his power until he can no longer fight.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To make progress gradually; to slowly work toward a goal despite difficulty.

> [!example] Examples
> - Chip away at your goals one small step at a time.
> - Even weak fighters can chip away at victory if they persist.
> - She's chipping away at her training regimen every single day.
> - I've been chipping away at my lab report all week.

## 📜 Etymology
Phrasal verb combining "chip" (to break into small pieces, possibly from Old Norse "kip") and "away" (from Old English "aweg"). The phrase emphasizes the gradual, incremental nature of reduction.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually separable: "chip away at the defense" or "chip the defense away"
> - Implies sustained effort over time
> - Works both literally (physical removal) and figuratively (progress, reduction)
> - 📺 Also encountered in [[Life]]
