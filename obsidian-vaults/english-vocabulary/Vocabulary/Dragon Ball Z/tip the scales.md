## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (frequent)
To decide the outcome of something that is in balance; to change the balance in favor of one side; to be the deciding factor.

> [!example] Examples
> - His newfound power tipped the scales in their favor.
> - A single strong warrior could tip the scales of the battle.
> - Her arrival tipped the scales toward victory.

## 📜 Etymology
From the image of scales (balance) that tip over when one side becomes heavier. Dating back to at least the 17th century, metaphorically applied to situations where equilibrium is disrupted in favor of one side.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Vivid metaphorical language from the image of balancing scales
> - Indicates a turning point or decisive moment
> - Can be used in competitive, military, or personal contexts
