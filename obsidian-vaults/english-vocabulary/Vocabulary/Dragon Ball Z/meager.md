## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Lacking in quantity or quality; inadequate or insufficient; scant or scanty; thin and weak in appearance.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior possessed only meager resources for recovery.
> - Meager rations were hardly enough to sustain the fighters.
> - His meager defense crumbled against the powerful assault.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *megre*, from Latin *macer* (thin, lean). The root suggests something lacking in substance or nutrition. Used in English since the 15th century to describe insufficiency or scarcity.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective describing insufficient or inadequate amounts
> - Can refer to physical quantity or quality
> - More formal than "small" or "little"
> - Related: meagerness (noun), meagerly (adverb)
> - Often carries negative connotation of deprivation
> - Common in contexts describing poverty or scarcity
> - British spelling: meagre
