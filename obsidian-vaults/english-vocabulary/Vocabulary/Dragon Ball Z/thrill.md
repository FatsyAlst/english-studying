## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Very Frequent)
A sudden feeling of excitement, joy, or intense pleasure; a thrilling experience.

> [!example] Examples
> - The combat gave Goku a thrill he hadn't experienced in years.
> - The audience felt a thrill of excitement during the final match.
> - There's nothing like the thrill of victory.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Frequent)
To cause someone to feel excitement or intense pleasure.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dangerous mission thrilled the brave warriors.
> - Her victory thrilled the spectators.
> - A powerful opponent thrills a true martial artist.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *thrillen*, possibly from Old Norse *þrilla* meaning "to bore" or "to pierce." Originally described the physical sensation of something piercing or penetrating, evolved to describe emotional excitement.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 emphasizes the emotional experience
> - Sense 2 is transitive, requiring an object
> - Positive connotation, suggesting excitement and joy
> - Common in entertainment, sports, and adventure narratives
> - Related to adrenaline and intense emotions
