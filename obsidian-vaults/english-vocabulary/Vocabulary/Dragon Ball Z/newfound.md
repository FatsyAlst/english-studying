## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Recently discovered or obtained; newly acquired or developed; newly established.

> [!example] Examples
> - He discovered a newfound strength within himself.
> - With her newfound confidence, she challenged the strongest fighters.
> - Their newfound friendship was unbreakable.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "new" (from Old English "niwe") + "found" (past participle of "find," from Old English "findan"). The compound emerged in Middle English to describe things recently discovered or obtained.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always precedes a noun (attributive adjective)
> - Conveys excitement about recent discovery or acquisition
> - Common in character development and transformation narratives
