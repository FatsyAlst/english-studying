## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To make a harsh, high-pitched sound; to cry out shrilly or loudly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tires screeched as the car came to a sudden stop.
> - She screeched in terror at the sight of the monster.
> - The owl screeched in the night.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A harsh, high-pitched sound or cry; a shriek.

> [!example] Examples
> - A screech of metal echoed through the corridor.
> - The screech of the brakes startled everyone.
> - A screech escaped her lips.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *scrichen*, possibly imitative in origin. Related to Old Norse *skriekja*. The word mimics the harsh sound it describes.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Imitative/onomatopoetic word—the sound resembles the meaning
> - Suggests an unpleasant, high-pitched, often involuntary sound
> - Synonyms: shriek, screech, shriek, yell, cry out
> - More dramatic or intense than "shout" or "cry"
> - Common in describing emergency sounds (brakes, tires) or frightened reactions
