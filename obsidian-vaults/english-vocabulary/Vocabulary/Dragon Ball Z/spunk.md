## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal)
Courage, determination, or courage; liveliness or spirit

> [!example] Examples
> - She had the spunk to stand up to the villain.
> - Despite his size, the character had spunk.
> - His spunk and determination inspired others.

## 📜 Etymology
From Scottish and Northern English *spunk* meaning a spark, touchwood (wood that catches fire easily), or courage. Possibly from Gaelic *spong* or from the notion of "spark" of courage.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and positive in connotation
> - Emphasizes courage and determination combined with liveliness
> - The adjective "spunky" is very commonly used
> - Often describes characters who are small but brave or lively
