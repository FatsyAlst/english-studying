## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
Physical strength, especially muscular strength.

> [!example] Examples
> - In this job you need brains as well as brawn.
> - What they lack in brawn they make up for in skill.
> - He's all brawn and no brains.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Meat from a pig's (or calf's) head that has been boiled and pressed, served cold (British English).

> [!example] Examples
> - They served brawn as an appetizer.
> - Brawn is made from the pig's head.
> - She enjoyed a thick slice of brawn with mustard.

Late 13th c. Middle English *braun* 'boar's flesh' from Old French *braon* 'fleshy/muscular part' (of Germanic origin, related to German *Braten* 'roast meat'). Muscular strength sense from 1865.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Strength sense contrasts physical power vs. intelligence.
> - Meat sense is regional (British English).
> - Often used in contrast with 'brains' (intelligence).
> - Adjective form: brawny (muscular, strong).
