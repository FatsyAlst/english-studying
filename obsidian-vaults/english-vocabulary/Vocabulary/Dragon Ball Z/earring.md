## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A piece of jewelry worn attached to the ear, typically a decorative accessory.

> [!example] Examples
> - Several fighters in the series wear distinctive earrings as part of their appearance.
> - Golden earrings gleamed in the sunlight.
> - The warrior removed his earrings before the intense battle.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound word from "ear" (from Old English "eare") and "ring" (from Old English "hring"). The combination literally describes jewelry that forms a ring on or through the ear.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually used in plural form: "earrings" (one pair)
> - Can be singular when referring to a single piece
> - Important visual characteristic for several Dragon Ball Z characters
