## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
A person or thing that is mocked, ridiculed, or treated with contempt by others; someone who is the object of laughter or scorn

> [!example] Examples
> - After losing all three matches, he became the laughingstock of the tournament.
> - The failed experiment made the company a laughingstock in the industry.
> - He refused to become a laughingstock by admitting his mistake.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound word formed from "laughing" (present participle of laugh) and "stock" (originally meaning a worthless tree trunk, extended to mean a person of no value or standing).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always used in negative context
> - Often implies that someone's reputation or dignity has been damaged
> - Frequently follows "become" or "make"
