## 📖 Definition
*idiom* — (Common)
In a state of unconsciousness; knocked unconscious; asleep very deeply

> [!example] Examples
> - The boxer was knocked out cold in the third round.
> - He drank so much he passed out cold on the couch.
> - After the accident, she was out cold until the paramedics arrived.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Out" (unconscious) combined with "cold" (completely, without feeling or response). The phrase emerged in the 19th century in boxing contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Commonly used in sports (boxing) or accident contexts
> - Means completely unconscious, not just sleeping lightly
> - Often emphatic and dramatic in tone
