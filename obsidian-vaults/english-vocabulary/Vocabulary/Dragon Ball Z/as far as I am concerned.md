## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrase* (very frequent)
In my opinion; from my perspective; as it affects or matters to me personally

> [!example] Examples
> - As far as I am concerned, he's the strongest warrior alive
> - As far as I am concerned, that decision was wrong
> - As far as I am concerned, we should leave immediately
> - As far as I am concerned, it doesn't matter what anyone else thinks
> - As far as I am concerned, the battle is already won

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Concern" comes from Latin "concernere" (to mix together, relate to). The phrase developed in English to express personal perspective or interest in something. "As far as" indicates the scope or extent of the concern.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Expresses strong personal opinion or viewpoint
> - Often used to introduce a subjective judgment
> - Can be modified with pronouns (you, he, she, etc.)
> - Formal and assertive in tone
> - Commonly appears in character dialogue expressing conviction
