## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Acting or done without careful consideration; reckless, impulsive, or hasty.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a rash decision to make without thinking.
> - Don't do anything rash in anger.
> - His rash behavior often gets him into trouble.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (medical)
An outbreak of red spots or irritation on the skin; a skin irritation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The child developed a rash after eating strawberries.
> - A heat rash appeared on his neck.
> - The dermatologist examined the rash.

## 📜 Etymology
The adjective "rash" comes from Middle English rashe, possibly from Old Norse raskr (brave, quick). The noun "rash" (skin) comes from Middle English rashe, from Old French rasche.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The adjective sense means impulsive or careless
> - The noun sense is medical and refers to skin conditions
> - "Rash decision" is a common phrase
> - Can be negative when describing personality traits
