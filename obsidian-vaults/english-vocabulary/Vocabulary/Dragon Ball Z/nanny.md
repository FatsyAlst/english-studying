## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A person, usually a woman, employed to care for children; a caregiver or guardian

> [!example] Examples
> - The nanny was responsible for the young boy's upbringing.
> - A nanny accompanied the children on their adventure.
> - The strict nanny enforced discipline.

## 📜 Etymology
From *nanny*, a diminutive or familiar form of "Anne" (a common woman's name), used as a general term for a caregiver. The word dates back to the 18th century in English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Traditionally referred to female caregivers, though now can be gender-neutral
> - Often implies professional childcare in a household
> - Can also refer to caring attitudes (as in "nanny state")
> - Associated with authority and discipline in traditional contexts
