## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal/historical)
A soldier, particularly an American infantryman from World War I or World War II; a foot soldier or common soldier.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old warrior had been a doughboy during his youth before discovering martial arts.
> - The doughboys were drafted to fight in the distant war.
> - Many doughboys returned home changed by their experiences.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (informal)
A person with a soft, rounded, or heavyset body; someone who is overweight or lacks muscle definition (somewhat derogatory).

> [!example] Examples
> - The pudgy opponent looked like a doughboy compared to the lean fighter.
> - He transformed from a doughboy into a powerful warrior through rigorous training.
> - The doughboy trainer surprised everyone with his hidden strength.

## 📜 Etymology
The historical military sense likely comes from the clay-white appearance of uniform buttons or the doughy appearance of uniforms. The physical appearance meaning derives from "dough," referring to soft, malleable, or soft body composition. The term has roots in American English, particularly from the World Wars era.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (soldier) is historical and less commonly used in modern contexts
> - Sense 2 (physical appearance) is informal and potentially derogatory
> - The term is dated and may not be widely used by younger speakers
> - Often carries nostalgic or dismissive connotations
> - Less common than more modern fitness terminology
