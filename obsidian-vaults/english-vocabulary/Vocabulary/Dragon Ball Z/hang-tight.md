## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To hold on firmly; to wait patiently; to remain steady or composed. Used as an encouragement to persevere through difficulty.

> [!example] Examples
> - Hang tight, I'm coming to help you!
> - Just hang tight a little longer
> - The bridge is shaking—everyone hang tight!

## 🔗 Collocations
- hang tight + object (to someone, to a rope, etc.)
- hang tight + time expression (a minute, a second)
- just hang tight
- please hang tight

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "hang" (to grip or hold) + "tight" (firmly, with strength). Originated in nautical contexts where sailors needed to maintain firm grips during storms.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational usage
> - Can be both literal (hold onto something physically) and figurative (endure mentally)
> - Often used as encouragement or reassurance
> - Frequently used in emergency or crisis situations
