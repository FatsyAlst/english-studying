## 📖 Definition
*noun* (plural only) — (Frequent)

Small fragments or pieces; often used in the phrase "blown to smithereens" meaning completely destroyed or shattered.

> [!example] Examples
> - The massive explosion blew the building to smithereens.
> - The blast reduced the fortress to smithereens.
> - After the intense battle, the arena was torn to smithereens.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin unclear, possibly from Irish *smidiríní* meaning "small fragments." First recorded in English in the early 19th century. May be related to "smite" (to strike) or Gaelic elements.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost always used in plural form and negative contexts
> - Emphasizes complete destruction into tiny pieces
> - Vivid and dramatic, common in action narratives
> - Often used with "blow," "smash," "tear," or "reduce"
> - Conveys extreme violence or force
