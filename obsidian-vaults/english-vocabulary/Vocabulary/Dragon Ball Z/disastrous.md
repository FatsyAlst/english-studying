## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Frequent)

Extremely harmful, damaging, or calamitous; resulting in terrible consequences or failure.

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain's plan would have been disastrous for the entire world.
> - His strategy proved disastrous when he underestimated his opponent's power.
> - The earthquake had disastrous effects on the region.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *désastreux*, from *désastre* (disaster), from Italian *disastro*, from *dis-* (bad) + *astro* (star). The belief was that bad stars brought misfortune.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Strong negative connotation, emphasizing severity
> - More emphatic than "bad" or "unfortunate"
> - Formal register, suitable for serious contexts
> - Describes results, outcomes, or decisions with major negative impacts
