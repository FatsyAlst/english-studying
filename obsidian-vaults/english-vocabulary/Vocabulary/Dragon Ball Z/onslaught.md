## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A violent, forceful attack or assault; the beginning or start of a strong attack.

> [!example] Examples
> - The onslaught of enemy forces overwhelmed the defenders.
> - The boxer launched an onslaught of punches.
> - They had to endure the onslaught of criticism.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A large number of things arriving or happening suddenly; a rush or wave.

> [!example] Examples
> - The onslaught of customers during the sale was overwhelming.
> - An onslaught of questions from the reporters
> - The onslaught of winter weather

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch aanslag (attack), from aan (on) + slag (blow, strike).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies intense, overwhelming force
> - Often used in battle or combat contexts
> - Can be literal (physical attack) or figurative (questions, criticism)
> - Suggests the beginning of a sustained attack
