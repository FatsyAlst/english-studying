## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Common

Silly, foolish, or amusing in a playful way; behaving in a ridiculous or comic manner; not serious or sensible.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku has a goofy personality despite his power
> - That's a goofy joke
> - Don't be goofy in the middle of a fight
> - He made a goofy face to make everyone laugh

## 📜 Etymology
From "goof" (a silly person, a mistake), which came into English in the early 1900s. Possibly derived from the Disney character Goofy. The "goof" root may trace to earlier dialectal words meaning "foolish" or "absent-minded".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational adjective
> - Generally has positive or neutral connotations
> - Describes playful silliness, not harmful foolishness
> - Common in casual speech and writing
> - Often used affectionately about people or their behavior
