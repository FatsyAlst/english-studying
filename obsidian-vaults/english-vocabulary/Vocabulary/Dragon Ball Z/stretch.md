## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Most Common)
To extend or straighten the body or limbs to full length; to lengthen something by pulling or extending it.

> [!example] Examples
> - Stretch your arms before the fight
> - He stretched across the arena to reach his opponent
> - The warrior stretches his muscles before combat

---
### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To extend over time or distance; to continue for a longer period than expected; to reach or span.

> [!example] Examples
> - The battle stretches on for hours
> - The plain stretches for miles
> - The tournament stretches across several days

---
### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Common)
A continuous expanse or period of time; the act of extending or lengthening something.

> [!example] Examples
> - A long stretch of desert
> - For a stretch, he was winning
> - The final stretch of the tournament

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "streccan" and Germanic origins, related to Old Saxon "streccan". Likely connected to "straight" through Indo-European roots. The word has maintained consistent meaning since ancient times.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be physical (extending body) or temporal (extending time)
> - "Stretch out" emphasizes full extension
> - "Stretch the truth" means to exaggerate
> - Common in athletic and fitness contexts
> - Can function as both verb and noun
