## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Informal)
1. A person who lives on the streets without fixed employment or income; 2. Something disappointing, unpleasant, or unlucky; a bad or unfortunate situation

> [!example] Examples
> - Losing the game was a real bummer.
> - What a bummer that the concert got cancelled!
> - It's a bummer that you can't come to the party.

## 📜 Etymology
From "bum" (a person without a fixed home or job, from German "Bummler," meaning vagabond). First used in the 1960s as slang for something disappointing.
