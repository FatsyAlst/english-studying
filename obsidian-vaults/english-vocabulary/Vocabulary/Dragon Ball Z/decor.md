## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Frequent)

Decoration; the furnishings, ornaments, and aesthetic elements used to decorate a space or environment.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fortress had elaborate decor with intricate designs.
> - The battle arena's decor reflected the ancient temple's grandeur.
> - Simple decor adorned the warrior's training chamber.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *décor*, from Latin *decorem* meaning "fitting" or "proper." Originally meant "that which adorns," became standard for interior decoration elements and aesthetic design.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually uncountable in English
> - More formal than "decoration" in design contexts
> - Often used in interior design and aesthetic discussions
> - Can describe both the overall style and the items used
> - Emphasizes visual appeal and design harmony
