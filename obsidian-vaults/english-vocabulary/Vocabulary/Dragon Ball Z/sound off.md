## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To speak loudly and express one's opinion, often in a critical or confrontational way; to voice a complaint or grievance.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's always sounding off about something.
> - The workers sounded off about unfair working conditions.
> - Don't sound off at me about this!

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (military)
To announce oneself or call out; to make one's presence known by speaking.

> [!example] Examples
> - The soldiers sounded off in response to roll call.
> - Sound off when your name is called.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "sound" (to make noise/speak) and "off" (expressing direction or intensity). The phrasal verb developed from the military practice of calling out.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and often implies complaining or criticism
> - Can suggest the speaker is being loud or obnoxious
> - Common in everyday speech when someone complains
> - Has military origins from roll call procedures
