## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (very frequent)
To evaluate, examine, or estimate the value, nature, or quality of something; to judge

> [!example] Examples
> - He assessed his opponent's strength carefully
> - She had to assess the situation quickly
> - They assessed the damage after the battle
> - He assessed the threat level

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To determine the amount of tax or payment owed; to levy a charge

> [!example] Examples
> - The council assessed property taxes
> - They assessed a fine for the damages
> - The amount was assessed at a fair price

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "assesse" and Latin "assidere" meaning to sit beside or assist, which evolved to mean to evaluate or estimate value. The word came into English in the 14th century with the meaning of determining value for taxation.
