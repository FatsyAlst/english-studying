## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A body of water that is partly enclosed by land; a wide indentation in a coastline.

> [!example] Examples
> - The ships sailed into the bay.
> - The bay was protected from the storm.
> - San Francisco Bay is famous for its beauty.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A compartment or section of a building (e.g., loading bay, cargo bay).

> [!example] Examples
> - The cargo was loaded into the bay.
> - The loading bay was busy with activity.
> - Engine bay (the compartment of a vehicle containing the engine)

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Literary)
To bark loudly; to howl (used of dogs or wolves).

> [!example] Examples
> - The hounds bayed at the moon.
> - Wolves bayed in the distance.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *baie*, from Latin *baia*. The verb "to bay" (to bark) comes from Old French *bayer*, from Latin *badare* (to gape, open).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Geographic/maritime term for a body of water
> - Sense 2: Technical/structural term for a compartment
> - Sense 3: Less common; literary or poetic use
> - Bay window: a window that projects outward from a building
> - "Bay leaf": an herb used in cooking (from the bay tree)
> - Related phrase: "keep at bay" (see separate entry)
