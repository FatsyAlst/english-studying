## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Common)
Stubbornly adhering to one's opinion; unwilling to listen to reason or change one's mind; obstinate.

> [!example] Examples
> - His pigheaded attitude prevented him from accepting help.
> - She was too pigheaded to admit she was wrong.
> - A pigheaded warrior refuses to retreat even when the battle is lost.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "pig" + "headed". The idiom refers to the perceived stubbornness of pigs. Similar to "bullheaded" or "hardheaded" but specifically invoking pig imagery.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and somewhat derogatory
> - Synonyms: stubborn, obstinate, hardheaded, mulish, bullheaded
> - Often used to criticize someone's unwillingness to listen
> - Frequently appears in character descriptions in anime/manga
