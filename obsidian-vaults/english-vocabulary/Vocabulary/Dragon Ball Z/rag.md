## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
A piece of cloth, especially worn, torn, or tattered material; old cloth used for cleaning or wiping.

> [!example] Examples
> - She wiped the counter with a damp rag.
> - He tore his shirt to rags.
> - Old rags are good for cleaning.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common, Informal)
To scold or criticize someone harshly; to tease someone persistently.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't rag on me about being late again.
> - His friends ragged him about his new haircut.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Informal/Slang)
A newspaper or magazine considered to be of low quality or unreliable.

> [!example] Examples
> - That tabloid is nothing but a gossip rag.
> - He writes for some local rag.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *ragge*, possibly from Old Norse *rogg* (tuft of hair, shaggy). The word evolved to mean any tattered or torn cloth, and later extended to mean worn-out newspaper or magazine.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "From rags to riches" = transformation from poverty to wealth (idiomatic).
> - "Rag doll" = a soft, stuffed toy made from cloth scraps.
> - Verb form "rag on" = to criticize or tease repeatedly (informal).
> - Can be used literally (cloth) or figuratively (teasing, criticism).
