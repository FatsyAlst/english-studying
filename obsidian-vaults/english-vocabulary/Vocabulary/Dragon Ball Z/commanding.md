## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Impressive and making a strong visual impact; authoritative and able to inspire respect or obedience; exerting authority or control.

> [!example] Examples
> - His commanding presence dominated the battlefield.
> - She had a commanding voice that silenced everyone.
> - The commanding officer directed the entire operation.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "commendare" (to entrust, commit to) through Old French "commander" (to command). Related to "mandate" and "commend." The "-ing" form creates the adjective describing commanding qualities.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Suggests both physical dominance and emotional authority
> - Often used for powerful leaders or authority figures
> - Can describe both people and positions of advantage
