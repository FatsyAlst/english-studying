## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Never done or known before; having no previous precedent; occurring for the first time.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku's power level reached unprecedented heights during his battle with Cell.
> - The tournament attracted an unprecedented number of warriors from across the universe.
> - His Saiyan transformation displayed unprecedented strength and speed.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle French "précédent," derived from Latin "praecedent" (going before). The prefix "un-" negates the meaning, literally meaning "not preceded" or "without precedent."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary tone; commonly used in academic and professional contexts
> - Often precedes nouns like "level," "power," "growth," or "success"
> - Emphasizes the historical or exceptional nature of something
