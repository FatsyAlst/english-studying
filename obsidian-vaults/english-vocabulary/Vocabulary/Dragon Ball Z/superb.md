## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Extremely good or impressive; of the highest quality; excellent.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku demonstrated superb martial arts technique during the tournament.
> - The choreography of the battle sequences was superb.
> - Vegeta's fighting skills were superb, though his pride often hindered him.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "superbus," meaning "proud" or "haughty." Originally denoting something elevated or lofty, it evolved to mean exceptionally excellent or impressive in quality.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and positive tone; used in professional and academic contexts
> - Often precedes abstract nouns like "quality," "skill," or "performance"
> - Synonymous with "excellent," "outstanding," or "magnificent"
