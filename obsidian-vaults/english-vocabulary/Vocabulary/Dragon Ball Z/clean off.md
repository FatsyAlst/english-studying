## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To remove dirt, dust, or unwanted substances from a surface by wiping or scrubbing.

> [!example] Examples
> - He cleaned off the mud from his armor after the battle.
> - She cleaned off the debris before inspecting the damage.
> - Clean off that blood and prepare for the next fight.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To wipe away or remove something completely; to clear away.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior cleaned off the sweat from his brow.
> - He cleaned off the dust from his face.
> - She cleaned off the soot with a cloth.

## 📜 Etymology
Phrasal verb combining "clean" (from Old English "clæne," pure, bright) and "off" (from Old English "of," away). The combination emphasizes removal and elimination.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable phrasal verb: "clean off the mud" or "clean the mud off"
> - Often followed by "with" (a cloth, water, etc.)
> - Common in literal contexts involving physical cleaning
> - Similar to "clean up" but often more specific to surfaces
