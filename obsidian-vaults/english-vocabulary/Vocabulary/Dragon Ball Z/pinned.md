## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense of pin)* (frequent)
To hold someone or something firmly in place, unable to move; to fasten with a pin

> [!example] Examples
> - The enemy was pinned against the wall
> - He pinned his opponent to the ground
> - The force pinned him in place
> - She pinned her hair back with a clip

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To identify or place responsibility on someone

> [!example] Examples
> - The crime was pinned on an innocent person
> - He pinned the blame on his rival
> - They pinned their hopes on the young warrior

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "pinn" referring to a wooden peg or bolt, related to Latin "pinna" meaning feather or wing. The sense of fastening or immobilizing developed from the literal use of pins.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In combat contexts, describes immobilizing or trapping an opponent
> - Can be literal (physical restraint) or figurative (assigning blame)
> - Often used in wrestling or close-quarters battle descriptions
