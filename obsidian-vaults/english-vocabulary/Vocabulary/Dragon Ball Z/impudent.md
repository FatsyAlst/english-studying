## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Not showing proper respect; disrespectfully bold or rude; cheeky or insolent; impertinent and presumptuous.

> [!example] Examples
> - His impudent attitude angered the veteran warrior.
> - The fighter's impudent remark drew gasps from the crowd.
> - Impudent challengers often underestimated their opponents.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *impudens*, from *im-* (not) + *pudens* (ashamed, from *pudere*, to be ashamed). The word suggests lacking shame or respect. Used in English since the 15th century to describe rude or disrespectful behavior.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective describing rude or disrespectful behavior
> - Suggests boldness without proper respect
> - More formal than "rude" or "disrespectful"
> - Related: impudence (noun), impudently (adverb)
> - Often carries negative connotation in formal contexts
> - Can suggest youthful boldness or arrogance
> - Common in describing character behavior
