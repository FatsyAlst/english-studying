## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (very frequent)
Burning intensely with bright flames; extremely hot and bright

> [!example] Examples
> - A blazing inferno engulfed the battlefield
> - His blazing power overwhelmed his opponents
> - The blazing heat from the explosion
> - A blazing energy attack shot across the sky

### Sense 2 — *verb (present participle of blaze)* (frequent)
Burning or shining intensely and brightly

> [!example] Examples
> - The sun was blazing overhead
> - His eyes were blazing with determination
> - Energy blazing from his palms

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "blaze" with Germanic origins, possibly related to Old Norse "blys" meaning torch. The word originally referred to a bright flame and extended metaphorically to intense light or energy.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Frequently used to describe intense power levels and energy attacks in Dragon Ball Z
> - Emphasizes both visual brightness and intensity of power
> - Can be used literally (actual fire) or metaphorically (fierce emotion or power)
