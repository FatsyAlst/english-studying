## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A short, showy piece of music played on brass instruments to announce or celebrate something; ostentatious or impressive display; a ceremonial flourish or public announcement.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament began with great fanfare and celebration.
> - Fanfare announced the arrival of the next challenger.
> - The victor was welcomed with fanfare and cheers from the crowd.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *fanfare*, of uncertain origin but possibly from Spanish *fanfarria*. The word may be imitative in nature, suggesting the sound of trumpets. Used in English since the 17th century to describe both musical flourishes and ostentatious displays.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun describing ceremonial musical announcement
> - Often used to describe grand or spectacular introductions
> - Can be literal (trumpet fanfare) or metaphorical (public acclaim)
> - Related: fanfare (musical style), fanfaronade (boastful display)
> - Common in sports, entertainment, and ceremonial contexts
> - Implies something impressive or attention-grabbing
