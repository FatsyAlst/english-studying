## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Mentally unstable or disturbed; insane or irrational

> [!example] Examples
> - The deranged villain's plans made no logical sense.
> - His deranged laughter echoed through the chamber.
> - The deranged tyrant ordered the destruction of everything.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *déranger*, from *dé-* (away, off) + *ranger* (to arrange, set in order). Literally means "thrown out of order" or "disarranged". The mental sense developed from this concept of disorder.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Suggests more extreme mental disturbance than just "crazy"
> - Often used in dramatic or villainous contexts
> - Implies unpredictability and danger due to mental state
