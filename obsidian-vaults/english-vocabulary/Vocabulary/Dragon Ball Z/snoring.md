## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The harsh, snorting sound made by a person while sleeping, caused by breathing through a partly open mouth

### Sense 2 — *verb (gerund)* (common)
The act of making snoring sounds while asleep

> [!example] Examples
> - His loud snoring kept everyone awake.
> - The character fell asleep, snoring heavily.
> - The comedic snoring echoed through the room.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *snoren*, of imitative origin. The word mimics the actual sound produced, related to "snort".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used as both noun and gerund form
> - Often associated with deep sleep or exhaustion
> - Frequently used for comedic effect in anime
