## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
An expression used when about to do something risky, uncertain, or with little confidence in success; saying you're about to take a chance.

> [!example] Examples
> - Here goes nothing, I'll try the audition anyway.
> - Well, here goes nothing! I'm jumping in.
> - Here goes nothing—I hope this works!

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "here" (indicating the present moment) + "goes" (a verb indicating something is about to happen) + "nothing" (suggesting the effort or attempt may fail). Developed as American English slang in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and conversational
> - Often said before attempting something risky or uncertain
> - Shows a sense of resignation mixed with determination
> - Very common in everyday speech
> - Often said with a shrug or brave face
