## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A confrontation or direct contest between two people or groups; a showdown.

> [!example] Examples
> - The face-off between Goku and Vegeta was intense.
> - They had a legendary face-off in the tournament.
> - The final face-off determined the strongest warrior.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Phrasal Verb: face off) (Common)
To confront directly; to stand in opposition to someone or something; to face in a duel or competition.

> [!example] Examples
> - The two fighters will face off in the championship.
> - They faced off against each other with everything they had.
> - The champions faced off in an epic battle.

## 📜 Etymology
From "face" (from Latin "facies" meaning appearance/face) + "off" (away/apart). A 20th-century compound meaning to place faces in opposition or confrontation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun form is hyphenated: "face-off"
> - Verb form is two words: "face off"
> - Implies equal opposition or direct competition
> - Very common in sports and action contexts
> - Can indicate final confrontation or climactic battle
> - Suggests mutual engagement and challenge
