## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The act of touching, holding, or managing something physically; the way something is dealt with or managed.

> [!example] Examples
> - The careful handling of the energy blast prevented further destruction.
> - Vegeta's rough handling of his subordinates showed his dominance.
> - Goku's skillful handling of various weapons impressed his opponents.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
The manner in which a situation, task, or problem is dealt with; management or control.

> [!example] Examples
> - The captain praised the team's handling of the crisis.
> - Her handling of the negotiations was diplomatic and effective.
> - The handling of the tournament rules was controversial.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "handle," derived from Old English "handlian," meaning "to touch or hold with the hand." The "-ing" suffix converts the verb to a gerund or noun form.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can refer to both physical management (touching/managing objects) and abstract management (dealing with situations)
> - Often used with adjectives like "careful," "rough," "skillful," or "proper"
> - Common in both formal and informal contexts
