## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Regarded as holy, sacred, or greatly respected; honored and revered.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hallowed ground of the cemetery
> - The hallowed halls of the university
> - A hallowed tradition passed down through generations
> - The hallowed name of the great leader

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English halgian (to make holy), from halig (holy). Related to "holy" and "hallow" (to honor or respect).

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and reverential tone
> - Often used for religious, historical, or culturally significant places
> - Suggests deep respect and sacredness
> - Common in formal writing and literary contexts
