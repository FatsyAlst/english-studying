## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Eager and enthusiastic; keen and ready to do something, often with impatience or excitement; champing at the bit.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was raring to go and face his opponent.
> - The warriors were raring for battle.
> - She's raring to prove her newfound strength.

## 📜 Etymology
From "rare" (an obsolete or dialectal form of "rear," meaning to rear up like a horse). Applied to horses that reared up excitedly, and extended metaphorically to describe people showing similar eager energy. The "-ing" ending creates the adjective form.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost always followed by "to" (raring to do something)
> - Conveys energetic anticipation rather than calm readiness
> - Informal and enthusiastic in tone
