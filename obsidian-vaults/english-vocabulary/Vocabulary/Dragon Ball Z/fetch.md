## 📖 Definition
*verb* — (Common)
1. To go get something and bring it back; 2. To be sold or exchanged for a certain price; 3. (in dog training) to retrieve

> [!example] Examples
> - She fetched a glass of water from the kitchen.
> - The puppy learned to fetch the tennis ball.
> - That painting should fetch a high price at auction.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "feccan" or Middle English "fecchen," possibly related to Old Norse. The word has been in use since Middle English times.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with animals (fetch a stick)
> - Can mean to retrieve and bring something back
> - "Fetching" as an adjective means attractive or appealing
