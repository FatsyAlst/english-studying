## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (Medium-High Frequency)

To begin or start something; to cause something to start or happen.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament will commence at noon tomorrow.
> - Combat commenced with fierce energy attacks.
> - Let us commence the final battle between these two warriors.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *commencer*, from Latin *com-* (together) + *initiāre* (to begin). Related to "initial" and "initiate." The word entered English through French influence.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "start" or "begin"
> - Commonly used in official or ceremonial contexts
> - Often appears in announcements or formal speech
> - Frequently used to describe the beginning of events, battles, or proceedings
> - "Commencement" (noun) refers to a graduation ceremony or formal beginning
