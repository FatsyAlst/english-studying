## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A cleaning tool with bristles attached to a long handle, used for sweeping

> [!example] Examples
> - He swept the floor with a broom
> - The witch flew on a magical broom
> - She grabbed a broom to clean
> - A broom leaned against the wall

### Sense 2 — *noun* (botanical)
A shrubby plant with small leaves and bright flowers; a type of climbing plant

> [!example] Examples
> - Broom flowers bloomed on the hillside
> - The golden broom covered the landscape

### Sense 3 — *verb* (less common)
To sweep with a broom; to clean by sweeping

> [!example] Examples
> - She broomed the courtyard
> - They broomed away the dust

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "brom" referring to the broom plant (Cytisus scoparius), later applied to the cleaning tool made from its branches. The word comes from Proto-Germanic origins.
