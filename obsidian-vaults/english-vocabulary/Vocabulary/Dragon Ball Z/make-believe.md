## 📖 Definition
*noun/adjective* — (Common)

Imagination or fantasy; a pretend or imaginary situation; something that is not real but imagined or invented.

> [!example] Examples
> - That story about his power was just make-believe.
> - The child played make-believe with imaginary enemies.
> - His confidence was make-believe, hiding his true fears.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "make" and "believe," literally "to make oneself believe." Became standard phrase in the 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be noun or adjective
> - Often hyphenated as adjective ("make-believe world")
> - Implies something unreal or imaginary
> - Used for children's play and figuratively for false pretenses
> - Similar to "pretend"
