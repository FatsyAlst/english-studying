## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To throw something lightly or carelessly through the air.

> [!example] Examples
> - He tossed the ball to his friend.
> - She tossed her keys onto the table.
> - They tossed coins into the fountain.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
(Of a person or animal) To move restlessly or with sudden movements.

> [!example] Examples
> - The patient tossed and turned all night.
> - The horse tossed its head in frustration.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
The act of throwing something lightly; a light throw.

> [!example] Examples
> - It's just a short toss across the room.
> - The toss of a coin will decide who goes first.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Scandinavian origins, possibly from Old Norse *tossa* or Norwegian *tøse*. The word entered English in the 16th century, originally meaning "to pull or jerk."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Toss up" or "it's a toss-up" = equally likely outcomes; uncertain result.
> - "Toss and turn" = to move restlessly in bed (common sleep phrase).
> - More casual than "throw"; suggests lighter, more informal movement.
