## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Informal)
A sudden blow or misfortune; originally a curse or the "evil eye". Mostly in *double whammy* = two bad things happening at the same time.

> [!example] Examples
> - Losing my job and my flat in the same week was a double whammy.
> - Rain and a train strike — a double whammy for commuters.
> - Someone must have put a whammy on our team.

## 📜 Etymology
American English, probably from the imitative word *wham*; recorded from 1927 and with the sense "evil spell" by the early 1930s. Al Capp's comic strip *Li'l Abner* (Evil-Eye Fleegle's "double whammy") made it popular.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Today almost always *double whammy*; typical of news headlines.
> - Related: [[wham]] = the sound of a hard hit.
