## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To dive, jump, or throw oneself suddenly into water or another medium.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior plunged into the raging river without hesitation.
> - He plunged off the cliff into the abyss below.
> - She plunged into the energy vortex fearlessly.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To insert or thrust something forcefully into something else; to push in abruptly.

> [!example] Examples
> - He plunged his sword through the armor.
> - She plunged her hand into the crystal to absorb its power.
> - The energy blast plunged through the mountain.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (common)
To fall or descend suddenly and steeply; to drop sharply.

> [!example] Examples
> - The temperature plunged after the ice attack.
> - His power level plunged when he lost concentration.
> - The ship plunged into darkness as the sun set.

### Sense 4 — *noun* (common)
The act of plunging; a sudden dive or immersion.

> [!example] Examples
> - He made a daring plunge into enemy territory.
> - The ocean plunge was dangerous and exhilarating.
> - A plunge into unknown danger awaited him.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "plungier," from Vulgar Latin "plumbicare" (to sound with a plumb line). The word originally referred to sounding depths with a lead weight, evolving to mean diving or falling.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be both literal (physical diving) and figurative (sudden action)
> - "Take the plunge" is an idiom meaning to commit to something risky
> - Often implies sudden, decisive action
> - Common in action and adventure contexts
