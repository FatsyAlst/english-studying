## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A V-shaped cut or indentation in a surface or edge.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sword had a notch in its blade from the intense battle.
> - Each notch on the staff represented a conquered enemy.
> - A notch in the rock face helped climbers reach the summit.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A level, step, or degree in a series or sequence (often used figuratively).

> [!example] Examples
> - He moved up a notch in the tournament standings.
> - This victory represents a notch above his previous performance.
> - Each training session brings him up another notch in power.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (common)
To cut a notch or notches in something; to mark with a notch.

> [!example] Examples
> - He notched his staff to commemorate each victory.
> - The blade was notched from hitting the stone.
> - She notched the tree to mark the path.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "nocche," possibly related to Old French "oche" (a notch or point). The word entered English in the 15th century. The exact origins are uncertain but may relate to "nock" (the notch in an arrow).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is literal (physical indentation)
> - Sense 2 is figurative (level or degree)
> - Common phrase: "a notch above" = one level better
> - "Top notch" = excellent, highest quality
