## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)

Affected by or suffering from disease; infected or contaminated; figuratively, morally or socially corrupted or unhealthy.

> [!example] Examples
> - The diseased plants had to be removed from the garden.
> - He had a diseased heart and needed surgery.
> - The country's diseased government needed reform.

## 📜 Etymology
From "dis-" (prefix indicating negation or bad condition) + "ease" (state of being at ease). A disease is a state of being "not at ease." The adjective "diseased" describes something afflicted by or infected with disease.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal sense: infected or affected by actual disease/illness.
> - Figurative sense: morally corrupted, twisted, or unhealthy (minds, governments, societies).
> - Often implies something damaged or needing cure/removal.
> - Can be used medically or metaphorically in literary contexts.
> - Negative connotation in both literal and figurative uses.
