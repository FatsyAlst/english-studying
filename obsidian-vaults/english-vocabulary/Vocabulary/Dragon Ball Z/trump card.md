## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A decisive advantage or secret resource that can be used to achieve victory or gain an advantage over others, often saved for a critical moment.

> [!example] Examples
> - His speed was his trump card in the tournament.
> - They revealed their trump card in the final round of negotiations.
> - Goku's Kamehameha proved to be his trump card against the enemy.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Derived from card games where trump cards have higher value. The term became metaphorical for any decisive advantage.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually singular when referring to a specific advantage
> - Often implies the advantage was kept hidden or reserved
> - Common in competitive and strategic contexts
