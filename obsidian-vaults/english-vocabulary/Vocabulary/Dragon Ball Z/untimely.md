## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Occurring at an unsuitable or disadvantageous moment; inopportune or badly timed; happening too early.

> [!example] Examples
> - An untimely arrival disrupted the ceremony.
> - His untimely death shocked the world.
> - The untimely interruption broke their concentration.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "un-" (not) + "timely" (coming at the right time). "Timely" comes from "time" + "-ly" suffix. First used in Middle English with meaning "inappropriate to the time."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in literary or formal contexts
> - Carries a sense of misfortune or bad luck
> - Emphasizes that something happened at the wrong moment
