## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A puff or gust of air or scent; to fail to hit or strike something aimed at.

> [!example] Examples
> - He missed the attack; his blade encountered only air with a whiff.
> - A whiff of smoke filled the chamber after the explosion.
> - His punch missed completely—just a whiff of air.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (frequent)
To swing at something and miss; to fail in an attempt to do something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior whiffed on the attack and left himself vulnerable.
> - He whiffed at the opportunity to defeat his enemy.
> - She whiffed the easy strike due to overconfidence.

## 📜 Etymology
Imitative origin from the 1600s, imitating the sound of a gust of wind or a swishing motion that misses. The verb sense emerged from baseball terminology in the 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can mean both a smell/puff of air and to miss
> - Informal and often humorous when describing failed attacks
> - Baseball term adopted for general use
