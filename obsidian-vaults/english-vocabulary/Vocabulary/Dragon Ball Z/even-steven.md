## 📖 Definition
*adjective/noun* — (Common)

Equal; having no advantage or disadvantage; breaking even in a situation where both parties are equally matched.

> [!example] Examples
> - After their draw, they called it even-steven.
> - The fighters were even-steven in terms of power level.
> - Both characters landed equal hits, so it was even-steven.

## 📜 Etymology
Rhyming reduplication combining "even" (equal) with the name "Steven." Popular in American English in the 20th century, origin of "Steven" unclear.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, playful tone
> - Often used in competitive or transactional contexts
> - Implies mutual agreement to stop competing
> - Can also mean "tied" or "equal in all respects"
