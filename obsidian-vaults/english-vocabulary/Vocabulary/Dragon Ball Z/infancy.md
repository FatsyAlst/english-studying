## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
The state or period of being an infant; early childhood, typically from birth to age 2-3.

> [!example] Examples
> - During infancy, babies depend entirely on their parents.
> - She has no memories from her infancy.
> - Proper nutrition is crucial during infancy.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common, Figurative)
An early or initial stage of development; the beginning of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The technology was still in its infancy.
> - The movement was in its infancy when the war broke out.
> - The company was in its infancy, with only a few employees.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *infans* ("unable to speak"), from *in-* (not) + *fari* (to speak). The word originally referred to very young children who could not yet speak, then extended to the state or period of early childhood, and metaphorically to the early stage of anything.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal sense: period from birth to approximately 2-3 years old.
> - Figurative sense: the earliest stage of development (technologies, movements, ideas).
> - "In its infancy" = in the early, developing stages (very common figurative phrase).
> - Related: "infant" (a baby), "infantile" (relating to infants, or childish).
> - Often used when something is new, undeveloped, or just beginning.
