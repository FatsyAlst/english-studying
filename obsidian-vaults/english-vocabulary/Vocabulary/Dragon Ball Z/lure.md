## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To tempt or entice someone with something attractive; to draw someone into a situation, often deceptively.

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain tried to lure Goku into a trap.
> - The enemy lured them away from their base.
> - He was lured by the promise of a strong opponent.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb: lure out* (Common)
To draw someone out of hiding or a safe place; to force someone into the open.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighters tried to lure out their opponent from behind the rocks.
> - They used bait to lure him out of his cave.
> - The strategy was to lure out the hidden enemy.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "luren," possibly from Old French "loure." Originally meant to allure or attract.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (attract with bait) or figurative (entice with promises)
> - Often implies deception or manipulation
> - "Lure out" is a phrasal verb meaning to draw out from hiding
> - Frequently used in contexts of traps or deception
