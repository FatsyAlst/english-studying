## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A small object or trinket; often used in the phrase "knick-knack" to refer to decorative items or miscellaneous small things. Can also mean a small cut or notch.

> [!example] Examples
> - The shelf was filled with various knick-knacks and trinkets.
> - Small knicks and scratches covered the armor.
> - A knick in the blade dulled its edge.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from German "Knicknack" (trinket), which is reduplicative. May be related to "nick" (to cut), with the reduplication adding emphasis. First documented in English in the 17th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually appears as "knick-knack" (with hyphen) for the trinket sense
> - Can function as both noun and verb
> - Informal and conversational tone
