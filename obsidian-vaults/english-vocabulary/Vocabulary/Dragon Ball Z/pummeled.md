## 📖 Definition
*Verb (Past Tense)* — (Common)
To strike repeatedly and violently with the fists; to bombard or attack intensely and continuously; to beat mercilessly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior pummeled his opponent with a relentless barrage of attacks.
> - He was pummeled into submission by the superior fighter.
> - The city had been pummeled by waves of destructive energy attacks.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *pomele*, referring to beating or striking. The root may be related to *pommel* (the rounded knob on a sword handle). Used in English since at least the 16th century to describe repeated striking or beating.
