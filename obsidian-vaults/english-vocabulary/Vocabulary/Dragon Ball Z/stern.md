## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Serious and strict, often showing disapproval.

> [!example] Examples
> - The professor gave me a stern look when my phone rang.
> - The police issued a stern warning to drivers.
> - Her father was stern but fair.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Nautical)
The back part of a ship or boat.

> [!example] Examples
> - We sat at the stern of the boat.
> - They searched the ship from stem to stern.

## 🔗 Collocations
- a stern warning / look / face / voice
- a stern test (= a difficult challenge)
- stern-faced
- from stem to stern
- bow and stern

---
## 📜 Etymology
Two different words: the adjective from Old English *styrne* "severe, strict"; the noun probably from Old Norse *stjórn* "steering" — ships were steered from the back.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Opposite of the ship's stern: the *bow* (front).
> - *A stern test* = a hard challenge (sports journalism).
> - *Stern* is stronger than *serious* but milder than *harsh*.
