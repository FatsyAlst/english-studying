## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (frequent)
All the time; from the beginning; throughout the entire period or duration of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - He knew the truth all along but didn't tell us.
> - The traitor was with us all along.
> - She had the power all along but never knew how to use it.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "all" (every) + "along" (throughout the length of). The phrase emerged in the 15th-16th century English to indicate continuous duration from past to present.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with discovery or revelation ("I knew all along...")
> - Expresses surprise that something was true from the beginning
> - Common in storytelling and plot twists
