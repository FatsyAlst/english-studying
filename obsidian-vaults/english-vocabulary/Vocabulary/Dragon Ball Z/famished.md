## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Medium Frequency)

Extremely hungry; suffering from lack of food.

> [!example] Examples
> - After the long battle, Goku was famished and ready to eat a huge meal.
> - The famished warrior could barely stand without food.
> - They arrived famished after traveling all day through the desert.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *famine* (hunger, starvation), from Latin *famēs* (hunger). The adjective form developed from the noun *famine*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal/literary than "hungry" or "starving"
> - Often used to emphasize extreme hunger
> - Can be used metaphorically to mean desperately wanting something
