## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun phrase* (common)
A state of extreme tension, fear, or anxiety, often shown by gripping something tightly so that the knuckles turn white from the pressure.

> [!example] Examples
> - She gripped the seat with white knuckles during the intense battle.
> - His white knuckles betrayed his nervousness.
> - The fight was so close they watched with white knuckles.

## 📜 Etymology
Derived from the physical phenomenon where gripping something very tightly causes the blood to drain from the knuckles, making them appear white. Used metaphorically to express fear or tension.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually describes either the physical gripping or the emotional state
> - Can be used as an adjective: "white-knuckled"
> - Indicates extreme tension or fear
