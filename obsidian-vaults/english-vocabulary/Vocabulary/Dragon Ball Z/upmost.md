## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Common)
Of highest importance; paramount; at the highest position or level; supreme or most significant.

> [!example] Examples
> - His survival was of upmost importance to the group.
> - Reaching upmost power levels became his goal.
> - Victory remained the upmost priority in the tournament.

### Sense 2 — *Adverb* (Less Common)
To the greatest degree or extent; at the highest level; with maximum priority or importance.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior pushed himself upmost to achieve victory.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *uppemest*, a variant of *utmost* (from *ut* meaning "out" + *-most*, superlative suffix). The form "upmost" is sometimes considered archaic or dialectal, with "utmost" being the preferred modern spelling, though both are used.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Variant spelling of "utmost"
> - Indicates highest importance or degree
> - Often used in formal or emphatic contexts
> - Related: utmost (preferred modern spelling)
> - Phrase: "of upmost importance"
> - Can mean highest position, level, or priority
> - Less common than "utmost" in contemporary usage
