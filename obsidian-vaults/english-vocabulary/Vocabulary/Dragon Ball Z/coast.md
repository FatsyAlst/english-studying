## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Very Frequent)
The seashore; the land bordering on the ocean or sea.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warriors trained on the coast near the ocean.
> - The coastal villages were peaceful and remote.
> - They traveled along the coast searching for the island.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Frequent)
To move without using power; to glide or drift effortlessly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The spaceship will coast through space without engine power.
> - He coasted down the hill on his skateboard.
> - The car coasted to a stop after running out of fuel.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Frequent)
To progress or get by without much effort or work.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't coast through training; push yourself harder.
> - He coasted through high school without studying.
> - The fighter cannot coast on past victories.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *coste*, from Latin *costa* meaning "rib" or "side." Originally referred to the side of land next to the sea, evolved to mean shoreline. The verb sense developed later, meaning to move along the side or glide.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is geographical, referring to seashores
> - Sense 2 is physical, describing effortless movement
> - Sense 3 is metaphorical, suggesting lack of effort or commitment
> - The verb "coast" implies momentum from previous effort
