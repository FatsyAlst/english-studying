## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Informal)
To kiss someone, usually playfully or affectionately.

> [!example] Examples
> - She smooched him on the cheek.
> - They smooched goodnight.
> - He smooched his girlfriend.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Informal)
A kiss, typically a quick or playful one.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Give me a smooch," she said playfully.
> - He planted a smooch on her forehead.

## 📜 Etymology
Likely onomatopoetic, imitating the sound of a kiss. Possibly influenced by or related to Old English/German words, though the exact origin is unclear. The word emerged in American English in the early 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and playful in tone
> - Used in casual conversation, often between friends or in romantic contexts
> - More affectionate or playful than formal "kiss"
> - Can be used jokingly or tenderly depending on context
> - Synonyms: kiss, peck, smack, buss (archaic)
> - Often appears in dialogue to convey intimacy or playfulness
> - Appropriate for informal writing and speech
