## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Massive, bulky, or unusually large in size; often describing something that appears imposing or threatening.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hulking creature blocked the entire doorway.
> - A hulking figure emerged from the darkness.
> - The hulking opponent towered over his rivals.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
(Of a person) Having a large, muscular build; broad and powerful-looking.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hulking warrior intimidated everyone around him.
> - His hulking physique made him appear invincible.

## 📜 Etymology
From "hulk," which originally referred to the hull of a ship. Extended to mean something large and massive or unwieldy.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes both size and sometimes an awkward or threatening quality
> - Often used in fantasy and action contexts
> - Can have slightly negative connotations (clumsy, awkward) in addition to size
