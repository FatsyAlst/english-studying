## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To cause someone severe pain, suffering, or mental anguish; to torture or afflict.

> [!example] Examples
> - The constant noise torments the residents.
> - She was tormented by guilt and regret.
> - Don't torment the animals.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Severe pain, suffering, or mental anguish; extreme distress or torture.

> [!example] Examples
> - The prisoners endured constant torment.
> - His life was filled with torment and suffering.
> - Emotional torment was worse than physical pain.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *torment*, from Latin *tormentum* (instrument of torture), related to *torquere* (to twist). The word originally referred to physical torture (using twisted instruments), then evolved to mean any severe suffering.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe physical torture or psychological suffering.
> - Verb: to cause suffering or pain (deliberately or as a consequence).
> - Noun: the suffering itself.
> - Related: "tormentor" (one who causes torment).
> - Often implies deliberate cruelty or persistent suffering.
> - Literary and formal in tone.
