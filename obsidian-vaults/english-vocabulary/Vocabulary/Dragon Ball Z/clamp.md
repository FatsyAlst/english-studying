## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A device for holding or securing something tightly in place, typically consisting of two parts that press together.

> [!example] Examples
> - The metal clamp holds the parts together
> - Use a clamp to secure the wood
> - The machinery has multiple clamps

---
### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To fasten or hold something tightly with or as if with a clamp; to grip firmly.

> [!example] Examples
> - Clamp the material down before cutting
> - He clamped his hand over his mouth
> - The character clamped the opponent in a tight hold

---
### Sense 3 — *Verb (phrasal: "clamp it")* (Less Common)
To remain quiet or stop speaking; to hold back words or information.

> [!example] Examples
> - Just clamp it and don't say anything
> - Clamp it, or you'll regret it
> - He clamped it and refused to talk

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch "klampe" or similar Germanic sources. Related to "cramp" and "clam" (to shut). The tool meaning dates to the 15th century, the verb sense to the 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As noun: standard hardware/tool term
> - As verb: can be literal (physically holding) or figurative (controlling, suppressing)
> - "Clamp down" means to enforce strict rules or restrictions
> - "Clamp it" is informal slang meaning "be quiet" or "shut up"
> - Phrasal verb "clamp down on" common in political/authority contexts
