## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Frequent)

Silly, eccentric, or crazy in a humorous and unpredictable way; absurdly funny.

> [!example] Examples
> - Master Roshi's wacky personality often amused his students.
> - The tournament had some wacky competitors with unusual fighting styles.
> - His wacky sense of humor made even the serious fighters laugh.

## 📜 Etymology
From *whack* (to hit) or possibly an onomatopoeia suggesting craziness. Emerged in American slang in the early 20th century, becoming more mainstream in mid-century entertainment.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and playful register
> - Positive or neutral connotation when describing humor
> - Emphasizes unpredictability and absurdity
> - Often used to describe comedic characters or situations
> - More lighthearted than "crazy" or "insane"
> - Common in comedy and entertainment contexts
