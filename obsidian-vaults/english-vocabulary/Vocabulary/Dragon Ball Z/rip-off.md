## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
Something that costs much more than it should; a cheat.

> [!example] Examples
> - Eight euros for a coffee? That's a rip-off!
> - The festival tickets were a total rip-off.
> - Don't buy that charger — it's a rip-off.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
A copy or imitation of something, usually of lower quality.

> [!example] Examples
> - The film is a cheap rip-off of *Star Wars*.
> - That game is basically an Overwatch rip-off.

## 📜 Etymology
From the phrasal verb *rip off* (to steal, to cheat), American slang from the 1960s–70s.
