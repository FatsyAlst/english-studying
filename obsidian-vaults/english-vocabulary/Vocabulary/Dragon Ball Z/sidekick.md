## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Very Frequent)

A person who helps and supports a more important or central character; a faithful companion or assistant.

> [!example] Examples
> - Krillin is Goku's loyal sidekick throughout his adventures.
> - Every superhero needs a trustworthy sidekick to help with their mission.
> - The detective and his sidekick solved the mystery together.

## 📜 Etymology
American slang, probably from *side* + *kick*. First appeared in late 19th century, possibly influenced by "side-pocket" (a less important position). Originally referred to a pocket on the side of trousers.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in entertainment and storytelling
> - Implies loyalty and support rather than equality
> - Can be affectionate but also implies secondary importance
> - Common in superhero narratives and adventure stories
