## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A large, open container made of plastic, wood, or metal, used for bathing, washing, or holding liquids or substances; a bathtub.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku relaxed in a hot tub after his grueling training session.
> - The warrior filled the tub with water for a healing bath.
> - A large tub of water was prepared for the martial artists to recover.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (informal)
Any large, round container used for storage or transport (butter tub, popcorn tub, etc.).

> [!example] Examples
> - The supplies were transported in several large tubs.
> - A tub of fresh water was brought to the village.
> - The tournament organizers stored equipment in plastic tubs.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (informal/slang)
A slow or awkward vehicle, particularly a car or ship; something that is large, clumsy, or poorly designed.

> [!example] Examples
> - That old spaceship is a real tub; it barely moves.
> - He drove that tub of a car across the desert.
> - The transport vessel was a slow tub compared to modern spaceships.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch "tubbe" or Old Norse "tubbi," meaning a large wooden vessel or tub. The word has Germanic roots and has been used for centuries to describe open containers. The slang meaning (clumsy vehicle) is a modern extension referencing the large, bulky shape.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (bathtub) is the most common modern usage
> - Sense 2 (general container) is common in both domestic and commercial contexts
> - Sense 3 (clumsy vehicle) is informal and somewhat dated
> - Can appear in compounds like "hot tub," "bathtub," "foot tub"
> - The word is concrete and tangible in Senses 1 and 2, more figurative in Sense 3
