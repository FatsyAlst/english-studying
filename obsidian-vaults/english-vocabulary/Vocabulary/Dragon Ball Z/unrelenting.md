## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Relentless; not softening or yielding; continuing with intensity without stopping or reducing.

> [!example] Examples
> - An unrelenting attack from the enemy
> - His unrelenting pursuit of perfection
> - The unrelenting heat of the desert
> - Unrelenting pressure from all sides

---
## 📜 Etymology
From un- (not) + relenting (from relent, to yield or soften). "Relent" comes from Latin relentare (to slacken).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes continuous, unwavering intensity
> - Often used for negative pressures or attacks
> - Can also describe positive determination
> - Similar to "relentless" but slightly more formal
