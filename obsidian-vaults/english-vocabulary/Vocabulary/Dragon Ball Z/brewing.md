## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
(Of beer, coffee, or tea) To be prepared by steeping or fermenting; to make a beverage by this process.

> [!example] Examples
> - The coffee is still brewing.
> - He's brewing a fresh pot of tea.
> - Let the tea brew for five minutes.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common, Figurative)
To be in the process of developing or preparing; to be about to happen (often used with negative situations).

> [!example] Examples
> - A storm is brewing on the horizon.
> - Trouble has been brewing between the neighbors.
> - Something is brewing; I can feel it.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *breowan*, from Proto-Germanic *breuwan*. The word originally meant to ferment or prepare beer. The figurative sense of "developing trouble" evolved from the literal process of slow preparation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal sense: making beverages by steeping or fermenting.
> - Figurative sense: something negative developing or approaching (threat, conflict, storm).
> - "A storm is brewing" = suggests an impending storm or conflict.
> - Common present participle form: "brewing" suggests ongoing development.
> - Often implies waiting or anticipation before something happens.
