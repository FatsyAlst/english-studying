## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A clumsy, stupid, or ill-mannered person; an oaf or boor; someone who behaves crudely or without social grace; a rude or brutish individual.

> [!example] Examples
> - The impudent lout challenged the experienced warrior without respect.
> - His crude behavior marked him as nothing but a lout.
> - The lout swung wildly without technique or finesse.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *lout*, of uncertain origin, possibly related to Old Norse *lútr* (stooped) or dialectal words meaning to crouch. The word has been used since the 16th century to describe crude or ill-mannered individuals.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun describing a crude or ill-mannered person
> - Often carries strong negative connotation
> - Can suggest stupidity, clumsiness, or rudeness
> - Related: loutish (adjective), loutishly (adverb), loutishness (noun)
> - More insulting than "rude person" or "boor"
> - Common in describing antagonists or crude characters
> - Emphasizes lack of manners and refinement
