## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
The quality or character of sound; the way something sounds; the pitch or timbre of a voice or musical instrument.

> [!example] Examples
> - He spoke in a harsh tone.
> - Her tone was cold and unwelcoming.
> - The violin has a beautiful tone.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
The general atmosphere or mood of something; the attitude or feeling conveyed in speech or writing.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tone of the meeting was serious.
> - The article's tone was critical and sarcastic.
> - The letter had a friendly tone.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Music)
A musical note or sound of a particular pitch; a unit of musical sound.

> [!example] Examples
> - The piano plays each tone clearly.
> - A musical scale consists of different tones.

### Sense 4 — *Verb* (Less Common)
To give a particular tone to something; to modify the tone; to strengthen muscles (tone up).

> [!example] Examples
> - She toned her remarks carefully.
> - He's trying to tone up his body.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *ton*, from Latin *tonus* (sound, pitch), from Greek *tonos* (tension, pitch). Related to "tendon" and "tennis" (all from the same root involving stretching/tension).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 & 2 most common in modern English
> - "Tone of voice" = the emotional quality of how someone speaks
> - "Set the tone" = establish the mood/atmosphere for something
> - "Tone down" = reduce the intensity or harshness of something
> - "Tone up" = strengthen (muscles) or improve
> - Important for understanding communication and emotional nuance
> - Synonyms: pitch, timbre, mood, atmosphere, attitude, manner
