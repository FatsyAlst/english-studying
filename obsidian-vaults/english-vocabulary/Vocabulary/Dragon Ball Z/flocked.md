## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — Less Common

Covered with or made of flock (soft, fluffy fibers); having a velvety or fuzzy texture produced by adhering small fibers to a surface.

> [!example] Examples
> - The toy has a flocked surface that feels soft
> - A flocked fabric covers the chair
> - The flocked texture was pleasant to touch
> - Flocked wallpaper was popular in that era

## 📜 Etymology
From "flock" (soft fibers), which comes from Old French "floc" from Latin "floccus" meaning tuft or lock of wool. The verb "flock" (to adhere fibers to a surface) developed in industrial contexts. "Flocked" is the adjective form describing something finished with this process.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Technical/material term used in manufacturing and textiles
> - Describes the process of applying small fibers to create texture
> - Common in fabric, wallpaper, and toy industries
> - Creates a soft, velvet-like appearance
> - More technical than casual usage
> - Opposite of smooth or sleek
