## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrase* (common)
A call to ready oneself for action; let's begin; time to start (originally from baseball).

> [!example] Examples
> - "Batter up!" the referee announced as the martial artists stepped into the ring.
> - Vegeta shouted "Batter up!" before engaging Goku in their next match.
> - With batter up attitude, the fighters prepared for the tournament.

## 📜 Etymology
From baseball terminology, where "batter up" signals the next player to take their position at the plate. Derived from the verb "bat" (to strike with a bat), combined with "up" indicating readiness or position.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Mainly used in sports contexts but can be extended metaphorically
> - Often used as an exclamation or interjection
> - Less common in formal or written English
