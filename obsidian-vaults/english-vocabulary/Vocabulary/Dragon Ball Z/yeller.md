## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Uncommon)

A person who yells or shouts; someone who speaks in a loud, forceful manner.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku is a natural yeller when he's in the midst of intense combat.
> - The coach was a yeller, constantly shouting instructions.
> - That character is quite the yeller during arguments.

## 📜 Etymology
"Yell" from Middle English "yellen," of uncertain origin. The "-er" suffix creates an agent noun (one who yells).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Agent noun formed with "-er" suffix
> - Can be descriptive or slightly derogatory
> - Often used to characterize someone's communication style
> - Common in informal speech
