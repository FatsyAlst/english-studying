## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A delay or interruption; something that causes a delay; a temporary obstacle

> [!example] Examples
> - What's the hold-up? Why aren't we leaving?
> - There was a hold-up in the production schedule.
> - Traffic caused a 30-minute hold-up on the highway.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A robbery, especially of a bank or store; a mugging or theft carried out with force or threat

> [!example] Examples
> - The bank reported a hold-up yesterday.
> - A hold-up occurred at the convenience store.
> - Three people were arrested for the hold-up.

## 📜 Etymology
From the phrasal verb "hold up" (to stop, to delay, or to rob at gunpoint), formed as a noun.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is common in everyday language (a delay)
> - Sense 2 specifically refers to robbery with threat or force
> - Usually hyphenated as a compound noun
> - The phrasal verb form "hold up" has multiple meanings
