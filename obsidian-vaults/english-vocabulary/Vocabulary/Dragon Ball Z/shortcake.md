## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
A light, crumbly cake, typically made with butter and flour and baked until golden; often served with fruit and whipped cream

> [!example] Examples
> - Strawberry shortcake is a classic summer dessert.
> - The bakery specializes in making delicious shortcakes.
> - We served vanilla shortcake with fresh berries.

## 📜 Etymology
From "short" (describing the texture that crumbles easily, from the high butter content) plus "cake."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often paired with fruit and whipped cream
> - The texture is crisp and crumbly due to high butter content
> - Strawberry shortcake is the most popular variety
