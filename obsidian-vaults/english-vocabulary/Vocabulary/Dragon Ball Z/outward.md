## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective/adverb* (Common)
Directed toward the outside; moving or directed away from a center or interior point

> [!example] Examples
> - The outward journey took three days.
> - The door opens outward from the building.
> - They traveled outward into the desert.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Relating to external appearance or external aspects; superficial or visible to others

> [!example] Examples
> - Despite his outward confidence, he was nervous inside.
> - The outward appearance of the building was deceiving.
> - She maintained an outward calm throughout the crisis.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "utweard," from "ut" (out) plus "-weard" (direction). Related to "toward" and "inward."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 describes physical direction away from center
> - Sense 2 describes external appearance versus internal reality
> - "Outwardly" is the adverb form for sense 2
> - "Outward" or "outwards" for direction (sense 1)
