## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A flat tool with a handle, used to propel a boat or stir things

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To propel a boat using a paddle; to move through water using a paddle

### Sense 3 — *verb* (informal)
To strike or spank with a paddle; to hit or beat

> [!example] Examples
> - He grabbed the paddle to propel the boat.
> - They paddled across the river swiftly.
> - The rival threatened to paddle him.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *padell*, possibly related to Old English *pađ* (path) or from an imitative origin describing the splashing motion of paddling.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Paddle" (with one paddle, shorter boat) vs "row" (with oars, larger boat)
> - "Paddling" in sports often refers to using hands/fingers in water
> - Informal sense: to hit or beat is less commonly used in modern English
> - Often associated with small boats or canoes
