## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A small firework that explodes loudly; figuratively, a person with a fiery, energetic, or explosive personality; someone who is spirited or temperamental.

> [!example] Examples
> - She was a true firecracker, full of energy and spirit.
> - That character is a firecracker on and off the battlefield.
> - Don't mistake her quiet demeanor—she's a real firecracker.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "fire" and "cracker." Originally referred to a type of exploding firework, extended metaphorically to describe energetic people.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal: a type of firework
> - Figurative: describes vibrant, spirited people
> - Usually complimentary when describing personality
> - Often used affectionately
> - Common in American English
