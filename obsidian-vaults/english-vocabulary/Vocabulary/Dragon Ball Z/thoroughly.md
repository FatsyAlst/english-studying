## 📖 Definition
*adverb* — (Very Frequent)

In a complete, detailed, or careful manner; with great care and attention to detail.

> [!example] Examples
> - Vegeta thoroughly examined his opponent's fighting style before attacking.
> - The martial artist trained thoroughly to master every technique.
> - We must thoroughly investigate the situation before taking action.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *thuruh* (through) + *-ly* (forming adverbs). Originally meant "all the way through" and evolved to mean "completely and carefully."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes completeness and care, not speed
> - Can modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs
> - Formal register, appropriate for academic and professional contexts
> - Often suggests methodical and careful approach
