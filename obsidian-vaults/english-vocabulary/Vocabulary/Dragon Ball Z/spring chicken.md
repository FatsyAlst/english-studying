## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun phrase* (informal)
A young or inexperienced person; a youthful individual (often used somewhat dismissively or humorously).

> [!example] Examples
> - At only twenty years old, Goku was still a spring chicken compared to the ancient masters.
> - The young warriors were mere spring chickens facing veterans like Vegeta.
> - Don't underestimate him just because he's a spring chicken in the martial arts world.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the practice of selling young, tender chickens in spring as premium poultry. The term metaphorically applies to people who are young and tender (inexperienced) or fresh, much like young birds. The phrase dates back to the 1800s in American English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Idiomatic and informal; carries a slightly humorous or dismissive tone
> - Often used to contrast youth with experience or age
> - The negative form "no spring chicken" is more common, meaning someone is no longer young
> - Can be affectionate or teasing depending on context
