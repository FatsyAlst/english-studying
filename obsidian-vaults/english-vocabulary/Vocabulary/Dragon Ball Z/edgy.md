## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (High Frequency)
Nervously tense or anxious; on edge; irritable or easily provoked.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior felt edgy before the final battle.
> - His edgy demeanor suggested he was ready for combat.
> - She seemed edgy and nervous about the upcoming tournament.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Medium Frequency)
(Of something creative or artistic) unconventional, daring, or provocative; cutting-edge or slightly transgressive.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter's edgy fighting style was unpredictable.
> - His edgy attitude made him popular with the younger crowd.

### Sense 3 — *Adjective* (Low Frequency)
Having sharp or well-defined edges or borders; on or near the edge of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The edgy blade gleamed in the sunlight.
> - An edgy cliff overlooked the valley.

## 📜 Etymology
From *edge* (sharp border or line) + *-y* (adjective suffix). The original sense referred to sharp edges; the metaphorical sense of nervous tension developed later.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most common meaning is nervous or tense
> - Can describe personality, attitude, or emotional state
> - In art/design contexts, suggests daring or unconventional
> - Often implies readiness or anticipation in action contexts
> - Related to "on edge" (nervous or tense)
