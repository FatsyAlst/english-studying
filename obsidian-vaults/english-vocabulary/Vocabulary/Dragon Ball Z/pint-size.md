## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)
Very small or diminutive; unusually short in stature; significantly smaller than average

> [!example] Examples
> - He's a pint-size boxing champion despite his small height.
> - The pint-size children were still able to reach the top shelf.
> - Don't underestimate him just because he's pint-size.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "pint" (a unit of liquid measure, much smaller than a gallon) plus "size," used metaphorically to describe something very small.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually hyphenated or written as two words
> - Used affectionately or dismissively depending on context
> - Often implies capability despite small size
