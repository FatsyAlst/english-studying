## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* — (Common)

To rip something into pieces; to damage or destroy something violently.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighters tore up the arena during their intense battle.
> - Goku's power tore up the ground beneath him.
> - The explosion tore up the entire landscape.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Tear" from Old English "teran" (to rip). "Up" is a particle indicating completion or intensity.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable phrasal verb (tear it up / tear up it)
> - Can be literal (rip paper) or figurative (damage/destroy)
> - Often implies violent or forceful action
