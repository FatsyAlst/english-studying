## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A wonderful or remarkable thing; something causing surprise, wonder, or astonishment; a marvel of engineering or nature.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku's miraculous recovery from his injuries was a true marvel of Saiyan physiology.
> - The ancient martial arts techniques were marvels of human achievement.
> - His transformation into a Super Saiyan was a marvel to behold.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To be filled with wonder or astonishment; to think with surprise and admiration.

> [!example] Examples
> - The spectators marveled at the incredible power display.
> - She marveled at his ability to master new techniques so quickly.
> - Everyone marveled at the mysterious warrior's true potential.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "merveille," derived from Latin "mirabilia" (wonderful things, from "mirabilis," meaning "wonderful" or "admirable," from "mirari," meaning "to wonder at"). The word originally meant something worthy of amazement or wonder.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used as both a noun (a marvel) and a verb (to marvel)
> - The verb form is typically followed by "at" (marvel at something)
> - Formal and somewhat literary tone; common in descriptive writing
> - Related adjective "marvelous" is more common in everyday speech
> - Conveys admiration mixed with wonder and surprise
