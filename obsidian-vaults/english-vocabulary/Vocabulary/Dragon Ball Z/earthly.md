## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Relating to the earth or world; worldly, material, or physical as opposed to spiritual or heavenly.

> [!example] Examples
> - No earthly reason could explain their sudden disappearance.
> - She had no earthly idea what he was talking about.
> - There is no earthly chance of winning against such odds.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Possible, conceivable, or feasible in the real world (often used in negative constructions to emphasize impossibility or lack).

> [!example] Examples
> - I have no earthly use for these old things.
> - There is no earthly reason to doubt his honesty.
> - Nothing on earthly could be better than this moment.

From Old English *eorþlic*, meaning 'worldly, secular,' from *eorße* (earth) + *-lic* (like). Evolved to imply practicality or impossibility by the 17th century.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in negative constructions: 'no earthly...' means 'absolutely no' or 'not the slightest.'
> - Avoid confusing with 'earthy' (coarse, natural, related to soil or sensuality; e.g., earthy humor).
> - Formal and literary tone; used in both British and American English.
> - Not to be misspelled as 'eartly.'
