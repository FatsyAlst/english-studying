## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Having an irregular, ugly, or abnormal shape; not properly or attractively formed.

> [!example] Examples
> - The creature had a misshapen body from years of battle damage.
> - His armor became misshapen from the intense attacks.
> - The misshapen rock blocked the path forward.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of prefix "mis-" (badly, wrongly, from Old English "mis-") and "shape" (from Old English "scaep"). The prefix indicates something done incorrectly or to a poor effect.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily descriptive, often with negative connotations
> - Can describe physical appearance, objects, or abstract concepts
> - Common in fantasy and action contexts to describe damage or deformity
