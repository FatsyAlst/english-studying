## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (frequent)
To eat quickly and noisily; to consume food greedily or without proper chewing.

> [!example] Examples
> - He gobbled down his meal before the fight began.
> - Goku would gobble food at an inhuman rate.
> - They gobbled up the dishes in minutes.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb, "gobble up" (figurative)* (Common)
To use up or take a large amount of something very quickly; (of a company) to take over a smaller one.

> [!example] Examples
> - Rent gobbles up half of my budget.
> - The game gobbled up 150 GB of disk space.
> - Big tech companies keep gobbling up start-ups.

## 📜 Etymology
From imitative origin, probably from "gob" (mouth, lump). The "-ble" ending is reduplicative. First used in English around 1550s to describe noisy eating behavior.
