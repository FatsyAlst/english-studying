## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (formal)
A person of importance, distinction, or high status; a notable or impressive individual

> [!example] Examples
> - He was a personage of great power and influence.
> - The mysterious personage arrived in the night.
> - A distinguished personage addressed the crowd.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *personage*, from Latin *persona* (character in a play, human being, mask). The word emphasizes the dramatic or important nature of the person being described.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary or archaic in modern usage
> - Often used for characters of power, mystery, or importance
> - Suggests dignity and significance beyond ordinary people
> - Common in fantasy, historical, or formal narratives
