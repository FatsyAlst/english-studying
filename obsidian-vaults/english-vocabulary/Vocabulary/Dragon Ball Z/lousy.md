## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)

Bad, poor quality, or unpleasant; contemptible or worthless.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a lousy attempt at a technique.
> - The weather was lousy during the tournament.
> - I have a lousy feeling about this battle.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "louse" (the parasitic insect), implying something infected or infested with lice, hence unpleasant or worthless.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational tone
> - Can describe situations, quality, or feelings
> - Often used to express dissatisfaction
> - Stronger than "okay" but less severe than some other criticisms
