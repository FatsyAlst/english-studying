## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To make or become less hard, firm, or stiff

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To make or become less harsh, severe, or intense

> [!example] Examples
> - The impact softened his resolve.
> - His expression softened when he saw his friend.
> - The intensity of the battle softened over time.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *softe* (soft) + *-en* (verb suffix). The word "soft" comes from Old English *softe*, possibly related to Germanic roots meaning comfortable or gentle.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (material becoming softer) or figurative (emotions becoming less intense)
> - Often suggests emotional change: someone's heart softens toward another person
> - "Soften the blow" = make something difficult easier to accept
