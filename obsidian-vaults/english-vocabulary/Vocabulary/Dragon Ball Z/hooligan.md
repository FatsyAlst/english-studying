## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Common)
A person, typically young, who engages in violent or destructive behavior; a rowdy or violent person; a troublemaker or gang member.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hooligans caused trouble at the sports stadium.
> - He was arrested for acting like a hooligan in public.
> - A group of hooligans vandalized the neighborhood.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain, possibly from Irish surname (Patrick Houlihan, Hooligan being an Irish surname common in 19th-century London slums). First recorded use in British English around the 1890s in reference to rowdy youth in East London.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically pejorative; carries negative connotations
> - Often refers to young troublemakers or gang members
> - Commonly used in British English, especially in sports contexts
> - Synonyms: thug, rowdy, vandal, troublemaker, delinquent, ruffian
> - Associated with violence, vandalism, and disruptive behavior
