## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb/adjective* (Common)
Breaking or fracturing; making sharp snapping sounds; showing signs of strain or failure

> [!example] Examples
> - The ice was cracking under the weight.
> - There were cracking sounds coming from the old house.
> - His voice was cracking with emotion.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Excellent, impressive, or outstanding (British informal)

> [!example] Examples
> - She's a cracking good tennis player.
> - That's a cracking idea!
> - He told some cracking jokes.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "cracian," meaning to make a sharp sound. Related to Old Norse "kraka" (raven) and possibly to crackling sounds.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 indicates physical breaking or showing strain
> - Sense 2 is primarily British slang for excellent or first-rate
> - Can describe sounds, physical objects, or performance quality
