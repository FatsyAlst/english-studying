## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common — Informal)
Something that is very easy to do.

> [!example] Examples
> - The first lab was a cinch.
> - Installing the game was a cinch.
> - Don't worry — the oral exam will be a cinch for you.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Less Common — Informal, AmE)
Something that is certain to happen; a sure thing.

> [!example] Examples
> - It's a cinch they'll win the league this year.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To tighten a belt or strap around something; to pull clothing tight at the waist; (AmE informal) to make something certain.

> [!example] Examples
> - She cinched her coat with a belt.
> - a dress cinched at the waist
> - That goal cinched the victory.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Spanish *cincha* "saddle girth" (Latin *cingula* "belt"), via 19th-century American cowboy English. A tight girth gives a secure grip, so *a cinch* came to mean "a sure thing", then "something easy".
