## 📖 Definition
*verb (past tense and past participle of "reckon")* — (Frequent)

Considered, believed, or estimated something to be true; calculated or counted something.

> [!example] Examples
> - I reckoned he was stronger than he appeared.
> - They reckoned the cost would be enormous.
> - Everyone reckoned the village was destroyed.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *recenian*, from Old Saxon *rakonian*. Originally meant "to count" or "to enumerate," evolved to mean "to consider," "to estimate," or "to believe."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily appears in past tense form "reckoned"
> - Can mean "calculated," "believed," or "considered"
> - More common in British English and American regional dialects
> - Often used with "to be" or "with" as a phrasal verb
> - Somewhat archaic in modern English but still understood
