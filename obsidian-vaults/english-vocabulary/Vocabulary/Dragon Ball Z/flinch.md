## 📖 Definition
*verb* — (Common)
To make a quick, involuntary movement backward or away from something as a reaction to pain, fear, or surprise

> [!example] Examples
> - She flinched when the doctor approached with the needle.
> - He didn't flinch despite the intense heat.
> - The loud noise made everyone flinch.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Possibly from Middle English or dialect, possibly related to Old English "flincian." The exact origin is uncertain but the word has been in use since the 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Describes a quick, automatic physical response
> - Often used to show courage (not flinching) or fear (flinching)
> - Can be used figuratively (flinch from responsibility)
