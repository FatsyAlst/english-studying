## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
A situation or story ending at a moment of suspense or uncertainty, leaving the outcome unknown until later; an unresolved ending that prompts audiences to seek resolution

> [!example] Examples
> - The show ended on a cliffhanger, leaving viewers wanting more.
> - That episode had such a dramatic cliffhanger!
> - The author left readers with a cliffhanger ending.

## 📜 Etymology
From "cliff" (a steep precipice) plus "hanger" (something hanging). Originally referred to early film serials where characters would literally be left hanging from a cliff at episode endings.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in TV series, movies, and novels
> - Used to maintain audience engagement and interest
> - Dragon Ball Z is famous for using cliffhangers
> - Creates anticipation for the next episode or chapter
