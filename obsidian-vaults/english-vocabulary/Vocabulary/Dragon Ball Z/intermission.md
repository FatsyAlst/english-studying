## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Frequent)

A pause or break in the middle of an event, performance, or activity.

> [!example] Examples
> - During the intermission of the tournament, fighters rest and prepare for their next matches.
> - The movie had a 15-minute intermission where audiences could buy snacks.
> - The band took an intermission before performing the second half of their concert.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *intermissio*, from *intermittere* meaning "to break off" or "to interrupt" (inter- "between" + mittere "to send").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in theaters, sports events, and performances
> - Provides time for rest, refreshment, or preparation
> - Distinguished from "break" which may be shorter and less formal
