## 📖 Definition
*Idiom/Adverbial Phrase* (High Frequency)

Immediately; from the very beginning; right away without delay.

> [!example] Examples
> - Right off the bat, Goku showed his incredible strength in the tournament.
> - He knew right off the bat that this opponent was dangerous.
> - The match started right off the bat with intense combat.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From baseball terminology. When a batter hits a ball, it travels "right off the bat" immediately upon contact. The phrase became an idiom meaning to do something immediately.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in American English, especially sports contexts
> - Informal and conversational
> - Can start a sentence or appear mid-sentence
> - Equivalent to "from the beginning," "immediately," or "at once"
> - Often emphasizes surprise or speed of an action
