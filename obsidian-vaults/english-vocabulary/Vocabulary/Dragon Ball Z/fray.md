## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A noisy fight or brawl; a heated conflict or combat.

> [!example] Examples
> - He joined the fray without hesitation.
> - The fray became more intense as warriors arrived.
> - They entered the fray to defend their allies.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To wear away by rubbing; to become tattered or worn.

> [!example] Examples
> - The rope began to fray from constant use.
> - His nerves were frayed from tension.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "freit," from Latin "fractus" meaning broken. Originally referred to a rout or breaking of ranks in battle.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Join the fray" is a common idiom meaning to enter a fight or competition
> - Can be used as noun (battle) or verb (wear away)
> - Carries connotations of chaos and conflict
