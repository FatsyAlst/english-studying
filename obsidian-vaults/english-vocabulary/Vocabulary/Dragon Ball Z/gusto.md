## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

Enthusiastic and energetic enjoyment or vigor in doing something.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku fought with great gusto, never tiring in battle.
> - The martial artists trained with gusto, eager to improve their skills.
> - Vegeta attacked with gusto, determined to prove his dominance.

## 📜 Etymology
From Spanish "gusto," meaning taste or pleasure, derived from Latin "gustus."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with "with" (with gusto)
> - Implies energetic and enthusiastic action
> - Conveys enjoyment alongside effort
