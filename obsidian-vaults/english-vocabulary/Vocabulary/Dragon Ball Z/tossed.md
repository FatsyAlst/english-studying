## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
Past tense of toss: To throw something lightly or casually.

> [!example] Examples
> - She tossed her jacket onto the bed.
> - He tossed the ball to me.
> - They tossed the letter aside.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
Past tense of toss: To move the head or body suddenly, especially when annoyed or restless.

> [!example] Examples
> - She tossed her head and walked off.
> - I tossed and turned all night.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
Past tense of toss: To mix food, especially salad.

> [!example] Examples
> - She tossed the salad.

### Sense 4 — *verb* (Less Common)
Past tense of toss: To be rocked or shaken violently.

> [!example] Examples
> - The boat was tossed by the waves.
