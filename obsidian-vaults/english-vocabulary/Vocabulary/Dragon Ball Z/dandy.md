## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A man who pays excessive attention to his appearance and dresses fashionably; a well-dressed man

> [!example] Examples
> - He was a dandy, always concerned with the latest fashions.
> - The dandy wore expensive suits and polished shoes.
> - He was such a dandy that he spent hours on his appearance.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Informal/Dated)
Excellent, fine, or satisfactory; very good

> [!example] Examples
> - That's just dandy!
> - Everything turned out dandy.
> - A dandy solution to the problem.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from the name "Jack Dandy" or from Scots "dandy" meaning sprightly. First appeared in late 18th century English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is historical and now often used humorously
> - Sense 2 is dated slang for "fine" or "excellent"
> - The adjective form is less common today
