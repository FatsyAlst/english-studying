## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Lacking the power or will to move or act; passive, sluggish, or immobilized; chemically inactive or unreactive.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior lay inert on the ground after the blast.
> - An inert gas cannot react with other substances.
> - His body remained inert despite the injuries.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "iners" (inactive, sluggish), from "in-" (not) + "ars" (art, skill). Originally meant lacking skill or activity, now applied to physical and chemical inactivity.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Suggests complete lack of movement or reactivity
> - More formal than "still" or "motionless"
> - Used in both scientific and dramatic contexts
