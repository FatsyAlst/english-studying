## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A sudden burst of activity or violent behavior; a period of unrestrained indulgence

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain went on a destructive spree through the city.
> - A killing spree left the village in ruins.
> - He embarked on a shopping spree after earning his prize money.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Scottish *spreach* or *spreath* meaning cattle raid or plunder. First used in English in early 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often paired with an adjective describing the type of spree
> - Typically suggests rapid, often chaotic or destructive activity
> - Can be used both negatively and casually (shopping spree)
> - 📺 Also encountered in [[Mushoku Tensei]]
