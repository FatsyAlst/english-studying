## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Frequent)

A heavily fortified, protected military stronghold or building designed for defense.

> [!example] Examples
> - The evil king's fortress was protected by powerful energy barriers.
> - The ancient fortress stood on top of the mountain.
> - Warriors must breach the fortress walls to reach their enemy.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *forteresse*, from Latin *fortis* meaning "strong." Originally referred to any strongly built and defended position, evolved to mean a specifically military stronghold.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies both structural strength and military purpose
> - Often used in fantasy, historical, or action contexts
> - Suggests a place of power, protection, and authority
> - Larger and more militaristic than "fort" or "castle"
