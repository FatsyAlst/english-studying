## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A person, especially a child, who is small in stature. Often used affectionately or dismissively to refer to someone shorter than average.

> [!example] Examples
> - Krillin is a half-pint compared to Goku's height
> - Don't underestimate him just because he's a half-pint
> - That kid is such a half-pint but don't let size fool you

## 🔗 Collocations
- little half-pint
- pint-sized (similar meaning)
- kid/child + half-pint

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "half" + "pint" (a unit of liquid measure), suggesting a person is as small as half a pint. Used metaphorically since the 1920s to describe small people.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used affectionately or insultingly depending on context and tone
> - More common in informal/casual speech
> - Often used about children or significantly shorter adults
