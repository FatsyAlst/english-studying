## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Informal)
A person who is mean, unkind, or cruel; a person who behaves in a spiteful or hurtful manner. Often used as a playful or derogatory term.

> [!example] Examples
> - "You're such a meanie for not sharing!"
> - He called the villain a meanie.
> - Don't be a meanie to your friends.

## 📜 Etymology
From "mean" (unkind, malicious) + "-ie" (diminutive or informal suffix). Developed in informal English, particularly in children's speech, as a playful way to refer to unkind people.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and often playful or mocking tone
> - Commonly used by children or in informal/casual contexts
> - Not standard for formal writing or serious contexts
> - Can be used affectionately or accusingly depending on tone
> - Synonyms: bully, unkind person, cruel person, jerk, monster
> - Less severe than "villain" but similar meaning
> - Often used in a lighthearted or exaggerated way
