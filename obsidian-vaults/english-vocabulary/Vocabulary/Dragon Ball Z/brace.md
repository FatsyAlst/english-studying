## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (frequent)
To prepare oneself mentally or physically for something difficult or unpleasant; to steady oneself

> [!example] Examples
> - He braced himself for the incoming attack
> - Brace yourself for impact!
> - She braced her feet against the ground
> - They braced for the massive explosion

### Sense 2 — *verb* (frequent)
To provide structural support or reinforcement; to make stable

> [!example] Examples
> - He braced the door shut with a bar
> - The beams braced the collapsing structure
> - She braced her body against the wall

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "brace" meaning arms or two things together, derived from Latin "bracchia" meaning arms. Originally referred to things that came in pairs; extended to mean structural supports and mental preparation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Brace yourself" is an idiomatic expression meaning to prepare mentally
> - Can describe both physical stance (stabilizing) and mental preparation
> - Often used in action contexts to show characters preparing for danger
> - Common imperative form in urgent situations
