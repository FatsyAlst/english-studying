## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb/adjective* (common)
To beat or pulsate rhythmically with a steady, pounding motion; experiencing a dull, persistent pain.

> [!example] Examples
> - His muscles were throbbing after the intense battle.
> - Goku felt a throbbing sensation in his ribs where Vegeta had struck him.
> - The energy from the blast left his arm throbbing with pain.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "throbbe," likely of imitative origin, reflecting the sound and rhythm of a persistent beating or pulsing. The word mimics the rhythmic quality of the sensation it describes.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically used to describe dull, persistent pain rather than sharp pain
> - Often accompanies descriptions of injuries or fatigue
> - Can also describe rhythmic beating sounds (heartbeat, music)
> - Creates a vivid, sensory image of persistent pulsation
