## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (High Frequency)
A large hole in the ground; an arena or depression where contests or battles take place.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighters entered the combat pit for their match.
> - The pit was surrounded by eager spectators.
> - They fought in a deep pit carved into the rock.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Medium Frequency)
The hard seed of certain fruits; the stone in fruit like peaches or plums.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fruit had a large pit in the center.
> - Remove the pit before eating the cherry.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Medium Frequency)
To set someone or something in opposition or competition; to remove the pit from fruit.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament pits warriors against each other.
> - Pit the olives before adding them to the dish.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *pytt*, from Latin *puteus* (well, pit). Related to Germanic languages. The "fruit seed" meaning developed from the shape and function of the pit.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Commonly used in action contexts to describe fighting arenas
> - "Pit against" is a phrasal verb meaning to oppose
> - Can be used metaphorically for difficult situations
