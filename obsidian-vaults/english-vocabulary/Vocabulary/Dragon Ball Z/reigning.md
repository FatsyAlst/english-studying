## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)

Currently holding a title or position; the present champion or ruler; dominant at the present time.

> [!example] Examples
> - The reigning champion faced a new challenger.
> - The reigning martial arts master defended his title.
> - The reigning king of all saiyans was dethroned.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "regner," from Latin "regnare" (to rule). The "-ing" form describes a current, ongoing state.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically used with titles or positions
> - Implies current status at the time of speaking
> - Common in sports, competitions, and titles
> - Can be literal (ruling) or figurative (dominance)
