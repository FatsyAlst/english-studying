## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A sport or physical contest in which two people grapple and try to throw or force each other to the ground; a form of hand-to-hand combat using holds and throws rather than strikes.

> [!example] Examples
> - The martial arts tournament included wrestling matches.
> - Goku had experience with wrestling techniques from his training.
> - Wrestling was one of the key skills in the tournament.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "wræstlung," from "wræstlian" (to wrestle), possibly related to "wrist."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can refer to the sport or the activity itself
> - Often involves grappling, holds, and throws
> - Different styles exist (Greco-Roman, freestyle, professional)
> - Used literally for the sport and figuratively for struggling
