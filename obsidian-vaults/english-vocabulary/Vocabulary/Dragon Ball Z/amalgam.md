## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A mixture or blend of different elements, parts, or things combined together.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior's fighting style was an amalgam of different techniques.
> - The army was an amalgam of soldiers from various lands.
> - His power was an amalgam of his predecessors' abilities.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
In chemistry/dentistry, an alloy or mixture of mercury with another metal.

> [!example] Examples
> - The ancient metal was an amalgam of rare elements.
> - Dental amalgam was commonly used for fillings.
> - The artifact contained an unusual amalgam of metals.

## 📜 Etymology
From Medieval Latin "amalgama," from Arabic "al-malgham" (soft mass, amalgam). Originally referred specifically to a mercury alloy, but later extended to mean any mixture or combination of different elements.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "mixture" or "blend"
> - Often used when discussing combinations of diverse or disparate elements
> - Sense 1 is most common in general usage
> - Sense 2 is technical and primarily historical in modern usage
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[concoction]] (from [[Dragon Ball Super]])
