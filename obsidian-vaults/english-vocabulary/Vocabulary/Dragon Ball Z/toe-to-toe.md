## 📖 Definition
*adverbial phrase* — (Common)

In direct confrontation or competition; facing each other closely; at very close range, often referring to fighting or arguing face-to-face.

> [!example] Examples
> - The two fighters went toe-to-toe in an intense battle.
> - They stood toe-to-toe, neither backing down.
> - The rivals faced off toe-to-toe in the tournament.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Literal image of two boxers standing very close together, with their toes nearly touching, ready for combat.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Hyphenated when used as adjective ("toe-to-toe battle")
> - Can be unhyphenated when used in phrases ("go toe to toe")
> - Implies close, direct confrontation
> - Common in sports and combat contexts
> - Often suggests equal competition
