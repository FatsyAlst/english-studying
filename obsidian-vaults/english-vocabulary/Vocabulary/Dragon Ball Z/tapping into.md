## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* (Common)
To tap into something means to connect to or draw upon a resource, supply, or source of power/knowledge/ability; to gain access to something for use.

> [!example] Examples
> - He tapped into an inner power he didn't know he had.
> - They tapped into the energy grid to power the city.
> - She tapped into her experience to solve the problem.

## 📜 Etymology
"Tap" comes from Old English *tæppa* (a faucet or spigot), referring to literally opening a connection to a supply. "Into" adds the sense of directing toward or accessing. The phrasal verb developed from the literal meaning of tapping into a barrel or pipe.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Originally literal (tapping into a water/gas/electrical supply)
> - Extended to figurative uses (tapping into emotions, potential, knowledge)
> - Common in fantasy/sci-fi contexts (tapping into magical power, inner power, etc.)
> - Synonyms: draw on, access, utilize, harness, employ, connect to
> - Conveys the idea of unlocking or discovering something previously unavailable
> - Frequently used in character development and action narratives
