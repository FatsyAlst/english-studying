## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To steal goods from a place or person, especially during war or conflict; to rob or pillage.

> [!example] Examples
> - The invaders plundered the city for treasure.
> - Pirates plundered merchant ships.
> - The soldiers plundered the village for supplies.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
Goods stolen or taken forcibly from a place; spoils of war.

> [!example] Examples
> - The plunder was divided among the soldiers.
> - They collected the plunder and left.
> - The museum recovered some of the plunder from the war.

## 📜 Etymology
From Dutch plunderen or German plündern, from Dutch plunje (bedding, clothing).

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often associated with warfare, piracy, or crime
> - More dramatic than simple "steal" or "rob"
> - Common in historical and fantasy contexts
