## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (very frequent)
To destroy completely; to eliminate or wipe out entirely

> [!example] Examples
> - The blast annihilated the entire building
> - His power annihilated the enemy forces
> - The attack annihilated everything in its path
> - They annihilated their opponents in battle

### Sense 2 — *verb* (physics)
To cause matter and antimatter to combine, resulting in energy release; to eliminate completely through particle collision

> [!example] Examples
> - Particles annihilated in the reaction
> - Matter and antimatter annihilated each other

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "annihilare" combining "ad-" (to) and "nihil" (nothing), literally meaning to reduce to nothing. The word entered English in the 16th century meaning to destroy completely.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very strong verb expressing total destruction
> - Common in action and combat descriptions
> - Often used for large-scale destruction or defeating many opponents
> - Emphasizes complete elimination rather than mere damage
> - Frequent in Dragon Ball Z's intense battle scenes
