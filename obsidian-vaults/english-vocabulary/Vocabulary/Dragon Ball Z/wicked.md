## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Evil, immoral, or morally wrong; sinful or unethical

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (slang)
Excellent, impressive, or cool; awesome or amazing

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (common)
Playful, mischievous, or teasing in nature; impish

> [!example] Examples
> - The wicked villain plotted his revenge.
> - That move was absolutely wicked!
> - He had a wicked grin on his face.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *wicked*, from *wick* (to move, turn, behave) + *-ed* (past participle suffix). Later associated with *wicca* (witch) and concepts of wrongdoing. The slang sense emerged in late 20th century, possibly from African American Vernacular English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Formal/literary moral condemnation
> - Sense 2: Slang usage, very positive (originated in 1980s-90s)
> - Sense 3: Suggests mischief without true malice
> - "Wicked" in slang is often intensified: "wicked good," "wicked awesome"
