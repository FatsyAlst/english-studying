## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Muscular strength or power, particularly physical muscle mass; the physical substance or meat of a body.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku's beef was visible in his well-defined muscular physique.
> - The Saiyan warriors displayed enormous beef and power.
> - Years of training added serious beef to his frame.

### Sense 2 — *noun/informal* (common)
A complaint, grievance, or disagreement; a source of conflict or dispute.

> [!example] Examples
> - Vegeta had a beef with Goku over their rivalry for supremacy.
> - What's your beef with him? Why are you so angry?
> - The two warriors settled their beef through combat.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb: "beef up"* (informal)
To strengthen, reinforce, or increase the power or intensity of something; to make more robust or muscular.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior beefed up his training regimen to prepare for the tournament.
> - Goku needed to beef up his defenses against Frieza's attacks.
> - The team beefed up security around the martial arts venue.

## 📜 Etymology
Sense 1 derives from Old French "buef" (ox or beef meat), from Latin "bos" (bovine). Sense 2 (complaint) is modern slang with uncertain origin, possibly related to the physical strength needed to resolve conflicts, or borrowing from the strength/substance meaning. "Beef up" is a phrasal verb derived from these meanings.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (muscular strength) is slang but widely understood in fitness contexts
> - Sense 2 (complaint) is informal/slang, common in casual speech and arguments
> - Sense 3 (beef up) is common in both formal and informal contexts
> - The term "beef" for complaint is primarily North American English
> - Can convey both literal physical mass and figurative strength/substance
