## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *preposition* (frequent)
On the top of something; positioned above or on top

> [!example] Examples
> - He stood atop the mountain
> - Atop the building, he surveyed the city
> - The statue stood atop a tall pedestal
> - The warrior landed atop the cliff
> - Sitting atop his throne

### Sense 2 — *adverb* (less frequent)
On or at the top; above

> [!example] Examples
> - He climbed atop
> - The flag flew atop the fortress

## 📜 Etymology
Derived from "a-" (on) + "top." The "a-" is a prefix meaning "on" or "at," while "top" comes from Old English "topp" meaning a peak or summit. The word entered English usage in the 17th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary preposition
> - Often used in descriptive passages to indicate elevation or prominence
> - Common in fantasy and action narratives
> - Creates a sense of dominance or high position
> - More formal than "on top of"
