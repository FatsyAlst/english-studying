## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective* (Common)
Inclined or willing to do something; having a particular tendency or mental attitude (usually in the phrase "disposed to" or "disposed toward").

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm not disposed to accept his excuses.
> - He was disposed toward helping others.
> - The committee was disposed to approve the proposal.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Less Common)
Arranged or placed in a particular order or manner; settled or arranged.

> [!example] Examples
> - The troops were disposed strategically across the terrain.
> - The furniture was disposed in the room.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *disposer*, from Latin *disponere* (to arrange, distribute). Related to "disposition" (arrangement, mood, temperament).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most common in the phrase "disposed to" meaning "inclined to" or "willing to"
> - "Well-disposed toward" = having favorable feelings toward
> - "Ill-disposed toward" = having negative feelings toward
> - Formal or literary in tone
> - Can also mean physically arranged or placed (less common in modern usage)
> - Synonyms: inclined, willing, prone, apt, ready
