## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

Something granted or admitted, often unwillingly; an acceptance of defeat or acknowledgment of someone else's point; a compromise made to reach agreement.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter made a concession and accepted defeat.
> - After a long negotiation, both sides made concessions.
> - His concession of the match surprised everyone.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "concession," from Latin "concessio," meaning "a granting" or "yielding," from "concedere" (to yield, allow).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies something given up reluctantly
> - Can be a single item or multiple items ("concessions")
> - Used in negotiations, competitions, and agreements
> - Related to "concede" (to admit or yield)
> - Common in business, politics, and sports
