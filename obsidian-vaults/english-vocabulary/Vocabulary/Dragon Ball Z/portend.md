## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (Medium Frequency)

To be a sign or warning of something that will happen in the future; to foreshadow or indicate.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dark clouds portend an approaching storm.
> - Strange energy readings portend the arrival of a powerful enemy.
> - These events portend great danger ahead.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *portendere* (to stretch forward, indicate), from *pro-* (forward) + *tendere* (to stretch). Related to "portent" (an omen or sign).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal and literary than "predict" or "suggest"
> - Often used to describe omens or warning signs
> - Frequently appears in dramatic or suspenseful contexts
> - The subject is usually something that appears as a sign, not a person
