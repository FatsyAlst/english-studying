## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common) — _formal_
Completely unreasonable or ridiculous, especially in a way that shocks or annoys; absurd, outrageous.

> [!example] Examples
> - These claims are absolutely preposterous!
> - It's preposterous to suggest that everything was her fault.
> - The whole idea sounds quite preposterous.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Less Common) — _formal_
Unusual in a silly or shocking way (often about clothes, behaviour, prices, etc.).

> [!example] Examples
> - The band were famous for their preposterous clothes and haircuts.
> - Ticket prices are absolutely preposterous.

---
Mid-16th century, from Latin *praeposterus* 'reversed, absurd' (*prae* 'before' + *post(er)us* 'coming after'), originally 'with the order reversed, back-to-front' (like 'putting the cart before the horse'), then generalized to 'absurd, ridiculous.'

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Strong, emphatic word; often used to reject something as totally absurd or beyond reason, not just 'a bit silly.'
> - Common in spoken reactions ('That's preposterous!').
> - Similar: absurd, ridiculous, ludicrous; opposite: reasonable, sensible, logical.
