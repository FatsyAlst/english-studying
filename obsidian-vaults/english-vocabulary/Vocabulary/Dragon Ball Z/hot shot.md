## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
An important, successful, or skillful person; often used sarcastically or disparagingly to refer to someone who is arrogant or overconfident.

> [!example] Examples
> - That hot shot thinks he can defeat me easily.
> - Just because you're a hot shot doesn't mean you'll win.
> - He's acting like a hot shot after one victory.

## 📜 Etymology
American English slang from the early 20th century. "Hot" meaning impressive or successful + "shot" meaning person. Originally neutral but evolved to carry sarcastic or contemptuous undertones.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically used with sarcasm or mockery
> - Often implies arrogance or inflated self-importance
> - Conversational and informal tone
