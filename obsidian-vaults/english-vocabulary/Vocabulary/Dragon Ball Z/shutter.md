## 📖 Definition
*noun/verb* — (Common)
1. A hinged panel or covering that opens and closes to control light, ventilation, or access; 2. To close or cause to close suddenly

> [!example] Examples
> - The wooden shutters rattled in the strong wind.
> - She shut the window shutters before the storm arrived.
> - He shuttered the shop for the night.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "shutter," related to "shut" (to close), with the agent suffix "-er" indicating something that shuts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be used literally (physical shutters on windows) or figuratively (to shutter a business = to close it permanently)
> - The verb form often implies a sudden or forceful closing
