## 📖 Definition
*verb* (present participle/gerund) — (Frequent)

To fight with the fists; to exchange blows in combat, often used in the phrase "duking it out."

> [!example] Examples
> - The two fighters were duking it out in the arena.
> - Goku and Vegeta spent hours duking it out with all their power.
> - The warriors are duking it out to determine the winner.

## 📜 Etymology
From British slang *duke* (from Cockney rhyming slang "Duke of Yorks" for "forks," referring to fists). Later evolved to mean "hand" in boxing slang, then the verb "to box" or "to fight with fists."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and colloquial register
> - Primarily used in the phrase "duking it out"
> - Emphasizes physical combat with fists rather than weapons
> - Common in sports, boxing, and action narratives
> - More lighthearted than formal combat terminology
> - Often implies evenly matched opponents
