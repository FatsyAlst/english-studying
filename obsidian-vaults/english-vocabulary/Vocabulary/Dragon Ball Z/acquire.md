## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To obtain or come to have possession of something, especially through effort or purchase

> [!example] Examples
> - He acquired new powers during his training.
> - The warrior acquired a legendary sword.
> - Through determination, she acquired the skills needed to fight.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *acquirere*, from *ad-* (to) + *quirere* (to seek). Related to "query" and "require".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "get"; suggests deliberate obtaining
> - Can refer to obtaining objects, skills, knowledge, or properties
> - Often implies a process or effort involved
