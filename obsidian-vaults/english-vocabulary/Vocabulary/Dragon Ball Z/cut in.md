## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To interrupt someone who is speaking; to break into a conversation.

> [!example] Examples
> - He cut in with a sharp remark.
> - Don't cut in while they're talking.
> - She cut in to ask an important question.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To move ahead of someone in a line or queue without waiting one's turn.

> [!example] Examples
> - Someone cut in ahead of me at the store.
> - Don't cut in on people who are waiting.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To share or participate in something; to get involved.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can I cut in on this dance?
> - He wanted to cut in on the deal.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "cut" (to interrupt) and "in" (to position oneself within). The combination suggests suddenly inserting oneself into a situation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can have negative connotations (rude interruption)
> - Often followed by prepositions like "on" or "with"
> - Common in everyday speech
