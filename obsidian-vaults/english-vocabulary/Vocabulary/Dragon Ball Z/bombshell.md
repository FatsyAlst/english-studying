## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Frequent)
A surprising and usually shocking piece of news or information; a revelation that has major impact.

> [!example] Examples
> - The revelation was a bombshell that shocked everyone at the tournament.
> - Dropping such a bombshell during the broadcast surprised the viewers.
> - The political bombshell dominated the news cycle.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Frequent)
A large explosive bomb or artillery shell.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fortress was destroyed by powerful bombshells from the enemy artillery.
> - Historical bombshells littered the battlefield.
> - The explosion of the bombshell left a massive crater.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Informal)
A very attractive woman, typically used as a compliment.

> [!example] Examples
> - She was called a blonde bombshell in the entertainment industry.
> - The actress was labeled a bombshell by the media.
> - He was captivated by the bombshell in the room.

## 📜 Etymology
From *bomb* + *shell*. Originally referred to the literal explosive shell of a bomb. The metaphorical use for "shocking news" emerged in the 20th century, comparing the impact of shocking information to the explosion of a bomb.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is most common in modern usage, emphasizing impact
> - Sense 2 is historical or military context
> - Sense 3 is informal, sometimes considered dated or objectifying
> - "Drop a bombshell" is a common idiom meaning to reveal shocking information
> - Conveys suddenness and significant impact
