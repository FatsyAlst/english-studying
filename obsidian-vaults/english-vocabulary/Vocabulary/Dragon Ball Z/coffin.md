## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Medium Frequency)

A box in which a dead body is placed for burial or cremation; a casket; something that resembles a coffin in shape.

> [!example] Examples
> - The defeated warrior was placed in a coffin for his funeral.
> - The narrow space felt like a coffin, trapping him inside.
> - They built an elaborate coffin from the finest wood.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *cofin* (small box, container), from Latin *cophinus* (basket, chest), from Greek *kophinos* (basket). The connection to death became prominent in English usage.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal term for a burial container
> - "Nail in the coffin" is an idiom meaning something that causes failure
> - Can be used metaphorically for anything confining or tomb-like
> - Often appears in solemn or dramatic contexts
> - Related term: casket (American English more commonly uses casket)
