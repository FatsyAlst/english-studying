## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To tell a secret or private matter to someone you trust not to repeat it to others.

> [!example] Examples
> - She confided in her best friend about the breakup.
> - He confided that he was unhappy in his job.
> - Can I confide something to you?

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To place trust or confidence in someone or something; to rely on.

> [!example] Examples
> - We confide in our doctors for medical advice.
> - I confide in your judgment.
> - You can confide in me with anything.

---
Late Middle English (originally 'place trust in'); from Latin *confīdere* 'trust fully' (*con-* 'together' + *fīdere* 'trust'). Secret-sharing sense from mid-18th c.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary; 'confide in' for sharing secrets with someone you trust.
> - Intransitive: 'confide in someone' = share a secret with them.
> - Transitive: 'confide something to someone' = tell them a secret.
> - Related words: confidence (noun, trust), confidant (noun, person confided in).
