## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A coin in the United States and Canada worth five cents.

> [!example] Examples
> - He dropped a nickel in the fountain for good luck.
> - The old man collected nickels from around the world.
> - A nickel isn't worth much in today's economy.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A hard, silvery-white metal element used in alloys and plating.

> [!example] Examples
> - The armor was reinforced with nickel plating.
> - Nickel is often added to steel to improve its strength.
> - The weapon contained trace amounts of nickel.

## 📜 Etymology
From German "Nickel," a name given to the ore by miners (from German "kupfernickel" meaning "false copper"). The name "Old Nick" (the devil) was applied humorously to the deceptive ore. Entered English in the 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (coin) is most common in everyday usage
> - Sense 2 (metal) is technical and industrial
> - Nickel was historically one of the standard U.S. coins
> - The metal is valued for its hardness and corrosion resistance
