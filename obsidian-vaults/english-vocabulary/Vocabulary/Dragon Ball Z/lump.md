## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A compact mass of something; a hard or solid piece or chunk; a swelling or protuberance; to endure or tolerate something unpleasant.

> [!example] Examples
> - A lump of hardened energy formed in his chest.
> - The impact created a large lump where it struck the ground.
> - He had to lump it and accept the painful defeat.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To put or treat different things together as one group; to tolerate or accept something unpleasant; to move heavily or awkwardly.

> [!example] Examples
> - Lumped together as a single warrior class.
> - If you don't like it, you'll have to lump it.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *lumpe*, of uncertain origin. Possibly related to Old Norse *lumpr* (lumpfish) or Dutch *lomp*. Used since the 14th century to describe compact masses of material.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: a compact, solid mass
> - Verb: to group together or to tolerate
> - "Lump it" is an idiomatic expression meaning to accept something unwillingly
> - Related: lumpy (adjective), lumpiness (noun)
> - Can be literal (physical lump) or figurative (grouping things)
> - Common in informal speech
