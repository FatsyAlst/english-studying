## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb: Snap Out* — (Common)
To regain consciousness or composure; to suddenly return to a normal state of mind from confusion, sleep, or distraction.

> [!example] Examples
> - She tried to snap out of her daze.
> - He snapped out of the mind control when his friends called.
> - Can you snap out of it and focus on the fight?

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "snap" (to break suddenly, to make a quick sound) + "out" (away from). The combination creates an image of suddenly breaking free from something holding you mentally or physically.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually followed by "of" and a condition (trance, daze, shock)
> - "Snap out of it" is an idiomatic command or suggestion
> - Implies a sudden, sharp return to normal
> - Often used in contexts of hypnosis, sleep, or mental confusion
> - Can be literal (waking up) or figurative (mental state)
> - Very fitting for anime scenes with mind control or confusion
