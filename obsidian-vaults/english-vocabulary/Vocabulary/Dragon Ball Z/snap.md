## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To break suddenly with a cracking sound; to crack or break sharply.

> [!example] Examples
> - The wooden staff snapped under the force of Goku's strike.
> - The villain's neck snapped from the final blow.
> - The rope snapped as they pulled with all their strength.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To speak or respond in a sharp, brusque, or angry manner.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Don't interrupt!" Vegeta snapped at his opponent.
> - She snapped back at him without hesitation.
> - He snapped an order at his subordinates.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (common)
To move quickly or suddenly; to snap one's fingers or position one's body sharply.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku snapped into fighting stance immediately.
> - He snapped his fingers to signal the start of combat.
> - The fighter snapped back to alertness after a brief distraction.

### Sense 4 — *adjective* (Common)
Done or decided suddenly, without careful thought.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't make a snap decision about your major.
> - It was a snap judgement and I regret it.

### Sense 5 — *noun / verb* (Common — Informal)
A photograph, especially an informal one (BrE *holiday snaps*); *to snap* = to take a photo quickly.

> [!example] Examples
> - She showed us her holiday snaps.
> - He snapped a quick picture of the whiteboard.

### Sense 6 — *noun* (Less Common — Informal, AmE)
*It's a snap* = it's very easy.

> [!example] Examples
> - Setting up the router was a snap.

## 📜 Etymology
Middle English "snappen," likely of Germanic origin, related to Old Norse "snapa" (to rebuke or rebuff). The onomatopoetic quality of the word reflects the sound of breaking or cracking.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Multiple meanings: physical breaking (Sense 1), speaking sharply (Sense 2), sudden movement (Sense 3)
> - Sense 1 is most literal; Senses 2 and 3 are more figurative
> - Often used with prepositions: "snap at," "snap back," "snap in"
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[cinch]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
> - 📺 Also encountered in [[Dr. Stone]]
