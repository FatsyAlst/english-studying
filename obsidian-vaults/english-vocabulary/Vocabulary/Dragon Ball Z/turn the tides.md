## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb phrase* (common)
To reverse the course of events; to change a situation from unfavorable to favorable, or to gain the upper hand when previously losing.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku managed to turn the tides with his new power.
> - The warriors turned the tides by working together.
> - One decisive victory turned the tides of war.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Derived from nautical imagery where ocean tides could change the course of a journey. Metaphorically extended to mean reversing the course of any situation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in battle or competitive contexts
> - Can be used with "in someone's favor" for clarity
> - Similar to "turn the tables" but more dramatic in tone
