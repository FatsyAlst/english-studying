## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To gradually weaken or deplete someone's strength, energy, or resources; to drain progressively; to undermine.

> [!example] Examples
> - The continuous battle saps his remaining energy reserves.
> - Fear saps the warrior's confidence and determination.
> - Repeated attacks sap the opponent's power level.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Less Common)
Plural of sap; the vital fluid or liquids within plant tissues; foolish or gullible person.

> [!example] Examples
> - The trees lost their saps during the harsh winter.
> - Crystalline saps dripped from the damaged vegetation.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *sappe*, from Old English *sæp*, related to Old Saxon and Old High German *sap*. Originally referred to the fluid in plants. The metaphorical meaning (to weaken) developed later, suggesting the draining of vital fluids.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb form commonly used in modern English
> - Emphasizes gradual, progressive weakening
> - Often used with abstract concepts (energy, morale, confidence)
> - Can be literal (plant sap) or metaphorical (draining strength)
> - Past tense: sapped
> - Related: sappy (containing sap or overly emotional)
