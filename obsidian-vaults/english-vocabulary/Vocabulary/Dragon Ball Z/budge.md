## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To move slightly, or to make something heavy move slightly (mostly in negatives).

> [!example] Examples
> - The door was stuck and wouldn't budge.
> - I pushed the car, but it didn't budge an inch.
> - The queue hasn't budged in twenty minutes.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To change your opinion or decision (mostly in negatives).

> [!example] Examples
> - The professor won't budge on the deadline.
> - I tried to convince him, but he wouldn't budge.

### Sense 3 — *phrasal verb, "budge up / over"* (Less Common — Informal, BrE)
To move along a seat to make space for someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - Budge up so I can sit down.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French *bouger* "to move", from Vulgar Latin *bullicare "to boil, bubble".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Mostly negative: *won't budge*, *didn't budge*.
> - Figurative: refusing to change your mind or compromise.
> - Don't confuse with *budget*!
