## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Arrogantly confident; overly proud of one's abilities; showing excessive self-confidence.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was too cocky to admit his mistake.
> - Don't be cocky just because you won once.
> - The cocky athlete underestimated his opponent.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From cock (related to rooster) + -y. Roosters are known for their proud, assertive behavior.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Definitely negative connotation
> - Often leads to downfall or defeat in stories
> - Similar to "arrogant" but more informal
> - Common in competitive or combat contexts
