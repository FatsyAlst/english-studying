## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The amount that can be held in one hand; a small quantity.

> [!example] Examples
> - A handful of nuts
> - She grabbed a handful of sand.
> - Add a handful of salt to the water.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (informal)
A person or group that is difficult to manage or control.

> [!example] Examples
> - Those kids are quite a handful!
> - He's a real handful when he gets excited.
> - Managing that crew was a handful.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English handfull, from hand + full. A compound of "hand" and "full."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used to describe small amounts that can fit in one's hand
> - Also used figuratively for difficult people or situations
> - Plural can be either "handfuls" or "handsfull" (though handfuls is more standard)
