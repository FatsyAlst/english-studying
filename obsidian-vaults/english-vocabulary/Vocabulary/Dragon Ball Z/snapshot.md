## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A photograph taken quickly without formal preparation; figuratively, a brief moment in time or a quick look at something.

> [!example] Examples
> - Take a snapshot of the powerful technique.
> - This moment is just a snapshot of the larger battle.
> - The tournament gave a snapshot of martial arts skill in the world.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "snap" (quick action) and "shot" (photograph), popularized with the introduction of handheld cameras in the late 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal: informal photograph
> - Figurative: brief glimpse or moment in time
> - Often implies quick, unposed quality
> - Used in photography and metaphorical contexts
