## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Most Common)
A solid substance used for polishing, making candles, or other purposes; typically made from petroleum, beeswax, or plant sources.

> [!example] Examples
> - She used wax to polish the floor
> - The candle is made of wax
> - Wax provides a protective coating
> - Beeswax was used historically

---
### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To cover, polish, or treat with wax; to apply a wax coating to something.

> [!example] Examples
> - Wax the wooden furniture to protect it
> - He waxes the floor every month
> - Wax your surfboard before going out
> - The workers are waxing the hardwood

---
### Sense 3 — *Verb (metaphorical)* (Less Common)
To remove unwanted hair by applying hot wax; to grow increasingly (of the moon); to speak or write at length.

> [!example] Examples
> - She went to wax her legs
> - The moon waxes and wanes
> - He waxed poetic about the battle
> - Don't wax philosophical about it

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "weax" and Germanic origins (similar to German "Wachs", Dutch "was"). Related to Old Norse "vax". The word has been used for thousands of years to describe this substance. "Wax poetic" developed as a metaphorical extension in the 19th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: a solid substance with various applications
> - Verb: to apply wax, to remove hair, to grow (moon), or to speak lengthily
> - "Wax poetic" = to speak eloquently or at length about something
> - "Wax and wane" = cycle of increasing and decreasing
> - Common in household maintenance, beauty, and literary contexts
> - The moon "waxes" (grows) and "wanes" (shrinks)
