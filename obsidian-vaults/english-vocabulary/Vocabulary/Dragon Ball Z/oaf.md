## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Medium Frequency)

A stupid or clumsy person; a large, ungainly person who lacks grace or intelligence.

> [!example] Examples
> - He called his opponent a clumsy oaf for falling during the fight.
> - The oaf stumbled over his own feet trying to dodge the attack.
> - Don't be such an oaf and pay attention to what I'm saying.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *álfar* (elves, but also used for changed children or dwarves believed to be dim-witted). The meaning evolved from mythological references to foolish creatures.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and often derogatory
> - Implies both physical clumsiness and lack of intelligence
> - Can be used affectionately in some contexts
> - Common in casual insults or character descriptions
> - Often used to describe clumsy fighters or foolish opponents
