## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Medium Frequency)

Abnormally thin and bony; extremely thin with very little muscle or fat; skinny and appearing unhealthy or malnourished.

> [!example] Examples
> - The young fighter looked scrawny compared to his muscular opponent.
> - He started out scrawny but developed incredible strength through training.
> - The scrawny village boy surprised everyone with his hidden power.

## 📜 Etymology
From dialectal English *scrawny*, possibly related to Scandinavian words meaning "thin" or "meager." The exact origin is uncertain but may be connected to Old Norse roots.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More derogatory than "thin" or "skinny"
> - Often implies weakness or malnourishment
> - Common in character descriptions or comparisons
> - Can be used affectionately in some contexts
> - Frequently appears in action stories to contrast appearance with actual strength
