## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common, informal)
To interrupt or intrude in a conversation or situation without being invited; to interfere.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't butt in while we're talking.
> - He butted in with his own opinion.
> - She butted in to defend her friend.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common, informal)
To involve oneself in something without permission; to meddle.

> [!example] Examples
> - Stop butting in on my business.
> - They're always butting in where they're not welcome.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "butt" (to push with horns) and "in" (to position oneself within). The combination suggests forcefully inserting oneself into a situation, similar to how a goat butts with its head.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More informal and slightly ruder than "cut in"
> - Often considered impolite behavior
> - Common in casual, everyday speech
