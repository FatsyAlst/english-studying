## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (gerund/participle)* (common)
Moving back and forth or up and down in short, quick movements

> [!example] Examples
> - The tail was wagging excitedly.
> - He was wagging his finger disapprovingly.
> - The creature's antenna were wagging frantically.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *wagian* meaning to shake or move. Related to "wave" and possibly to "waver". The word suggests rhythmic, repetitive motion.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most commonly associated with animals wagging tails
> - Can also describe quick movements of fingers, heads, or other body parts
> - Suggests rhythm and often playful or animated movement
