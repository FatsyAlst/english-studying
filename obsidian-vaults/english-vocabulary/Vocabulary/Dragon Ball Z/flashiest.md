## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Superlative of flashy; Common)
Most striking, showy, or ostentatious in appearance; most likely to attract attention through bold or flamboyant display; most gaudy or brilliant.

> [!example] Examples
> - The flashiest fighter drew the crowd's attention with every move.
> - His flashiest technique was his most destructive signature attack.
> - The tournament showcased the flashiest warriors from across the realm.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *flash* + superlative suffix *-iest*. "Flash" originates from Middle English, possibly imitative in origin, suggesting a sudden bright light or sudden action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Superlative form of "flashy"
> - Often implies excessive showiness or ostentation
> - Can be positive (impressive, bold) or negative (overly showy)
> - Common in contexts describing fighters, performers, or display
> - Sometimes carries connotation of superficiality
