## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (very frequent)
To move on hands and knees or by dragging the body along the ground; to move slowly and close to the ground

> [!example] Examples
> - He crawled across the battlefield
> - The injured warrior crawled toward safety
> - She had to crawl through the narrow passage
> - They crawled away from the explosion

### Sense 2 — *verb* (frequent)
To move slowly and laboriously; to progress at a slow pace

> [!example] Examples
> - The procession crawled down the road
> - Traffic crawled along the highway
> - Progress crawled forward slowly
> - The battle crawled on endlessly

### Sense 3 — *noun* (frequent)
The act of moving on hands and knees; a slow movement

> [!example] Examples
> - The crawl to the exit seemed endless
> - At a crawl, they moved forward

### Sense 4 — *noun* (sports)
A fast swimming stroke performed on the front

> [!example] Examples
> - He swam using the crawl
> - She won with a powerful crawl

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "crawlen" or "crauelen," possibly from Old Norse "krafla" meaning to grasp or claw. The word suggests movement that resembles clawing or grasping.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies slow, difficult, or undignified movement
> - Often describes characters who are injured, exhausted, or desperate
> - Can describe both literal movement and figurative slow progress
> - Informal and vivid vocabulary
> - In swimming, refers to the front crawl stroke (freestyle)
