## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)
1. Causing feelings of nausea or disgust; extremely unpleasant; 2. Disturbing or morally repulsive

> [!example] Examples
> - The sickening smell made everyone leave the room.
> - The violence in the movie was absolutely sickening.
> - He felt a sickening wave of dread wash over him.

## 📜 Etymology
From "sicken" (to make ill), derived from Old English "seoc" and Old Norse "sjúkr," both meaning sick or ill.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used to emphasize strong disgust or revulsion
> - Can describe physical sensations or moral reactions
> - Often used with intensifiers like "absolutely" or "utterly"
