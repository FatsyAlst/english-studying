## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
The general population of a place; the common people or masses of a particular area

> [!example] Examples
> - The populace feared the approaching threat
> - The city's populace was evacuated
> - Local populace gathered to watch
> - The entire populace was affected by the disaster
> - The populace demanded justice

## 📜 Etymology
From French "populace" derived from Latin "popularis" meaning of the people, which comes from "populus" meaning people or nation. The word emphasizes the common or general population.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary vocabulary
> - Often used to describe large groups of ordinary people
> - Commonly appears when describing large-scale events affecting communities
> - Singular noun (despite plural appearance) referring to a collective group
> - Elevates the tone compared to simply saying "people"
