## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To have or feel an irritating sensation on the skin that causes a desire to scratch; to feel or experience an intense desire or restless urge.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior was itching for a real challenge.
> - His power itched to be unleashed in combat.
> - The defeated opponent was itching for a rematch.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
An irritating skin sensation that provokes the urge to scratch; a restless desire or yearning for something.

> [!example] Examples
> - An itching sensation coursed through his entire body.
> - He felt the itching of his fighting spirit awakening.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *giccan*, related to Old Saxon *gikken* and Old Norse *kitla*. The word has Germanic roots and has been used since ancient times to describe both physical sensations and metaphorical desires.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Itching for" is an idiomatic expression meaning eager or restless desire
> - Can describe physical skin sensations or metaphorical eagerness
> - Progressive form (-ing) used as both verb and adjective
> - Related: itchy (adjective describing tendency to itch)
> - Common in action contexts describing eager combatants
> - Conveys impatience and restless anticipation
