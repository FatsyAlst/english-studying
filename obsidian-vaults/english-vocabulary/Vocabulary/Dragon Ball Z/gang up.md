## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
To join together as a group to oppose, criticize, attack, frighten, or pressure someone, often unfairly or in a bullying way.

> [!example] Examples
> - The older boys ganged up on him at school and called him names.
> - They all ganged up to try and get him to change his decision.
> - The kids ganged up on me and convinced me to get pizza for dinner.

---
**Pronunciation:** `[ɡæŋ]`

### *noun* (Most Common)
A group of people, friends, or criminals acting together.

> [!example] Examples
> - He hangs out with a gang of troublemakers.

---
'Gang up' from early 20th century (c. 1920s), colloquial; 'gang' from Old Norse *gangr* 'journey, course' (via Old English 'gang' 'going, walk') → 'group going together' → modern 'group/criminal band.'

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Negative connotation; implies unfairness or bullying; common in spoken, informal English.
> - Can be physical (attack) or verbal/emotional (criticize/pressure).
