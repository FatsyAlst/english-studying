## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (formal/literary)
A place of residence; a dwelling place or home.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior's abode was hidden in the mountains.
> - They sought refuge in the sage's abode.
> - His humble abode contained all he needed.

### Sense 2 — *verb (archaic past tense)* (rare)
Past tense of abide; remained or dwelt.

> [!example] Examples
> - He abode there for many years.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "abod," past tense of "abidan" (abide). Related to modern English "abide" meaning to remain or dwell.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary in tone
> - More poetic than "home," "house," or "dwelling"
> - Common in fantasy and historical narratives
