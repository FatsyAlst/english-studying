## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective/verb* (common)
Decreasing in size, amount, or number; becoming smaller or fewer gradually.

> [!example] Examples
> - Their dwindling supplies worried the team.
> - The audience was dwindling as people left early.
> - Resources are dwindling faster than expected.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English dwindle, possibly from Old English dwina (to waste away) combined with -le (frequentative suffix).

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies a gradual, continuing decrease
> - Commonly used for negative situations (resources, population, hope)
> - The participle form "dwindling" is frequently used as an adjective
