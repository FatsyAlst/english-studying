## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense of strike)* (very frequent)
To hit, punch, or attack someone or something with force

> [!example] Examples
> - He struck the villain with a powerful punch
> - The lightning struck the building during the storm
> - She struck him across the face in anger

### Sense 2 — *verb* (frequent)
To make a sudden impression or occurrence; to suddenly happen

> [!example] Examples
> - An idea struck him while he was meditating
> - It struck me as odd that he didn't respond
> - Fear struck the hearts of the enemies

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "strican" meaning to rub or spread; related to Germanic roots. The sense of hitting developed in Middle English and became the primary meaning.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Struck" is the regular past tense; "stricken" is the past participle used in formal/literary contexts
> - Used frequently in action sequences and combat scenes
> - Can be both physical (hitting) and metaphorical (affecting the mind/emotions)
