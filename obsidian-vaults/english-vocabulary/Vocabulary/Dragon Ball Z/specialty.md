## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
A particular area of expertise, knowledge, or skill that someone is known for; something special that is distinctly made or produced

> [!example] Examples
> - Her specialty is medieval history.
> - The restaurant's specialty is fresh seafood dishes.
> - His specialty in engineering made him invaluable to the team.

## 📜 Etymology
From Medieval Latin "specialitas," derived from Latin "specialis" (individual, particular), from "species" (appearance, kind).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often refers to professional or technical expertise
> - Can also describe a featured or special product
> - More common in American English than "specialism" (British)
