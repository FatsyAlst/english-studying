## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To assume or adopt a particular physical position or attitude; to sit or stand in a specific way (often for a photo or artistic purpose).

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter posed dramatically before the battle.
> - They posed for a photograph.
> - He posed as a wealthy businessman.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To present or constitute (a threat, question, problem, etc.); to raise or put forward.

> [!example] Examples
> - This situation poses a serious challenge.
> - The question posed by the teacher was difficult.
> - His power posed a threat to the entire world.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Common)
A position of the body; a way of standing or sitting assumed for effect or artistic purposes.

> [!example] Examples
> - He struck a heroic pose.
> - Her pose was graceful and elegant.
> - A dramatic pose before the attack.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *poser*, from Latin *pausare* (to rest, stop). The sense of "to present or put forth" developed from the idea of "placing" something before someone.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can mean physical positioning (for aesthetics or photos)
> - Can mean to present or constitute something abstract (a threat, question, etc.)
> - Can mean to pretend or assume a false identity ("pose as")
> - In anime contexts, often refers to striking dramatic fighting poses
> - Synonyms: position, stance, attitude, present, constitute, represent
