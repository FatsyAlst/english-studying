## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (moderate frequency)
Wicked, evil, or criminal in intent or nature; extremely immoral

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain's nefarious plan threatened the entire world
> - He engaged in nefarious deeds to gain power
> - The nefarious scheme was finally exposed

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "nefarius" derived from "nefas" meaning wrong or crime, composed of "ne-" (not) and "fas" (divine law). The word has carried the sense of wickedness since its Latin origins.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary vocabulary, often used to describe villains' intentions
> - Emphasizes moral evil rather than mere wrongness
> - Typically describes deliberate, intentional wickedness
