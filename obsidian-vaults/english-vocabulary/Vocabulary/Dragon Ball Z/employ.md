## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To use something as a tool or method; to put something into action or use.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior employed his special technique to defeat the enemy.
> - She employed a new strategy in the battle.
> - He employed all his power in that final attack.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To hire someone to work for you; to give someone a job.

> [!example] Examples
> - The king employs many soldiers to protect the kingdom.
> - The company employs thousands of workers.
> - She was employed as a trainer at the martial arts academy.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "employer" (to engage, make use of), from Old French "emploier." The word comes from Latin "implicare" (to enfold, involve). It entered English in the 15th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (using/deploying) is common in action and strategy contexts
> - Sense 2 (hiring) is more formal and business-related
> - Often followed by "as" (employ as) or direct object (employ a strategy)
