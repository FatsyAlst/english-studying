## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (frequent)
Something that initially appears bad or unfortunate but turns out to be beneficial or good in the end; a hidden advantage.

> [!example] Examples
> - His defeat was a blessing in disguise; it made him stronger.
> - Being sent away turned out to be a blessing in disguise.
> - The interruption was a blessing in disguise that saved their lives.

## 📜 Etymology
From "blessing" (divine favor) + "in disguise" (hidden, not obvious). The metaphor suggests that something good is concealed under a false appearance of evil, emerging in the 18th century in English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Used when looking back on events with new perspective
> - Conveys unexpected positive outcomes
> - Often retrospective (used to describe past events)
