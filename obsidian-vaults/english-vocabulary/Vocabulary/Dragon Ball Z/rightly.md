## 📖 Definition
*adverb* — (Common)

Correctly; properly; accurately; in the right manner or in accordance with the truth.

> [!example] Examples
> - If I rightly remember, Goku won that tournament.
> - He rightly identified the fighter's weakness.
> - She rightly concluded that the plan was dangerous.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "rihte," related to "right" meaning straight, correct, or just. The "-ly" suffix forms an adverb.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to express certainty about something recalled
> - Can mean "correctly" or "properly"
> - Frequently used in phrases like "if I rightly remember"
> - More formal than simple "right"
> - Emphasizes accuracy or correctness
