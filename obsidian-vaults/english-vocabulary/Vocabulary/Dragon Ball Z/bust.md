## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Very Frequent)
To break or damage something forcefully.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku will bust through the wall to reach his opponent.
> - The explosion busted the machine into pieces.
> - He busted through the door with incredible strength.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Frequent)
A sculpture of a head and shoulders, typically displayed on a pedestal.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sculpture garden displayed marble busts of famous warriors.
> - A bronze bust stood in the entrance hall.
> - The artist created a bust to commemorate the hero.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Informal)
A police raid or arrest.

> [!example] Examples
> - The authorities conducted a drug bust in the city.
> - The criminal gang faced a major bust.
> - Breaking news: police bust criminal operation.

## 📜 Etymology
From French *buste*, from Italian *busto*, from Latin *bustum* meaning "tomb" or "monument" (for the sculpture sense). The verb usage is a back-formation from the noun, popularized in 20th-century slang.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As a verb, it's dynamic and action-oriented, common in action scenes
> - The sculpture meaning is formal and artistic
> - The law enforcement meaning is informal and modern
> - Often used with particles: bust through, bust down, bust out
