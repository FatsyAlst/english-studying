## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Informal)
A silly or stupid person.

> [!example] Examples
> - Some nitwit left the fridge door open all night.
> - I felt like a complete nitwit when I went to the wrong lecture hall.
> - Don't be a nitwit — read the instructions!

---
## 📜 Etymology
Recorded in American English by 1914; probably *nit* "nothing" (from dialectal German or Yiddish, cf. *nix*) + *wit* "intelligence".
