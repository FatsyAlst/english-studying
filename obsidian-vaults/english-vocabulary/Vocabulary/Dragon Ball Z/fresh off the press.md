## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
Very recent; just published or released; newly made or created.

> [!example] Examples
> - Fresh off the press! The latest edition of the newspaper.
> - Here are some statistics fresh off the press.
> - The results are fresh off the press.

---
## 📜 Etymology
The phrase comes from the literal image of papers coming directly from a printing press, still warm and fresh. Developed in the 19th century with the rise of newspapers and printing technology.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes that something is very new or recent
> - Often used for news, publications, or announcements
> - Can be slightly humorous or dramatic
> - Similar to "hot off the press" but less common
