## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Finely chopped or minced meat; ground meat, especially pork or beef.

> [!example] Examples
> - The recipe calls for one pound of mincemeat.
> - Add the mincemeat to the pot.
> - Mincemeat is used in meat pies.

### Sense 2 — *idiom* (common)
To make mincemeat of someone means to defeat them thoroughly, to beat someone badly, or to severely criticize someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - The champion made mincemeat of his opponent.
> - I'll make mincemeat of you in the next match!
> - The critics made mincemeat of the film.

## 📜 Etymology
"Mince" comes from Middle English minsen, from Old French mincier. "Meat" is from Old English mete (food). The idiom developed from the image of meat being chopped into tiny pieces.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The literal sense refers to finely chopped meat
> - The idiom "make mincemeat of" is very common in English
> - Implies complete defeat or destruction
> - Often used in competitive or conflict contexts
