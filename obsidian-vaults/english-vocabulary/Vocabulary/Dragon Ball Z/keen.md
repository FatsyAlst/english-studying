## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Having or showing great interest, enthusiasm, or eagerness; very desirous of something

> [!example] Examples
> - He is keen on learning new languages.
> - She was keen to participate in the competition.
> - They're both keen swimmers and love the water.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Sharp, acute, or keen-edged; able to cut well (of a blade or tool)

> [!example] Examples
> - The knife had a keen edge after sharpening.
> - A keen blade is essential for precision cutting.

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (Common)
Mentally sharp, quick-witted, or having keen perception; having acute sensory abilities

> [!example] Examples
> - She had a keen sense of smell.
> - His keen mind quickly solved the problem.
> - With keen observation, he noticed every detail.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "cene," meaning bold or brave, and from Old Norse "kenn," meaning to know. Also related to "keen" meaning sharp.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is most common today (enthusiasm)
> - Sense 2 is more archaic or technical
> - Sense 3 is formal or literary
> - Often used with "on" or "to" for enthusiasts
