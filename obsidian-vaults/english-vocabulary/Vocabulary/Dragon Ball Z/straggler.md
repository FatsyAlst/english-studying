## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Frequent)

A person who falls behind or arrives late compared to others in a group or competition.

> [!example] Examples
> - The last straggler finally crossed the finish line ten minutes after the winner.
> - In the tournament, the weakest fighters became stragglers as they were eliminated early.
> - The hiking group had to wait for the stragglers to catch up.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *stragelen*, possibly from Old French *estragil*, meaning to wander or roam irregularly.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in sports, races, or group activities
> - Can refer to those who are physically slower or less competitive
> - Neutral to slightly negative connotation depending on context
