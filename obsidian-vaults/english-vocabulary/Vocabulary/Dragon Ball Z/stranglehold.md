## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A tight grip around someone's neck; figuratively, complete control or dominance over something or someone that prevents freedom.

> [!example] Examples
> - Vegeta had a stranglehold on Goku during the fight.
> - The villain maintained a stranglehold on the planet's resources.
> - One fighter caught the other in a stranglehold.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "strangle" (from Old French "estrangler") and "hold" (from Old English "haldan").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal meaning: a wrestling move or physical grip
> - Figurative meaning: complete control or monopoly
> - Often implies oppression or restriction of freedom
> - Used in political, economic, and social contexts
