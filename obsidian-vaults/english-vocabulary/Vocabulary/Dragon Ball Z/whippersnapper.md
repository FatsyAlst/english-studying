## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A young, presumptuous, or cheeky person; someone who is disrespectful of authority or tradition; often used by older people to criticize the young for being impertinent.

> [!example] Examples
> - That whippersnapper doesn't respect his elders.
> - A mere whippersnapper dares challenge us?
> - These young whippersnappers think they know everything.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "whipper" (one who whips) + "snapper" (one who snaps), both probably imitative and deprecatory. First appeared in English around the 1690s. The reduplicative structure emphasizes contempt.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Old-fashioned and primarily used humorously in modern English
> - Conveys both affection and disapproval
> - Typically used by older characters to scold younger ones
