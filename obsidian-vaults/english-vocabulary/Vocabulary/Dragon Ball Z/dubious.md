## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Uncertain or undecided about something; doubtful or skeptical; of questionable quality or integrity.

> [!example] Examples
> - His dubious claim about his power level raised suspicion.
> - The plan seemed dubious at best.
> - She had dubious reasons for joining the group.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "dubiosus" (doubtful, hesitating), from "dubium" (doubt). Related to "doubt" and "dubitate" (to hesitate). Originally described wavering between two possibilities.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Suggests both uncertainty and suspicion
> - Often implies something may be false or disreputable
> - More formal than "questionable" or "suspicious"
