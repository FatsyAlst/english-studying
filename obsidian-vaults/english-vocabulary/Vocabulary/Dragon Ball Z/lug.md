## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Common)

To carry or drag something heavy with difficulty or effort; to transport something cumbersome.

> [!example] Examples
> - I had to lug my suitcase up three flights of stairs.
> - He lugged the equipment across the field.
> - They were lugging boxes of supplies through the warehouse.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Verb           | lug / lugs / lugged / lugging |
| Noun           | lug (a projection or handle on an object) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- lug something around
- lug something over/across
- lug heavy equipment
- lug bags/suitcases

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *luggen*, of uncertain origin. Possibly related to Norwegian *lugga* (to pull). The word emphasizes the difficulty and effort of carrying.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies carrying something awkward or heavier than ideal.
> - Common in everyday contexts (luggage, equipment, groceries).
> - Less formal than "carry" or "transport"; more colloquial.
