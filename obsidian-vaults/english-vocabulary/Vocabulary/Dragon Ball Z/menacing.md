## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Threatening, dangerous, or suggesting the possibility of harm; appearing hostile or ominous.

> [!example] Examples
> - Frieza's menacing presence struck fear into the hearts of his enemies.
> - The villain's menacing smile revealed his dark intentions.
> - The menacing clouds of energy signaled an imminent battle.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "menace," derived from Old French "menace," which comes from Latin "minacia," meaning "threats" (from "minari," meaning "to threaten"). The "-ing" suffix creates the present participle or adjective form.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary tone; commonly used in descriptive narratives
> - Conveys both physical threat and psychological intimidation
> - Often precedes nouns like "presence," "look," "tone," or "appearance"
> - Synonymous with "threatening," "ominous," or "sinister"
