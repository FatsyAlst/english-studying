## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (formal/literary)
A very poor person; someone who has little or no money or possessions.

> [!example] Examples
> - He went from prince to pauper overnight.
> - The pauper survived on scraps and charity.
> - Once wealthy merchants became paupers after the disaster.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "pauperis" meaning poor. The term entered English through legal and formal contexts, particularly in discussions of poverty and poor relief.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat archaic in modern usage
> - Often appears in "rags to riches" or "riches to rags" narratives
> - Can have historical or literary connotations
