## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Common)
To swallow quickly or with effort; to take in or consume rapidly; to show nervousness or fear by swallowing heavily.

> [!example] Examples
> - He gulped nervously as the powerful opponent approached.
> - The warrior gulped down a recovery potion before the next battle.
> - Fear made him gulp as the energy blast headed toward him.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
A deep or sudden swallow; the sound of swallowing; an amount swallowed at once.

> [!example] Examples
> - He took a gulp of water to recover his strength.
> - A loud gulp echoed through the chamber.

## 📜 Etymology
From Dutch *gulpen*, possibly imitative in origin, suggesting the sound of swallowing. The word has been used in English since the 17th century to describe both the action of swallowing and the characteristic sound it makes.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb: to swallow quickly or audibly
> - Noun: a quantity swallowed or the sound of swallowing
> - Often suggests nervousness or rapid consumption
> - Related: gulping (present participle)
> - Common in action contexts to show emotion (fear, urgency)
> - Can describe physical action or emotional reaction
> - Sometimes carries onomatopoetic quality
