## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The layer of fat under the skin of whales and other marine mammals; fat or grease

### Sense 2 — *verb* (informal)
To cry loudly and uncontrollably; to weep or sob

### Sense 3 — *adjective* (informal)
Fat or flabby; referring to blubbery tissue

> [!example] Examples
> - Whales have thick blubber to keep warm.
> - He started to blubber uncontrollably after his loss.
> - The blubber jiggled as he ran.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle Dutch *blubber* or from an imitative origin describing soft, wobbly substance or the sounds of crying. Related to "bubble" and "blob" in terms of visual and aural association.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: Biological/scientific use regarding marine mammals
> - Sense 2: Informal, somewhat childish or undignified way to describe crying
> - Sense 3: Derogatory when describing human appearance
> - The crying sense suggests loud, messy, uncontrolled weeping
