## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To become firmly stuck somewhere.

> [!example] Examples
> - A fish bone got lodged in his throat.
> - The bullet lodged in the wall.
> - My kite lodged in a tree.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common — Formal)
To make a formal complaint, appeal or claim to an official body.

> [!example] Examples
> - I lodged a complaint with the landlord about the heating.
> - She lodged an appeal against the exam result.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
A small house in the countryside or mountains, often for holidays or hunting; a small building at the entrance of a large estate or college.

> [!example] Examples
> - We stayed in a ski lodge in the Alps.
> - Ask at the porters' lodge at the main entrance.

### Sense 4 — *verb* (Less Common)
To stay in someone's house and pay rent; to provide someone with accommodation.

> [!example] Examples
> - As a student, he lodged with an elderly couple.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *loge* "hut, arbour", from Frankish *laubja "shelter" — related to *lobby* and *loggia*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Lodge a complaint* is formal/official — useful with landlords and university offices.
> - *A lodger* = someone renting a room in another person's home.
> - *Lodging(s)* = accommodation.
