## 📖 Definition
*Verb (past tense: defied)* (Common)
To resist openly or boldly; to challenge the authority or power of someone; to refuse to obey or comply with.

> [!example] Examples
> - He defied the law and refused to surrender.
> - She defied her parents' wishes and pursued her own path.
> - The warrior defied all odds and emerged victorious.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *defier*, from Latin *diffidare* (to distrust). Com­posed of Latin *dis-* (apart) + *fidare* (to trust). Originally meant "to distrust" or "to renounce faith in."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Carries a sense of courage and boldness
> - Implies active resistance, not passive refusal
> - Often used in contexts of heroic or rebellious action
> - Synonyms: resist, challenge, refuse, oppose, rebel, flout
> - Common in action/adventure narratives and inspirational contexts
> - Can be used both literally (defy a ruler) and figuratively (defy expectations)
