## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Professional guidance or advice given by an expert to help someone with personal, emotional, or psychological problems.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior attended counseling to deal with trauma from his battles.
> - She offered counseling to the troubled young fighter.
> - Counseling can help address emotional issues after intense combat.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
The act of giving advice or recommendations; consultation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The elder provided wise counseling on strategy and discipline.
> - He sought counseling from experienced masters before the tournament.
> - Counseling from mentors helped shape his fighting style.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "conseil," from Latin "consilium" (consultation, advice). The noun form "counseling" adds the suffix "-ing" to indicate the act or process of providing advice.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Primarily American spelling; British English often uses "counselling" (double L)
> - Can be both professional (therapy) and informal (advice)
> - Common in mental health, education, and personal development contexts
