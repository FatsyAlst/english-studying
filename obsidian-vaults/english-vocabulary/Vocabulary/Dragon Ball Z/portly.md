## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Formal)
(Of a person) large and heavy in build; stout; overweight. Often used as a euphemistic or polite way to describe someone who is fat or obese.

> [!example] Examples
> - A portly gentleman walked into the room.
> - He had grown portly in his middle age.
> - The portly king sat upon his throne.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *port* (bearing, deportment, carriage) + "-ly" (suffix meaning full of or characterized by). Originally referred to dignified bearing or demeanor, later applied to physical heaviness and bulk.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal, somewhat dated, or humorous tone
> - Considered more polite/euphemistic than "fat" or "obese"
> - Often used in literature, especially classic works
> - Synonyms: stout, heavy, large, rotund, corpulent, overweight
> - Often carries a slightly dignified or humorous connotation
> - Commonly paired with character descriptions of authority figures or historical figures
