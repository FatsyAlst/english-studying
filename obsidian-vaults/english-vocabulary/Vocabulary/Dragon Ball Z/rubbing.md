## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Present Participle; Common)
The action of applying friction by moving something back and forth across a surface; massaging or applying pressure; irritating or annoying (figuratively).

> [!example] Examples
> - Rubbing his injured shoulder helped ease the pain.
> - He was rubbing salt in the wound with his taunts.
> - The warrior began rubbing his eyes after the blinding flash.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
The action or friction of rubbing something; a massage or application of friction; an irritating situation.

> [!example] Examples
> - A gentle rubbing of his wounded area soothed the pain.
> - The constant rubbing of the armor caused discomfort.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *rubbian*, of uncertain origin, possibly from Old Saxon or Middle Low German *rubben*. Used since the 10th century to describe the action of friction or massaging.
