## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
About to happen or occur very soon; imminent or threatening to occur in the immediate future.

> [!example] Examples
> - The impending invasion filled everyone with dread.
> - They sensed the impending danger.
> - The impending battle was unavoidable.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "impendere" (to hang over, threaten), from "im-" (over) + "pendere" (to hang). The image is of something literally hanging over one's head, ready to fall.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually precedes a noun (attributive adjective)
> - Creates a sense of urgency and foreboding
> - Common in dramatic or formal writing when describing threats
