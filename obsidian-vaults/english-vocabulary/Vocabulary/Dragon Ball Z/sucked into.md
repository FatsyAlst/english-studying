## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (frequent)
To be pulled or drawn into something against one's will or intention; to become involved in something, often unwillingly or unintentionally.

> [!example] Examples
> - He got sucked into the conflict despite wanting no part of it.
> - Don't get sucked into their rivalry.
> - She was sucked into the vortex and disappeared.

## 📜 Etymology
From "suck" (to draw in as if by suction) + "into" (toward the interior). The metaphorical sense of being drawn unwillingly into situations emerged in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies lack of choice or resistance
> - Suggests being drawn in unwillingly
> - Common in both physical and social/emotional contexts
