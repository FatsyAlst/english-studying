## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense/past participle)* (common)
Past tense of shrink; became or made smaller in size.

> [!example] Examples
> - His power had shrunk after the intense battle.
> - The magical field shrunk as they moved away from its center.
> - His muscles appeared to have shrunk from lack of training.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "scrincan" meaning to contract. Related to "shriveled" and "wither."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past tense and past participle of "shrink"
> - Can be used literally (size) or figuratively (power, influence)
> - "Shrunken" is the adjective form used before a noun
