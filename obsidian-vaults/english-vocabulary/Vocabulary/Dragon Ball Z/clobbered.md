## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Common, Informal)

To hit or strike someone or something heavily; to defeat decisively in a competition or fight.

> [!example] Examples
> - The boxer clobbered his opponent in the first round.
> - They clobbered the opposing team 5-0 in the match.
> - He clobbered me with a pillow during our fight.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly imitative or from Yiddish *klop* (to hit). First recorded in American English in the early 20th century. The onomatopoetic quality suggests it imitates the sound of a heavy blow.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/colloquial term; more casual than "strike" or "beat."
> - Can be literal (physical hitting) or figurative (defeating in competition).
> - Suggests a decisive, overwhelming victory or heavy blow.
> - Often used in sports contexts and casual speech.
> - Emphatic and forceful; conveys dominance or superiority.
