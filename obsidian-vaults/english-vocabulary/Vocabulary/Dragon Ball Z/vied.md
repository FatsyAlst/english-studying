## 📖 Definition
*verb (past tense: vied)* — (Common)

Competed eagerly with others to achieve something; strove or struggled to be better than others.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighters vied for supremacy in the tournament.
> - They vied with each other to become the strongest.
> - The martial artists vied for the title of champion.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle French "envier," related to "envie" (challenge to competition). Originally meant to invite or challenge.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always implies competition
> - Usually followed by "for" or "with"
> - More formal than "competed"
> - Emphasizes eager or earnest effort
> - Past tense is "vied," not "vyed"
