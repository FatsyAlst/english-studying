## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Past tense of chunk; Common)
To throw forcefully or carelessly; to break into chunks or pieces; to divide into segments.

> [!example] Examples
> - The impact chunked the rock into smaller pieces.
> - He chunked his weapons aside before the final confrontation.
> - The explosion chunked the landscape into fragmented terrain.

### Sense 2 — *Adjective* (Common)
Divided into chunks; composed of thick or chunky pieces; irregular or uneven in texture.

> [!example] Examples
> - A chunked mass of debris littered the battlefield.
> - The chunked ice scattered across the frozen ground.

## 📜 Etymology
From *chunk*, of uncertain origin, possibly from dialectal English or onomatopoetic in nature. The term emerged in American English and has been popularized since the 20th century, particularly in computing (data chunking) and informal contexts.
