## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
The lowest surface of a room or building that one walks on; the ground level

> [!example] Examples
> - The kitchen floor was made of marble.
> - He dropped the plate on the floor.
> - The floor was covered with dust.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To knock someone down; to cause to fall; to surprise or confuse completely

> [!example] Examples
> - The boxer floored his opponent in the first round.
> - Her response completely floored him.
> - That question floored the contestant.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
The right to speak or the platform for speaking in a legislative body

> [!example] Examples
> - The senator was given the floor to speak.
> - She demanded the floor to present her argument.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "flor," from Proto-Germanic "*floruz," possibly related to Latin "pratum" (meadow). The verb sense developed later.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is literal (walking surface)
> - Sense 2 is figurative (to knock down or surprise)
> - Sense 3 is used in formal settings like legislatures
