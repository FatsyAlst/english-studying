## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Common)
A short, encouraging speech or words of motivation intended to inspire someone to do better or feel more confident; a pep talk is typically given to boost morale or courage.

> [!example] Examples
> - The coach gave the team a pep talk before the game.
> - She needed a pep talk to boost her confidence.
> - He delivered an inspiring pep talk to motivate the troops.

## 📜 Etymology
"Pep" is short for "pepper," originally slang for energy or vigor (as in "pep up" = to energize). "Talk" is literal. The compound "pep talk" emerged in American English in the early 20th century to describe motivational speeches.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal but widely used in sports, military, and workplace contexts
> - Typically short and direct, designed for quick motivation boost
> - Often delivered before important events (games, battles, exams)
> - Synonyms: motivational speech, words of encouragement, pep rally, inspirational address
> - Can be serious or humorous
> - Frequently featured in sports and action narratives
