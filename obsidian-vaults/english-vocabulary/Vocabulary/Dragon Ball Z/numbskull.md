## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal, derogatory)
A stupid or foolish person; a dimwit or blockhead

> [!example] Examples
> - That numbskull didn't understand the obvious danger.
> - What kind of numbskull would challenge him?
> - Don't be such a numbskull!

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *numb* (lacking sensation, dull) + *skull* (head, brain). First used in 17th century English to describe someone with a "numb" or dull brain, combining the idea of insensitivity with the seat of intelligence.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Clearly insulting and derogatory
> - Often used humorously or in mild frustration
> - Informal and casual, not used in formal writing
> - More lighthearted than some other insults despite negative meaning
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[nitwit]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
