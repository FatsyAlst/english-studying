## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A bet or the amount of money risked in a bet; something staked on an uncertain outcome.

> [!example] Examples
> - They made a wager on who would win the fight.
> - The wager was 1000 zeni for the tournament.
> - He placed a wager that Vegeta couldn't defeat Goku.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To risk or stake something on an uncertain outcome; to bet.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'll wager my house that Goku will win.
> - Would you wager your life force in this battle?
> - She wagered everything she had on the final match.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "wageure" meaning "pledge" or "security," related to "wage" in the sense of "security offered." Ultimately from Proto-Germanic *wadi- meaning "pledge."

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "bet"
> - Can be used as both noun and verb
> - Often involves significant stakes in storytelling contexts
> - Common in fantasy, gaming, and anime dialogues
> - Implies serious consequences for the outcome
