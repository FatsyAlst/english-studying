## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A person or thing that is certain to succeed, win, or be chosen. Something regarded as a guaranteed winner or inevitable choice.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku is a shoo-in for the strongest fighter in the tournament
> - With those power levels, he's a shoo-in to win
> - She's a shoo-in for first place

## 📜 Etymology
From "shoo" (to drive away or urge forward) + "in". Originated in American English around 1912, possibly from the idea of shooing someone into a position or victory.

**Note:** Often misspelled as "shoe-in" but the correct spelling is "shoo-in".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal/colloquial usage
> - Used in sports, competitions, and predictions
> - Indicates near-certainty, not absolute certainty
> - Common variant misspelling: "shoe-in" (incorrect)
