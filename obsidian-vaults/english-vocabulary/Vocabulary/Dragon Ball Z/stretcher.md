## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)

A framework, typically consisting of two long poles with fabric or a platform stretched between them, used for carrying an injured or sick person.

> [!example] Examples
> - The paramedics arrived with a stretcher to transport the injured man.
> - They carried the wounded soldier on a stretcher to the hospital.
> - The nurse wheeled the stretcher into the emergency room.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *strecchen* (to stretch), combined with the agent suffix *-er*. The device is called a stretcher because it stretches fabric or netting between poles.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often associated with emergency medical situations (accidents, injuries).
> - A "stretcher bearer" is a person trained to transport injured individuals.
> - In British English, "stretcher" is standard; in American English, "gurney" is also common (though technically different).
> - Common in medical, military, and rescue contexts.
