## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Verb* (Very Common)
To hit or deal a blow to someone or something with force.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku strikes with incredible speed and power.
> - The blast strikes the mountain, destroying it completely.
> - He strikes his opponent with a devastating punch.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Common)
To come into contact with forcefully; to collide with.

> [!example] Examples
> - Lightning strikes the tallest building.
> - The missile strikes its target.
> - The waves strike the shore.

### Sense 3 — *Noun* (Common)
A hit or blow; an attack or assault.

> [!example] Examples
> - His final strike defeated the enemy.
> - The preemptive strike caught them off guard.
> - Each strike grew more powerful than the last.

### Sense 4 — *Noun* (Common)
A work stoppage by employees protesting conditions; labor action.

> [!example] Examples
> - The workers organized a strike.
> - The strike lasted three weeks.
> - They went on strike for better wages.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "strīcan" meaning "to go, flow, or stroke." Related to German "streichen" (to stroke). Proto-Germanic *strīkaną.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Extremely versatile word with many meanings
> - Past tense "struck" is most common; "strook" is archaic
> - "Strike" vs. "stroke" have different connotations
> - Very common in combat/action contexts like anime
> - Idiomatic uses include "strike a chord" and "strike it rich"
> - 📺 Also seen in [[Dragon Ball Z]]
