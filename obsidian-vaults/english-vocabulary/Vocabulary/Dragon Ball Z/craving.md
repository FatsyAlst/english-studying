## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A strong, persistent desire for something, especially food; an intense need or longing.

> [!example] Examples
> - She had a sudden craving for chocolate.
> - His cravings for sweets are out of control.
> - I've been having strange food cravings lately.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To have a strong desire for something; to long for or want intensely.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm craving a pizza right now.
> - She craves adventure and excitement.
> - The plant is craving more water.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English craven, possibly from Old Norse krafan (to demand, require). Developed to mean "to desire intensely."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very common in everyday conversation, especially about food
> - Can refer to physical cravings (pregnancy) or emotional desires
> - More intense than simply "wanting" something
> - Often used with food, but can apply to experiences or objects
