## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (High Frequency)
A large and elaborate meal, typically prepared for celebration or special occasion with abundant food.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku prepared a magnificent feast for all his friends.
> - They celebrated the victory with a grand feast.
> - The feast included dishes from across the kingdom.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (Medium Frequency)
To eat a large meal or to enjoy food and drink abundantly; to entertain lavishly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warriors feasted after winning the tournament.
> - They feasted on the finest foods available.
> - We feasted until midnight celebrating the occasion.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *feste* (festival), from Latin *festa* (festivals, holidays). Related to festive and festival. Originally meant a celebration or holy day associated with special meals.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies celebration and abundance
> - "Feast for the eyes" is an idiom meaning visually appealing
> - Can be used metaphorically for any abundant experience
