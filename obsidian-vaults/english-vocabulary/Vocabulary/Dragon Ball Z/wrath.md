## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
Intense and extreme anger; violent rage or fury; divine punishment for wrongdoing.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku's wrath was unleashed upon the enemy.
> - The wrath of the gods was terrible to behold.
> - He felt the wrath building inside him.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "wræthu" (anger, cruelty), related to Proto-Germanic "wraithunis." One of the seven deadly sins in Christian tradition, representing uncontrolled anger.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal and literary than "anger" or "rage"
> - Often associated with powerful, dramatic moments
> - Can refer to both human anger and divine punishment
