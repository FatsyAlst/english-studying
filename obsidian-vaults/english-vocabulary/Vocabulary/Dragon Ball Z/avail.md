## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (formal)
To be of use or benefit; to help or assist

### Sense 2 — *noun* (formal)
Useful result or benefit; advantage

> [!example] Examples
> - His strength availed him nothing against the powerful enemy.
> - The negotiations were of little avail.
> - Training will avail you in the final battle.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *availen*, from Old French *availlir*, from *a-* (to) + *valoir* (to be worth). Related to "value" and "valid".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in negative constructions: "to no avail"
> - Somewhat formal or literary in modern usage
> - "Avail oneself of" = to use or take advantage of something
