## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A rebellion or revolt against a person in authority; an armed protest or insurrection.

> [!example] Examples
> - The uprising was quickly suppressed by the military.
> - An uprising of the oppressed people
> - The city experienced an uprising against the dictator.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A sudden or spontaneous rise or emergence of something; an uprising of emotions or energy.

> [!example] Examples
> - An uprising of public support for the cause
> - An uprising of anger among the crowd

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English uprisinge, from up + rising. Literally means "a rising up" and developed to mean "a rebellious rising up against authority."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies organized or semi-organized rebellion
> - Can be political, military, or social in nature
> - Common in historical and political contexts
> - More specific and dramatic than "protest"
