## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Of poor quality; disappointing or bad; broken or not functioning properly.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a bum call by the referee.
> - He had a bum knee from the previous fight.
> - The character gave a bum performance in the tournament.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common — Informal, AmE)
A lazy, useless person; (offensive) a homeless person.

> [!example] Examples
> - Get off the sofa, you lazy bum!
> - In his previous life he was a jobless bum.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common — Informal, BrE)
The bottom (the part of the body you sit on).

> [!example] Examples
> - I slipped on the ice and landed on my bum.

### Sense 4 — *verb* (Less Common — Informal)
*Bum something (off someone)* = to ask for something without paying; *bum around* = to spend time doing nothing.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can I bum a pen off you?
> - We just bummed around the city all day.
