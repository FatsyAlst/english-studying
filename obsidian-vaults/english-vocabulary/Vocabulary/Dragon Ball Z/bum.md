## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)

Of poor quality; disappointing or bad; broken or not functioning properly.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a bum call by the referee.
> - He had a bum knee from the previous fight.
> - The character gave a bum performance in the tournament.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from "bum" (a vagrant or person of no fixed abode), implying something of low quality.
