## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To exaggerate one's performance, behavior, or expression; to act in an overly dramatic or theatrical manner; to overplay a part.

> [!example] Examples
> - He's hamming it up for the crowd
> - Don't ham up your reaction
> - She hammed up her victory celebration
> - He was really hamming it up during the interview

## 📜 Etymology
"Ham" (bad actor) possibly comes from "hamfatter" (poor vaudeville actor) from the 1880s. The origin of "hamfatter" itself is uncertain but may relate to the ham-fat used in theatrical makeup removal. "Ham up" developed in American English in the early 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational usage
> - Often implies the exaggeration is noticeable or excessive
> - Can be playful or critical depending on context
> - Common in entertainment and performance contexts
> - "Ham it up" emphasizes the deliberateness of the overacting
