## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Strong and solid; not easily broken or damaged; built to last.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fortress had sturdy walls that could withstand any attack.
> - His sturdy frame proved his years of training.
> - The sturdy construction of the building prevented collapse.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (common)
Strong and healthy in body; robustly built.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was a sturdy fighter with excellent stamina.
> - The sturdy warrior showed no signs of fatigue.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "stórr" meaning great or large. Originally described something that was large and strong.
