## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To conquer and bring under control through force; to overcome resistance; to quiet, calm, or suppress; to make quiet or less intense.

> [!example] Examples
> - He managed to subdue the powerful enemy through relentless fighting.
> - The hero attempted to subdue his opponent without dealing fatal damage.
> - Special techniques were needed to subdue the rampaging monster.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *subduer*, from Latin *subdere* (to place under), from *sub-* (under) + *dare* (to give, place). The root suggests bringing something under one's authority or control. Used in English since the 14th century in both military and emotional contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb meaning to overcome through force or effort
> - Can mean military conquest or emotional suppression
> - Related forms: subdued (adjective), subduing (adjective)
> - Past tense: subdued
> - Suggests control achieved rather than destruction
> - Common in fantasy and martial contexts
> - Different connotation than "defeat" (suggests control vs. destruction)
