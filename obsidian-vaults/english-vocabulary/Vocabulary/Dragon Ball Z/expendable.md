## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Able or intended to be used up, consumed, or dispensed with; not essential or irreplaceable; able to be sacrificed.

> [!example] Examples
> - The soldier considered himself expendable in the greater mission.
> - Frieza viewed his minions as expendable resources.
> - In his mind, all opposition was expendable.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "expendere" meaning "to weigh out" or "to spend," from "ex-" (out) + "pendere" (to weigh). The "-able" suffix means "able to be." Combined: "able to be spent or used up."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often has a negative connotation when applied to people
> - Suggests lack of value or importance
> - Common in military, business, and sci-fi contexts
> - "Expendable income" is a neutral economic term
> - Implies something can be replaced or isn't necessary
> - Fitting for villains who view others as disposable
