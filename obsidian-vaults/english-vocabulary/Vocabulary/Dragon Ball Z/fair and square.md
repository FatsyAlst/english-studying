## 📖 Definition
*adverbial phrase* — (Common)

Honest, just, and without cheating; according to the rules; in a legitimate and straightforward manner.

> [!example] Examples
> - The match was decided fair and square with no tricks.
> - He won the tournament fair and square through hard work.
> - The victory was won fair and square, no shortcuts taken.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "fair" (just, honest) and "square" (honest, straightforward). "Square deal" was popularized by Theodore Roosevelt in the early 1900s.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes honesty and adherence to rules
> - Often used to contrast with cheating or shortcuts
> - Typically used in competitive or transactional contexts
> - Both words carry similar meanings of "honest"
> - Common in sports and formal dealings
