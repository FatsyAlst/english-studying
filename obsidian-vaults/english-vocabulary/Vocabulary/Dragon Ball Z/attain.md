## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (formal)
To achieve or reach a goal, objective, or desired state through effort or development; to accomplish.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku worked tirelessly to attain his goal of becoming the strongest warrior.
> - Vegeta sought to attain immortality and godlike power.
> - The fighters trained for years to attain a higher level of strength.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle French "attaindre," derived from Latin "attingere," meaning "to reach or touch" (from "ad-" meaning "to" and "tangere" meaning "to touch"). The word originally meant "to reach" and evolved to mean "to achieve."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary tone; common in academic and professional contexts
> - Often used with abstract nouns like "goal," "power," "success," or "enlightenment"
> - Implies effort, development, or persistence over time
> - Synonymous with "achieve," "accomplish," or "reach"
