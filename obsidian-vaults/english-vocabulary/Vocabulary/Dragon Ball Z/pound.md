## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To strike heavily and repeatedly; to hit with force; to beat or pound something

> [!example] Examples
> - He pounded on the door demanding entry.
> - She pounded the meat with a hammer.
> - The waves pounded against the rocks.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
A unit of weight equal to 16 ounces or approximately 453.6 grams

> [!example] Examples
> - The bag weighs five pounds.
> - Apples cost three dollars per pound.
> - This medication comes in half-pound containers.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
The basic monetary unit of several countries, especially the United Kingdom

> [!example] Examples
> - The hotel costs 100 pounds per night.
> - She paid 50 pounds for the ticket.
> - Exchange rates affect the pound's value.

## 📜 Etymology
Verb: From Old English "punian." Noun (weight): From Latin "pondo," meaning by weight. Noun (currency): From Old English "pund," from Latin "pondo."
