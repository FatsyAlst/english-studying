## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal)
A crazy, eccentric, or peculiar person; someone who behaves strangely

> [!example] Examples
> - That kook is always saying weird things.
> - He's a bit of a kook, but harmless.
> - The old kook lived in the mountains by himself.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From American slang, possibly from "cuckoo" (the bird, associated with madness) or derived from earlier nautical slang. First documented in mid-20th century American English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and somewhat humorous or derogatory
> - Not necessarily negative; can be affectionate
> - Related adjective "kooky" is more commonly used than "kook"
> - Often implies harmless eccentricity rather than danger
