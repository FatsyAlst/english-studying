## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (derogatory)
People considered disreputable, worthless, or of low social status; rabble or common people

> [!example] Examples
> - The villain dismissed the warriors as mere riffraff.
> - The city was filled with riffraff and lowlifes.
> - They refused to associate with such riffraff.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English/Old French *rif* and *raff*, or from *riff-raff* - a reduplication. The *riff* part may come from Old French *rif* (abundant) and *raff* from *raff* (to sweep up, collect). The pattern suggests sweepings or odds and ends.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Clearly derogatory and insulting term
> - Refers to people considered undesirable by the speaker
> - Often reflects class prejudice or snobbery
> - Treated as singular or plural depending on context
