## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (frequent)
A person or thing that corresponds to or has the same function as another in a different place or situation

> [!example] Examples
> - He is the counterpart of his alternate universe self
> - The two warriors are counterparts in strength and skill
> - The villain's counterpart in this dimension is even more powerful
> - Each character has a counterpart on the opposing team

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English combining "counter-" (against, opposite) and "part" (portion). The word originally meant a duplicate or matching part in commerce, later extending to mean an equivalent or corresponding person/thing.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to describe alternate versions of characters in different universes or timelines
> - Implies similarity or correspondence rather than identity
> - Can be used for both positive and negative comparisons
