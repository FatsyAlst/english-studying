## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To pay close attention; to start listening carefully; a command to make someone focus on what is about to be said.

> [!example] Examples
> - Listen up, everyone! Important announcement!
> - If you're going to survive, listen up
> - Listen up, I'm only going to say this once
> - They had to listen up when the master spoke

## 📜 Etymology
"Listen" comes from Old English "hlysnan". "Up" is a particle that intensifies the verb, similar to other English phrasal verbs. "Listen up" developed as an emphatic, attention-getting command in American English during the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, commanding tone
> - Usually used to initiate speech or get attention
> - Often appears at the beginning of statements
> - Can be emphatic or urgent
> - Friendly or drill-sergeant-like depending on context
> - Similar meaning to "pay attention" or "hear me out"
