## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Very serious and often causing great pain, suffering, or damage; synonym: grave.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was the victim of a grievous injustice.
> - She suffered grievous bodily harm.
> - A grievous error that cost millions.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Less Common)
Extremely bad or offensive.

> [!example] Examples
> - Grievous sin / offence.
> - The grievous loss of so many lives.

Middle English: from Old French greveus, from Latin gravis 'heavy, grave' (via gravare 'burden').

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal adjective meaning very serious or extremely bad.
> - Often used in legal contexts: 'grievous [[bodily]] harm' (GBH) - serious physical injury (UK law).
> - Similar to 'grave' but more formal and emphasizes severity.

---
**Tradução:** Grave, doloroso, atroz, sério; in law: grave (lesões corporais graves).
