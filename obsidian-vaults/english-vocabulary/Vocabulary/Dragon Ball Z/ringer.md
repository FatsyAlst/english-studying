## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A person or thing that is strikingly similar to another; a look-alike

### Sense 2 — *noun* (sports slang)
A skilled, athletic person, especially one brought in to compete for a team

### Sense 3 — *noun* (common)
Something that makes a ringing sound; an object or person that rings

> [!example] Examples
> - That character is a ringer for the legendary warrior.
> - They brought in a ringer to win the tournament.
> - The bell is a loud ringer.

## 📜 Etymology
From *ring* (to make a ringing sound) + *-er* (agent noun suffix). Developed multiple meanings: something that rings, and later someone who rings bells. The "look-alike" sense developed from substituting one similar-looking person for another.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Dead ringer for" = looks exactly like
> - Sports sense: often implies the person is exceptionally skilled
> - Can carry slight connotation of deception (substitution)
> - Very common in English for both similarity and competition contexts
