## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Common)
Extremely loud; so loud as to deafen or cause hearing damage; overwhelmingly loud and often startling.

> [!example] Examples
> - A deafening explosion shook the building.
> - The crowd erupted in a deafening roar.
> - The deafening silence after the noise was unsettling.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "deafen" (to make deaf or cause hearing loss) + "-ing" (suffix forming adjectives). Related to "deaf," from Old English *deaf*, Germanic origin.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically describes very loud, often shocking or impressive sounds
> - Can be used figuratively (e.g., "deafening silence" = remarkably quiet or lack of response)
> - Synonyms: thunderous, earsplitting, blaring, booming, loud, cacophonous
> - More intense and vivid than simply "loud"
> - Common in action scenes, descriptions of crowds, or dramatic moments
> - Can carry both literal and figurative meanings
