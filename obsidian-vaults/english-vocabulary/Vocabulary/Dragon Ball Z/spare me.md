## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Verb* — Common

To excuse someone from something unpleasant; to refrain from something (criticism, details, etc.); to ask someone not to subject you to something unwanted.

> [!example] Examples
> - Spare me the lectures about power levels
> - Spare me the dramatic explanation
> - Can you spare me the details?
> - Just spare me the excuses!

## 📜 Etymology
"Spare" comes from Old English "sparian" and Germanic origins, meaning to refrain from or avoid. "Spare me" developed as a phrasal usage meaning to refrain from doing something to someone (like criticizing them or telling them unpleasant details).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, conversational usage
> - Expresses frustration or dismissal
> - Can be impatient or rude depending on tone
> - Often followed by the thing you want spared (details, lectures, excuses)
> - Can also mean "excuse me from" or "release me from"
> - Frequently used in dialogue when annoyed
