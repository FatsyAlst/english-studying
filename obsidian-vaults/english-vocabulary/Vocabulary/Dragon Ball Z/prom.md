## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A formal dance or ball, especially one held by a high school or college at the end of the school year; a social event with music and dancing.

> [!example] Examples
> - Bulma invited Yamcha to the school prom.
> - He asked her to prom in front of everyone.
> - The prom was held at the fancy hotel downtown.

## 📜 Etymology
Short for "promenade," from French "promenade" meaning "a walk" or "a place for walking." Related to "promenade" which refers to a formal walk or leisurely stroll, often used for social events.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Short for "promenade" - common abbreviation
> - Primarily associated with American high schools
> - Typically occurs in spring, near end of school year
> - Important social event for teenagers
> - "Prom date" refers to one's companion at the event
> - "Prom king" and "prom queen" are titles given to popular students
> - Fitting for teenage slice-of-life scenes in anime
