## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* — (Common)
Frightening or intimidating in appearance or manner; causing fear or dread; formidable and alarming; arousing awe and dread.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fearsome warrior approached with devastating power.
> - His fearsome reputation preceded him into every battle.
> - A fearsome transformation occurred during the intense confrontation.

## 📜 Etymology
From *fear* + *-some* (adjective suffix meaning having a quality). The suffix "-some" comes from Old English *-sum*. Combined form has been used since the 15th century to describe things that inspire fear.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Adjective describing something inspiring fear or awe
> - Often applied to warriors, creatures, or their appearances
> - Suggests both danger and intimidation
> - More formal/literary than "scary" or "scary-looking"
> - Related: fear (verb/noun), fearful (adjective), fearlessly (adverb)
> - Emphasizes visual or reputational intimidation
