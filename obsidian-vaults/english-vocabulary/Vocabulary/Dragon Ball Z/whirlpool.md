## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (High Frequency)

A rapidly rotating current of water, or a similar rotational motion in air or liquid that pulls things toward its center; a situation of turmoil or confusion.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter was caught in a whirlpool of energy attacks and couldn't escape.
> - The conversation descended into a whirlpool of conflicting opinions.
> - A whirlpool formed in the river after the explosion.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *whirlpool*, combining *whirl* (to rotate rapidly) + *pool* (body of water). Related to Old English *hwierfan* (to turn).
