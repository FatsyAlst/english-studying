## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The fat of a pig, used in cooking and baking; a soft, white grease.

> [!example] Examples
> - The baker used lard to make the pie crust flaky.
> - Traditional cooking often relied on lard instead of oil.
> - Lard was stored in containers in the pantry.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To insert strips of fat into lean meat before cooking; to add interest or detail to something.

> [!example] Examples
> - Lard the roast before putting it in the oven.
> - She larded her speech with humorous anecdotes.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English larde, from Old French lard, from Latin lardum (bacon, lard).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Less commonly used in modern cooking due to preference for oils
> - The noun form refers specifically to pig fat
> - The verb form is more literary when meaning "to add details"
