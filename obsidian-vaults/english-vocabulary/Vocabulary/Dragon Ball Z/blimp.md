## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)
1. A large airship that is not rigid (does not have an internal frame); 2. A person who is overweight (slang, derogatory)

> [!example] Examples
> - The advertising blimp floated above the stadium.
> - A blimp circled the downtown area, displaying the company's logo.
> - The Goodyear blimp was visible during the football game.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from "B-limp" (a non-rigid airship designation in British military), or onomatopoeia. First used in the early 1900s.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most commonly refers to advertising or recreational airships
> - The derogatory usage should be avoided
> - Often used in compound terms (advertising blimp, weather blimp)
