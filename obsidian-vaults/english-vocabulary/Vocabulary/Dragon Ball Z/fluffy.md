## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Soft, light, and airy; having a soft, puffy texture

> [!example] Examples
> - Fluffy clouds drifted across the sky
> - A fluffy tail swayed behind the creature
> - The character had fluffy white hair
> - Soft and fluffy feathers covered the creature

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (informal)
Light and insubstantial; superficial or lacking substance

> [!example] Examples
> - His fluffy remarks didn't help the situation
> - That's just fluffy talk with no real meaning
> - A fluffy explanation that doesn't clarify anything

## 📜 Etymology
From "fluff" which comes from imitative origins, possibly from the sound or movement of something soft and light. First used in English in the 19th century to describe light, downy material.
