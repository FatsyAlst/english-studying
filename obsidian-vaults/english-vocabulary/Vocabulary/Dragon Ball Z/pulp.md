## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (High Frequency)
A soft, wet, shapeless mass; the soft tissue inside fruit or the soft parts of plants.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter was beaten to a pulp by the powerful attack.
> - Orange pulp is used to make juice.
> - The explosion reduced the metal to pulp.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Medium Frequency)
Cheap or sensational magazines or literature; material of low quality.

> [!example] Examples
> - He enjoyed reading pulp fiction magazines.
> - The scandal was featured in pulp magazines across the city.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Medium Frequency)
To reduce to pulp; to process into a soft mass.

> [!example] Examples
> - The machine pulped the fruit for juice production.
> - The impact pulped the damaged vehicle.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *pulpa* (soft flesh, pulp). Related to Romance languages and possibly connected to words meaning "soft" or "spongy."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in action contexts to describe severe damage
> - "Beat to a pulp" is an idiom meaning to beat someone severely
> - Can refer to either literal soft masses or figurative low-quality material
> - "Pulp fiction" refers to cheap adventure or detective stories
> - Pulpy can describe texture or low quality
