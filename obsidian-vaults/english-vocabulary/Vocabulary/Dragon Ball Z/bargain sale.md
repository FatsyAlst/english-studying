## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Common

A sale offering goods at reduced prices; a commercial event where items are sold at lower than usual cost. A good deal or opportunity to purchase something cheaply.

> [!example] Examples
> - There's a bargain sale happening at the market today
> - I found some great clothes at the bargain sale
> - The bargain sale prices are incredible

## 📜 Etymology
"Bargain" comes from Old French "bargaigne" (to haggle, dispute). "Sale" comes from Old Norse "sala" meaning to give or pass. Combined as a compound noun referring to discounted merchandise events.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Compound noun combining two commerce-related words
> - Used to describe promotional retail events
> - Common in advertising and shopping contexts
> - Implies both reduced prices and good value
