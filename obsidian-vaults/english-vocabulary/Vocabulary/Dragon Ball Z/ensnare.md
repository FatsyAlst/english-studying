## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Common, Literary)

To trap or capture someone or something in a snare; to catch in a trap or net; figuratively, to deceive or involve someone in a difficult situation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The hunter ensnared the rabbit in a trap.
> - Be careful not to be ensnared by their false promises.
> - Evil forces tried to ensnare the hero in their schemes.

## 📜 Etymology
From "en-" (prefix meaning to put into or cover with) + "snare" (trap). "Snare" comes from Old Norse *snara*. The word originally referred to physical trapping but evolved to include figurative entrapment.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (physical trapping with a snare/net) or figurative (deception, entrapment).
> - Literary and somewhat formal in tone.
> - Often implies danger, deception, or malice.
> - Related word: "snare" (a trap or to trap).
> - Common in fantasy, adventure, and dramatic narratives.
