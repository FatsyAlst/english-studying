## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
Skill in presenting or performing something in an impressive and entertaining way; the ability to capture and hold an audience's attention; theatrical or dramatic flair in performance.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighter displayed incredible showmanship with each dramatic technique.
> - His showmanship made the tournament entertaining for all spectators.
> - Pure showmanship rather than pure power determined the match's outcome.

## 📜 Etymology
From *showman* (one who presents shows or spectacles) + *-ship* (state or condition of being). The term emerged in the 19th century to describe the professional skills of entertainers and performers.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Describes the art of entertaining performance
> - Often implies style or drama alongside substance
> - Can be positive (skilled performance) or slightly negative (excessive theatrical display)
> - Common in sports, entertainment, and performance contexts
> - Related: showman (performer), showy (overly decorative)
> - Emphasizes audience engagement and impact
