## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (frequent)
To keep watch on someone or something; to maintain awareness of; to monitor closely; to track or observe.

> [!example] Examples
> - We need to keep tabs on the enemy's movements.
> - Keep tabs on his power level; it's rising dangerously.
> - The commander kept tabs on all his warriors.

## 📜 Etymology
From "tabs" (short for tabulations or records), originally referring to written records or notes. "Tab" comes from "tablet" or "tabulation." Emerged in American English in the early 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always followed by "on"
> - Suggests vigilant, ongoing monitoring
> - Common in military and competitive contexts
