## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Common)
Able to deal with difficult situations effectively; creative and practical in problem-solving; capable of finding solutions quickly and cleverly.

> [!example] Examples
> - A resourceful warrior can adapt to any battlefield.
> - She was resourceful enough to fix the broken equipment.
> - The team's resourceful approach saved them from disaster.

## 📜 Etymology
From "resource" + "-ful" (suffix meaning full of). Resource comes from Old French *ressource* (recovery, relief), from Latin *surgere* (to rise up). The word emphasizes having abundant inner resources to draw upon.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Positive quality; used to praise someone's adaptability and ingenuity
> - Implies both practical skills and creative thinking
> - Often paired with "clever," "intelligent," or "capable"
> - Synonyms: ingenious, creative, capable, practical, enterprising, inventive
> - Antonyms: helpless, incompetent, dependent
> - Common in character descriptions in action/adventure stories
