## 📖 Definition
*Noun/Verb* (Common)
A faint or wavering light; to shine faintly or intermittently. Can also mean a slight trace or hint of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - A glimmer of hope appeared when they heard the news.
> - The stars glimmer in the night sky.
> - His eyes glimmered with determination as he entered the ring.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *glimeren*, possibly related to Old English *glim* (brightness, light). Germanic origin.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to describe light that is subtle, faint, or intermittent
> - Metaphorically used to express small amounts of hope, doubt, or other emotions
> - More poetic/literary than "shine" or "gleam"
