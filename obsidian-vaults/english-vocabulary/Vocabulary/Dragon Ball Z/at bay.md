## 📖 Definition
*Phrasal Expression* (Common)
To keep something or someone at bay means to prevent it from coming close; to hold it at a distance; to keep it under control or prevent it from happening.

> [!example] Examples
> - The guards kept the enemy at bay.
> - He struggled to keep his fears at bay.
> - The medication keeps the disease at bay.

## 📜 Etymology
"At bay" likely comes from hunting terminology. "Bay" may derive from Old French *abaier* (to bark), referring to hunting dogs that bark at cornered prey, preventing their escape. The phrase developed to mean "held at a distance" or "prevented from advancing."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Idiom: the meaning is not literal but figurative
> - Used both for literal threats (enemies, predators) and abstract ones (fear, disease, problems)
> - Common pattern: "keep/hold + [thing] + at bay"
> - Emphasizes prevention, restraint, or control
> - Synonyms: keep away, ward off, prevent, hold off, check, restrain
> - Frequently used in action scenes and dramatic narratives
