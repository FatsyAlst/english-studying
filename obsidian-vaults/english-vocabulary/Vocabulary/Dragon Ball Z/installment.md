## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A partial payment of a debt or purchase; one of several partial payments made over time.

> [!example] Examples
> - She paid for the equipment in monthly installments.
> - The tournament prize will be paid in three installments.
> - He arranged an installment plan for his new spaceship.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
One episode or part of a serialized story, magazine, or television series.

> [!example] Examples
> - The next installment of the series airs next week.
> - This manga installment was the most intense yet.
> - Wait for the next installment to see what happens.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "estaler" meaning "to fix" or "to set up," from Proto-Germanic *stall- meaning "place" or "position." The word evolved to mean "setting up a payment plan" and later "one part of a series."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Two meanings: financial (payments) and sequential (episodes)
> - Alternative spelling: "instalment" (British)
> - "Installment plan" is common for consumer purchases
> - Very relevant in anime context for episode releases
> - "On installments" or "by installments" means in partial payments
> - Sense 2 is increasingly common with digital media and streaming
