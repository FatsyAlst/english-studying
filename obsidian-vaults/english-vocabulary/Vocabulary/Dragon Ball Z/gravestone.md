## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A stone slab or monument placed on a grave to mark where a person is buried; a tombstone.

> [!example] Examples
> - Many warriors ended up with gravestones marking their final resting places.
> - The gravestone bears the name and dates of the deceased.
> - Even the strongest fighters eventually face death and a gravestone.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound word from "grave" (from Old English "græf," burial place) and "stone" (from Old English "stan," hard rock). Combined to form a word specifically describing the marker placed at burial sites.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat solemn term
> - Often synonymous with "tombstone" or "headstone"
> - Common in contexts involving death, cemeteries, and memorials
