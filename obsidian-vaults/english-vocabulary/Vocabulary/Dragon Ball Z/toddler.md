## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Most Common

A child who has recently begun to walk, typically between 12-36 months old. Refers to a young child learning to walk and develop basic language skills.

> [!example] Examples
> - Young Gohan was just a toddler when his father died
> - That toddler is already doing superhuman tricks
> - The toddler can barely walk but fights like a warrior

## 📜 Etymology
From "toddle" (to move unsteadily on short legs) + "-er" (one who does). The verb "toddle" likely comes from "totter" or similar Middle English words for unsteady walking.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Standard, formal term for young children
> - Age range typically 1-3 years old
> - Used in developmental, medical, and parenting contexts
> - More specific than "baby" or "child"
