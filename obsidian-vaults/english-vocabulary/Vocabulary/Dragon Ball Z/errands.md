## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — Most Common

Short trips or tasks to accomplish specific purposes, typically to buy something or deliver a message; plural of "errand" meaning a mission or purpose requiring a journey.

> [!example] Examples
> - I have some errands to run in town today
> - Gohan helps with household errands
> - She's out running errands this afternoon
> - They sent him on some dangerous errands

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "ørendi" meaning message or business. Passed through Middle English as "erend" or "errand". Related to Old High German "irrung" (journey, mission). The word originally meant "message" before shifting to mean "task requiring a journey".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Always used in plural form "errands" when referring to multiple tasks
> - "Run errands" is the most common collocation
> - Typically refers to routine, short-term tasks
> - Common in daily life and household contexts
> - "Errand boy" refers to a young person sent on tasks
