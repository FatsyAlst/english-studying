## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The periodic rise and fall of sea levels caused by the gravitational pull of the moon and sun.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tide came in and covered the beach.
> - High tides can be dangerous for swimmers.
> - The tide went out, leaving shells on the sand.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (figurative, common)
A general tendency or current trend, especially in events or emotions.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tide of public opinion is shifting.
> - They rode the tide of success throughout the year.
> - The tide of battle turned in their favor.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "tid" meaning "time" or "season." The connection to water tides developed from observing the regular, time-based patterns of ocean levels.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used metaphorically in "turn the tides" meaning to reverse a situation
> - Plural "tides" is common when discussing multiple occurrences
> - Frequently paired with adjectives like "high," "low," or "strong"
