## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)
Wanting or aspiring to be something but not actually being it; pretending to be something one is not; prospective or intended

> [!example] Examples
> - The would-be hero failed miserably in his first challenge.
> - A would-be actress worked as a waitress while auditioning.
> - The would-be champion trained for years but never won.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "would" (past tense of will, expressing intention) and "be," used as a single adjective to describe someone who wishes to be or claims to be something.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually has a slightly negative or ironic connotation
> - Always hyphenated when used as an adjective before a noun
> - Indicates aspiration that hasn't been realized
