## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
The quality of treating people equally or justly without bias or favoritism.

> [!example] Examples
> - A true warrior fights with fairness and honor, not through deception.
> - The tournament requires fairness to ensure all fighters have an equal chance.
> - Fairness demands that we judge both sides equally.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "fægr" (beautiful, pleasing), combined with the suffix "-ness" to form an abstract noun. The word evolved to mean just and equitable treatment.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in discussions of justice, sports, and social issues
> - Can refer to both physical appearance and moral character (fair/fairness)
> - Common phrase: "in all fairness" = when trying to be just or acknowledge a valid point
