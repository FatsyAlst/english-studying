## 📖 Definition
*adjective / verb (past participle of "deplete")* (Common)
Reduced in amount so that there is not enough left; (of a person) exhausted.

> [!example] Examples
> - The phone's battery was completely depleted.
> - Fish stocks in the North Sea are seriously depleted.
> - After the exam week I felt totally depleted.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *dēplētus*, past participle of *dēplēre* "to empty out", from *dē-* (reversal) + *plēre* "to fill" (cf. *complete*, *replete*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in science, engineering and economics: *resource depletion*, *battery depletion*, *ozone depletion*.
> - Stronger than *reduced* — it suggests running out.
> - Related idea: [[saps]] (to drain energy gradually).
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[dwindling]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
