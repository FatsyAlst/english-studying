## 📖 Definition
*Noun* — (Common)
A person who defeats an opponent in a contest, battle, or competition; the winner; one who achieves victory.

> [!example] Examples
> - The victor of the tournament was declared the strongest warrior.
> - Every battle produces a victor and a defeated opponent.
> - He emerged as the victor after an intense and exhausting fight.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *victor*, meaning "conqueror" or "one who conquers," derived from *vincere* (to conquer, overcome). Used in English since medieval times, particularly in contexts of warfare and competition.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal or literary term for "winner"
> - Often used in formal competition contexts
> - More dignified than casual "winner"
> - Emphasizes conquest or overcoming
> - Related: victorious (adjective), victory (noun)
> - Common in sports, martial arts, and warfare contexts
