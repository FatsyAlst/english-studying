## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adverb* (very frequent)
To a great extent; significantly; considerably; in large amounts or degree

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior's power increased substantially
> - His strength improved substantially after training
> - The damage was substantially greater than expected
> - The two versions are substantially different
> - Substantially all of his power was unleashed

### Sense 2 — *adverb* (formal)
In essence; essentially; in substance rather than merely in appearance

> [!example] Examples
> - The agreement is substantially the same
> - The results are substantially correct
> - Substantially what I expected

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "substantia" meaning essence or nature, derived from "substare" meaning to stand under. The adverbial form "-ly" was added in English. The word emphasizes significance and considerable degree.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and analytical vocabulary
> - Used to emphasize degree or significance
> - Often appears in comparisons or descriptions of change
> - Can describe both quantitative (amount) and qualitative (nature) differences
> - Slightly more formal than "significantly" or "considerably"
