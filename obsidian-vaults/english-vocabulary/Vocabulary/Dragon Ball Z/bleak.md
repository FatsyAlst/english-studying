## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Cold, exposed, and barren in appearance; desolate and unwelcoming

> [!example] Examples
> - The bleak landscape offered no shelter
> - The mountain was cold and bleak
> - A bleak desert stretched before them
> - The bleak terrain reflected their despair

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (frequent)
Miserable, hopeless, or without promise; lacking hope or encouragement

> [!example] Examples
> - The situation looked bleak
> - Their prospects seemed bleak
> - A bleak future awaited them
> - The bleak reality of their defeat

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "bleikr" meaning pale or whitish. The word came into English in the 16th century, initially describing pale or exposed appearance, then extended to mean desolate and metaphorically hopeless.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can describe both physical landscapes (cold, barren) and emotional situations (hopeless, grim)
> - Often used to convey a sense of despair or danger
> - Formal and literary vocabulary
> - Creates atmosphere of bleakness and difficulty
