## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A partnership or arrangement in which two people or things work together or are associated closely.

> [!example] Examples
> - The two fighters worked in tandem to defeat their common enemy.
> - The producers and director work in tandem on every project.
> - They fought in tandem, combining their powers for maximum effectiveness.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A bicycle built for two people, or any arrangement where things are placed one behind the other.

> [!example] Examples
> - They rode a tandem bicycle down the street.
> - The cars were hitched in tandem.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "tandem" meaning "at length" or "finally." Originally used in the 18th century to describe two horses hitched one behind the other.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually used in the phrase "in tandem" meaning "working together"
> - Commonly appears in discussions of teamwork and coordination
> - Can be literal (bicycles) or figurative (working together)
