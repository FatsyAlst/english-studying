## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To rub or drag a sharp or hard object across a surface, making a harsh sound

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To remove a layer or coating from something by scraping

### Sense 3 — *noun* (common)
A mark or wound caused by scraping; a difficult situation

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior scraped his sword against the ground.
> - He scraped together enough resources for the journey.
> - The explosion left scrapes and burns on his body.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse *skrapa* meaning to scrape. Related to modern Scandinavian words for the same action. The harsh sound quality is inherent in the word's origins.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Scrape by/together" = to manage with difficulty, gather with effort
> - Creates mental image of harsh, abrasive action
> - Common in action sequences for sound effects
