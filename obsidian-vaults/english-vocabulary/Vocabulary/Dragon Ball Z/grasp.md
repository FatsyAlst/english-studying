## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Most Common)
To hold something firmly in the hand; to grip or clasp.

> [!example] Examples
> - He grasped the rope tightly.
> - She grasped his arm for support.
> - The baby grasped his father's finger.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common, Figurative)
To understand or comprehend something; to perceive or realize.

> [!example] Examples
> - She quickly grasped the concept.
> - He struggled to grasp the significance of the discovery.
> - Do you grasp what I'm saying?

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
A firm grip or hold; the act of holding something.

> [!example] Examples
> - He had a strong grasp on the situation.
> - The rope was within my grasp.
> - Within one's grasp = achievable or attainable.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English *graspen*, of uncertain origin. Possibly related to German *greifen* (to grasp). The word evolved from meaning a physical grip to metaphorically meaning understanding or comprehension.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Physical sense: to hold or grip firmly.
> - Figurative sense: to understand or comprehend.
> - "Within grasp" = able to be reached or achieved.
> - "Grasp at straws" = desperately try any option, however unlikely.
> - "Grasping" as adjective = greedy or avaricious (negative connotation).
> - Often used in academic/intellectual contexts for understanding.
