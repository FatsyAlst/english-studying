## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Deep sorrow and distress caused by loss, especially the loss of someone through death

> [!example] Examples
> - His grief was overwhelming after losing his mentor.
> - She struggled with grief following the tragedy.
> - The warrior's grief transformed into fierce determination.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *grief*, from Latin *gravis* meaning heavy or weighty. The metaphorical extension to emotional weight developed naturally from the physical sense of heaviness.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and serious word for deep emotional pain
> - Usually follows significant loss, particularly death
> - "Come to grief" = to fail or have an accident
> - Can be used in both literary and everyday contexts
> - 📺 Also seen in [[My Hero Academia Vigilantes]]
