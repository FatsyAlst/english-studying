## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
The act of digging or boring through something to create a passage or tunnel.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warriors were tunneling through the mountain to escape.
> - The machine is tunneling beneath the city.
> - They were tunneling under the fortress walls.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (figurative, common)
To move or make one's way through something with focused determination; to focus intensely on a goal.

> [!example] Examples
> - He kept tunneling forward despite the obstacles.
> - They tunneled through the defenses with their combined power.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "tonel," from Latin "tunna" meaning barrel or cask. The shape of a barrel resembled an underground passage, leading to the modern meaning.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes

> - Often used in action sequences for dynamic descriptions
> - Implies determination and forward momentum
