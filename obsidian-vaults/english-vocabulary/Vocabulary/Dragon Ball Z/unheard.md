## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (frequent)
Exceptional or remarkable to an unprecedented degree; not previously known or done; extraordinary or unimaginable.

> [!example] Examples
> - His power level was unheard of for his age.
> - Such a fusion technique was unheard of in their universe.
> - An unheard-of victory against the strongest enemy.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "un-" (not) + "heard" (past participle of "hear," from Old English "hieran"). Literally means "not heard before," metaphorically extended to mean "unprecedented" or "unknown."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with "of" (unheard of)
> - Emphasizes rarity or unprecedented nature
> - Conveys amazement or shock at something extraordinary
