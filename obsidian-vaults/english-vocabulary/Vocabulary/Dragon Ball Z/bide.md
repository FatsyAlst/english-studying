## 📖 Definition
*verb* (Formal/Archaic)

To wait or remain; to stay in a place or condition; to wait for something to happen.

> [!example] Examples
> - Bide your time until the right moment comes.
> - He would bide here until morning.
> - Bide a while; the answer will come.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *bīdan*, from Proto-Germanic *bīdaną*. Related to German *beiten* (to await). The word is primarily archaic or literary in modern English, though "bide one's time" remains in common use.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Archaic/literary; rarely used in modern English except in the phrase "bide one's time."
> - "Bide one's time" = wait for the right moment or opportunity (common idiom).
> - Past tense can be "bode" or "bided" (bode is archaic).
> - Often conveys patience, strategic waiting, or inevitability.
> - Primarily found in formal literature, fantasy, or historical contexts.
