## 📖 Definition
*noun* — (Common)

A situation or event that is ridiculous, absurd, or a mockery; something that is not genuine or sincere.

> [!example] Examples
> - The tournament rules were a complete farce.
> - Calling that match a fair fight was a farce.
> - The villain's promises turned out to be a farce.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French "farce," from Latin "farcia" (to stuff). Originally referred to a type of comedy play with broad humor.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies something is ridiculous and unworthy of respect
> - Often used critically or sarcastically
> - Can describe an event, situation, or institution
