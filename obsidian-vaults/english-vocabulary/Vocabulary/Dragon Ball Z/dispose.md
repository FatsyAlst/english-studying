## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To get rid of or throw away something; to discard or eliminate.

> [!example] Examples
> - How should we dispose of the garbage?
> - The company must dispose of its hazardous waste safely.
> - I need to dispose of these old boxes.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Formal/Literary)
To arrange, place, or position something; to organize or settle.

> [!example] Examples
> - He disposed the troops strategically.
> - The furniture was disposed throughout the room.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Literary/Archaic)
To deal with, handle, or settle a matter; to finish or complete.

> [!example] Examples
> - They quickly disposed of the business at hand.
> - The army disposed of the enemy quickly.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *disposer*, from Latin *disponere* (to arrange, set in order), from "dis-" (apart) + "ponere" (to place). The primary meaning related to arrangement evolved to include the sense of getting rid of or removing.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (most common): to discard or eliminate something (waste, objects).
> - Sense 2 (formal): to arrange or position (troops, furniture, etc.).
> - Sense 3 (archaic): to deal with or resolve quickly.
> - Phrase: "at someone's disposal" = available for their use.
> - Related: "disposition" (arrangement, temperament).
> - "Disposed to" = inclined or willing to do something.
