## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (Common)
A rope, cord, or chain used to hold and control an animal, especially a dog.

> [!example] Examples
> - The dog pulled against its leash.
> - He kept his power on a tight leash.
> - They released the beast from its leash.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (Common)
Figuratively, control or restriction imposed on someone or something.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior strained against the leash of his limitations.
> - She finally broke free from her leash of obligations.
> - The power was kept on a short leash to prevent disaster.

### Sense 3 — *Verb* (Common)
To put a leash on or to restrain with a leash.

> [!example] Examples
> - They leashed the dragon to prevent it from escaping.
> - Can you leash your anger before it destroys everything?
> - He leashed his true power during the preliminary matches.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "lesse" meaning "to let loose" or "to release," from Latin "laxus" meaning "loose." Related to "lax" and "relax."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Literal meaning refers to physical dog/animal control
> - Figurative meaning emphasizes control, restriction, or limitation
> - "Short leash" indicates tight control
> - "Long leash" indicates freedom or loose control
> - Common in action contexts when characters are holding back power
> - "Unleash" (without "on a") means to release or let loose
