## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
At the cost or harm of someone or something else; to the detriment of; in a way that damages or harms someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - He made jokes at the expense of his friend.
> - Progress was made at the expense of safety.
> - She achieved success at the expense of her family relationships.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "at" (preposition) + "the" (article) + "expense" (cost, harm). The phrase developed from the idea of someone bearing the cost or loss.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies unfairness or that a sacrifice was made
> - Can be used critically or descriptively
> - Commonly used in discussions of morality or fairness
> - Can refer to financial cost or emotional/reputational harm
