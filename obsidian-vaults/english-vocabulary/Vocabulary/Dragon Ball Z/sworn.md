## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (Medium Frequency)

Bound by or made under oath; committed by a solemn promise; bound by allegiance or loyalty.

> [!example] Examples
> - They were sworn enemies, destined to fight each other.
> - The sworn protector would defend the village at any cost.
> - He made a sworn statement to the authorities.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *swaran* (to swear), past participle *sworn*. Related to Germanic languages and ultimately from Indo-European roots meaning "to speak" or "to swear."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past participle of "swear"
> - Often used in compounds like "sworn enemies" or "sworn allies"
> - Implies a binding commitment or legal obligation
> - Common in legal or formal contexts
> - Emphasizes the serious nature of a promise or enmity
