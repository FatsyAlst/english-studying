## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Very Frequent)
A sudden and important discovery, development, or success after a period of difficulty or struggle.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku's new transformation was a major breakthrough in his power level.
> - The scientific breakthrough revolutionized the entire field.
> - After years of training, he finally achieved a breakthrough in his technique.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Frequent)
To break through a barrier or obstacle; to achieve a sudden advance or discovery.

> [!example] Examples
> - The fighters will breakthrough the energy barrier.
> - The army attempted to breakthrough enemy defenses.
> - Scientists hope to breakthrough in cancer research soon.

## 📜 Etymology
From *break* + *through*, a compound formed in the early 20th century. Originally a military term referring to penetrating enemy lines, later adopted in science and technology contexts.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies overcoming obstacles after sustained effort
> - Positive connotation, suggesting progress and success
> - Commonly used in scientific, technological, and personal development contexts
> - Can function as both noun and verb
