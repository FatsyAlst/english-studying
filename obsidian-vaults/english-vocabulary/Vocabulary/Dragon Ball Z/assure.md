## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To tell someone positively and confidently that something is true or will happen; to make certain.

> [!example] Examples
> - I assure you that everything will be fine.
> - He assured her of his commitment to the project.
> - Let me assure you, we have the situation under control.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English assuren, from Old French assurer, from Latin assecurare (to make secure).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used to convince someone or remove doubt
> - More formal than "tell" or "promise"
> - Common phrase: "I assure you" as a formal reassurance
