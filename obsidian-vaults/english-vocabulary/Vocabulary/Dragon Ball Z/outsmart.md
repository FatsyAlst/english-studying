## 📖 Definition
*verb* — (Common)

To defeat or get the better of someone by being cleverer; to outthink someone; to use superior intelligence or strategy.

> [!example] Examples
> - Goku outsmarted his opponent with a clever strategy.
> - The fighter tried to outsmart his rival with unexpected moves.
> - You cannot outsmart experience and skill.

## 📜 Etymology
Compound of "out-" (surpass) and "smart" (clever, shrewd). Became common in early 20th-century American English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Emphasizes intelligence and cunning over physical strength
> - Often contrasts with brute force
> - Can be used in competitive, strategic contexts
> - Similar to "outwit" but slightly less formal
> - Common in sports and competitive situations
