## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Quick, light, and agile in movement or action.

> [!example] Examples
> - You need nimble fingers for that job.
> - She was extremely nimble on her feet.
> - The dancer's nimble movements impressed the audience.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Quick to understand, think, or react; mentally agile.

> [!example] Examples
> - As a comedian, she has a quick ear and a nimble mind.
> - The company has been outsmarted by more nimble competitors.
> - His nimble wit kept everyone laughing.

---
From Old English *nǀmel* 'quick to seize or comprehend,' related to *niman* 'to take.' The -b- was added for ease of pronunciation (Middle English *nemel*).

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Positive term; describes both physical and mental quickness.
> - Can be used for both movements/actions and intellectual abilities.
> - Often used to describe quick, precise, or graceful movements.
> - Comparative: nimbler; superlative: nimblest.
