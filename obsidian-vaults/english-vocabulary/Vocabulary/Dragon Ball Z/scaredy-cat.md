## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal)
A person who is easily frightened or afraid; a cowardly person; someone who lacks courage.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't be such a scaredy-cat!
> - He's a scaredy-cat and won't go on the roller coaster.
> - The scaredy-cat refused to watch the horror movie.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "scared" + "cat" - combining the adjective "scared" with "cat" as a familiar, humorous diminutive. The term developed in the 20th century as informal children's language.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and often used in a teasing or playful way
> - Frequently used to describe someone who is fearful
> - Common in children's speech but used by adults too
> - Usually not meant as serious insult, more teasing
