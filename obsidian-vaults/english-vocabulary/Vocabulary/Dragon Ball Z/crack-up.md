## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Phrasal Verb* (Common)
To laugh loudly or uncontrollably; to break up laughing.

> [!example] Examples
> - The funny scene made everyone crack up
> - I crack up every time I see that moment
> - They all cracked up at the joke

---
### Sense 2 — *Phrasal Verb* (Less Common)
To experience a mental or emotional breakdown; to fail under stress or pressure.

> [!example] Examples
> - The pressure caused him to crack up mentally
> - Don't let the situation make you crack up
> - He almost cracked up during the ordeal

---
### Sense 3 — *Noun: Crack-up* (Less Common)
A breakdown or failure; also a humorous situation or something funny.

> [!example] Examples
> - That was a total crack-up
> - He had a crack-up after the losses
> - The scene was a crack-up

## 📜 Etymology
"Crack" comes from Old English "cracian" (to make a sharp sound). "Up" is a particle modifying the meaning. The compound "crack up" developed in early 20th century American English, with the laughing sense becoming dominant by mid-20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Most common meaning today: to laugh uncontrollably
> - Less common but still valid: to have an emotional/mental breakdown
> - "Crack up laughing" emphasizes intense laughter
> - Informal, conversational usage
> - Can be transitive (make someone crack up) or intransitive (crack up)
