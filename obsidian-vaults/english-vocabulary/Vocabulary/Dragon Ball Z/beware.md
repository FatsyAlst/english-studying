## 📖 Definition
*Verb/Interjection* — Common

To be cautious or wary of something; to take care to avoid something dangerous or unpleasant; to watch out for.

> [!example] Examples
> - Beware of that powerful enemy!
> - Beware the darkness within
> - Beware of falling rocks
> - Everyone should beware his strength

- beware + that clause
> - "Beware!" (warning)

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English "be" (prefix) + "ware" (aware). "Ware" comes from Old English "waer" meaning cautious or wary. The compound "beware" dates to around the 15th century as a combination of "be" + "aware", creating an imperative form meaning "be aware/cautious".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Somewhat archaic or formal register
> - Often used as a warning or command
> - Can be used as an imperative ("Beware!") or advisory ("Beware of...")
> - Common in fantasy, adventure, and dramatic contexts
> - More formal than "watch out" or "be careful"
> - Always followed by "of" + noun or the object directly
