## 📖 Definition
*Verb* — (Common)
To continue to have or hold something; to keep in possession or use; to maintain or preserve; to remember or keep in mind.

> [!example] Examples
> - The warrior managed to retain his power despite the injuries.
> - She retained her composure throughout the brutal battle.
> - He could not retain consciousness after the devastating blow.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *retenir*, from Latin *retinere*, from *re-* (back) + *tenere* (to hold). The root suggests holding something back or keeping it in place. Used in English since the 14th century in legal, medical, and general contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Verb meaning to keep, hold, or maintain
> - Can refer to physical possession or abstract qualities
> - More formal than "keep" or "hold"
> - Related: retention (noun), retentive (adjective), retainer (noun)
> - Common in legal, medical, and business contexts
> - Past tense: retained
> - Implies active maintenance or preservation
