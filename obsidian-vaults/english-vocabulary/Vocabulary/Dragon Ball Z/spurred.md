## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past tense)* (common)
Past tense of spur; motivated or encouraged someone to do something; gave an impetus or incentive.

> [!example] Examples
> - The challenge spurred him to train harder.
> - His anger spurred him into action.
> - The tournament spurred renewed interest in martial arts.

### Sense 2 — *verb (past tense)* (literal, common)
Used spurs (spiked devices) on a horse to make it move faster.

> [!example] Examples
> - He spurred his horse forward.
> - The rider spurred the horse up the hill.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "spura" referring to the device worn on a rider's heel. Extended metaphorically to mean anything that prompts action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually followed by "by" or "on" when expressing motivation
> - Commonly used in action and training contexts
> - Can be literal (with horses) or figurative (with motivation)
