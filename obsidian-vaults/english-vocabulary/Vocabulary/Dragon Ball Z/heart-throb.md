## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (Medium Frequency)

A person who is considered attractive and is the object of someone's romantic interest, especially a celebrity or public figure; the rhythmic beating of the heart.

> [!example] Examples
> - She thinks he's a real heart-throb with his charming smile.
> - The teenage girls screamed when their favorite heart-throb appeared on stage.
> - His heart-throb quickened as the battle intensified.

## 📜 Etymology
From *heart* + *throb* (to beat with a pronounced rhythm). The compound became popular in the 20th century to describe attractive celebrities.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can refer to either the person or the heartbeat sensation
> - Common in romantic or comedy contexts
> - Often used with adjectives describing physical attractiveness
