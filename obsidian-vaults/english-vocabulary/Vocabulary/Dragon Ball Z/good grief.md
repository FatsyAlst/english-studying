## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *interjection* (informal)
An exclamation expressing surprise, frustration, or exasperation; a mild curse or expression of dismay.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Good grief! He just destroyed the entire tournament arena!" exclaimed the announcer.
> - Good grief, that power level is off the charts!
> - "Good grief, what happened to the planet?" Bulma cried out in shock.

## 📜 Etymology
A euphemistic interjection combining "good" (a mild intensifier) with "grief" (sorrow or distress). The phrase dates back to at least the 19th century as a way to express mild shock or frustration without using profanity. It became widely known through Charles M. Schulz's "Peanuts" comic strip.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and exclamatory; typically used in speech or casual writing
> - Expresses surprise, frustration, or exasperation without being vulgar
> - Most commonly used as a standalone exclamation
> - Can appear at the beginning of a sentence or as a separate utterance
> - Somewhat dated in modern usage but still understood and used
