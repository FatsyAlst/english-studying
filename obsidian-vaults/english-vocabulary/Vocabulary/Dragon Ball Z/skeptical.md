## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (formal)
Not easily convinced; having doubts about something; inclined to question or disbelieve claims without sufficient evidence.

> [!example] Examples
> - The scientist was skeptical about Goku's claims of training in the afterlife.
> - Vegeta's skeptical attitude toward Earthlings reflected his superior nature.
> - The observers remained skeptical until they witnessed the incredible power display themselves.

## 📜 Etymology
From Greek "skeptikos," meaning "inquiring" or "reflective" (from "skeptesthai," meaning "to consider or examine"). The word originally referred to ancient Greek philosophers who questioned everything. It entered English in the 16th century with the same meaning of systematic doubt.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and neutral tone; used in academic, professional, and analytical contexts
> - Can be used positively (critical thinking) or negatively (stubborn disbelief)
> - Often appears with prepositions: "skeptical about," "skeptical of"
> - Synonymous with "doubtful," "dubious," or "cynical"
> - Implies questioning based on reason, not faith
