## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Most Common)
Enough or more than enough; plentiful or generous in quantity, size, or amount.

> [!example] Examples
> - There was ample time to get to the airport.
> - Ample free parking is available.
> - They had ample money for the trip.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Less Common)
(of a person's figure) Large or full, often in an attractive or euphemistic way.

> [!example] Examples
> - She stood with her hands on her ample hips.
> - These clothes belonged to a man of more ample girth.

---
**Pronunciation:** `/ˈæmplʊs/`

### *adjective* (archaic)
Large, abundant.

> [!example] Examples
> - The Latin amplus means spacious or plentiful.

---
Late Middle English: Via French *ample* from Latin *amplus* 'large, abundant.' Originally described physical spaciousness, extended to sufficiency by 16th century.

---
> [!tip] Notes
> - Positive connotation of abundance, more formal than 'plenty/enough.'
> - Euphemistic for body size (e.g., 'ample figure' = curvaceous/full).
> - Synonyms: abundant, generous, sufficient.
> - Avoid casual overuse like 'ample food.'
