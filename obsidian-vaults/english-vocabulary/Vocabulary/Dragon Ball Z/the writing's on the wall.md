## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
An unmistakable sign that something bad or inevitable is about to happen; doom or failure is apparent or certain.

> [!example] Examples
> - The writing was on the wall—they were going to lose the tournament.
> - When he refused to train, the writing was on the wall for his defeat.
> - The villain knew the writing was on the wall as his power weakened.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Biblical reference to the story of Belshazzar's Feast, where mysterious handwriting appeared on the wall, predicting the downfall of the Babylonian king. Metaphorically extended to mean any obvious sign of coming disaster.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Typically used when failure or defeat seems certain
> - Often implies that the outcome was predictable
> - Can be used before something happens to predict the future
