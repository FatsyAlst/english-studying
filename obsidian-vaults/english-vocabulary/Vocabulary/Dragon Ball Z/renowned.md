## 📖 Definition
*adjective* — (Common)

Widely known and respected; famous for a particular quality or achievement; having an excellent reputation.

> [!example] Examples
> - The renowned martial artist was invited to the tournament.
> - Goku became renowned throughout the world for his fighting skills.
> - The renowned master trained many students.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "renomee," meaning "fame" or "reputation," derived from "renomer" (to name again, to make famous).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal, prestigious tone
> - Often used in written or formal contexts
> - Emphasizes widespread recognition
> - Can describe people, places, or things
> - More formal than "famous"
