## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (present participle of "babble")* (Common)
Talking quickly in a way that is hard to understand, or that is silly or annoying.

> [!example] Examples
> - What is he babbling about?
> - She was so nervous that she started babbling.
> - Stop babbling and get to the point!

### Sense 2 — *verb / adjective* (Common)
(Of a baby) making speech-like sounds; (of water) making a continuous gentle sound.

> [!example] Examples
> - The baby is babbling — she'll be talking soon.
> - We sat by a babbling brook.

## 🔗 Collocations
- babble on (about something)
- incoherent babbling
- a babbling brook
- technobabble / psychobabble

---
## 📜 Etymology
Imitative of the repeated syllables of baby talk (*ba-ba*), Middle English *babelen* — not, as often believed, from the Tower of Babel.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Babble on* = keep talking for too long.
> - *Technobabble* = confusing technical jargon (classic in sci-fi).
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[yapping]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
