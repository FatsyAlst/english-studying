## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Common — Informal, mainly AmE)
Used to ask someone to stop or wait so that you can catch up with them.

> [!example] Examples
> - Hey, wait up! I'm coming with you!
> - Wait up — I need to tie my shoelace.
> - Can you wait up for me after class?

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb, "wait up (for someone)"* (Common)
To stay awake until someone comes home.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't wait up for me — I'll be home late.
> - My mum always waited up for me when I went out.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Wait* (Old North French *waitier* "to watch") + *up* in the sense of staying awake (cf. *stay up*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 is mainly AmE; BrE more often says *Wait for me!* or *Hang on!*
> - *Don't wait up* is the classic message when you'll get home late.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[hang tight]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
