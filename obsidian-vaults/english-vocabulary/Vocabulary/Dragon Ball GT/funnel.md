## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A tube that is wide at the top and narrow at the bottom, used for pouring liquids or powders into a small opening; a ship's chimney.

> [!example] Examples
> - Use a funnel to pour the oil into the bottle.
> - The ship's funnels were painted red.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To direct people, money, information etc. through a narrow channel or towards a particular place.

> [!example] Examples
> - The government funnelled money into renewable energy.
> - Fans were funnelled through two narrow entrances.
> - The map funnels the enemy team into a choke point.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Provençal *fonilh*, from Latin *infundibulum* "funnel", from *infundere* "to pour in" (*in-* + *fundere* "to pour").
