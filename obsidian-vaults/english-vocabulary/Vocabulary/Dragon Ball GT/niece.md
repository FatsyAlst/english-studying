## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Most Common)
The daughter of your brother or sister, or of your husband's or wife's brother or sister.

> [!example] Examples
> - My niece just turned six.
> - In Dragon Ball GT, Pan is Goten's niece.
> - I bought presents for my niece and nephew.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *niece*, from Late Latin *neptia*, from Latin *neptis* "granddaughter, niece".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Family vocabulary: *niece* (sobrinha) / *nephew* (sobrinho) / *cousin* (primo/a).
> - Pronunciation: /niːs/ — like *knees* with an *s* sound at the end.
> - Pan is Gohan's daughter, so she is Goku's granddaughter and Goten's niece.
