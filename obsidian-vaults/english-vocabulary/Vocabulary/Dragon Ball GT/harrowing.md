## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Extremely upsetting or frightening because it involves suffering.

> [!example] Examples
> - The survivors described their harrowing escape from the fire.
> - The first months abroad, alone and broke, were harrowing.
> - The documentary shows harrowing images of the war.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Adjective | harrowing |
| Verb | harrow (to distress; to break up soil) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- a harrowing experience / ordeal / journey / account / tale
- harrowing images / scenes

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the verb *harrow* "to distress deeply", a figurative use of *harrow*: to drag a harrow — a heavy frame with spikes — over the soil, tearing it up.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Stronger than *upsetting* or *difficult*.
> - Common in journalism about real suffering.
> - *A harrowing* [[ordeal]] is a very frequent collocation.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[ordeal]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
