## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb, "balk at"* (Common)
To be unwilling to do or accept something; to hesitate or refuse.

> [!example] Examples
> - Many students balk at the price of textbooks.
> - He balked at the idea of moving to the countryside.
> - She didn't balk at the challenge.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Less Common)
(Of a horse) to stop suddenly and refuse to go on; (baseball) to make an illegal motion as a pitcher.

> [!example] Examples
> - The horse balked at the fence.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English *balca* "ridge, bank of earth"; the verb developed the sense "to stop short as at an obstacle", like a horse refusing a jump.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Spelling: *balk* (AmE) / *baulk* (BrE).
> - Almost always followed by *at*.
> - Synonyms: *hesitate*, *refuse*, *shy away from*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[hesitant]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
