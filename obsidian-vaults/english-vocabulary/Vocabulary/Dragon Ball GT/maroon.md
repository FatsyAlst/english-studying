## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (usually passive)* (Common)
To leave someone in a place they cannot escape from, such as an island.

> [!example] Examples
> - They were marooned on a desert island.
> - The heroes were marooned on a distant planet.
> - The strike left thousands of passengers marooned at the airport.

### Sense 2 — *noun / adjective* (Common)
A dark brownish-red colour.

> [!example] Examples
> - He wore a maroon hoodie.
> - The team plays in maroon and white.
> - The walls were painted maroon.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Two words: the colour from French *marron* "chestnut"; the verb from *maroon* = a runaway slave in the Caribbean (Spanish *cimarrón* "wild, runaway"), which led to "to abandon someone on an island" (18th century).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - FALSE FRIEND: English *maroon* ≠ Portuguese *marrom*. *Maroon* is a dark **red**; *marrom* is **brown**.
> - Fun fact: Dragon Ball GT's colour in this vault is a dark red — basically maroon.
> - Synonym for the verb: *strand* (*stranded*).
