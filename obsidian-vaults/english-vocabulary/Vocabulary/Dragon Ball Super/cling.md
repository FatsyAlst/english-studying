## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb, "cling (on) to"* (Most Common)
To hold someone or something very tightly.

> [!example] Examples
> - The child clung to her mother's hand.
> - He clung on to the rope.
> - Wet clothes cling to your body.

### Sense 2 — *verb, "cling to" (figurative)* (Common)
To refuse to stop believing in or doing something; to keep something you are afraid to lose.

> [!example] Examples
> - She clung to the hope that her dog would come back.
> - The dictator clung to power for decades.
> - Don't cling to old habits.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
To stick to something, such as a smell or a material.

> [!example] Examples
> - The smell of smoke clung to my jacket.
