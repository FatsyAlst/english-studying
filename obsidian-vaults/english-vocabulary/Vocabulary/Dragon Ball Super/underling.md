## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Derogatory)
A person with a lower rank or position than someone else (used to show lack of respect).

> [!example] Examples
> - The boss always makes his underlings do the boring work.
> - Frieza's underlings were terrified of him.
> - He treats his assistants like underlings.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Under* + *-ling*, a suffix that forms nouns, often diminutive or contemptuous (*weakling*, *hireling*).

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Disrespectful; neutral alternatives: *subordinate*, *junior colleague*, *team member*.
> - See also [[henchman]].
