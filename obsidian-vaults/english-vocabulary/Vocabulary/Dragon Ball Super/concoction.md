## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
A mixture of different things, especially an unusual food or drink; (figurative) a made-up story or excuse.

> [!example] Examples
> - He drank a strange concoction of energy drink and coffee.
> - The bartender's latest concoction was surprisingly good.
> - His excuse was a complete concoction.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *concoctiō* "digestion", from *concoquere* "to boil together", from *com-* "together" + *coquere* "to cook".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often slightly humorous or suspicious in tone.
> - *Concoct an excuse / a story* = invent it, usually dishonestly.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[amalgam]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
