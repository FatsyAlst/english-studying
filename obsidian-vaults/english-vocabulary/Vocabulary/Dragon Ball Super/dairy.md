## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Most Common)
Milk and food made from milk, such as butter, cheese and yogurt.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm trying to cut down on dairy.
> - She's allergic to dairy.
> - Is this chocolate dairy-free?

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Relating to milk or milk products.

> [!example] Examples
> - dairy products
> - dairy cows
> - The yogurt is in the dairy aisle.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Less Common)
A place where milk is processed; a farm that produces milk.

> [!example] Examples
> - My uncle runs a dairy in Minas Gerais.

## 📜 Etymology
Middle English *deierie*, from *deie* "female servant, dairymaid" (Old English *dǣge* "kneader of bread") + *-ery*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Classic confusion: **dairy** (DAIR-ee, *laticínios*) vs **diary** (DIE-uh-ree, *diário*)!
> - Supermarket vocabulary: *Is this dairy-free / lactose-free?*
