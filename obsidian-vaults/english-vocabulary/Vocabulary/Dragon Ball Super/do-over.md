## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Informal, AmE)
A second chance to do something, especially because the first attempt was bad or unfair.

> [!example] Examples
> - The server crashed, so we got a do-over on the quiz.
> - Can I get a do-over? I wasn't ready!
> - If I could have a do-over, I'd choose a different major.

---
## 📜 Etymology
American English from children's games (20th century), from the verb phrase *do over* = do again.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal AmE; BrE says *another go*, *a redo*, *start again*.
> - Gaming: *a rematch*.
> - Related: *mulligan* (golf, card games) = a free second attempt.
