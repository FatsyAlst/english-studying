## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* (Less Common — Informal, AmE)
To get rid of something unwanted by giving or selling it to someone else, often dishonestly; to make someone else deal with a task you don't want.

> [!example] Examples
> - He tried to pawn off his old phone on me for 300 euros.
> - Don't pawn your chores off on your roommate!
> - They pawned the boring tasks off on the new intern.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Phrasal verb | pawn off (on / onto someone), pawned off |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- pawn something off on / onto someone
- pawn off the work / responsibility

---
## 📜 Etymology
Probably an alteration of *palm off* (from cheating at cards by hiding a card in your palm), influenced by *pawn* (to leave something at a pawnshop).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Pattern: pawn **X** off **on Y**.
> - *Palm off* is the more standard form, especially in BrE; *fob off* (BrE) = give someone something worse than they want.
> - Not the same as [[pawn]] (chess piece / pawnshop).
