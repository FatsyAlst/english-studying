## 📖 Definition
*noun (plural)* (Common)
Very small pieces of sugar or chocolate, often brightly coloured, used to decorate cakes, doughnuts and ice cream (BrE also *hundreds and thousands*).

> [!example] Examples
> - I'd like a vanilla ice cream with sprinkles, please.
> - The birthday cake was covered in rainbow sprinkles.
> - a doughnut with chocolate sprinkles

---

## 🔗 Collocations
- rainbow / chocolate sprinkles
- covered in sprinkles
- sprinkle salt / sugar / water on
- a sprinkle of rain (= light rain)

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the verb *sprinkle* (Middle English *sprenklen*, probably from Middle Dutch *sprenkelen*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - BrE: *hundreds and thousands* (coloured) / *chocolate strands*.
> - In Brazil: *granulado*; in Belgium and the Netherlands people eat *hagelslag* on bread!
> - Verb *sprinkle* = scatter small drops or pieces.
