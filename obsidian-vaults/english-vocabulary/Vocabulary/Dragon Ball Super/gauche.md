## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Less Common — Formal)
Socially awkward; lacking ease or grace in social situations.

> [!example] Examples
> - He felt gauche and out of place at the formal dinner.
> - It would be a bit gauche to talk about money at the wedding.
> - As a teenager I was shy and gauche.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French *gauche* "left; awkward" — reflecting the old prejudice that left-handed people were clumsy.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Pronunciation: /ɡoʊʃ/ (like *go* + *sh*).
> - Formal/literary; the everyday word is *awkward*.
> - Same left-hand prejudice: *sinister* (Latin "left") vs *dexterous* (Latin "right").
