## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Less Common)
The skill of shooting accurately at a target.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sniper's marksmanship was incredible.
> - Widowmaker players need excellent marksmanship.
> - The army tests soldiers' marksmanship regularly.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Noun | marksman, markswoman, marksmanship |
| Related | sharpshooter, sniper |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- excellent / expert marksmanship
- marksmanship training / skills
- a skilled marksman

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Marksman* ("one who shoots at a mark, a target", 17th century) + *-ship* (quality, skill).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Spelling: mark**s**manship — the common misspelling is *markmanship*.
> - In gaming the everyday word is *aim* (*good aim*, *aim training*).
> - Same suffix for a skill: [[showmanship]].
