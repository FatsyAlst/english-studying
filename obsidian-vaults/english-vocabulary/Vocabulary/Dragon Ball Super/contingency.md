## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common — Formal)
Something that might happen in the future, usually causing problems or requiring extra plans.

> [!example] Examples
> - We need a contingency plan in case the server crashes.
> - The budget includes 10% for contingencies.
> - The team prepared for every contingency.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Noun | contingency, contingencies; contingent (a group of people) |
| Adjective | contingent (on / upon = depending on) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- a contingency plan
- a contingency fund / budget / reserve
- prepare for every contingency
- contingent on / upon

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Late Latin *contingentia*, from *contingere* "to touch; to happen" (*com-* + *tangere* "to touch").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Project management and engineering: *contingency plan* and *contingency reserve* are standard terms.
> - *Contingent on* = depending on: *The offer is contingent on your exam results.*
> - Spelling: conti**n**gency (not *contigency*).
> - Portuguese cognate: *contingência*.
