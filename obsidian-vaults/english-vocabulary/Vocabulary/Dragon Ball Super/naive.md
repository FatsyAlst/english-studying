## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common)
Lacking experience or judgement, and therefore trusting people too easily or believing that things are simpler than they are.

> [!example] Examples
> - I was naive to think the paperwork would take one day.
> - It's naive to believe everything you read online.
> - Sorry if this is a naive question, but how does a transformer work?

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French *naïve*, feminine of *naïf* "natural, simple", from Latin *nātīvus* "native, natural".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Spelled *naive* or *naïve*; pronounced ny-EEV.
> - Negative but not insulting — it suggests innocence and lack of experience.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[gullible]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
