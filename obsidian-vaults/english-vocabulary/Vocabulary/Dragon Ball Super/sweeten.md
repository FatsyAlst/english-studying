## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (Common)
To make food or drink sweeter.

> [!example] Examples
> - She sweetened her tea with honey.
> - Sweeten the sauce with a little sugar.

### Sense 2 — *verb (figurative)* (Common)
To make an offer, deal or piece of news more attractive or easier to accept.

> [!example] Examples
> - They sweetened the deal by offering free shipping.
> - The company sweetened its job offer with a signing bonus.
> - To sweeten the bad news, the boss bought pizza for everyone.

## 🔗 Collocations
- sweeten the deal / offer / pot
- artificially sweetened
- unsweetened tea / yogurt
- sweeten the pill (BrE = make bad news easier to accept)

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Sweet* (Old English *swēte*) + the verb suffix *-en* (as in *brighten*, *darken*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Sweeten the deal* is very common in business and negotiation.
> - *Sweetener* = artificial sugar, or an extra incentive (sometimes a [[bribe]]).
> - Supermarket labels: *unsweetened* vs *sugar-free*.
