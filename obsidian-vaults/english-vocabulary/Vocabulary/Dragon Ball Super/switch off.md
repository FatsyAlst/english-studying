## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (Most Common)
To turn off a light, machine or device.

> [!example] Examples
> - Switch off the lights when you leave the lab.
> - Please switch off your phones during the exam.
> - Did you switch the oven off?

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (Common — Informal)
To stop paying attention; to relax and stop thinking about work.

> [!example] Examples
> - Ten minutes into the lecture, I completely switched off.
> - I find it hard to switch off after a long day.
> - Gaming helps me switch off.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Switch* (originally a thin flexible stick; in the 19th century a device for changing the direction of railway tracks or electric current) + *off*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Separable: *switch off the light* / *switch the light off* / *switch it off*.
> - AmE often prefers *turn off* for devices.
> - *Switch off* (= relax) is very common in BrE.
