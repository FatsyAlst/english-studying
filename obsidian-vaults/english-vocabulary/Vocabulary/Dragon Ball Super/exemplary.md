## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common — Formal)
So good that it should be copied by others; excellent.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her behaviour during the crisis was exemplary.
> - The student received an award for exemplary work.
> - He has an exemplary attendance record.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Less Common — Legal)
(Of a punishment) severe, to serve as a warning to others.

> [!example] Examples
> - The court imposed an exemplary sentence.
