## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (past of "fell")* (Common)
Cut down a tree.

> [!example] Examples
> - Thousands of trees were felled to build the road.
> - The storm felled several old oaks.

### Sense 2 — *verb (past of "fell")* (Less Common)
Knocked someone down with a hard blow; (figurative) made someone fall or become ill.

> [!example] Examples
> - He felled his opponent with a single punch.
> - Half the team was felled by the flu.

## 🔗 Collocations
- fell trees / timber
- felled by a blow / an illness
- in one fell swoop (= all at once)

---
## 📜 Etymology
Old English *fellan / fyllan* "to cause to fall" — the causative form of *fall*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Confusing pair: *fall* (fell, fallen) = intransitive; *fell* (felled, felled) = transitive, *make something fall*.
> - *In one fell swoop* — here *fell* is an old adjective meaning "fierce"; the phrase comes from Shakespeare's *Macbeth*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[topple]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
