## 📖 Definition
*adjective / verb (past participle of "botch")* (Common — Informal)
Done badly or carelessly, especially because of a lack of skill; ruined.

> [!example] Examples
> - The botched repair made the leak worse.
> - The robbers' botched plan ended with their arrest.
> - We botched the last team fight because nobody grouped up.

## 📜 Etymology
Late Middle English *bocchen* "to repair (badly)", of uncertain origin.
