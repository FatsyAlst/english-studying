## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common — Formal / literary)
A lack of good judgement; a foolish action, idea or plan.

> [!example] Examples
> - It would be folly to start the project without a plan.
> - Ignoring the warnings was sheer folly.
> - the follies of youth

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Less Common)
A building with no practical purpose, built as decoration in a park or garden (especially in 18th-century Britain).

> [!example] Examples
> - There's a small Greek-style folly in the castle gardens.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *folie* "madness, folly", from *fol* "fool, mad" (Latin *follis* "bellows, windbag").

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal/literary — common in fantasy and historical dialogue.
> - *It would be folly to…* = it would be very unwise to…
> - Architectural *follies* can be seen in many British and Belgian parks.
