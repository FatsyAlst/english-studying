## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
Making someone look more attractive than usual (clothes, photos, light).

> [!example] Examples
> - That jacket is really flattering on you.
> - The lighting in this room isn't very flattering.
> - It's the only flattering photo of me from the trip.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (Common)
Praising someone, sometimes too much; pleasing because it shows that people admire you.

> [!example] Examples
> - It was flattering to be invited to speak at the conference.
> - He made some flattering comments about my work.
> - It's flattering that you think I'm a pro player, but I'm not.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the verb *flatter*, from Old French *flater* "to flatter, caress".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Flattering* can be completely positive (clothes, photos) — unlike [[flattery]], which usually suggests insincerity.
> - *I'm flattered* = I'm pleased and honoured.
> - *Unflattering* = makes you look worse.
