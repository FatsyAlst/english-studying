## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
Cream that has been beaten until it is thick and light, often sweetened and served on desserts and drinks.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can I have whipped cream on my hot chocolate?
> - The waffle came with strawberries and whipped cream.
> - a can of whipped cream

---

---
## 📜 Etymology
From *whip* in the cooking sense "to beat cream or eggs quickly" (17th century) + *cream*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - In Brazil: *chantilly*. In English, *Chantilly cream* is specifically sweetened whipped cream with vanilla.
> - *Whipping cream* = the liquid cream you buy before whipping it.
> - Waffles with whipped cream — very Belgian!
> - Related: [[whip]].
