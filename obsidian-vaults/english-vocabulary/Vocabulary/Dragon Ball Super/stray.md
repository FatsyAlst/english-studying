## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (Common)
(Of an animal) lost or without a home; (of a thing) separated from the others or in the wrong place.

> [!example] Examples
> - There are lots of stray cats near the station.
> - A stray bullet hit the window.
> - I found a stray sock behind the washing machine.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To move away from where you should be, or from the subject you should be talking about.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't let the kids stray too far.
> - Sorry, I'm straying from the topic.
> - We strayed from the path and got lost.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Common)
An animal that has no home.

> [!example] Examples
> - She adopted a stray from the shelter.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *estraier* "to wander", probably from Vulgar Latin *estragare, from Latin *extra vagārī* "to wander outside" (cf. *astray*).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Go astray* = get lost or go wrong (*the letter went astray*).
> - *Stray from the point* = digress — useful in presentations.
