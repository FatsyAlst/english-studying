## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
A serious promise or agreement, especially a public or official one.

> [!example] Examples
> - The government made a pledge to cut emissions.
> - She kept her pledge to call home every Sunday.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Common)
To make a formal promise to give or do something.

> [!example] Examples
> - They pledged €1 million to the charity.
> - I pledge to study grammar for 30 minutes every day.
> - Goku pledged to protect the Earth.

### Sense 3 — *noun* (Less Common)
Something valuable left as a guarantee; (AmE) a student who has promised to join a fraternity or sorority.

> [!example] Examples
> - He left his watch as a pledge.

## 📜 Etymology
Mid-14th century *plegge* "surety, bail", from Old French *plege* "hostage, security", probably from a Frankish verb meaning "to guarantee".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal and solemn than *promise*.
> - Crowdfunding: a *pledge* = the amount someone promises to give.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[sworn]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
