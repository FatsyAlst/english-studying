## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Common — Informal)
Good at speaking in a pleasant, persuasive way, often to get what you want, so that you may not be completely trustworthy.

> [!example] Examples
> - A smooth-talking salesman convinced me to buy the extended warranty.
> - Don't trust that smooth-talking guy.
> - He's a smooth talker, but he never keeps his promises.

---
## 📜 Etymology
*Smooth* (Old English *smōþ*) in the sense "polished, pleasant in manner" + *talking*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually negative — it suggests manipulation.
> - Similar: *silver-tongued* (more positive, literary), *slick*, *glib*.
> - Opposite: [[blunt]], *plain-spoken*.
