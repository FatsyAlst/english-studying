## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Less Common — Informal, mainly AmE)
Behaving as if you are more important than you are, especially without respect for people in authority; arrogant.

> [!example] Examples
> - Don't get uppity with me, young man!
> - The new manager seemed a bit uppity.
> - Beerus has no patience for uppity mortals.

## 📜 Etymology
American English, recorded in 1880 (in the *Uncle Remus* stories), from *up* + *-ity*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and disapproving.
> - Historically used in the US South as a racist insult for Black people who asserted their rights — be careful with it today.
> - Neutral alternatives: *arrogant*, [[cocky]], *presumptuous*.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[stuck-up]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
