## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (Less Common — Formal / humorous)
(Of food or drink) extremely pleasant to taste; delicious.

> [!example] Examples
> - The bakery sells delectable chocolate pastries.
> - Beerus finds Earth's food absolutely delectable.
> - a delectable dessert

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Adjective | delectable |
| Adverb | delectably |
| Noun | delectation (formal: enjoyment) |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- a delectable dish / dessert / treat / aroma
- absolutely delectable

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin *dēlectābilis*, from *dēlectāre* "to delight, charm".

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More elaborate than *delicious* — often used playfully.
> - Taste scale: *tasty* → *delicious* → *delectable* / *scrumptious* (informal).
