## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (formal)
To praise or express approval of someone or something; to recommend highly.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (formal)
To entrust or hand over to someone's care.

> [!example] Examples
> - The teacher commended the students for their excellent work.
> - I commend you on your bravery and determination.
> - We commend this project to your attention and support.

## 🔗 Collocations
- commend (someone) for (something)
- highly commend

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "commendare," composed of "com-" (with) and "mandare" (to entrust), originally meaning to put in someone's care or hands.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than "praise" or "compliment"
> - Often used in official or professional contexts
> - Can also mean to entrust or commit to someone's care (archaic usage)
