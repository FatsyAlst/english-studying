## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* (common)

To obtain or acquire something, especially with difficulty; to visit someone briefly; to pass by

> [!example] Examples
> - How did you come by this information?
> - Good jobs are hard to come by these days.
> - I'll come by your house tomorrow.
> - He came by the opportunity through a friend.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "cuman" (come) + "by". The phrasal verb combines "come" with "by" to suggest obtaining something or passing by a location.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Hard to come by" is a very common expression
> - Multiple meanings: obtain, visit, or pass by
> - Context clarifies the intended meaning
