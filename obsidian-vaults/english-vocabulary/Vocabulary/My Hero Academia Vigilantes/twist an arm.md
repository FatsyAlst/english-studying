## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
To persuade or pressure someone into doing something against their will; to use coercion or influence.

> [!example] Examples
> - You didn't have to twist my arm; I was already willing to come.
> - She twisted his arm to get him to invest in the project.
> - It took a lot of arm-twisting to convince them to agree.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the literal image of physically twisting someone's arm to force them to comply. The figurative sense has been used since at least the 19th century to mean persuading or pressuring someone.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Figurative idiom meaning to persuade or pressure
> - "Take some arm-twisting" means require persuasion
> - Often used with "not" to say persuasion wasn't needed
> - Can be both serious and humorous in tone
