## 📖 Definition
*noun* (informal)

The entertainment industry; show business; the world of theater, film, television, and live performance

> [!example] Examples
> - She dreams of making it big in showbiz.
> - Showbiz is a competitive and glamorous industry.
> - Many celebrities come and go in showbiz.

---

|----------------|-------|
| Noun | showbiz, show business |

## 🔗 Collocations

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "show" + "business", contracted into "showbiz" in the mid-20th century. The shortened form became standard in casual entertainment journalism and conversation.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal, colloquial term
> - More casual than "show business"
> - Associated with glamour, fame, and entertainment culture
