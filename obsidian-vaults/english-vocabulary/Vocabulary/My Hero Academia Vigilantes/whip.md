## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A device consisting of a long flexible cord or strap with a handle, used for striking or controlling animals.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To strike with a whip; to beat or lash.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (figurative)
To move quickly and suddenly; to whip around.

### Sense 4 — *noun* (slang)
An impressive or excellent car; a vehicle.

> [!example] Examples
> - The whip cracked loudly in the distance.
> - He whipped the horse to make it go faster.
> - She whipped around the corner at high speed.
> - That's a nice whip you're driving!

### Sense 5 — *verb* (Common — Cooking)
To beat cream, eggs etc. quickly until thick and light.

> [!example] Examples
> - Whip the cream until it forms soft peaks.
> - Fold the whipped egg whites into the batter.

### Sense 6 — *adjective, "whipped"* (Less Common — Slang)
(Of a person) completely controlled by their partner.

> [!example] Examples
> - Ever since he got a girlfriend, he's totally whipped.

## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English and Old English "hwip," possibly imitative of the sound a whip makes. The slang sense for "car" emerged in African American Vernacular English.
