## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Lacking foresight or the ability to see consequences; failing to consider long-term effects.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (medical)
Having myopia; able to see objects close up clearly but not those far away.

> [!example] Examples
> - It would be shortsighted to ignore climate change.
> - His decision was shortsighted and caused problems later.
> - She is shortsighted and needs glasses to see the board at school.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "short" and "sighted." The figurative sense developed from the literal medical term for myopia, meaning inability to see far distances.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (figurative): most common usage meaning lacking foresight
> - Sense 2 (medical): less common, usually replaced by "nearsighted" or "myopic"
> - Often used to criticize poor planning or judgment
> - Formal and somewhat negative connotation
