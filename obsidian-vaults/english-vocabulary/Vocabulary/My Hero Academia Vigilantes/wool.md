## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

The soft, thick fiber from the fleece of sheep or other animals, used to make yarn and cloth; fabric made from this material

> [!example] Examples
> - This sweater is made of pure wool and very warm.
> - Wool is a natural, renewable fiber.
> - She knitted a wool scarf for the winter.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English "wull" from Germanic origins, related to German "Wolle". The word refers to the natural fiber that has been used for clothing since prehistoric times.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used attributively: "wool coat", "wool sweater"
> - "Pull the wool over someone's eyes" = to deceive someone
> - Wool is valued for warmth and durability
