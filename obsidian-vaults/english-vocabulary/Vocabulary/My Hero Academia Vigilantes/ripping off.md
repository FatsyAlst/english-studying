## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To tear or pull something away suddenly and forcefully.

### Sense 2 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To cheat or defraud someone; to steal from or overcharge someone.

> [!example] Examples
> - He ripped off his shirt in anger.
> - The vendor was ripping off tourists by overcharging them.
> - She ripped off the label from her new clothes.

## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "rip" (to tear) and "off" (away/from). The fraudulent sense developed in the mid-20th century as slang.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: literal tearing or pulling action
> - Sense 2: deceptive or fraudulent action (slang)
> - "[[rip-off|Rip-off]]" as a noun means a scam or overpriced product
> - Common in both literal and figurative contexts
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[rip-off]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
