## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A long, narrow excavation or channel dug in the ground; often used for drainage or boundaries.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To get rid of something or someone; to abandon or leave behind.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (common)
To force an aircraft to land in water.

> [!example] Examples
> - There was a ditch running along the edge of the road.
> - She ditched her boyfriend after he lied to her.
> - He ditched his classes to go to the beach.
> - The pilot ditched the plane in the ocean.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "dic" and Middle English "diche," related to Old Saxon "dik" meaning dike or embankment. The slang sense "to abandon" developed in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Noun: a physical excavation in the ground
> - Verb (slang): to get rid of or abandon someone/something
> - "Ditch school" is a common phrase meaning to skip classes
> - Informal tone when used as a verb
