## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A pneumatic or electrical tool with a percussion action, used for breaking up or drilling through hard surfaces like concrete or pavement.

> [!example] Examples
> - The construction worker used a jackhammer to break the concrete.
> - The noise from the jackhammer echoed through the street.
> - A jackhammer is essential for demolition work.

## 📜 Etymology
A compound word combining "jack" (a device for lifting) and "hammer," referring to the mechanical action that resembles repetitive pounding. The term emerged in the early 20th century with the development of pneumatic tools.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Jackhammers are loud and powerful tools requiring safety precautions.
> - Common in construction and demolition work.
> - The rhythmic noise is often associated with urban construction.
