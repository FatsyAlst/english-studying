## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A small cake made of batter fried in fat, typically twisted or ring-shaped and coated with sugar or cinnamon.

> [!example] Examples
> - She bought a chocolate cruller from the bakery.
> - The café serves fresh crullers every morning.
> - He bit into the crispy cruller, savoring the sweet coating.

## 📜 Etymology
From Dutch "krullen" (to curl), referring to the twisted or curled shape of the pastry. The word entered English in the 18th century through Dutch influence in New Amsterdam (New York).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Crullers are similar to doughnuts but typically smaller and have a lighter texture.
> - The twisted shape is a defining characteristic of traditional crullers.
> - Often served at breakfast or as a snack with coffee.
