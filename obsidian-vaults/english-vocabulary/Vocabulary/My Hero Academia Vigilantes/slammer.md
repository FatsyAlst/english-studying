## 📖 Definition
*noun* (slang)

Slang term for jail or prison

> [!example] Examples
> - He spent five years in the slammer for his crimes.
> - The criminals were thrown in the slammer.
> - Don't break the law or you'll end up in the slammer.

## 🔗 Collocations
- in the slammer
- throw someone in the slammer
- get thrown in the slammer
- end up in the slammer

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "slam" (to shut forcefully). The term refers to the slamming sound of jail cell doors. It's slang that became popular in the early 20th century.
