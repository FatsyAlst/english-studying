## 📖 Definition
*Idiom* (frequent)

No skin off (one's) nose/back: it is not a concern to someone; something doesn't matter or affect someone negatively.

> [!example] Examples
> - If you want to skip the party, it's no skin off my back.
> - It's no skin off your nose if he doesn't come.
> - Whether they agree or not is no skin off my back.

## 📜 Etymology
An American idiom with uncertain origins. "Skin" may refer to the idea of losing something valuable (one's skin), making it metaphorically meaningless to lose something unimportant. The phrase has been used in American English since at least the early 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - This is an informal idiom expressing indifference or lack of concern.
> - "Nose" and "back" versions are both common; "back" is more modern.
> - Often used to reassure someone that a decision won't negatively affect you.
