## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A piece of useful information given secretly or informally; a hint or warning, often provided confidentially to warn someone of danger or opportunity.

> [!example] Examples
> - He received a tip-off about the upcoming police raid.
> - The detective got a tip-off from an informant.
> - Thanks to a tip-off from a friend, she knew to avoid that street.

## 📜 Etymology
A compound of "tip" (a piece of information) and "off" (indicating separation or warning). The term emerged in early 20th-century English, particularly in American usage, referring to confidential information or advance warning.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Tip-off" suggests confidential or insider information.
> - Often used in crime fiction and law enforcement contexts.
> - Can indicate either a warning of danger or an opportunity.
> - The hyphen is standard and maintained in written form.
