## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

A sea creature with a soft body, ten arms with suction cups, and the ability to change color and release ink as defense

> [!example] Examples
> - The giant squid is one of the largest creatures in the ocean.
> - A squid can change its color to match its surroundings.
> - Squid is a popular food in many Asian cuisines.

## 📜 Etymology
From Italian "squid" (possibly from Latin origins), the term entered English in the 17th century. The exact etymology is debated, but it may relate to the squirting action of ejecting ink.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Related to octopus, but has ten arms instead of eight
> - Known for intelligence and camouflage abilities
> - Common in marine biology and cuisine
