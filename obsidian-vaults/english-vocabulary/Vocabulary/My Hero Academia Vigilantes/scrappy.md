## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (informal)

Feisty, eager to fight or compete; determined and spirited despite being small or weak; consisting of scraps or fragments

> [!example] Examples
> - The scrappy fighter refused to give up despite being outnumbered.
> - His scrappy team somehow made it to the finals.
> - She had a scrappy attitude that impressed everyone.

## 📜 Etymology
From "scrap" (fragment or leftover) + "-y" suffix. Originally referred to something made of scraps; evolved to mean scrappy as in feisty and determined (possibly from scrapping/fighting).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal but positive connotation (determination and spirit)
> - Often describes underdogs or smaller fighters with big hearts
> - Common in sports and action narratives
