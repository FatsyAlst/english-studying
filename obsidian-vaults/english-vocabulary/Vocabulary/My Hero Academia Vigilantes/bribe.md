## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Money or a gift given to someone to influence their judgment or to persuade them to do something dishonest or illegal

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To give money or a gift to someone to persuade them to do something dishonest or illegal

> [!example] Examples
> - The official was caught taking a bribe from a construction company.
> - They attempted to bribe the witness to change his testimony.
> - No amount of money could bribe that honest judge.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "bribe" meaning a piece of bread or morsel, later evolved to mean a gift given to influence someone. The etymology suggests the practice of offering small gifts evolved into corrupt inducements.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes

> - Formal legal term
> - Bribery is illegal in most countries
