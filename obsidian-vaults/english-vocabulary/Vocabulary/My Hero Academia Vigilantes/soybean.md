## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

A legume plant that produces protein-rich beans, widely used as food and in various products

> [!example] Examples
> - Tofu is made from soybeans and is a staple in Asian cuisine.
> - Soybean oil is commonly used in cooking and food manufacturing.
> - Farmers often rotate soybean crops with other plants to maintain soil fertility.

## 📜 Etymology
From Japanese "daizu" (大豆, large bean) blended with English "soy", which itself comes from the Chinese "soy" (醤油, soy sauce). The term became standard in English agriculture in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually plural "soybeans" when referring to multiple beans
> - Used attributively in phrases like "soybean crop" or "soybean meal"
