## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A thin cord or rope made of twisted fibers

### Sense 2 — *noun* (figurative)
Control or influence over someone; a condition or limitation attached to something

### Sense 3 — *noun* (music)
One of the wires or cords stretched across a musical instrument that vibrate to produce sound

> [!example] Examples
> - The puppet was controlled by strings attached to its limbs.
> - He had a string of victories in the tournament.
> - There are no strings attached to this offer—it's completely free.
> - The guitar has six steel strings.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English "streng" from Germanic origins, related to "strong". The word originally referred to something that binds or connects, reflecting its function.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Pull strings" = to use influence to get something done
> - "No strings attached" = without conditions or obligations
> - Metaphorical use is common in everyday language
