## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A reward offered for capturing or killing someone, or for information leading to their capture.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A large amount of something desirable; an abundance or generous supply.

> [!example] Examples
> - There was a bounty on the criminal's head.
> - The harvest brought a bounty of fresh vegetables.
> - The hunter tracked the outlaw for the bounty money.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "bonté" meaning "goodness" or "kindness." Later evolved to mean a generous gift or reward, especially one offered by a government.
