## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A person who is intolerant of differing opinions and who maintains an unreasonable, prejudiced stance, especially regarding race, religion, ethnicity, or politics.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was labeled a bigot for his discriminatory comments.
> - The organization works to educate bigots about tolerance.
> - Some people hide their bigotry, while others are open bigots.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "bigot," with uncertain origins possibly from Old French or Scandinavian sources. The word entered English in the 16th century and has been used to describe intolerant individuals.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Bigot" is a derogatory term used to describe someone with intolerant views.
> - The word is often used in serious social and political discussions.
> - Related words include "bigotry" (the behavior/attitude) and "bigoted" (showing bigotry).
