## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (frequent)

To seal off or close an area with a cord, tape, or barrier to prevent access; to rope off an area for security or investigation.

> [!example] Examples
> - Police were cordoning off the crime scene with yellow tape.
> - The construction site was cordoning off the dangerous area.
> - They cordoned the area to prevent public access during the investigation.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French "cordon," derived from Old French "corde" (rope). The word refers to a line of rope, tape, or barriers used to enclose or separate an area. Entered English in the 17th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Cordoning off" is commonly used in police and emergency contexts.
> - Often associated with crime scene investigations.
> - Related noun: "cordon" (the cord or barrier itself).
