## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (very frequent)

Close and careful examination or inspection of something; detailed and often critical attention.

> [!example] Examples
> - Her proposal came under close scrutiny from the board.
> - The investigation involved careful scrutiny of all evidence.
> - His financial records faced intense scrutiny.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "scrutinium" (examination, inquiry), derived from "scrutari" (to search, examine). The word entered English in the 17th century and is used in formal and official contexts.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Scrutiny" often implies critical or suspicious examination.
> - Commonly used in legal, academic, and official contexts.
> - Related verb: "scrutinize" (to examine carefully and critically).
