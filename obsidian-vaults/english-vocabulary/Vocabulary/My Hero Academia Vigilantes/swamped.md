## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Overwhelmed with work, responsibilities, or tasks; having too much to do.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To sink or become flooded with water; to cause to sink.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (figurative)
To overwhelm or bury under a large amount of something.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'm completely swamped with work right now.
> - The boat was swamped by the large wave.
> - We've been swamped with requests since the announcement.

## 📜 Etymology
From "swamp," which comes from possibly Algonquian origin referring to a wetland or marshy area. The figurative sense of "overwhelm" developed from the image of being buried or flooded in water.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: most common usage, often describing being busy or stressed
> - Sense 2: literal meaning about water or flooding
> - Sense 3: figurative meaning about being overwhelmed
> - Often used in informal, conversational contexts
