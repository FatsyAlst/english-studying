## 📖 Definition
*noun* (formal)

The quality of being discreet; judgment or prudence in making decisions; the power or freedom to make choices based on one's own judgment

> [!example] Examples
> - The agent handled the sensitive information with discretion and care.
> - At the principal's discretion, some students received extra credit.
> - He showed great discretion in keeping the secret.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "discretio" meaning separation or distinction, derived from "discretus" (separated, distinct). The word evolved to mean careful judgment about what to reveal or do.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal register; common in legal and professional contexts
> - "At someone's discretion" = left to their judgment or choice
> - Often paired with trust and confidentiality
