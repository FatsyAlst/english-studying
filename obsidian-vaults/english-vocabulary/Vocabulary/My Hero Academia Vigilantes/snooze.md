## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To sleep lightly or briefly; to doze off for a short period of time.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A short period of light sleep; a nap.

> [!example] Examples
> - He hit snooze on his alarm three times before getting out of bed.
> - I took a quick snooze after lunch.
> - Don't snooze during the important meeting!

---
## 📜 Etymology
Originally came from British slang in the 1820s, possibly influenced by the word "snore" or similar onomatopoetic origins.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used with "snooze button" on alarm clocks
> - Informal and colloquial in tone
> - Common in everyday speech about sleep
