## 📖 Definition
*noun phrase* (idiom)

A bad person who has a negative influence on others; one corrupt or dishonest person in a group

> [!example] Examples
> - One bad apple spoiled the whole team's reputation.
> - The coach wanted to remove the bad apple from the lineup.
> - A bad apple in the organization can damage everything.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From the proverb "one bad apple doesn't spoil the barrel" (actually, it does—rotting apples release ethylene gas). The phrase has been used idiomatically for centuries to refer to problem individuals in groups.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Idiomatic expression—not literal apples
> - Often used to explain negative influence on a group
> - Related saying: "One bad apple spoils the bunch"
