## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* (formal)

To respect and admire someone greatly; to regard someone with deep respect and honor

> [!example] Examples
> - The community held the teacher in high esteem for her dedication to students.
> - He was held in high esteem by his colleagues for his ethical practices.
> - The founder was held in high esteem throughout the organization.

---
## 📜 Etymology
"Hold" from Old English "haldan" meaning to keep or maintain; "esteem" from Latin "aestimare" meaning to value or appraise. The phrase combines these to express maintained valuation of a person.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and somewhat literary expression
> - Often used in passive voice: "be held in high esteem"
> - Frequently appears in formal writing or professional contexts
