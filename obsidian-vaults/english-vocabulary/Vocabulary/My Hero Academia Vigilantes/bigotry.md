## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

Intolerance and prejudice, especially against people of a different race, religion, ethnicity, or political belief; the state of being a bigot.

> [!example] Examples
> - The country has a history of bigotry against minorities.
> - Religious bigotry has caused conflicts throughout history.
> - Fighting against bigotry requires education and awareness.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "bigoterie," derived from "bigot" (excessive devotion or prejudice). The origin of "bigot" itself is uncertain but possibly from Old French or Scandinavian roots. The word entered English in the 16th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Bigotry refers to the attitude or behavior of intolerance.
> - It's a serious term used in formal and social discourse.
> - Related words include "bigot" (a person with these views) and "bigoted" (showing bigotry).
