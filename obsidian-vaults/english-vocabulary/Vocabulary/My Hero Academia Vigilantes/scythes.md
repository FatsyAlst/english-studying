## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A tool with a long handle and a curved blade, traditionally used for cutting grass, grain, or other crops.

> [!example] Examples
> - The farmer used a scythe to harvest the wheat.
> - An old scythe hung on the barn wall.
> - The Grim Reaper is often depicted carrying a scythe.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "siþe," related to "sickle." The word has Germanic roots and has been used in English since at least the 14th century to describe the harvesting tool.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - The scythe is an ancient agricultural tool predating modern machinery.
> - It's iconic in cultural imagery, particularly associated with the Grim Reaper.
> - Requires skill and technique to use effectively for harvesting.
