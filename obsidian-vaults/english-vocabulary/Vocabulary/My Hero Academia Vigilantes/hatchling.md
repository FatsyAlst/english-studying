## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (uncommon)
A young animal that has recently hatched from an egg, especially a bird or reptile.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (figurative)
A young or newly established person, organization, or entity.

> [!example] Examples
> - The sea turtles are just hatchlings, barely an inch long.
> - Baby dragons in fantasy stories are often depicted as tiny hatchlings.
> - He's a hatchling in the film industry, just starting his career.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Derived from "hatch" (to emerge from an egg) plus the diminutive suffix "-ling," indicating a young or small creature.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually refers to birds, reptiles, or fish
> - Can be used figuratively for newly established entities
> - More common in fantasy or nature contexts
