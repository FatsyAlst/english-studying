## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (frequent)

To force someone to leave a property, typically a rented apartment or house, usually through legal action or court order.

> [!example] Examples
> - The landlord is evicting tenants who haven't paid rent.
> - They were evicted from their apartment due to lease violation.
> - The notice of eviction was posted on the door.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "evicere" (to overcome, conquer), composed of "ex-" (out) and "vincere" (to conquer). The word entered English in the 17th century with legal connotations.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Eviction is typically a legal process requiring proper notice and court involvement.
> - Common reasons for eviction include non-payment of rent and lease violations.
> - The word is formal and commonly used in legal contexts.
