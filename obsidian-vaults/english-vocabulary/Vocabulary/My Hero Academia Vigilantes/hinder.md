## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (frequent)

To create difficulties for someone or something; to obstruct, delay, or prevent progress or movement.

> [!example] Examples
> - Bad weather hindered the rescue efforts.
> - His injuries hindered his ability to work.
> - We must remove obstacles that hinder our progress.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "hinderian," related to Old High German "hintar" (back), combined with the suffix "-an" (to make). The word has Germanic roots and has been used in English since at least the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Hinder" is more formal than "block" or "stop."
> - Often used in formal and technical contexts.
> - Related noun: "hindrance" (something that hinders).
