## 📖 Definition
*noun* (slang/informal)

Coffee; a cup of coffee

> [!example] Examples
> - I need a cup of joe to wake up in the morning.
> - Let's grab a joe before work.
> - A hot joe hit the spot on that cold day.

---
## 📜 Etymology
The origin is uncertain. It may derive from the name "Joe" (associated with the common man—"ordinary joe"), or possibly from the coffee port of Mocha in Yemen. The term became popular in the early 20th century in American English.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Very informal/slang term
> - Extremely common in casual American English
> - "Ordinary joe" can also mean an average person
