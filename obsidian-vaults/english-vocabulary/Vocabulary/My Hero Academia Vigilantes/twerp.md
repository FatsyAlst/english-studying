## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (informal)
A small, insignificant, or annoying person; a pesky or impudent youngster.

> [!example] Examples
> - That little twerp is always causing trouble!
> - Don't pay attention to that twerp; he's just trying to get a reaction.
> - The older kids bullied that poor twerp on the playground.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain, possibly related to "twirp" or derived from Twerpington, a character from children's literature. First appeared in early 20th century British slang.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and often derogatory
> - Usually refers to a small or young person, especially an annoying one
> - Common in youth-oriented media and stories
> - Not considered offensive, but mildly insulting
