## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
Delicate skill or ingenuity in performance; refined or elegant execution of something

### Sense 2 — *verb* (less common)
To accomplish something with finesse; to manipulate skillfully

> [!example] Examples
> - The dancer performed with remarkable finesse.
> - The artist showed great finesse in handling the delicate brushwork.
> - She finessed her way through the difficult negotiation.

## 📜 Etymology
From French "finesse" meaning delicacy or subtlety, derived from Latin "finitus" (finished, refined). The term emphasizes skillfulness and elegance rather than brute force.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies sophisticated skill and elegance
> - Often contrasted with [[brute]] force or crude methods
> - Used in sports, art, writing, and negotiation contexts
