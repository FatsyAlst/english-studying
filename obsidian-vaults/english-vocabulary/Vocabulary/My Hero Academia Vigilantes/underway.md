## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Adjective/Adverb* (very frequent)
In progress; in the process of being done or happening.

> [!example] Examples
> - The construction project is underway and should be completed by June.
> - Plans are underway for the conference.
> - Once the investigation was underway, more evidence emerged.

### Sense 2 — *Adverb* (frequent, nautical)
In motion; said of a ship that has begun moving.

> [!example] Examples
> - The ship is underway and heading toward port.
> - Once underway, the vessel maintained steady speed.

## 📜 Etymology
A nautical term composed of "under" and "way" (way = motion or passage). Historically used to describe a ship in motion, the term has extended to general usage meaning "in progress."

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Underway" and "under way" are both acceptable spellings, with "underway" being more common in modern usage.
> - Used in both formal and informal contexts.
> - Frequently used in news reports and official announcements.
