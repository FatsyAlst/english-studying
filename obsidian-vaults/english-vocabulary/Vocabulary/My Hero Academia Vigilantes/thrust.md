## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To push someone or something with force; to shove or propel forward

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A sudden forceful push; a powerful forward movement

### Sense 3 — *noun* (figurative)
The main purpose, point, or direction of something

> [!example] Examples
> - He thrust his hand into his pocket.
> - She thrust the sword forward with all her strength.
> - The thrust of his argument was compelling.
> - The rocket's thrust propelled it into space.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old Norse "thrysta" meaning to push or compress. The word has Germanic roots and entered Middle English with the sense of sudden forceful action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Past tense can be "thrust" (not "thrusted")
> - Implies forceful, direct action
> - Common in action/fighting scenes
