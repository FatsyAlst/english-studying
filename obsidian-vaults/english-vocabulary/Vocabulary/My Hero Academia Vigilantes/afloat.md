## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective/adverb* (common)
Floating on water; buoyant; on the surface of water.

### Sense 2 — *adjective/adverb* (figurative)
In circulation; widespread; circulating among people.

### Sense 3 — *adjective/adverb* (figurative)
Operating or surviving despite financial or other difficulties; staying solvent or afloat.

> [!example] Examples
> - The boat remained afloat despite the storm.
> - There are rumors afloat about a merger.
> - We're trying to keep the business afloat during this crisis.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "a-" (prefix indicating a state) and "float." Originally referred to the physical state of being floating, later extended to figurative uses.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1: literal meaning about floating on water
> - Sense 2: figurative meaning about rumors or news circulating
> - Sense 3: figurative meaning about survival or solvency
> - Can be both adjective and adverb
