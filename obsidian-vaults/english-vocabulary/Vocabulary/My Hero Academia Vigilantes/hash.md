## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (very frequent)
Chopped or diced food, especially meat and potatoes mixed together.

> [!example] Examples
> - The diner served corned beef hash for breakfast.
> - She made a quick hash from the leftover vegetables.
> - Hash browns are a popular side dish.

### Sense 2 — *Noun* (frequent)
A jumble or mess; a confused mixture.

> [!example] Examples
> - He made a hash of the project with his poor planning.
> - The situation was a complete hash of miscommunication.
> - She didn't want to make a hash of her first assignment.

### Sense 3 — *Phrasal Verb: "hash out"* (frequent)
To discuss and work through something thoroughly; to settle a disagreement.

> [!example] Examples
> - They need to hash out the details of the contract before signing.
> - Let's hash out our differences and find a solution.
> - We hashed out the plan over several meetings.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French "hacher" (to chop), derived from "hache" (axe). The word entered English in the 17th century referring to chopped or minced food.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Make a hash of" is an idiom meaning to do something poorly or badly.
> - "Hash out" implies reaching an agreement through discussion.
> - The noun "hash" can refer to both food and confusion/disorder.
