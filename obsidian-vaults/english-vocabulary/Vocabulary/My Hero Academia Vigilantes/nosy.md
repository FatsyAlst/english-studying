## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (common)

Inquisitive to an excessive degree; showing undue interest in other people's affairs

> [!example] Examples
> - The nosy neighbor was always prying into everyone's business.
> - It's nosy to ask such personal questions.
> - She had a nosy tendency to read other people's messages.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From "nose" + "-y" suffix. The image of sticking one's nose into places creates the metaphor for intrusive curiosity. The term has been used since the 1800s.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal and somewhat derogatory
> - Similar to "nosey" (alternate spelling)
> - Often describes gossipy or intrusive people
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[pry]] (from [[Dragon Ball Z]])
