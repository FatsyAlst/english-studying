## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

The bone that joins the shoulder blade to the breastbone, also called the clavicle

> [!example] Examples
> - She fractured her collarbone in a fall from her bike.
> - The collarbone is one of the most commonly broken bones in the human body.
> - His collarbone was visibly prominent due to his thin frame.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From English "collar" + "bone". The term refers to the bone's location at the base of the neck near the collar area. It's a compound word combining two Old English/Germanic origins.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Common in medical and anatomical contexts
> - The scientific/medical term is "clavicle"
> - Collarbones are one of the first bones to form in the fetus
