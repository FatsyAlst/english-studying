## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

The force that causes something to stick to a surface by removing air or reducing pressure; the process of drawing or sucking something toward a source

> [!example] Examples
> - The suction cups on the robot's hands allowed it to climb the wall.
> - The vacuum cleaner works by creating suction.
> - The drain was clogged, so there was no suction.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "sugere" meaning to suck, combined with the suffix "-tion". The word refers to the physical process of drawing in or adhering through reduced pressure.
