## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adverb* (common)
In a frank, open, and honest manner; without hiding the truth; candidly.

> [!example] Examples
> - Frankly, I don't think that plan will work.
> - To be frank, I'm not impressed by your performance.
> - Frankly speaking, we need to make some major changes.

## 📜 Etymology
Derived from the adjective "frank," which comes from Old French "franc" meaning "free" or "sincere." The "-ly" suffix converts it to an adverb.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used at the beginning of a sentence to introduce an honest opinion
> - Can be followed by a comma for emphasis
> - Indicates sincerity and direct communication
> - Common in both formal and informal speech
