## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A period of leave or rest taken by an employee, usually lasting several months to a year, often for study, travel, or personal reasons.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (formal)
A year of rest observed every seventh year in ancient Jewish tradition, when the land was left fallow.

> [!example] Examples
> - She took a sabbatical from her job to travel around the world.
> - The professor is on sabbatical to write a book.
> - Many companies offer sabbatical leaves after several years of employment.

## 📜 Etymology
From the biblical Hebrew word "shabbat" meaning "rest" or "the seventh day." The term refers to the ancient Jewish practice of leaving land fallow every seventh year, and later applied to extended rest periods for employees.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and professional context
> - Usually implies an extended break, not just vacation
> - Common in academic and corporate settings
> - Often unpaid but job is protected upon return
