## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A pair of protective spectacles that fit snugly around the eyes, typically worn for safety, swimming, or as a fashion accessory.

> [!example] Examples
> - The pilot wore aviator goggles to protect his eyes from the wind.
> - Swimming goggles help you see clearly underwater.
> - She wore trendy ski goggles for the winter sports competition.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "goggle" meaning to stare with wide eyes. The noun "goggles" refers to spectacles with large, prominent lenses, first recorded in the late 18th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Usually used in plural form ("goggles" not "goggle")
> - Common in sports, aviation, and industrial safety contexts
> - Often a distinctive feature in anime and manga character designs
