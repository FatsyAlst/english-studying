## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A loud noise or disturbance; a commotion or fuss, often involving confusion or angry argument.

> [!example] Examples
> - There was a ruckus in the hallway when the alarm went off.
> - The crowd kicked up a ruckus about the unfair decision.
> - Don't cause a ruckus; we need to keep things calm.

---
## 📜 Etymology
The origin is uncertain but possibly from a blend of "rumpus" and "fracas" or from Scandinavian roots. It entered American English in the 19th century and is primarily North American in use.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Ruckus" is informal and carries a somewhat lighthearted connotation compared to more serious disturbances.
> - Often used to describe noisy or chaotic situations.
> - Common in American English; less common in British English.
