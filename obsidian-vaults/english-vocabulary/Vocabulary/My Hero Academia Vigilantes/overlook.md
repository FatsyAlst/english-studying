## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To fail to notice or consider something; to ignore or disregard

### Sense 2 — *verb* (common)
To have a view of something from above; to look out over

### Sense 3 — *verb* (formal)
To supervise or manage; to oversee

> [!example] Examples
> - I overlooked an important detail in the report.
> - The balcony overlooks the beautiful valley below.
> - Don't overlook the warnings in the manual.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "over" + "look". Originally meant to look over something (supervise), later developed the meaning of failing to notice something by looking over it.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1 (miss/fail to notice) is most common in modern English
> - Sense 2 (view from above) is more literal and descriptive
> - Context determines which meaning applies
