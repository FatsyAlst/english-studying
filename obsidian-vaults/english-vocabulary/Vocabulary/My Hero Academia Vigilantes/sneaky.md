## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (common)

Done in a quiet or furtive way to avoid being noticed; deceitful or dishonest in a subtle manner

> [!example] Examples
> - He took a sneaky glance at her across the room.
> - That was a sneaky trick, but I caught you.
> - The villain used sneaky tactics to avoid detection by heroes.

## 📜 Etymology
From the verb "sneak" (of Scandinavian origin, possibly from Old Norse), combined with the -y suffix to form an adjective. The term carries connotations of stealth and deception.
