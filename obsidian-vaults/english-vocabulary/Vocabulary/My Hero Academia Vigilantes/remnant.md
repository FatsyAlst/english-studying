## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A small remaining quantity or part of something; a leftover or surviving portion of what was once larger or greater.

> [!example] Examples
> - The old building was a remnant of the city's historical past.
> - Only a small remnant of the original forest remains.
> - The fabric shop sold the remnants at a discount.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "remanant," derived from Latin "remanere" (to remain), composed of "re-" (again) and "manere" (to stay or dwell). The word entered English in the 14th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Remnant" often implies something that has survived or persists.
> - Commonly used in retail contexts (fabric remnants, sales remnants).
> - Can be used literally or metaphorically.
