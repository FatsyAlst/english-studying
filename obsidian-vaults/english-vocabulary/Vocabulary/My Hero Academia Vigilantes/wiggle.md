## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To move back and forth with small, twisting motions; to squirm or twist.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A small twisting or wriggling movement.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (figurative)
To escape or get out of a situation through clever maneuvering.

> [!example] Examples
> - The worm wiggled in the dirt.
> - Stop wiggling in your seat!
> - She tried to wiggle out of her responsibilities.
> - The baby gave a little wiggle of excitement.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Middle English and possibly related to "waggle" and "wag." The exact origin is uncertain, but it's been used since the 16th century to describe this type of movement.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Sense 1-2: literal physical movement
> - Sense 3: figurative meaning about escaping responsibility
> - Often used in informal, conversational contexts
> - Frequently associated with small creatures or children
