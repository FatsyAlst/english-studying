## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A place where an event, performance, or gathering is held, such as a concert hall, stadium, or meeting room.

> [!example] Examples
> - The concert venue was packed with thousands of fans.
> - They booked a beautiful venue for the wedding reception.
> - The conference is being held at the downtown convention center venue.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "venue" and Latin "venire" meaning "to come." Originally referred to the place from which a person comes, later evolved to mean the place where an event is held.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and professional tone
> - Commonly used in event planning and entertainment contexts
> - Often used in announcements and invitations
> - Can refer to indoor or outdoor locations
