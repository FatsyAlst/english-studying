## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A person who attacks or assaults another person, often violently and sometimes with criminal intent.

> [!example] Examples
> - The assailant fled the scene of the crime.
> - Police arrested the assailant within hours.
> - The victim could not identify the assailant.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From French "assaillant," derived from "assaillir" (to assail, attack). The word comes from Latin "assalire" (to jump upon), composed of "ad-" (toward) and "salire" (to jump or leap).

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Assailant" is a formal and legal term commonly used in crime reporting.
> - Often used in news reports and official documentation.
> - Related verb: "assail" (to attack physically or verbally).
