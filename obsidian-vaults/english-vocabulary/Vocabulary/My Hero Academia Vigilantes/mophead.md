## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (informal)

A person with thick, messy, or disheveled hair; someone whose hair resembles a mop in appearance.

> [!example] Examples
> - He was called a mophead because of his wild, curly hair.
> - The old mophead stared at him with confusion.
> - Her mophead of tangled locks made her look unkempt.

---
## 📜 Etymology
A compound word combining "mop" and "head," referring to the visual similarity between the texture of a mop and disheveled human hair. The term emerged in casual English usage, likely in the 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Mophead" is an informal and somewhat derogatory term for someone with unkempt hair.
> - It's descriptive rather than strictly insulting in some contexts.
> - Less commonly used in modern English; more prevalent in older usage.
