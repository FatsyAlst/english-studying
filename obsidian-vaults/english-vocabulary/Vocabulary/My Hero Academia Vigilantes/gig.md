## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A live performance or concert, especially one by a musician or band.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (common)
A temporary job or freelance engagement; a piece of work.

> [!example] Examples
> - The band has a gig at the local venue tonight.
> - She picked up a gig as a freelance graphic designer.
> - He plays several gigs a month to earn extra money.

## 📜 Etymology
Origin uncertain; possibly from the 18th century slang "jig" or related to a small carriage called a "gig." First used in musical contexts in the early 20th century.
