## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (very frequent)
A heavy metal object attached to a ship or boat by a cable and lowered to the seabed to keep the vessel in place.

> [!example] Examples
> - The ship dropped anchor in the bay.
> - They hauled up the anchor to prepare to leave port.
> - The old anchor was covered with barnacles.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (frequent)
To secure with an anchor; to fasten firmly in place.

> [!example] Examples
> - The boat was anchored offshore.
> - We anchored the tent with strong stakes.
> - The structure is anchored to the foundation.

### Sense 3 — *Phrasal Verb: "anchor down"* (frequent)
To secure firmly; to prevent from moving or being moved.

> [!example] Examples
> - We had to anchor down the loose panels during the storm.
> - The equipment needs to be anchored down before use.
> - They anchored down the heavy machinery to the floor.

## 📜 Etymology
From Greek "ankyre," through Latin "ancora." The word has been used in English since at least the 13th century, originally referring to nautical equipment and later extending to metaphorical uses.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Drop anchor" means to lower the anchor and come to rest.
> - "Weigh anchor" is a nautical term meaning to prepare to sail by pulling up the anchor.
> - "Anchor down" emphasizes the act of making something completely secure or fixed.
