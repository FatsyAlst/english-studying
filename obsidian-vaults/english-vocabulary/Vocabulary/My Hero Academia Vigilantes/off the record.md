## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *idiom* (common)
Not for official publication or record; intended to be confidential or private; not for attribution.

> [!example] Examples
> - The politician spoke off the record about the scandal.
> - Off the record, I think the company is struggling financially.
> - He told me off the record that he's considering retiring next year.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Derived from "record" meaning an official written account or documentation. The phrase emerged in journalism and diplomacy contexts to distinguish between official statements and private remarks.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often used in journalism, politics, and business contexts
> - Indicates information should not be publicly attributed or published
> - Common in interviews and confidential discussions
> - Can be used as an adverbial phrase at the beginning or end of a statement
