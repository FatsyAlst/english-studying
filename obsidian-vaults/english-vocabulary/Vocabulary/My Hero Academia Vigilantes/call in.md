## 📖 Definition
*phrasal verb* (common)

To ask someone to come or help; to summon; to telephone someone; to visit someone briefly

> [!example] Examples
> - We called in an expert to fix the problem.
> - She called in sick to work yesterday.
> - The police called in backup for the situation.
> - I'll call in on you this weekend.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "ceallian" (call) + "in". The phrasal verb combines the basic meaning of call with the directional particle "in" to suggest bringing something closer or into action.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Multiple meanings depending on context
> - "Call in sick" is very common in work contexts
> - Can mean summon, telephone, or visit
