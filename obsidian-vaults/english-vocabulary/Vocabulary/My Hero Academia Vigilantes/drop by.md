## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To visit someone briefly or unexpectedly, often on the way to somewhere else; to stop by.

> [!example] Examples
> - I'll drop by your house later this afternoon.
> - Feel free to drop by anytime you're in the neighborhood.
> - She dropped by the office to pick up some documents.

---

| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Verb | drop by, drops by, dropped by, dropping by |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- drop by (someone's house)
- drop by for (a visit)
- drop by later
- drop by the office

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "drop" (to fall or visit briefly) and "by" (near or at a location). This phrasal verb emerged in colloquial English to describe brief, casual visits.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Implies a brief, casual visit, often unplanned
> - Can be spontaneous or mentioned in advance
> - Informal and conversational
> - Similar to "stop by" or "visit"
