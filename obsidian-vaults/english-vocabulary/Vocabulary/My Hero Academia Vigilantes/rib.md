## 📖 Definition
*noun* (common)

One of the curved bones in the chest that protect the lungs and other organs; also to tease or make fun of someone

> [!example] Examples
> - The injury cracked several ribs on his left side.
> - Her friends would rib her about her love for organizing everything.
> - The doctor examined his ribs carefully after the accident.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Old English "ribb", related to German "Rippe". The word has Germanic roots and refers to the curved skeletal bones. The verb meaning "to tease" developed from the idea of poking someone gently.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - As a noun (anatomy): usually used in plural "ribs"
> - As a verb: means to tease gently or affectionately
