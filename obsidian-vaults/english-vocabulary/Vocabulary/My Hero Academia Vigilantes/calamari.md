## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

Squid prepared as food, typically fried in a ring shape and served as an appetizer or main dish.

> [!example] Examples
> - The seafood appetizer included calamari and shrimp.
> - Fried calamari is a popular dish in Mediterranean cuisine.
> - She ordered calamari with marinara sauce at the restaurant.

## 📜 Etymology
From Italian "calamaro," derived from Latin "calamarium" (writing pen), because of the squid's ink sac. The term entered English through Italian culinary influence, particularly from Mediterranean cuisine.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Calamari refers specifically to squid when prepared as food.
> - The plural form is typically the same as singular in English usage.
> - Traditional preparation involves frying or grilling.
