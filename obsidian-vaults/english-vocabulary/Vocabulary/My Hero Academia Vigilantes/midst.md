## 📖 Definition
*noun/preposition* (formal)

The middle point or central area of something; among or surrounded by

> [!example] Examples
> - In the midst of chaos, she remained calm.
> - He found himself in the midst of a dangerous situation.
> - The city was built in the midst of beautiful mountains.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "middes" related to "middle". The term originally referred to the physical middle point and evolved to include figurative uses for situations surrounded by events or circumstances.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Formal and literary in tone
> - Usually used in the phrase "in the midst of"
> - Common in narrative and descriptive writing
