## 📖 Definition
*Adjective* (very frequent)

Extremely unpleasant, annoying, or offensive; causing disgust or strong dislike.

> [!example] Examples
> - His obnoxious behavior made everyone uncomfortable.
> - That obnoxious noise kept me awake all night.
> - She made obnoxious comments about the other team.

## 📜 Etymology
From Latin "obnoxious" (exposed to harm, liable to), composed of "ob-" (toward) and "noxious" (harmful, poisonous). The meaning shifted in English usage to mean extremely unpleasant or offensive.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Obnoxious" is a strong negative descriptor used for people or behaviors.
> - Can describe both actions and the people who perform them.
> - Often used in informal contexts to express strong disapproval.
