## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (frequent)

A marine mollusk with eight or ten arms, including octopuses, squid, and cuttlefish; characterized by highly developed nervous systems and intelligence.

> [!example] Examples
> - The octopus is a highly intelligent cephalopod.
> - Cephalopods can change color and texture instantly.
> - Squid and cuttlefish are examples of cephalopods.

## 📜 Etymology
From Greek "cephalopus" (head-footed), composed of "kephal-" (head) and "pous" (foot). The word entered scientific English in the 19th century to classify this class of marine animals.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Cephalopods are among the most intelligent invertebrates.
> - They include octopuses, squid, cuttlefish, and nautiluses.
> - Known for their ability to change color and pattern.
