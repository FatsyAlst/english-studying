## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb* (common)
To move or shape something so that it is no longer straight; to flex joints in the body

### Sense 2 — *verb* (figurative)
To force someone to submit; to influence or persuade

### Sense 3 — *noun* (common)
A curved or angled part in something

> [!example] Examples
> - Bend your knees and touch your toes.
> - The road bends sharply to the left.
> - Don't bend the pages of the book.
> - He refused to bend to pressure from his enemies.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Old English "bendan" of Germanic origin. The word originally meant to bind or fasten and evolved to describe the physical action of bending.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Around the bend" = near, close by (also means crazy)
> - Can be used literally (physical bending) or figuratively (capitulating)
> - Past tense can be "bent" or "bended" (though "bent" is more common)
