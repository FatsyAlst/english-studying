## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *Noun* (very frequent)
A tool with a blade or series of teeth, used for cutting wood, metal, or other materials.

> [!example] Examples
> - He picked up a saw and began cutting the wood.
> - The carpenter's saw had a fine-toothed blade.
> - A handsaw is useful for detailed woodworking.

### Sense 2 — *Verb* (frequent)
Past tense of "saw": to cut using a saw; to move a saw back and forth.

> [!example] Examples
> - He sawed through the thick branch carefully.
> - They sawed the lumber into shorter pieces.
> - She had sawed the plank in half by hand.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "sagu," related to Old Norse "sög" and German "Säge." The word has Germanic roots and has been used in English for over a thousand years to describe the cutting tool.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Sawed" and "sawn" are both acceptable past participles in modern English.
> - Different saw types exist for different materials and purposes.
> - Often used in woodworking, construction, and craft contexts.
