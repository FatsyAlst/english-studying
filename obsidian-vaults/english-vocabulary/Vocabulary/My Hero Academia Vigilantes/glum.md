## 📖 Definition
*adjective* (common)

Sad, morose, and lacking in cheerfulness; showing a dejected mood

> [!example] Examples
> - He wore a glum expression after hearing the bad news.
> - She felt glum about missing the party.
> - The team looked glum after their defeat.

## 📜 Etymology
Possibly from dialectal English or Scandinavian roots, related to "gloom". The exact origin is uncertain, but it appeared in English in the 17th century meaning morose or sullen.

## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Informal but widely understood
> - Conveys sadness with a hint of sulleness
> - Often used to describe facial expressions and moods
