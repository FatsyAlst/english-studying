## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *phrasal verb* (common)
To go somewhere with someone else, often without being invited or expected to come; to follow along.

> [!example] Examples
> - Can I tag along with you to the movies?
> - He's always trying to tag along when we hang out.
> - The little kid wanted to tag along on the hike with the older children.

---
## 🌳 Word Family
| Part of Speech | Forms |
|----------------|-------|
| Verb | tag along, tags along, tagged along, tagging along |
| Noun | tag-along |

---
## 🔗 Collocations
- tag along with (someone)
- let (someone) tag along
- want to tag along
- tag along on (something)

---
## 📜 Etymology
Combination of "tag" (to follow or accompany) and "along" (in the same direction). "Tag" in this sense means to follow closely like a tag attached to clothing.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Often implies the person wasn't originally invited
> - Can have both positive and negative connotations
> - Informal and conversational
> - Common in youth-oriented or casual contexts
