## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (common)
A precious or semi-precious stone, typically cut and polished, used in jewelry; a gem.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (figurative)
Something that is treasured, valuable, or highly prized.

> [!example] Examples
> - The crown was decorated with jewels and gold.
> - This painting is the jewel of the museum's collection.
> - She wore a necklace with sparkling jewels.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "jouel," related to "jeu" meaning "game" or "toy." The word eventually came to refer to valuable ornamental stones.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Can be literal (actual gems) or figurative (something precious)
> - "Crown jewels" is a famous idiom referring to the most precious possessions
> - Often appears in formal or literary contexts
