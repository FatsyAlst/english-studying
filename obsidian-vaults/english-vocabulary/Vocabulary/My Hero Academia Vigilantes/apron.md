## 📖 Definition
*Noun* (very frequent)

A garment worn over clothes to protect them from dirt or damage, typically tied around the waist and covering the front of the body.

> [!example] Examples
> - The chef wore a white apron while cooking.
> - She tied on her apron before starting to paint.
> - The carpenter's apron had many pockets for tools.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old French "naperon," derived from "nappe" (tablecloth, cloth). The word has been used in English since the 14th century to describe a protective garment.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Aprons are commonly worn in kitchens, workshops, and art studios.
> - Come in various styles and designs depending on the use.
> - "Tied to someone's apron strings" is an idiom meaning overly dependent on someone.
