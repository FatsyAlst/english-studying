## 📖 Definition
*Verb* (frequent)

To leave hastily or quickly, often in response to a threat or command; to run away or get out.

> [!example] Examples
> - "Scram! Get out of here!" the security guard shouted.
> - The kids scrambled when they heard the principal coming.
> - They told him to scram and not come back.

## 🔗 Collocations
- scram out
- scram away
- tell someone to scram
- scram from (a place)

---
## 📜 Etymology
The origin is uncertain but likely from German "schrammen" (to scratch or scrape), possibly suggesting a hasty escape that involves scrambling. It entered American English slang in the early 20th century.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - "Scram" is informal and somewhat old-fashioned slang.
> - It carries an imperative tone when used as a command.
> - Often used in situations of urgency or danger.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[bolted]] (from [[Dragon Ball Super]])
