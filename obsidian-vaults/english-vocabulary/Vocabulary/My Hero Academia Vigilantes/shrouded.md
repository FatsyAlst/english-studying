## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *adjective* (common)
Covered with or wrapped in something; concealed or hidden from view.

### Sense 2 — *adjective* (figurative)
Obscured, hidden, or made mysterious; surrounded by secrecy or mystery.

> [!example] Examples
> - The mountain peaks were shrouded in mist and fog.
> - His past is shrouded in mystery and secrets.
> - The corpse was shrouded in white cloth for the funeral.

## 📜 Etymology
From Old English "scroud" or "shroud," originally meaning a garment or covering. Later came to mean a cloth wrapper for the dead or anything that conceals.
