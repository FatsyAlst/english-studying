## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *verb (present participle)* (Common)
*Delve into* = to examine or research something carefully and in detail.

> [!example] Examples
> - In today's lecture we'll be delving into thermodynamics.
> - The book delves into the history of the city.
> - I'd rather not delve too deeply into my personal life.

### Sense 2 — *verb* (Less Common)
To reach inside a bag, pocket or container to search for something.

> [!example] Examples
> - She delved into her bag for her keys.
> - He delved in his pocket and found a coin.

---
## 📜 Etymology
Old English *delfan* "to dig"; the figurative meaning "to search deeply, research" developed later.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Almost always followed by *into*.
> - More formal than *look into* or *dig into*; typical of academic writing and lectures.
> - Overused in essays it can sound artificial — *delve into* became famous as a word that AI-generated texts love.
