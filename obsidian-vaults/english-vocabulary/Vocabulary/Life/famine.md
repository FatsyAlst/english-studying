## 📖 Definition
*noun* (Common)
A situation in which a large number of people have little or no food for a long time, and many of them die.

> [!example] Examples
> - The famine killed millions of people.
> - Drought and war led to famine in the region.
> - Aid agencies are trying to prevent a famine.

---
## 📜 Etymology
From Old French *famine*, from Vulgar Latin *famina, from Latin *famēs* "hunger" — the same root as [[famished]].

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - Refers to a whole population; for one person use *starving* or [[famished]].
> - *Drought* (no rain) often causes *famine* (no food).
> - Figurative but rare: *a famine of new ideas*.
> - Portuguese *fome* comes from the same Latin word *famēs*.
