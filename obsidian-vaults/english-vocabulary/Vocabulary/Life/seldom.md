## 📖 Definition
*adverb* (Common — Formal)
Not often; rarely.

> [!example] Examples
> - I seldom eat breakfast on weekdays.
> - It seldom snows in Brazil.
> - He seldom, if ever, complains.
> - Seldom have I seen such a beautiful sunset. (formal, inverted)

---
## 📜 Etymology
Old English *seldum* (earlier *seldan*) "rarely"; related to German *selten* and Dutch *zelden*.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - More formal than *rarely* or *not often*; in speech people usually say *hardly ever*.
> - Position: before the main verb, after *be* — *I seldom go*, *He is seldom late*.
> - Fronted for emphasis it needs inversion: *Seldom **have I** seen…* (very formal).
> - Already negative in meaning — don't add *not*.
> - Contrast with [[every now and then]] (= occasionally).
