## 📖 Definition
### Sense 1 — *noun* (Common)
Courage and determination that make you keep going when things are difficult.

> [!example] Examples
> - Finishing an engineering degree takes grit.
> - She showed real grit after failing the first exam.
> - The team played with grit and won in overtime.

### Sense 2 — *noun* (Common)
Very small pieces of stone or sand; (technical) the coarseness of sandpaper.

> [!example] Examples
> - I've got some grit in my shoe.
> - They spread grit on the icy roads.
> - Use 400-grit sandpaper for a smooth finish.

### Sense 3 — *verb* (Common)
*Grit your teeth* = to press your teeth together; to accept something unpleasant with determination.

> [!example] Examples
> - I gritted my teeth and finished the run.
> - Just grit your teeth — it's only two more weeks of exams.

## 📜 Etymology
Old English *grēot* "sand, gravel, dust". The sense "courage, firmness of character" is first recorded in American English in 1808 — the idea of something hard that resists being ground down.

---
## 💡 Usage Notes
> [!tip] Notes
> - *Grit* (determination) is a popular term in psychology and education.
> - *Gritty* can mean determined, or harsh and realistic (*a gritty drama*).
> - Sandpaper grit: the lower the number, the coarser the paper.
> - ⚠️ Similar meaning in [[guts]] (from [[Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo]])
