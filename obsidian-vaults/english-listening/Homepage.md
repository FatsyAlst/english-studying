## How I listen
Five steps. Can apply it almost always.

1. **Listen once, with no transcript.**
2. **Listen again, pausing.** Note the timestamps where I lost the thread.
3. **Open the transcript.** For each gap, write down what I heard, what they said, and why I missed it.
4. **Shadow a minute.** Speak along with the audio, matching rhythm and linking, not just the words.
5. **Keep the words.** New words go to the english-vocabulary [Vocabulary To-Do List](obsidian://open?vault=english-vocabulary&file=Resources%2FVocabulary%20To-Do%20List).
